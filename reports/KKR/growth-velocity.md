| FY2027E | $7.97 | +20.9% |

### EPS Acceleration: ✅ MASSIVE INFLECTION

The FY2025 EPS trough of $2.34 was driven by:
1. Lower carried interest realizations (timing issue, not structural)
2. Private credit mark-to-market headwinds
3. One-time carried interest repayment obligation

The FY2026E jump to $6.59 reflects:
1. $19B in embedded gains beginning to monetize
2. Record fundraising translating to higher management fees
3. Arctos acquisition earnings contribution
4. Infrastructure investment returns crystallizing

**Consensus EPS growth of +182% in FY2026 is one of the highest among S&P 500 companies.**

## Profitability Path

KKR is already highly profitable — the question is **growth velocity of high-quality earnings:**

1. **Management fees** (recurring, sticky): Growing 24% YoY
2. **Carried interest** (lumpy but high-margin): $19B embedded pipeline
3. **Insurance** (Global Atlantic): Generates permanent capital + investment spread
4. **Capital markets fees**: Transaction-driven, growing with deployment

---

## Growth Velocity Verdict: ACCELERATING ✅

**What the market sees:** FY2025 EPS decline of -29%, negative headline revenue growth
**What's actually happening:** Record fundraising, accelerating quarterly revenue, management fee growth of 24%, $19B in embedded gains, K Series doubling annually

**The disconnect between headline GAAP numbers and underlying business momentum is the opportunity.** KKR's high-quality recurring fee base is growing 20%+ while the market prices it at 11x forward earnings because of private credit sector contagion.
