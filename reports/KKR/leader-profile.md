# KKR & Co. (KKR) — Leader Profile 👤

*Research date: March 9, 2026*

---

## Co-CEOs: Scott Nuttall & Joseph Bae

KKR operates with a dual-CEO structure, with both leaders having been at the firm for decades:

### Scott Nuttall (Co-CEO since Oct 2021)
- **Background:** Joined KKR in 1996 straight from Goldman Sachs. Previously ran KKR's Capital Markets, Credit, and Client & Partner Group. Architect of KKR's credit platform expansion.
- **Style:** Capital markets and credit-focused. Built the engine that drives KKR's fee-related earnings. Known for strategic deal-making.
- **Key accomplishment:** Grew KKR's credit and capital markets businesses from near-zero to KKR's largest revenue contributor.

### Joseph Bae (Co-CEO since Oct 2021)
- **Background:** Joined KKR in 1996 (same class as Nuttall). Previously led KKR's Asia-Pacific operations and built it into a $50B+ platform. Harvard Business School.
- **Style:** Operations and global expansion focused. Turned KKR Asia into the firm's most profitable region per dollar invested.
- **Key accomplishment:** KKR's Asia-Pacific franchise, now a significant contributor to fundraising and returns.

### Founders: Henry Kravis & George Roberts
- Transitioned to Executive Co-Chairmen in Oct 2021.
- Still highly involved in firm strategy and LP relationships.
- Combined ownership stake remains substantial (part of the 23.11% insider ownership).

## Insider Buying Activity — THE SIGNAL 🔥

### Recent Purchases (Feb-March 2026)

The co-CEOs have been buying aggressively into the sell-off:

- **175,000 shares purchased** while retail investors were selling
- **March 4, 2026:** Co-CEOs purchased another **$8.8 million** in stock (Barron's reported "KKR Co-CEOs Continue Buying the Dip")
- **February-March 2026:** Multiple tranches, buying consistently as the stock declined from ~$120 to ~$90

### Why This Matters

1. **Insider ownership is 23.11%** — among the highest of any S&P 500 company. Management's wealth is deeply tied to the stock price.
2. **Open market purchases** (not options exercises or grants) — they're spending their own cash.
3. **Co-CEOs buying simultaneously** — it's not one executive's personal view, it's a coordinated signal from both leaders.
4. **Buying into sector panic** — they clearly believe the private credit fears are overdone relative to KKR's actual exposure.

### Insider Transaction Net: -14.14%

The negative insider transaction percentage reflects some routine selling/tax-related transactions, but the recent open-market buying spree at the lows is the dominant signal. Scale and timing of buying matters more than routine option-exercise sales.
