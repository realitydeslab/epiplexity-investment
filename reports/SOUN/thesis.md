# SOUN — Investment Thesis (1-Page)

*Updated: 2026-03-09*

## The Catalyst

SoundHound AI is approaching an **EBITDA breakeven inflection point** in late 2026, with $225M–$260M revenue guidance (+34–54% YoY) after nearly doubling revenue in FY2025. The Amelia acquisition created an enterprise conversational AI platform spanning restaurants, automotive, financial services, and healthcare. Five Guys expansion (1M+ AI ordering interactions) proves the product works at scale.

## The Trade

- **Entry:** ~$8.07 (near 52-week low of $7.00, down 63% from highs)
- **Base target:** $13.00 (+61%) — if guidance is met and margins improve
- **Bull target:** $20.00 (+148%) — if EBITDA breakeven achieved + short squeeze (35.6% SI)
- **Stop-loss:** $6.50 (-19%)
- **Asymmetry:** 3:1 risk/reward in base case

## The Risk

- **Unprofitable** — $58M adj. EBITDA loss in FY2025, $98M cash burn
- **Competition** — Amazon, Google, Microsoft have vastly more resources
- **Insider selling** — $33M sold by insiders, zero buys in 12 months 🚩
- **Valuation** — $3.8B market cap on $169M revenue = 22x P/S (still expensive)
- **Growth decelerating** — from 99% FY2025 to ~44% guided FY2026

## The Verdict

**SPECULATIVE BUY — Small Position Only (2-3% of portfolio)**

The setup is interesting: near 52-week lows, massive short interest creating squeeze potential, and a credible path to profitability. But the red flags are real — insider selling, big tech competition, and still-expensive valuation. This is a high-conviction-required, small-position trade. The asymmetry favors longs at these levels, but size accordingly.

**Epiplexity Score: 72/100** — Interesting catalyst setup but execution risk is high.
