# SOUN — Structured Intelligence

*Updated: 2026-03-09*

## Sector Signal

SoundHound sits at the intersection of two mega-trends:
1. **Enterprise AI adoption** — companies replacing human tasks with AI agents
2. **Voice-first commerce** — ordering food, booking services, transacting via voice

The fact that Five Guys renewed and expanded after 1M+ AI interactions validates that voice AI ordering WORKS at scale. This is a leading indicator for the entire QSR (quick service restaurant) industry adopting voice AI.

## Adjacent Plays

- **Restaurant tech:** Toast (TOST), Olo (OLO) — if voice ordering grows, these platforms integrate
- **Contact center AI:** Five9 (FIVN), NICE (NICE) — same enterprise AI buyer
- **Auto voice AI:** Cerence (CRNC) — direct auto competitor, struggling
- **AI infrastructure:** Benefits from general AI compute buildout (NVDA, cloud providers)

## Contrarian Angle — What's the Market Missing?

1. **35.6% short interest may be overdone.** Bears focus on unprofitability, but the path to breakeven is credible at ~$250M revenue with 60%+ non-GAAP gross margins.
2. **Amelia integration is undervalued.** Market sees it as dilutive acquisition. Reality: it brought hundreds of enterprise customers in finance/insurance/healthcare that SoundHound can now cross-sell voice AI to.
3. **Agentic AI re-categorization.** SoundHound is still classified as "voice AI" when it's becoming an "agentic commerce" platform. If the market recognizes this, multiple expansion follows.

## What Could Go Wrong

1. **Competition from big tech** — if Amazon/Google seriously target enterprise voice ordering
2. **Gross margin compression** — if custom enterprise deals require heavy professional services
3. **Customer concentration risk** — dependency on few large QSR/auto clients
4. **The AI bubble pops** — broad sentiment shift away from AI growth stocks
