## Growth Assessment: DECELERATING but still strong

- FY2025 nearly doubled revenue (+99%), but Q4 growth rate (59% YoY) is slowing from mid-year peaks
- Guidance midpoint ($242.5M) implies ~44% growth — meaningful deceleration from 99%
- **However:** Absolute dollar growth remains impressive. $55M→$60-65M/Q trajectory is healthy.
- QoQ growth slowed in Q4 (+12%), suggesting lumpiness in deal timing
