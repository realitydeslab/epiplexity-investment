# SOUN — Competitive Map

*Updated: 2026-03-09*

## Direct Competitors

### Amazon (AMZN) — Alexa for Business
- Vastly larger resources, established ecosystem
- However, Alexa has struggled with enterprise monetization
- SoundHound advantage: purpose-built for enterprise voice ordering, not a consumer assistant

### Google (GOOGL) — Dialogflow / Contact Center AI
- Deep NLP capabilities, massive scale
- SoundHound advantage: independence (not tied to Google ecosystem), better suited for multi-platform deployment

### Nuance/Microsoft (MSFT) — Dragon, Azure AI Speech
- Enterprise stronghold, especially healthcare (DAX)
- SoundHound advantage: more agile, purpose-built for restaurant/auto verticals

### LivePerson (LPSN)
- Conversational AI for enterprise, struggling financially
- SoundHound in stronger position post-Amelia acquisition

## Who Benefits from Same Catalysts?

- **Verint (VRNT)** — Enterprise conversational AI
- **Five9 (FIVN)** — Contact center AI (also on watchlist, pending)
- **NICE (NICE)** — CX AI solutions

## Who Loses if SoundHound Wins?

- **Traditional call center operators** — replaced by AI ordering
- **LivePerson (LPSN)** — direct competitor losing share
- **Legacy IVR providers** — outdated voice menu systems

## Pair Trade Opportunities

- **Long SOUN / Short LPSN** — both in conversational AI, SOUN gaining share while LPSN struggles
- **Long SOUN + Long FIVN** — basket approach to enterprise AI voice/contact center
