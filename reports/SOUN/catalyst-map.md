# SOUN — Catalyst Map (12-Month Focus)

*Updated: 2026-03-09*

## 1. Revenue Acceleration → EBITDA Breakeven (Q3-Q4 2026)

- **What:** Management targets adjusted EBITDA breakeven by late 2026. Guidance: $225M–$260M revenue (34–54% YoY growth after 99% in FY2025).
- **When:** Q3-Q4 2026 earnings (expected Aug & Nov 2026)
- **Impact:** +30–50%. Profitability inflection is the single most important catalyst for re-rating.
- **Probability:** Medium. Revenue trajectory supports it, but gross margins (47.9% GAAP) need expansion.
- **Market expectation:** Skeptical — 35.6% short interest suggests market doubts profitability path.
- **Our edge:** If restaurant/auto voice AI adoption accelerates faster than bears expect, operating leverage kicks in. Five Guys renewal (1M+ interactions) proves sticky enterprise demand.

## 2. Major Enterprise Partnership Announcement (H1-H2 2026)

- **What:** New tier-1 enterprise deal (fast food chain, auto OEM, or healthcare system). Amelia acquisition expanded reach to hundreds of enterprise brands.
- **When:** Could come any quarter — watch for press releases
- **Impact:** +15–25% on announcement.
- **Probability:** High. Pipeline strong with record Q4 deal closings.
- **Market expectation:** Priced in partially.
- **Our edge:** Amelia integration unlocking cross-sell into financial services, insurance, healthcare — verticals not yet in consensus models.

## 3. Short Squeeze Potential (Any time)

- **What:** 134.9M shares short (~35.6% of float). Any positive surprise could trigger forced covering.
- **When:** Unpredictable — most likely around earnings or major news
- **Impact:** +40–100% in squeeze scenario
- **Probability:** Low-Medium. High short interest necessary but not sufficient.
- **Our edge:** Concentrated short positioning creates asymmetric upside if any catalyst hits.

## 4. Agentic AI Wave — Platform Positioning (H1-H2 2026)

- **What:** CEO positioned SoundHound as "agentic AI" platform — AI agents that take actions (place orders, schedule, transact), not just answer questions.
- **When:** Ongoing through 2026
- **Impact:** +20–30% if SoundHound captures meaningful share of agentic commerce.
- **Probability:** Medium. Narrative is real but competition fierce.

## 5. Automotive AI Expansion (H2 2026)

- **What:** Voice Commerce for Auto connecting drivers to merchants. Potential new OEM partnerships.
- **When:** H2 2026
- **Impact:** +10–20%.
- **Probability:** Medium. Auto deals slow but sticky.

## 6. Index Inclusion / Analyst Coverage Expansion

- **What:** Currently covered by H.C. Wainwright ($20 PT) and DA Davidson ($14 PT). More coverage = more institutional buying.
- **When:** Throughout 2026
- **Impact:** +10–15% per major coverage initiation
- **Probability:** Medium-High.

*Sources: Q4 2025 earnings (Feb 26, 2026), Motley Fool, Seeking Alpha, Barchart, MarketBeat*
