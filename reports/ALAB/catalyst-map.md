# ALAB Catalyst Map — Astera Labs

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## 1. Q1 2026 Earnings Beat (~May 2026)

- **What:** Q1 2026 guidance of $286-297M massively above Street consensus of $259M at time of guidance. Management guided 6-9% sequential growth after a 92% YoY Q4.
- **When:** Early-mid May 2026
- **Impact:** +15-25% if beat-and-raise continues. Prior Q4 miss of buyside whisper ($280M+) caused 10% drop despite beating consensus.
- **Probability:** High — management has a pattern of guiding conservatively then beating.
- **Market expectation:** Consensus ~$290M. Buyside whisper likely $300M+.
- **Our edge:** Scorpio production shipments started Jan 2026 and Taurus 400G ramp should drive incremental revenue not yet fully modeled. The connectivity bottleneck narrative is accelerating.

## 2. Scorpio X-Series Fabric Switch Ramp (H1-H2 2026)

- **What:** Production shipments began Jan 22, 2026. Targeting the merchant scale-up switching market projected at $20B by 2030. Five new feature areas: increased radix, hyperscaler-specific protocols, in-network computing, Hypercast, and optical connectivity.
- **When:** Revenue contribution ramps through 2026, meaningful by Q2-Q3.
- **Impact:** +20-30% if Scorpio becomes a material revenue contributor ($50M+ quarterly run rate by year end).
- **Probability:** Medium-High — initial deployments with hyperscalers confirmed.
- **Market expectation:** Street models limited Scorpio contribution in 2026.
- **Our edge:** CEO called Scorpio an "anchor SoC" at Morgan Stanley conference. Hyperscaler co-development suggests design wins are locked in.

## 3. Taurus 400G/800G AEC Ramp (2026-2027)

- **What:** Active electrical cables were the fastest-growing product in Q4 2025. Ramping from 200G to 400G deployments, with 800G coming to market.
- **When:** 400G revenue Q1-Q2 2026; 800G later in 2026.
- **Impact:** +10-15%. Diversifies customer base beyond largest customer.
- **Probability:** High — natural upgrade cycle.
- **Our edge:** 800G solutions open new customers, reducing concentration risk — a key bear argument.

## 4. Amazon $466M Warrant Deal (2026)

- **What:** New warrant for Amazon to buy ~$466M in ALAB stock. Amazon owned $43M as of Dec 31. Strategic alignment signal.
- **When:** Ongoing through 2026.
- **Impact:** +5-10% sentiment. Validates strategic importance.
- **Probability:** High (already announced).
- **Our edge:** Warrant structure means Amazon is incentivized to grow business with Astera.

## 5. CXL Adoption Inflection (H2 2026 - 2027)

- **What:** Leo CXL controllers now in Azure private beta. General-purpose compute deployment expected to begin later in 2026.
- **When:** H2 2026 for initial deployments, ramp into 2027.
- **Impact:** +10-20% if CXL adoption accelerates.

- **Market expectation:** Most analysts have minimal CXL revenue modeled for 2026.
- **Our edge:** Azure private beta is a concrete milestone. First-mover advantage if CXL-capable CPUs ship at scale.

## 6. PCIe Gen6 Leadership (Ongoing 2026)

- **What:** Astera is "probably the only company" shipping PCIe Gen6 retimer solutions in volume.
- **When:** Throughout 2026 as new GPU/accelerator platforms launch.
- **Impact:** Sustains Aries retimer growth at 70%+ YoY.
- **Probability:** High — already shipping.
- **Our edge:** Near-monopoly position in Gen6 retimers is underappreciated.

## 7. Macro: AI Capex Continuation (All 2026)

- **What:** Hyperscaler capex for 2026 remains robust. Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta all guiding higher AI infrastructure spending.
- **When:** Each hyperscaler earnings (April-May, July-Aug) validates this.
- **Impact:** +/- 20% depending on direction.
- **Probability:** High for continued spending, but growth rate deceleration is the risk.
- **Our edge:** Astera plays the connectivity bottleneck, which scales with GPU count regardless of which GPU wins.
