# ALAB Growth Velocity — Astera Labs

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## Revenue Trajectory

| Quarter | Revenue | YoY Growth | QoQ Growth |
|---------|---------|------------|------------|
| Q1 2025 | ~$113M (est) | ~100%+ | — |
| Q2 2025 | ~$238M (est) | ~100%+ | — |
| Q3 2025 | $230.6M | +104% | — |
| Q4 2025 | $271.0M | +92% | +17.5% |
| Q1 2026E | $286-297M (guided) | ~75-80% est | +6-10% |

**FY2025 Revenue:** ~$852M (9M: $581.9M + Q4: $271M)
**FY2026 Run Rate:** $286-297M x 4 = ~$1.14-1.19B annualized from Q1 alone

## Growth Assessment: DECELERATING from ~100% but STILL VERY FAST

- YoY growth declining from 104% (Q3) to 92% (Q4) to ~75-80% (Q1E) — natural deceleration from a high base.
- QoQ sequential growth of 17.5% in Q4 and guided 6-10% in Q1 is solid.
- The key question: does Scorpio ramp re-accelerate growth in H2 2026?

## Product Mix Growth

- **Aries retimers:** +70% YoY in 2025. Maturing but still growing — PCIe Gen6 leadership sustains this.
- **Taurus AECs:** Fastest grower in Q4 2025. Scorpio + Taurus collectively = 30% of total revenue. Ramping from 200G to 400G.
- **Scorpio switches:** Production shipments began Jan 2026. This is the growth engine for H2 2026+.
- **Leo CXL:** Slower than expected. Azure private beta is positive but not a 2026 revenue driver.

## Profitability

| Metric | Q3 2025 | Q4 2025 |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Net Income | $91.1M | $45.0M |
| EPS (diluted) | $0.50 | $0.58 (beat $0.51 consensus) |
| Gross Margin | Strong (improving with scale) | Strong |

- Company is solidly profitable — not a speculative pre-revenue play.
- Q4 net income dipped QoQ ($45M vs $91.1M Q3), potentially due to one-time costs or mix shift. EPS still beat consensus.

## Guidance vs. Actual History

Management guides conservatively and beats. Q4 beat on both top and bottom line (revenue $271M vs $250M consensus, EPS $0.58 vs $0.51). Q1 2026 guide of $286-297M was 10%+ above Street at $259M.

**Pattern: guide conservatively → beat → raise.** This is a high-quality earnings cycle.

## Unit Economics

- Software-first approach (COSMOS platform) creates stickiness and potential for higher margins over time.
- Expanding from retimers (ASP ~$X) to switches (Scorpio, much higher ASP) should improve blended revenue per customer.
- Israel R&D center adds costs but is necessary to support growth trajectory.

## Path to $1B+ Revenue

At Q1 2026 guided rate ($286-297M), annualized run rate is ~$1.15B. FY2026 revenue likely $1.2-1.4B depending on Scorpio ramp. That's ~40-65% YoY growth on a much larger base.

## Velocity Verdict

**Growth is decelerating from triple-digit but remains elite** (~75-80% YoY). The critical variable is Scorpio: if it ramps to $50-100M quarterly revenue by Q4 2026, growth re-accelerates. If it disappoints, YoY growth could slow to 50% range. Either way, this is one of the fastest-growing semiconductor companies in the market.
