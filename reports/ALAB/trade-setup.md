## Risk Management

- **Stop-loss:** $95 (-20%). Below $100 psychological support invalidates the technical setup.
- **Position sizing:** 3-5% of portfolio. High-growth semiconductor with binary catalyst risk (Scorpio ramp).
- **What kills the trade:** Hyperscaler capex cuts. If NVIDIA, Microsoft, or Amazon signal reduced AI infrastructure spend, ALAB sells off hard. Also: if Scorpio loses a key design win to Broadcom or Marvell.
- **Correlation risk:** Highly correlated with NVDA, MRVL, CRDO. A broader AI semiconductor selloff takes ALAB down regardless of fundamentals.
