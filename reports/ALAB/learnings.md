# ALAB Learnings — Astera Labs

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## What This Tells Us About the Sector

1. **The connectivity bottleneck is real.** As GPU clusters scale from hundreds to thousands to tens of thousands, the interconnect becomes the binding constraint. Compute is abundant; moving data between compute nodes is the problem. This is a secular shift, not a cycle.

2. **"Sell the good news" pattern in AI semis.** ALAB beat on both top and bottom line in Q4 yet fell 10% because buyside whisper was higher. This pattern (positive product/partnership news → negative price reaction) suggests the market has moved from "buy anything AI" to demanding flawless execution. The easy money phase is over; now it's a stock-picker's market.

3. **Platform companies win.** Astera's COSMOS software platform is the real moat, not the chips. Hardware is commoditizable; software that integrates into customer operating stacks creates switching costs. This mirrors NVIDIA's CUDA moat at a different layer.

4. **Hyperscaler vertical integration is accelerating.** Amazon's $466M warrant deal shows hyperscalers want strategic equity stakes in key suppliers. This is good for Astera (validation) but signals that hyperscalers increasingly want to own or control their supply chain.

## Adjacent Plays

- **Corning (GLW):** Fiber optic bottleneck is the macro version of what Astera solves at the chip level. Meta's $6B deal validates. Already researched — Epiplexity score 81.
- **Arista Networks (ANET):** AI networking switches at the rack/datacenter level. Complementary to Astera's intra-server and scale-up connectivity.
- **Credo Technology (CRDO):** Direct competitor but also plays the same theme. If you believe in the connectivity bottleneck, a basket approach (ALAB + CRDO + GLW) reduces single-name risk.

## Contrarian Angle

The market is treating ALAB as a "too expensive" AI stock after the 55% drawdown. But at forward P/S of ~15-17x on 40-80% growth, it's actually cheaper than many slower-growing software companies. The contrarian case: Scorpio is a hidden growth engine that the market is undervaluing because it's new and unproven. If it ramps to even 20% of revenue by Q4 2026, the stock re-rates significantly.

**The biggest risk the market is missing:** CXL could be the sleeper catalyst. Azure private beta is the first real deployment. If CXL adoption follows the PCIe retimer adoption curve (slow, then explosive), Astera's first-mover advantage could unlock a new $5-10B TAM that nobody is modeling.

**The biggest risk the market sees correctly:** Customer concentration. If one customer (likely NVIDIA) represents 30-40%+ of revenue, any pause in NVIDIA's spending hits ALAB disproportionately.
