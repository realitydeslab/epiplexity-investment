# ALAB Investment Thesis — Astera Labs

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## One-Line Thesis
Astera Labs owns the AI connectivity bottleneck — the only pure-play company offering a full stack (retimers + cables + switches + CXL) for AI infrastructure, trading at a 55% discount from highs with 75%+ revenue growth.

## The Catalyst
**Scorpio X-Series fabric switch production shipments began Jan 2026**, targeting a $20B merchant scale-up switching market by 2030. This is Astera's "second act" — the transition from a retimer company to a full-stack AI connectivity platform. If Scorpio ramps to $50M+/quarter by Q4 2026, growth re-accelerates and the stock re-rates.

## The Trade
- **Entry:** ~$119 (current) or $100-110 on pullback
- **Target:** $175 (base, +47%) to $220 (bull, +85%)
- **Stop:** $95 (-20%)
- **Timeframe:** 12 months
- **Position size:** 3-5% of portfolio

## The Edge
1. **Beat-and-raise machine:** Q1 2026 guided $286-297M vs. Street $259M. Management consistently under-promises and over-delivers.
2. **Scorpio is undermodeled:** Street has limited Scorpio revenue in 2026 models. Initial production shipments + hyperscaler co-development suggest faster ramp.
3. **COSMOS software moat:** Software-first approach creates switching costs that hardware-only competitors can't match.
4. **Amazon strategic alignment:** $466M warrant deal signals deep partnership commitment.
5. **Valuation reset:** At ~15-17x forward P/S on 40-80% growth, it's cheaper than many slower-growing peers.

## The Risk
1. **Customer concentration:** Largest customer (likely NVIDIA) may represent 30-40%+ of revenue.
2. **AI capex deceleration:** Any hyperscaler capex cut hits ALAB hard — pure infrastructure play.
3. **Scorpio execution:** New product ramp always carries risk. Broadcom and Marvell are formidable competitors in switching.
4. **Buyside whisper > consensus:** Stock can drop on "beats" if whisper number is higher. Happened in Q4.

## The Verdict
**BUY** at current levels with a 12-month target of $175 (base). The 55% drawdown has created an attractive entry point for a company growing 75%+ with a clear product roadmap. Scorpio is the swing factor — if it ramps, this re-rates to $200+. If it disappoints, downside is contained at ~$85-95 (-20-25%).

**Conviction: 7/10.** High growth and clear moat, but customer concentration and the "sell the good news" pattern require patience.
