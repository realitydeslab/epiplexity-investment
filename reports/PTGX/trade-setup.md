### What Kills the Trade?

1. **FDA Complete Response Letter (CRL)** — rusfertide not approved. Stock drops 40–50%. Probability: 15–20%.
2. **Icotyde Phase 2b/3 failure** — oral psoriasis doesn't match biologics. Removes $3–5B of long-term value.
3. **Commercial execution failure** — weak launch, can't build salesforce fast enough. Slower bleed, not a crash.
4. **M&A falls through at lower price** — acquirer bids below current price, or bidding war doesn't materialize.
5. **Dilutive financing** — unexpected equity raise to fund commercialization. Less likely given $567M cash.
