### Understanding the Revenue Trajectory

The revenue picture is distorted by one-time events:

1. **FY 2024 ($434M):** This was NOT recurring revenue. It was primarily the massive one-time payment from Takeda opting out of the rusfertide profit-share agreement. Think of it as Takeda "buying out" their commercial rights.

2. **FY 2025 ($46M):** The "real" underlying revenue — collaboration and licensing income without the Takeda windfall.

3. **FY 2026E ($373M avg):** The inflection point. If rusfertide launches in Q4 2026 post-PDUFA approval, analysts expect rapid uptake. The wide range ($19.6M–$588.5M) reflects uncertainty about launch timing and speed.
