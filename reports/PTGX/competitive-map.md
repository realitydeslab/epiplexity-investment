## Polycythemia Vera (Rusfertide) Competitive Landscape

### Current Standard of Care
- **Phlebotomy** — invasive, burdensome, patients hate it
- **Hydroxyurea** — cytoreductive, side effects, old drug
- **Jakafi (ruxolitinib, Incyte/INCY)** — JAK inhibitor, approved for PV but has side effects (anemia, thrombocytopenia)
- **Besremi (ropeginterferon alfa-2b, PharmaEssentia/DAVA)** — newer option, interferon-based, gaining share

### Rusfertide's Differentiation
- **First-in-class hepcidin mimetic** — completely new mechanism
- Specifically reduces iron-driven erythrocytosis WITHOUT cytoreduction
- Targets the root cause (iron-driven red blood cell overproduction) rather than symptoms
- Reduces/eliminates need for phlebotomy
- No direct competitor with same mechanism

### Key Competitors & Their Response

| Company | Drug | Mechanism | Threat Level |
|---------|------|-----------|-------------|
| Incyte (INCY) | Jakafi (ruxolitinib) | JAK inhibitor | LOW — different mechanism, different patients |
| PharmaEssentia (DAVA) | Besremi | Interferon | MEDIUM — growing, but injection burden similar |
| CTI BioPharma (acquired) | Vonjo (pacritinib) | JAK/PAK inhibitor | LOW — myelofibrosis focused |
| **No one** | Hepcidin mimetic | Same class | **NONE — first-in-class monopoly** |

**Assessment:** Rusfertide has NO direct competitor in its mechanism class. The closest threat is Besremi gaining more market share, but the two drugs likely address different patient subpopulations and could be complementary.

## Psoriasis (Icotyde) Competitive Landscape

### Current Market Leaders (All Injectable)
- **AbbVie:** Skyrizi (risankizumab) — IL-23, $10B+ peak sales
- **J&J:** Tremfya (guselkumab) — IL-23, ~$4B sales
- **UCB:** Bimzelx (bimekizumab) — IL-17A/F, growing fast
- **Bristol-Myers:** Sotyktu (deucravacitinib) — TYK2 inhibitor, **ORAL**, launched 2023

### Icotyde's Differentiation
- **First oral IL-23 inhibitor** — the holy grail of psoriasis treatment
- Current IL-23 blockers (Skyrizi, Tremfya) are all injectables
- Sotyktu (BMS) is oral but a TYK2 inhibitor — different mechanism, lower efficacy
- If icotyde matches injectable IL-23 efficacy in an oral form, it's a potential $5B+ drug

### Direct Competitors in Oral Psoriasis

| Company | Drug | Mechanism | Stage | Threat Level |
|---------|------|-----------|-------|-------------|
| BMS | Sotyktu (deucravacitinib) | TYK2 | Approved | MEDIUM — oral but lower efficacy |
| AbbVie | Multiple | Various oral | Phase 1-2 | MEDIUM — resources |
| Nimbus (Takeda) | NDI-101150 | TYK2 | Phase 2 | LOW-MEDIUM |
| **PTGX** | **Icotyde** | **Oral IL-23** | **Phase 2b/3** | **LEADER in oral IL-23** |

## Who Benefits from the Same Catalysts?

- **PharmaEssentia (DAVA):** PV awareness rising → all PV drugs benefit
- **Incyte (INCY):** Same hematology prescriber base — rusfertide awareness helps all PV therapies
- **Peptide/oral biologic platform companies:** PTGX success validates the modality for others

## Who Loses if PTGX Wins?

- **Phlebotomy clinics/blood banks:** Reduced phlebotomy volume in PV patients
- **Generic hydroxyurea makers:** Market share erosion in PV
- **BMS (Sotyktu):** If icotyde proves oral IL-23 > oral TYK2, Sotyktu loses competitive positioning
