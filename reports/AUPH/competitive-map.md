# AUPH Competitive Map — Aurinia Pharmaceuticals

*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

## 🗺️ Lupus Nephritis Treatment Landscape

### Current Market Position

LUPKYNIS (voclosporin) is the **only FDA-approved oral therapy specifically for lupus nephritis**. This is a powerful moat. Competitors include:

| Product | Company | Status | Mechanism | Threat Level |
|---------|---------|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **LUPKYNIS** (voclosporin) | **Aurinia (AUPH)** | Approved (2021) | Calcineurin inhibitor | N/A — incumbent |
| **Benlysta** (belimumab) | GSK (GSK) | Approved (2020 for LN) | Anti-BAFF monoclonal | Medium |
| **Atacicept** | Vera Therapeutics (VERA) | PDUFA July 7, 2026 | Dual BAFF/APRIL (TACI-Fc) | Medium-High* |
| **Telitacicept** | RemeGen (9995.HK) | Approved in China | Dual BAFF/APRIL (TACI-Fc) | Low (US) |
| **Obinutuzumab** | Roche (RHHBY) | Phase 3 (REGENCY) | Anti-CD20 | Low-Medium |
| **Anifrolumab** (Saphnelo) | AstraZeneca (AZN) | Phase 3 for LN | Anti-IFNAR1 | Low-Medium |

*Atacicept is primarily targeting IgA nephropathy, not lupus nephritis directly, but is a BAFF/APRIL inhibitor that competes in the broader autoimmune kidney space

### Competitive Dynamics

**LUPKYNIS Advantages:**
- First-mover advantage — 5+ years head start in US
- Oral formulation (convenient vs. IV/SC infusions for biologics)
- 2024 ACR guidelines explicitly reference LUPKYNIS
- Rapid onset — proteinuria reduction in weeks
- Compatible with standard of care (can be added to existing regimens)

**LUPKYNIS Vulnerabilities:**
- Calcineurin inhibitor class has known side effects (nephrotoxicity, hypertension, metabolic)
- Requires monitoring (GFR, blood pressure)
- Growth could plateau as early adopters are captured
- Biologics with cleaner safety profiles could eventually displace

### Key Competitor: Vera Therapeutics (VERA)

Vera's atacicept has a **July 7, 2026 PDUFA date** — but for **IgA nephropathy**, not lupus nephritis. This is important:
- Atacicept targets a DIFFERENT kidney disease
- However, as a BAFF/APRIL inhibitor, it validates the mechanism Aurinia's aritinercept also targets
- If atacicept is approved, it's actually **good for AUPH** because it validates aritinercept's mechanism
- Aritinercept claims superior binding affinity (BCMA domain vs TACI domain)

### The Aritinercept Competitive Edge

Aurinia's pipeline drug aritinercept is positioned against multiple BAFF/APRIL inhibitors:

| Drug | Domain | BAFF Binding | APRIL Binding | IgG Backbone | Developer |
|------|--------|-------------|--------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Aritinercept** | **BCMA** | **High** | **Superior** | **IgG4 (less inflammatory)** | **Aurinia** |
| Atacicept | TACI | High | Lower | IgG1 (more inflammatory) | Vera |
| Telitacicept | TACI | High | Lower | IgG1 | RemeGen |
| Povetacicept | TACI | High | Medium | IgG1 | Novartis (from Aclaris) |
| Sibeprenlimab | Anti-APRIL mAb | None | High | IgG1 | J&J/Janssen |

Aritinercept's claimed advantages:
1. **Superior APRIL binding** — BCMA naturally binds APRIL better than TACI
2. **IgG4 backbone** — less inflammatory, potentially cleaner safety profile
3. **Broader B-cell depletion** — targets plasma cells more effectively
