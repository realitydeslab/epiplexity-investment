## Growth Drivers Assessment

### 1. ACR Guideline Tailwind (STRONG)
The 2024 American College of Rheumatology updated lupus nephritis treatment guidelines now emphasize early, aggressive treatment to preserve kidney function. LUPKYNIS as the only FDA-approved oral therapy is the primary beneficiary. This is a multi-year structural tailwind that's still in early innings.

### 2. Patient Penetration (MEDIUM-STRONG)
- US lupus nephritis: 200,000+ SLE patients, 20-60% develop LN
- Addressable market: 40,000-120,000 patients in the US alone
- Current penetration is still single digits — massive runway
- Each LUPKYNIS patient generates ~$60-80K annual revenue

### 3. Japan Expansion (MEDIUM)
- Otsuka partnership for Japanese market
- Japan lupus nephritis market growing but slower adoption cycle
- Contributing incremental revenue but not yet material
