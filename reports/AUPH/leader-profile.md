# AUPH Leader Profile — Aurinia Pharmaceuticals

*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

## CEO: Peter Greenleaf (Since 2019)

**Background:**
- President of MedImmune (now AstraZeneca) 2006-2013 — built it from mid-stage to one of the largest biologics companies
- SVP of the Remicade Franchise at Centocor (J&J) 1998-2006 — managed one of the most successful drug franchises in history
- CEO/Chairman of Sucampo Pharmaceuticals 2014-2018 — successfully sold to Mallinckrodt
- CEO of Cerecor, Chairman of BioDelivery Sciences, Director of Antares Pharma — all three were acquired under his watch
- B.S. Western Connecticut State, MBA St. Joseph's University

**Assessment:** Greenleaf is a seasoned commercial pharma executive with a strong track record of building value and facilitating M&A exits. His MedImmune and Centocor/Remicade experience is directly relevant to managing a specialty drug franchise like LUPKYNIS. He knows how to scale commercialization efficiently — demonstrated by the dramatic SGA reduction from $172M (2024) to $102M (2025) while growing revenue 20%.

**Grade: A-** — Right person for this phase (profitability optimization + positioning for strategic outcome)

## Board Chair: Kevin Tang (Since 2024)

**Background:**
- President of Tang Capital Management (founded 2002) — one of biotech's most successful activist investors
- Founded Concentra Biosciences (2023)
- Track record of successful exits:
  - **Ardea Biosciences** → sold to AstraZeneca (2012)
  - **Heron Therapeutics** — co-founded, served as Chairman
  - **La Jolla Pharmaceutical** → sold to Innoviva (2022)
  - **BioDelivery Sciences** → sold to Collegium (2022)
  - **Odonate Therapeutics** — founded, served as CEO
- Managing Director, head of Life Sciences research at Deutsche Banc Alex Brown (1993-2001)
- B.S. Duke University

**Assessment:** Tang's appointment as Board Chair is the single most important signal for AUPH shareholders. He doesn't join boards passively. His entire career has been about identifying undervalued biopharma companies, optimizing their operations, and driving M&A outcomes. The $12.6M personal investment aligns his interests completely with shareholders.

**Grade: A+** — The activist catalyst that changes the AUPH thesis
