## Catalyst 1: KRONOS MMR™ NRC Licensing Submission
- **What:** Formal regulatory licensing application submission for the KRONOS MMR™ Energy System to the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission. Company hired Michael Montecalvo (ex-NRC) as Licensing Director in Jan 2026, signaling imminent engagement with formal licensing process.
- **When:** Expected H2 2026 – H1 2027 (pre-application engagement likely begins Q2-Q3 2026)
- **Impact:** +30-50% on formal submission announcement. NRC engagement is THE credibility milestone for microreactor companies.
- **Probability:** Medium-High (team is being assembled, but NRC process is notoriously slow)
- **Market expectation:** Market is pricing in a very long timeline. Any acceleration would surprise.
- **Our edge:** The licensing team buildout (Montecalvo hire, existing NRC veterans) suggests the company is closer to formal submission than consensus realizes. However, NRC review itself takes 3-5+ years.

## Catalyst 2: UAE Joint Venture Formalization (EHC Investment MOU)
- **What:** MOU signed Feb 24, 2026 with Abu Dhabi-based EHC Investment to deploy KRONOS MMR™ reactors in UAE and Gulf region. Parties plan to formalize JV upon securing first commercial project or order.
- **When:** Q3-Q4 2026 for potential JV formalization or first commercial agreement
- **Impact:** +15-25% on JV announcement, +40-60% on actual commercial order
- **Probability:** Medium (MOUs are non-binding, but UAE has genuine nuclear energy ambitions — Barakah plant operational)
- **Market expectation:** Market largely ignores MOUs as PR. A binding contract would be a paradigm shift.
- **Our edge:** UAE is one of the few Middle Eastern countries with an existing nuclear regulatory framework and operational plants, making this more credible than typical MOU announcements.

## Catalyst 3: Ameresco Federal/Commercial Site Deployment
- **What:** MOU with Ameresco (NYSE: AMRC), a leading energy infrastructure EPC firm, to deploy microreactors on federal and commercial sites. Ameresco brings government relationships, EPC capabilities, and existing energy infrastructure.
- **When:** Site evaluation and regulatory assessment through 2026; potential definitive agreements H2 2026
- **Impact:** +10-20% on definitive agreement; federal contract could be +30-50%
- **Probability:** Medium (federal energy procurement cycles are long, but Ameresco's existing government footprint is real)
- **Market expectation:** Low expectations for near-term revenue from this partnership
- **Our edge:** Ameresco's existing government relationships (DOE, DOD) could accelerate site identification. Trump administration's pro-nuclear stance is a tailwind.

## Catalyst 4: NASA Lunar Surface Reactor Program
- **What:** NANO Nuclear issued RFI in Jan 2026 for partners to support DOE/NASA Lunar Surface Reactor Program using LOKI MMR™. Targets 100 kWe scalable reactor for lunar base.
- **When:** Partner selection through 2026; potential NASA/DOE contract award 2027+
- **Impact:** +20-40% on NASA contract. Space nuclear is a narrative accelerant even if revenue is years away.
- **Probability:** Low-Medium (early stage, competitive field, long government procurement cycle)
- **Market expectation:** Viewed as aspirational PR. Any concrete NASA involvement would surprise.
- **Our edge:** LOKI MMR™'s compact form factor (<15 metric tons) is genuinely suited for space applications. December 2025 executive order mandates lunar return by 2028, creating urgency.
