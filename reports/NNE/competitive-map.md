# NNE — Competitive Map 🗺️

**NANO Nuclear Energy Inc.** | NASDAQ: NNE | March 9, 2026

---

## Direct Competitors — Advanced Nuclear Microreactors

### 1. Oklo Inc. (OKLO) — $28.85 | MCap ~$7B
- **Sam Altman-backed**, Meta 1.2 GW deal signed
- Aurora compact fast reactor design
- **Advantage over NNE:** Higher-profile backers, Meta commercial agreement (not just MOUs), more institutional credibility
- **Disadvantage vs NNE:** Previously denied NRC license (had to reapply), larger but heavier reactor design not portable
- **Status:** Pre-revenue, but Meta deal provides de-risked demand pipeline
- **Verdict:** OKLO is the category leader with 6x NNE's market cap

### 2. NuScale Power (SMR) — ~$18 | MCap ~$3.8B
- **Only NRC-approved SMR design** — massive regulatory moat
- 77 MWe VOYGR modules
- **Advantage over NNE:** Already NRC-approved, years ahead in licensing
- **Disadvantage vs NNE:** Securities fraud lawsuit (class action filed), larger reactors (not microreactors), lost first customer (UAMPS project canceled), much larger/more expensive units
- **Status:** Pre-revenue, legal issues, but NRC approval is irreplaceable
- **Verdict:** Different market segment (utility-scale SMR vs. microreactor) but competes for nuclear investment dollars

### 3. X-energy (Private → IPO expected)
- Xe-100 high-temperature gas reactor (80 MWe)
- Dow Chemical partnership for industrial heat
- DOE ARDP (Advanced Reactor Demonstration Program) awardee
- **Advantage over NNE:** DOE funding, industrial partner (Dow), further along in development
- **Disadvantage vs NNE:** Not publicly traded (limited investment access), larger reactor format

### 4. Ultra Safe Nuclear Corporation (Private)
- Micro Modular Reactor (MMR) — similar size/concept to NNE
- DOD and Canadian partnerships
- **Advantage over NNE:** Government contracts secured
- **Disadvantage vs NNE:** Private, less visibility

### 5. BWX Technologies (BWXT) — $116 | MCap ~$11B
- Naval nuclear propulsion monopoly
- Micro-reactors for DOD forward bases
- **Advantage over NNE:** Revenue ($2.7B), profitable, existing DOD relationship, proven manufacturing
- **Disadvantage vs NNE:** Not focused on commercial microreactors or data centers
- **Verdict:** Different weight class — established defense contractor vs. startup

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### Long NNE / Short SMR
- **Thesis:** NuScale faces securities fraud lawsuit, lost first customer, while NNE has clean legal slate and fresh cash
- **Risk:** SMR has NRC approval, NNE doesn't
- **Attractiveness:** Medium — both are pre-revenue, SMR's legal issues could worsen

### Long OKLO / Short NNE
- **Thesis:** OKLO has Meta deal, stronger institutional backing, Sam Altman narrative. NNE has insider selling and no binding contracts
- **Risk:** NNE could squeeze (32% short float)
- **Attractiveness:** High — OKLO is the quality play in microreactor space

### Long LEU / Short NNE
- **Thesis:** LEU (Centrus) is the fuel supplier that benefits regardless of which reactor wins. Only US HALEU enricher with real revenue and DOE contracts
- **Risk:** Different sub-sectors within nuclear
- **Attractiveness:** High — LEU profits from the nuclear renaissance without reactor technology risk

## Competitive Position Assessment

| Company | NRC Status | Revenue | Cash | Market Cap | MOUs/Contracts |
|---------|-----------|---------|------|-----------|----------------|
| **NNE** | Pre-application | $0 | $578M | $1.24B | MOUs only |
| OKLO | Re-applying | $0 | ~$500M | ~$7B | Meta binding deal |
| SMR | **NRC Approved** | ~$30M | ~$200M | ~$3.8B | Multiple, but legal issues |
| BWXT | Licensed | $2.7B | ~$300M | ~$11B | DOD contracts |

**NNE is the smallest, earliest-stage, and least de-risked player in the nuclear microreactor space.** Its only advantage is a massive cash position relative to market cap ($578M cash on $1.24B mcap) and pure-play microreactor positioning.

---

## Verdict

NNE is a **speculative bet** in a crowded nuclear microreactor field. It has no regulatory approval, no revenue, no binding contracts, and heavy insider selling. Competitors are further along on every meaningful metric. The only edges are: (1) fortress cash position, (2) portable microreactor differentiation, and (3) short squeeze potential. For nuclear exposure, LEU and OKLO offer better risk/reward.
