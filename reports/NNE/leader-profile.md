## Leadership Verdict

**Strengths:**
- World-class nuclear engineering team (CTO Heidet)
- Aggressive international expansion strategy (UAE, South Korea, NASA)
- Strong cash position management ($400M raise)
- Strategic hiring for NRC licensing readiness

**Weaknesses:**
- ⚠️ Massive insider selling across ALL C-suite and board
- Company is 36 employees with $1.24B market cap — extreme valuation per employee ($34.4M/employee)
- No revenue, no prototype, no NRC application yet filed
- MOUs are non-binding and may be primarily PR-driven
- Founder selling $55M while touting future potential is a credibility gap

**Grade: C+** — Competent team with the right technical expertise, but insider selling at this scale in a pre-revenue company is a serious warning sign.
