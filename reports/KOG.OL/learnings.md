# Learnings & Intelligence: Kongsberg Gruppen (KOG.OL)

*Research date: March 9, 2026*

---

## What This Tells Us About the Sector

1. **European defense is not just "spending more" — it's restructuring.** The demerger signals that even integrated groups are splitting to capture defense-specific valuation premiums. Expect more European conglomerate break-ups.

2. **Counter-UAS is the next air defense mega-cycle.** Poland's NOK 16B CUAS program is just the beginning. Every NATO nation needs counter-drone capability. This is a market that barely existed 3 years ago and will be >$50B globally by 2030.

3. **The F-35 ecosystem creates monopoly rents.** Once a munition is integrated into F-35 internal weapons bays, switching costs are enormous. JSM's USAF adoption creates a decades-long revenue stream. Look for other F-35 ecosystem plays.

4. **Norway punches absurdly above its weight in defense.** Population 5.5M but produces NASAMS (air defense standard for 13+ NATO nations), NSM/JSM (adopted by US Navy and Air Force), PROTECTOR RWS (85% global market share). The Norwegian defense model (state ownership + NATO integration + tech excellence) is underappreciated.

## Contrarian Angle — What's the Market Missing?

1. **RemainCo as a $15-20B pure defense company.** Post-demerger, Kongsberg Defence will be one of the few pure-play European missile/air defense companies. The market hasn't modeled this properly because there's no historical pure-play comparable.

2. **US market penetration is just beginning.** Virginia factory + USAF JSM + US Marine Corps NSM = Kongsberg becoming a US defense prime. This is a TAM expansion from ~$8B (European defense) to ~$400B (US defense budget).

3. **NOMADS as a new franchise.** First customer (Netherlands). Mobile air defense for maneuvering land forces is a totally new product category with no real competition. Could become as big as NASAMS over time.

4. **Norwegian state ownership as a feature, not a bug.** 50% state ownership means Kongsberg will never be acquired at a discount, will always have government contracts, and has sovereign backing for export deals.

## Risk Factors to Monitor

1. **Ukraine ceasefire:** Would reduce urgency but NOT reverse spending (structural shift already locked in via budgets/legislation)
2. **NOK currency moves:** Strengthening NOK hurts export competitiveness
3. **Defense sector rotation:** After +53% YTD, any broad tech/growth rotation could hit defense names
4. **Execution risk on demerger:** Stranded costs, IT separation, contract reassignment
5. **Supply chain:** Kongsberg depends on component suppliers (semiconductors, propellants) that face their own constraints
