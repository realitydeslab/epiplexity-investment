# Growth Velocity: Kongsberg Gruppen (KOG.OL)

*Research date: March 9, 2026*

---

## Revenue Growth — Accelerating

| Period | Revenue (NOK B) | YoY Growth |
|--------|-----------------|------------|
| FY2023 | ~40.0B | ~15% |
| FY2024 | ~48.8B | ~22% |
| FY2025 | ~57.0B (est) | ~17% |
| Q4 2025 | 16.8B | +21% YoY |

- FY2025 full-year revenue growth of ~17%, with Q4 accelerating to 21%
- Revenue growth driven by both Defense (~20%+ growth) and Maritime (~15%)
- Post-demerger, RemainCo (Defense) should grow 20%+ annually as the massive backlog converts

## Order Backlog — Record and Growing

| Period | Order Backlog (NOK B) |
|--------|-----------------------|
| End 2023 | ~95B |
| End 2024 | ~120B |
| End 2025 | 157.4B |

- **Backlog grew 31% YoY** — faster than revenue, meaning growth is ACCELERATING
- Q4 2025 order intake: NOK 31.8B in single quarter
- Recent mega-orders post Q4: Poland CUAS NOK 16B (Jan 30), plus pipeline

### Book-to-bill ratio: ~1.4x
For every NOK 1 of revenue, Kongsberg is booking NOK 1.4 in new orders. This means growth is front-loaded and visibility is multi-year.

## Profitability

| Period | EBIT (NOK B) | EBIT Margin |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Q4 2025 | 2.5B | 14.7% |
| FY2025 | ~8.0B (est) | ~14% |

- Operating margins expanding as higher-margin defense mix increases
- Post-demerger RemainCo should see margins expand to 15-17% (defense pure-play)
- Maritime historically lower margin (~10-12%)

## Guidance vs. Actuals

- Kongsberg has consistently beat-and-raised over the past 3 years
- Q4 2025 beat expectations — stock rose 13.9% on results day (Feb 6)
- Management credibility is high

## Customer/Order Growth

- NASAMS: 13 nations and growing
- JSM: Added USAF as customer (world's largest F-35 operator) — transformative
- NOMADS: First export customer (Netherlands) — new product line
- CUAS: First major contract (Poland) — opens entirely new market
- PROTECTOR RWS: Installed on 20,000+ platforms globally

## Path to Profitability

Already highly profitable. Question is margin expansion:
- Defense mix shift post-demerger → higher margins
- Scale benefits on JSM/NASAMS serial production
- US factory adds local content but may initially dilute margins
- Target: 15-17% EBIT margin for RemainCo by 2027

## Unit Economics

- Defense products are high-margin, recurring (ammunition, maintenance, upgrades)
- NASAMS installed base creates decades of aftermarket revenue
- JSM is a consumable missile — repeat orders are structural
- CUAS is operationally intensive — high recurring content

## Verdict: Growth ACCELERATING

The combination of record backlog (31% growth), accelerating Q4 revenue (+21%), new product lines (NOMADS, CUAS), new geographies (USAF/US), and the demerger catalyst creates a rare profile: a defense company where BOTH revenue growth and margin expansion are accelerating simultaneously.
