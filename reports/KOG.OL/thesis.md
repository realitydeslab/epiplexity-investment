# Investment Thesis: Kongsberg Gruppen (KOG.OL)

*Research date: March 9, 2026*

---

## The One-Liner

**Kongsberg is Europe's most underappreciated defense pure-play, about to unlock value through a Maritime demerger while sitting on a NOK 157B backlog of missiles, air defense, and counter-drone systems that NATO desperately needs.**

## The Catalyst

April 2026 Maritime demerger creates two focused companies. RemainCo (Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace) becomes one of Europe's only pure-play missile/air defense companies — with monopoly products (NASAMS, NSM/JSM, PROTECTOR) — right as European defense spending enters its biggest cycle since the Cold War.

## The Numbers

- **Revenue:** ~NOK 57B FY2025 (+17% YoY), Q4 accelerating to +21%
- **Backlog:** NOK 157.4B record (+31% YoY) — 2.7 years of revenue visibility
- **EBIT margin:** 14.7% (Q4) and expanding
- **Recent mega-orders:** Poland CUAS NOK 16B, Netherlands NOK 12B, USAF JSM $241M
- **Price:** NOK 396.50 | Market cap: ~NOK 75B (~$7.1B)
- **Analyst consensus:** NOK 420-440 (6-11% upside pre-revision)

## The Edge

1. **Monopoly products:** NASAMS (13 NATO nations, no alternative), JSM (only F-35 internal anti-ship missile), PROTECTOR RWS (85% market share). These aren't competitive wins — they're structural monopolies.
2. **Demerger unlock:** Conglomerate discount disappears in April. Defense-only RemainCo should rerate to 25-30x earnings vs. current ~20x blended.
3. **US market entry:** Virginia missile factory + USAF JSM adoption opens the $400B US defense budget. This is a TAM expansion, not priced in.
4. **Counter-UAS first-mover:** Poland NOK 16B = largest European CUAS program. Every NATO nation needs this. Kongsberg got there first.

## The Risk

- **Valuation stretch:** +53% YTD, at all-time highs. Any defense rotation = pain.
- **Ukraine ceasefire:** Reduces urgency (but spending is now structurally locked in)
- **Execution:** Demerger complexity, supply chain constraints
- **Currency:** NOK exposure for international investors

## The Verdict

**BUY with conviction.** This is a rare combination: monopoly defense products, imminent catalyst (April demerger), record backlog with 31% growth, and secular European rearmament tailwind. The risk is valuation — you're buying at highs. But the demerger creates a re-rating event that most investors haven't fully modeled, and the backlog provides 2.7 years of revenue visibility.

**Position sizing:** 3-5% of portfolio. Ladder entry: 50% now, 50% on any pullback to NOK 365-375.
