*Research date: March 9, 2026*

### Key Differentiator: Kongsberg's Niche Moat
Kongsberg doesn't compete head-to-head with any of these. Its products occupy unique niches:
- **NASAMS:** Only NATO-certified GBAD with combat-proven performance (Ukraine). Raytheon is partner, not competitor.
- **NSM/JSM:** Only Western anti-ship cruise missile that fits inside F-35. No direct competitor.
- **PROTECTOR RWS:** 85%+ global market share for remote weapon stations. Dominant.
- **Counter-UAS:** Emerging but already landed biggest European contract (Poland NOK 16B).

## Who Loses if Kongsberg Wins?

| Diehl Defence (Germany) | IRIS-T SLM | Good system but smaller installed base, less NATO integration |
| Lockheed Martin | THAAD, PAC-3 | Different tier (high-end). NASAMS complements rather than competes |
| Turkish firms (Aselsan, Roketsan) | HISAR | NATO interoperability concerns |

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### Long KOG.OL / Short European defense laggards
- **Long KOG.OL / Short Thales (HO.PA):** Kongsberg is pure defense post-demerger with acceleration. Thales is diversified (transport, digital identity) and trades at lower defense premium.
- **Long KOG.OL / Short Leonardo (LDO.MI):** Leonardo has execution risk (Iveco acquisition), more complexity. Kongsberg cleaner.

### Portfolio Companion Trades
- **Long KOG.OL + RHM.DE:** European defense duo — Kongsberg for air/missile defense, Rheinmetall for ground. Non-overlapping. Both benefit from rearmament.
- **Long KOG.OL + RTX:** Raytheon is Kongsberg's NASAMS partner (provides AIM-120/AIM-9X missiles). Both benefit from NASAMS expansion. Not competitive.

## Key Insight
Kongsberg's competitive position is unusually strong because it operates in niches where it has near-monopoly status. It doesn't fight Rheinmetall for tank orders or BAE for ship contracts. It sells the missiles, air defense, and weapon stations that go ON those platforms. This makes it a "picks and shovels" play for European rearmament — everyone needs NASAMS batteries, everyone needs NSM coastal defense, everyone needs PROTECTOR RWS on their vehicles.
