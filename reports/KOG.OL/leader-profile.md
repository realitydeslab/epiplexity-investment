# Leader Profile: Kongsberg Gruppen (KOG.OL)

*Research date: March 9, 2026*

---

## Leadership Transition (Demerger)

The demerger creates two CEOs:
- **Eirik Lie** — CEO of RemainCo (KONGSBERG / Defence & Aerospace)
- **Lisa Edvardsen Haugan** — CEO of Kongsberg Maritime ASA

## Eirik Lie — CEO, Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace (RemainCo)

- **Born:** 1966
- **Background:** Software engineer by training. Joined Kongsberg in 1990 — 36-year career.
- **Education:** Kongsberg College of Engineering (Data Engineering), Royal Norwegian Navy Officer Candidate School
- **Career at Kongsberg:** Software development → systems engineering → project management → headed Integrated Defence Systems division (since 2006) → EVP of Kongsberg Gruppen / President of KDA
- **Key achievements:** Oversaw NASAMS expansion to 13 nations, secured USAF JSM program of record, negotiated Poland NOK 16B CUAS deal, Netherlands NOK 12B NASAMS/NOMADS deal
- **Shares held:** 15,722 shares (~NOK 6.2M at current prices)
- **Directorships:** Chairman of KSAT (Kongsberg Satellite Services), Board member of Patria Oyj (49.9% owned)

### Assessment
Lie is a defense lifer — deep technical background, intimate knowledge of every product line, and strong NATO relationships. Not a flashy CEO hire but exactly the right person for execution: he built the NASAMS business from a Norwegian niche to a global franchise. His military background and 36-year tenure mean he knows where every contract is buried. **Grade: A for this specific role.**

## Lisa Edvardsen Haugan — Incoming CEO, Kongsberg Maritime ASA

- Maritime heritage focus: "200 years of company history, 8,000 employees in 35 countries, systems on one third of global fleet (30,000+ vessels)"
