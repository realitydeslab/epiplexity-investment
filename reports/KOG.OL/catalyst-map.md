# Catalyst Map: Kongsberg Gruppen (KOG.OL)

*Research date: March 9, 2026*

---

## 1. Maritime Demerger & Listing (April 2026)

- **What:** Spin-off of Kongsberg Maritime as a separate listed company (Kongsberg Maritime ASA) on Euronext Oslo Børs. Approved at EGM on January 22, 2026.
- **When:** April 2026 (exact date TBD, likely mid-to-late April)
- **Impact:** +10-20%. Unlock of conglomerate discount. Pure-play defense RemainCo trades at defense multiples (~25-30x earnings); Maritime trades at industrial/maritime multiples (~15-18x). Sum-of-parts value exceeds current combined market cap.
- **Probability:** HIGH — Already approved by shareholders, demerger plan signed Dec 2025.
- **Market expectation:** Priced in at ~50%. Market knows it's coming but hasn't fully modeled RemainCo as a pure defense play.
- **Our edge:** Post-demerger, RemainCo becomes one of the purest European defense plays — NASAMS, NSM, JSM, PROTECTOR RWS — at a time when European defense spending is accelerating. Defense-only peers trade at significant premiums.

## 2. Poland CUAS Contract Execution (2026-2030)

- **What:** NOK 16 billion contract for 18 Counter-UAS batteries (SAN CUAS program) signed January 30, 2026, with PGZ consortium partner.
- **When:** Revenue recognition begins 2026, deliveries over 2026-2030
- **Impact:** +5-8%. Validates Kongsberg as Europe's go-to counter-drone provider.
- **Probability:** HIGH — Contract signed, funded.
- **Market expectation:** Known but follow-on potential underappreciated.
- **Our edge:** Counter-UAS is fastest-growing defense segment. Kongsberg first to land massive European CUAS program — NATO allies will follow.

## 3. Netherlands NASAMS/NOMADS Delivery (2026+)

- **What:** NOK 12 billion contract for NASAMS and NOMADS (first international NOMADS customer). Signed November 2025.
- **When:** Deliveries commence 2026
- **Impact:** +3-5%. NOMADS is a new product line — mobile air defense for land forces.
- **Probability:** HIGH — Contract signed.
- **Our edge:** NOMADS fills a gap no one else has — mobile short-range NATO-interoperable air defense for maneuvering forces.

## 4. USAF JSM Lot 3+ Orders (2026)

- **What:** Follow-on JSM orders for USAF F-35A fleet. Lot 1 (May 2024) + Lot 2 ($241M, Dec 2025) already contracted.
- **When:** H2 2026
- **Impact:** +3-5%. Each lot larger as USAF scales adoption.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH — JSM is only internally-carried anti-ship missile for F-35A/C. No competition.
- **Our edge:** USAF adoption makes JSM a program of record. Japan, Australia, other F-35 operators will follow.

## 5. European Defense Spending Surge (Ongoing 2026)

- **What:** ReArm Europe / SAFE EUR 150B facility, NATO members targeting 3.5-5% GDP defense spending.
- **When:** Ongoing, accelerating through 2026
- **Impact:** +15-30% on sector over 12 months.
- **Probability:** HIGH — Political constraint lifted, money flowing.
- **Our edge:** Kongsberg has products for Europe's top 3 gaps: air defense (NASAMS), anti-ship/land-attack missiles (NSM/JSM), counter-drones (CUAS).

## 6. US Missile Factory Construction (Q2 2026)

- **What:** New NSM/JSM manufacturing plant in Toano, Virginia. Full construction Q2 2026, production by end 2027.
- **When:** Groundbreaking Q2 2026
- **Impact:** +5-10% over time. Positions Kongsberg as US defense industrial base participant.
- **Probability:** HIGH — Already under construction.
- **Our edge:** Transforms Kongsberg from Norwegian exporter to US defense prime.

## 7. Additional NASAMS Nations (2026)

- **What:** 13 nations in NASAMS user group. Germany, Baltics under consideration.
- **When:** New announcements possible Q2-Q3 2026
- **Impact:** +3-5% per major new customer
- **Probability:** MEDIUM
