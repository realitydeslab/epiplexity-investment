## Risk Management

- **Stop-loss:** NOK 330 (-17%). Below Jan consolidation and pre-earnings level.
- **Position sizing:** 3-5% of portfolio. European defense is a high-conviction theme but KOG is at all-time highs.
- **What kills the trade:**
  1. Ukraine ceasefire + European defense spending reversal (low probability)
  2. Demerger delayed or cancelled (very low — already approved)
  3. Major contract cancellation
  4. NOK currency shock (oil price collapse → NOK weakens → mixed impact)

## FX Consideration

For non-NOK investors, currency is a factor. NOK tends to correlate with oil prices and risk appetite. If buying via the Oslo exchange, consider hedging NOK exposure for large positions.
