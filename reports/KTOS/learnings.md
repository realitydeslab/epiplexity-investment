# KTOS Structured Intelligence

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## What This Tells Us About the Sector

1. **Autonomous warfare is real and funded.** The CCA program is the single biggest signal that the Pentagon is serious about AI-powered combat. Not as a concept — as a funded, production-ready program.

2. **Affordability > Capability at the margin.** The defense industry's entire business model (cost-plus, decade-long development) is being disrupted by the realization that quantity has a quality of its own. Ukraine proved cheap drones beat expensive systems.

3. **Defense profitability lags revenue growth.** KTOS's gross margin compression during ramp-up is typical — defense manufacturing has thin margins until scale. Investors in this space need patience.

## Adjacent Plays

- **Defense AI Software:** PLTR, S (SentinelOne), PANW — the software layer that makes autonomous systems intelligent
- **Defense Manufacturing Scale:** AXON (law enforcement AI), RHM.DE (European defense hardware)
- **Drone Supply Chain:** Components, propulsion, sensors — harder to invest directly but watch for roll-ups
- **Nuclear for Defense:** BWXT — nuclear propulsion for Navy, same "next-gen defense" thesis

## Contrarian Angle

**The market may be WRONG about valuation.** At 109x forward P/E, KTOS looks absurdly expensive — but if EPS grows from $0.80 to $1.10 to $1.50+ over 2026-2028, it's actually cheap on a 2-year forward basis. The question is whether you trust the earnings inflection.

**Counter-contrarian:** The massive insider selling says management thinks the stock is ahead of itself at $87-120. They may be right. But insiders sold Amazon at $50 too.

## What the Market is Missing

1. **Hypersonic propulsion revenue** is largely classified and undercounted by analysts
2. **International expansion** potential — NATO rearmament creates a new $10B+ addressable market for affordable drones
3. **The "age of attrition"** — modern warfare favors disposable, mass-produced autonomous systems over expensive platforms. This structural shift benefits Kratos for 10+ years.
