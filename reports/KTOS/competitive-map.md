# KTOS Competitive Map

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## Direct Competitors in Autonomous Combat Drones

| Company | Product | Advantage | Disadvantage |
|---------|---------|-----------|-------------|
| **Kratos (KTOS)** | XQ-58A Valkyrie | Most flight hours, affordability, DoD relationships | Small scale, FCF negative |
| **Anduril** | Fury (CCA) | VC-funded speed, Lattice AI platform, Palmer Luckey hype | Private, unproven at scale production |
| **GA-ASI** (private) | MQ-20 Avenger | Established drone maker, Predator/Reaper heritage | Expensive, traditional approach |
| **Boeing** | MQ-28 Ghost Bat | Australia partnership, large company resources | Troubled track record, cost overruns |
| **Lockheed Martin (LMT)** | Various classified | Deep DoD relationships, full-spectrum capability | CCA may not be their focus |

## Who Benefits from Same Catalysts?

**Defense AI/Autonomy wave benefits:**
- **PLTR** — AI software for defense, Kratos hardware + Palantir software = complementary
- **LMT/RTX/NOC** — Traditional primes benefit from overall defense spending
- **AXON** — AI for law enforcement, different segment but same AI-defense thesis
- **ANET** — Networking infrastructure for C2 systems

**Counter-UAS demand benefits:**
- **RTX (Raytheon)** — Coyote interceptor, established counter-UAS
- **LHX (L3Harris)** — Electronic warfare counter-drone
- **KTOS** has an edge here on cost-per-intercept

## Who Loses if Kratos Wins?

- **Traditional manned aircraft programs** — if attritable drones prove effective, F-35 follow-on orders may shrink
- **LMT** somewhat — NGAD (manned) program could lose funding to CCA (unmanned)
- **Boeing** — already struggling, losing CCA would be another blow

## Pair Trade Ideas

1. **Long KTOS / Short Boeing (BA)** — autonomous vs troubled traditional defense
2. **Long KTOS + PLTR** — hardware + software autonomy stack
3. **Long KTOS / Short LMT** — attritable drones vs expensive manned fighters (aggressive)

## Competitive Assessment

Kratos' core moat is **affordability + speed + flight heritage.** In defense, where programs typically take 15 years and cost $50B, Kratos builds and flies drones in 2-3 years for millions (not billions). This is a genuine competitive advantage that's hard to replicate — the culture and engineering approach are embedded.

The biggest risk is Anduril. Palmer Luckey's company has similar DNA (tech-first, move fast) but with billions in VC funding and the Lattice AI software platform. The CCA program may be split between multiple vendors, which would benefit Kratos as one of the winners.
