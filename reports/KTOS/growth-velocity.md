# KTOS Growth Velocity

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## Revenue Trajectory (Annual)

| Year | Revenue | YoY Growth | Trend |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|
| FY2021 | $811.5M | +8.5% | Steady |
| FY2022 | $898.3M | +10.7% | Accelerating |
| FY2023 | $1,037M | +15.5% | Accelerating ✅ |
| FY2024 | $1,136M | +9.6% | Decelerated ❌ |
| FY2025 | $1,347M | +18.5% | Re-accelerating ✅ |
| FY2026E | $1,680M | +24.7% | Consensus: accelerating ✅ |
| FY2027E | $2,090M | +23.9% | Sustained |

**Verdict: Growth is ACCELERATING.** After a soft FY2024 (+9.6%), FY2025 re-accelerated to 18.5% and consensus expects 25% in FY2026. This is a growth inflection story.

## Profitability Trajectory

| Metric | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | FY2026E |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|---------|
| Gross Margin | 25.2% | 25.9% | 25.3% | 22.9% | ~24% |
| Operating Margin | -0.3% | 3.0% | 2.6% | 1.9% | ~4% |
| Net Income | -$36.5M | $8.5M | $36.7M | $46M | ~$130M |
| EPS | -$0.29 | -$0.07 | $0.11 | $0.13 | $0.80 |

**Key insight:** Gross margin compressed in FY2025 (22.9% vs 25.3%) as drone production ramped — early production is lower margin. As volumes scale, margins should expand. EPS is forecast to explode 513% in FY2026.

## Free Cash Flow — THE CONCERN ⚠️

| Year | FCF | FCF Margin |
|------|-----|------------|
| FY2023 | $12.8M | 1.2% |
| FY2024 | -$8.5M | -0.8% |
| FY2025 | -$137.4M | -10.2% |

FCF burned $137M in FY2025 — heavy capex for drone production facilities and working capital build. This is a company investing ahead of growth. FCF should improve as production ramps and working capital normalizes, but it's a risk if contracts slip.

## Guidance vs. Actual

Kratos has historically met or exceeded guidance. Management tends to be conservative — they've consistently raised revenue guidance throughout FY2025.

## Employee Growth

- 4,300 employees
- Revenue per employee: $313K — typical for defense/manufacturing

## Path to Profitability

Kratos is already profitable but thinly so. The story is operating leverage — once drone and counter-UAS production hits scale, fixed costs get absorbed across much higher volumes. FY2026 is expected to be the inflection year for meaningful profitability.

## Summary

**Growth velocity: ACCELERATING ✅**
- Revenue growth: 9.6% → 18.5% → 25% (consensus)
- EPS growth: expected 513% in FY2026
- Risk: FCF burn from investment, gross margin compression during ramp
- Opportunity: Operating leverage should compound as production scales
