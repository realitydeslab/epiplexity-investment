# KTOS Leader Profile — Eric DeMarco

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## CEO: Eric DeMarco

- **Role:** President & CEO since 2004
- **Background:** 20+ years at helm, transformed Kratos from a small IT services company into a defense technology leader
- **Style:** Aggressive internal R&D investor — Kratos funds its own development (unusual in defense). Focused on speed-to-market and affordability.
- **Reputation:** Known as a "disruptive change agent" in defense. Not a typical defense CEO — more tech entrepreneur mindset.
- **Key quote:** "Manufacturing military-grade hardware in large scale production runs, that must work every time, is hard and a clear differentiator."

## Near-Term Outlook Assessment

DeMarco is the right CEO for this moment — he built Kratos for the era of affordable, attritable autonomous systems. The insider selling is a concern for timing but not for the long-term thesis. The company's positioning in CCA, counter-UAS, and hypersonics is the result of decades of his strategic vision.

**Grade: B+** (right leader, insider selling is a timing risk)
