## Who Loses if Reddit Wins?

### AI Search Disruption
- **Google (GOOGL):** Reddit Answers competes with Google for "opinion" queries. Currently 48% of Google searches end with "reddit" appended. If users go direct to Reddit search, Google loses traffic.
- **Quora/Stack Overflow:** Reddit Answers directly cannibalizes Q&A platforms. Reddit has more content, more users, and now AI-powered answers.
- **Perplexity (private):** AI answer engine — Reddit building the same thing but with proprietary data.

### Advertising Wallet Share
- **Snap (SNAP):** Competes for incremental social ad budgets. Reddit's ad product improving faster.
- **Pinterest (PINS):** Overlapping advertiser base in lifestyle/shopping categories.

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### Long RDDT / Short SNAP
- **Thesis:** Reddit is the faster-growing, more differentiated platform. Both compete for social ad budgets. Reddit's AI moat is deepening while Snap faces AR/VR pivot uncertainty.
- **Risk:** If broad ad market rallies, SNAP rallies more from lower base.

### Long RDDT / Short PINS
- **Thesis:** Reddit's community-driven recommendations are becoming more shoppable. Pinterest's visual search moat is narrowing as AI-generated imagery proliferates.
- **Risk:** Pinterest's higher profitability makes it more defensive.

### Long RDDT + Long GOOGL (Paired Long)
- **Thesis:** Google's deal WITH Reddit (AI training data + driving traffic) means both benefit. Google's AI Overviews cite Reddit extensively, driving awareness. Net positive for both.

## Competitive Advantages Summary

1. **Network effects:** 100K+ active subreddits, each a self-organizing community. Cannot be replicated.
2. **Data moat:** 20 years of human-generated, upvote-validated, topic-structured content. Unique in AI era.
3. **Intent signal:** Users come to Reddit with purchase intent ("best laptop 2026 reddit"), making ad targeting highly efficient.
4. **Community moderation:** Volunteer moderators = free content quality control at massive scale.
5. **Switching costs:** Communities are loyal to their subreddits. Reddit's content *is* the platform.

## Competitive Vulnerabilities

1. **Google dependency:** ~48% of Reddit traffic comes from Google search. Algorithm change = traffic cliff.
2. **Content quality risk:** AI-generated posts/comments could dilute genuine human content value.
3. **Platform risk:** Subreddit blackouts (like 2023 API protest) show community power can hurt.
4. **Scale disadvantage vs META:** Meta's ad tech is decades ahead. Reddit's ad product is still maturing.
