## Unit Economics: IMPROVING

- **Revenue per employee:** $862,038 (excellent for a social platform)
- **Cost of revenue:** Only $194M on $2.2B revenue (91% gross margin)
- **CapEx:** Minimal — only $6.7M in FY2025 (asset-light model)
- **FCF:** $684M — 31% margin, up 217% YoY
- **Cash:** $2.48B with virtually zero debt ($23M)

### Revenue Composition
- **Advertising:** ~94% of revenue (~$2.06B in FY2025)
- **Other (AI licensing + premium):** ~6% ($140M in FY2025, +22% YoY)
- AI licensing (Google, OpenAI deals) is the high-margin growth driver

## Growth Velocity Verdict

**STRONGLY ACCELERATING.** Reddit is in a rare position where revenue growth is >60% AND margins are expanding AND the company just turned profitable. The combination of:
1. Advertising revenue scaling (core business)
2. AI licensing revenue compounding (new high-margin stream)
3. AI search monetization coming (not yet in numbers)

...suggests consensus 45% growth for 2026 may be conservative. This looks like a 50-55% growth year if AI licensing deals land.
