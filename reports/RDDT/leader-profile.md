## CEO: Steve Huffman

- **Role:** Co-Founder, CEO & President since 2015 (returned after departure)
- **Co-Founded:** Reddit in 2005 with Alexis Ohanian at age 21
- **Education:** UVA Computer Science
- **Age:** ~42
- **Experience:** Built Reddit from scratch, navigated content policy controversies, led IPO (March 2024)

### Leadership Assessment

**Strengths:**
- Product visionary — personally drives AI search strategy ("Reddit Answers")
- Survived and grew Reddit through multiple crises (API pricing controversy 2023, content moderation)
- Delivered first profitable year in 2025 (20-year journey to profitability)
- Revenue grew from $804M → $2.2B under his leadership in 2 years
- Clear articulation of AI opportunity: "better for most queries"
- Navigated IPO successfully at ~$47B initial pop

**Weaknesses:**
- History of controversial management decisions (API pricing war with third-party apps)
- Selling shares regularly — automatic plan of 18K shares biweekly
- Dual-class share structure gives him outsized voting control

### Verdict on Execution
Huffman is the right leader for the AI transformation. He's technical, has product instincts, and has been consistent in his vision for Reddit as the internet's town square. The AI search pivot is genuinely clever — Reddit's content is uniquely suited for it.
