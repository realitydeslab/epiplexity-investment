# RDDT Investment Thesis — Reddit, Inc.
**Date:** March 9, 2026 | **Price:** $138.86 | **Market Cap:** $26.5B

---

## The Catalyst

Reddit is the internet's only scaled repository of 20 years of structured human opinion data — and AI companies will pay increasingly enormous sums to access it. The stock is 40% off January highs despite **accelerating** revenue growth (69% YoY), a profitability inflection ($530M net income in first profitable year), and multiple unpriced catalysts: AI licensing revenue doubling, Reddit Answers monetization, S&P 500 inclusion, and a $1B buyback executing at distressed prices.

## The Trade

**Entry:** $138.86 (current)  
**12-Month Target:** $200 (base), $250 (bull)  
**Stop-Loss:** $105  
**Position Size:** 3-5% of portfolio

**Why now?** PEG ratio of 0.90 for a 60%+ revenue grower with 91% gross margins and 31% FCF margins. RSI at 34.7 (near oversold). Director bought $7.5M at these levels. $1B buyback creating a floor. 27 analysts average target $228 (+64% upside).

## The Risk

1. **Google traffic dependency** — ~48% of Reddit traffic comes from Google. Algorithm change or AI Overviews cannibalizing clicks = existential.
2. **Advertising cyclicality** — 94% revenue from ads. Recession hits fast at 2.42 beta.
3. **Content quality erosion** — AI-generated content flooding Reddit reduces data moat value.
4. **High insider selling** — C-suite has been selling aggressively (though via automatic plans).
5. **Valuation premium** — Still 34x forward PE; multiple compression in broader tech selloff.

## The Verdict

**BUY — High Conviction Contrarian Entry.**

This is Meta Platforms circa 2013 — a community platform just turning profitable, growing 60%+ with improving unit economics, and the market pricing it like growth will decelerate tomorrow. The AI data licensing optionality alone could be worth $5-10B in 2-3 years.

The 40% pullback is a gift. The PEG is under 1. A board director put $7.5M of personal money in at these levels. The fundamentals say buy, the technicals say oversold, and the catalysts are stacked for the next 12 months.

**Key risk to manage:** Google dependency. If Google changes its algorithm or AI Overviews reduce Reddit referral traffic by >30%, revisit thesis immediately.
