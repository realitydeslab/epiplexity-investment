### What Kills the Trade?
1. **AI traffic cannibalization** — If ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Overviews reduces traffic to Reddit (people get Reddit answers without visiting Reddit)
2. **Advertising recession** — 94% of revenue is ads. Macro downturn = direct hit
3. **Content quality degradation** — Bot/AI-generated content floods Reddit, reducing data value
4. **CEO Huffman controversy** — History of controversial decisions; another API-pricing-style incident
5. **Growth deceleration below 30%** — Multiple compression would be severe

### Correlation Risk
- Highly correlated with NASDAQ/tech sentiment (beta 2.42)
- Correlated with digital advertising spend (META, GOOGL, SNAP, PINS all move together)
- Macro-sensitive: recession fear = ad budget cuts
