## Margin Expansion — THE Key Story

### Gross Margin Trajectory

| FY | Gross Margin |
|----|-------------|
| FY2021 | 57.7% |
| FY2022 | 60.1% |
| FY2023 | 65.9% |

**Verdict: ✅ Consistently expanding.** From 57.7% → 74.7% in 5 years. SaaS-like margins approaching best-in-class cybersecurity (CrowdStrike at ~75-76%).

### Free Cash Flow — Inflection Point ✅

| Period | FCF | FCF Margin |
|--------|-----|------------|
| FY2023 | -$198M | -47% |
| FY2024 | -$70M | -11% |
| FY2025 | +$32M | +3.9% |

**This is the most important metric.** FCF went from -$198M to +$68M in 2 years. The company is generating real cash.

## Profitability Path

- **Non-GAAP EPS FY2026 forecast:** $0.19 (first profitable year)
- **Non-GAAP EPS FY2027 forecast:** $0.31 (+63%)
- **GAAP profitability:** Likely FY2027 or FY2028
- **Rule of 40:** Revenue growth (25%) + FCF margin (7%) = 32. Below 40 but improving rapidly. Could reach 40 by FY2027 (20% growth + 20% FCF margin)
