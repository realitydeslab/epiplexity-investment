## What This Tells Us About the Sector

### 1. The "AI Kills Cybersecurity" Narrative Is Wrong

The cybersecurity sector selloff (SentinelOne -28% YTD, CrowdStrike -15%, Zscaler -20%+) is driven by a fundamentally flawed narrative: that AI will replace cybersecurity companies.

**Reality check:**
- Every AI agent deployed is a NEW attack surface that needs protection
- AI-generated phishing, deepfakes, and automated exploits are INCREASING cyber threats
- Agentic AI systems (autonomous agents acting on behalf of users) create entirely new identity/access management challenges
- The cybersecurity TAM is EXPANDING because of AI, not shrinking

**Implication for the portfolio:** Cybersecurity is a sector-wide buy opportunity. The market is collectively mispricing the entire sector based on a misunderstanding of AI's impact.

### 2. Valuation Dispersion Is Extreme

The P/S gap between SentinelOne (5.1x) and CrowdStrike (26x) at similar growth rates (~23-24%) is historically unprecedented. This tells us:
- The market still views SentinelOne as a "lesser CrowdStrike" rather than a platform company
- CrowdStrike's July 2024 outage did NOT durably benefit SentinelOne's perception
- There's either massive upside in S or massive downside in CRWD (or both converge)

### 3. Profitability Inflection Is the New Growth

The market is rewarding companies transitioning from "growth at all costs" to "profitable growth." SentinelOne's FCF inflection from -$198M to +$68M is the type of trajectory that typically precedes multi-year re-ratings.

## Contrarian Angle — What's the Market Missing?

### The Platform Shift
Most analysts still model SentinelOne as an endpoint security company competing with CrowdStrike on endpoint. They're missing:
- **Data Solutions (SIEM):** Triple-digit bookings growth, now ~50% of new bookings
- **Cloud Security:** Expanding rapidly via Singularity Cloud
- **Identity Security:** Agentic AI identity protection is purpose-built for the era of AI agents

The correct comp isn't CrowdStrike at 26x P/S — it's the broader security platform players at 10-12x P/S. Even at 8x P/S, SentinelOne would trade at $22+ (55% upside).

### The Schwarz Digits Angle
The Schwarz Digits partnership for sovereign AI cybersecurity is barely covered by analysts. In the context of European ReArm spending and data sovereignty mandates (GDPR enforcement, EU AI Act), a sovereign cybersecurity platform could be a massive European government/enterprise play.

### AI Agent Security Is a New TAM
As agentic AI proliferates (autonomous agents making API calls, accessing databases, executing transactions), the identity and authorization challenge is enormous. SentinelOne's identity security product is positioned for this exact use case. This TAM doesn't exist in analyst models because it barely exists yet — but it will by 2027.
