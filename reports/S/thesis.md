# Investment Thesis — SentinelOne (S)

**One-Page Summary | March 9, 2026**

---

## The Catalyst

**FQ4 earnings on March 12, 2026** at the most negative sentiment in the stock's public history. SentinelOne trades at $14.22 — just 16% above its all-time post-IPO low — while crossing $1B ARR, turning FCF positive ($68M TTM), and approaching non-GAAP profitability ($0.19 EPS FY2026E). The market is pricing in "AI kills cybersecurity" — a narrative that is fundamentally backwards. Every AI agent deployed is a new attack surface. Purple AI (gen-AI security copilot) and agentic identity protection position SentinelOne as a BENEFICIARY of AI proliferation, not a victim.

Non-endpoint revenue (Data Solutions/SIEM, Cloud, Identity) is now ~50% of bookings with triple-digit growth — transforming the company from an endpoint player (5x P/S) to a platform company (10-12x P/S). This platform shift is not yet reflected in the valuation.

## The Trade

- **Entry:** $14.22 (split: 50% now, 50% post-earnings)
- **Bull target (12 months):** $24.00 (+69%) — platform re-rating at 7-8x P/S
- **Base target:** $19.00 (+34%) — modest multiple expansion, consistent execution
- **Bear target:** $10.00 (-30%) — earnings miss, AI narrative wins
- **Stop-loss:** $11.50 (-19%)
- **Position size:** 3-5% of portfolio

## The Risk

1. **CEO is a consistent seller** (10b5-1 plan, ~$5.7M sold in 5 months)
2. **Insight Partners VC overhang** — massive proposed sales creating supply pressure
3. **Microsoft Defender bundling** — "good enough" free security pressures entire sector
4. **CrowdStrike competitive dominance** — CRWD at $5.25B ARR vs S at $1B, 5:1 scale advantage
5. **Growth deceleration** continues if enterprise IT budgets tighten

## The Verdict

**BUY — Contrarian entry at peak pessimism.**

SentinelOne at 5.1x P/S with 24% revenue growth, 74.7% gross margins, positive FCF, and a PEG of 0.43 is the cheapest name in growth cybersecurity. The stock is priced for failure — anything better than failure is upside. The March 12 earnings report is the near-term catalyst, but the real trade is the 12-month transformation from "unprofitable endpoint competitor" to "profitable AI security platform."
