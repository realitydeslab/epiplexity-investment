# Leader Profile — SentinelOne (S)

**Research Date:** March 9, 2026

---

## CEO: Tomer Weingarten

- **Role:** Co-Founder, President & CEO (since founding in 2013)
- **Background:** Israeli-born cybersecurity entrepreneur. Co-founded SentinelOne at age ~29. Previously worked at security companies including Toluna. Deep technical background in endpoint protection
- **Track Record:** Built SentinelOne from startup to $1B ARR, IPO'd in 2021 at $35/share. Has navigated intense competition from CrowdStrike and Palo Alto
- **Leadership Style:** Technical CEO, hands-on with product. Known for aggressive competitive positioning — publicly challenged CrowdStrike's architecture after the July 2024 outage
- **Recent Public Statements:**
  - CNBC (Feb 2026): Said Claude and AI products "raise the bar for what cybersecurity products do" — framing AI as tailwind, not threat
  - Has been vocal that cybersecurity is one of the few sectors where AI creates MORE demand (more attack surface = more security needed)
- **Concern:** CEO has been consistently SELLING shares (see insider activity below)

## Assessment

**Mixed signal.** The CEO is a capable technical founder who has built a real company to $1B ARR, but the consistent insider selling (even if 10b5-1) combined with Insight Partners' exit overhang creates a supply headwind. The lone bright spot is Director Peek's $595K open-market buy in December — a genuine conviction signal at the lows.

**Grade: C+** — Competent leadership, concerning insider selling pattern, offset by one meaningful insider buy.
