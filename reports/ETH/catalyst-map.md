## Catalyst 1: Glamsterdam Upgrade (L1 Scaling + MEV Fairness)

- **What:** Major network upgrade implementing ePBS (enshrined Proposer-Builder Separation), gas limit increases, and L1 scaling improvements. Fixes MEV fairness issues and increases throughput directly on L1.
- **When:** Q2-Q3 2026 (devnets active since late 2025, audits underway)
- **Impact:** +15-25% if executed cleanly. Demonstrates Ethereum can scale L1 directly, countering the "L2s are parasitic" narrative. Could reignite fee burn and deflationary pressure.
- **Probability:** High (80%) — engineering work well underway, Fusaka shipped Dec 2025 on schedule
- **Market expectation:** Priced as "another upgrade" — market is focused on competition from Solana/alt-L1s
- **Our edge:** Market underestimates Vitalik's strategic pivot back to L1 scaling. If gas usage rises from current 0.0 Gwei, the deflationary flywheel restarts.

## Catalyst 2: ETH ETF Staking Approval

- **What:** SEC approval for spot Ethereum ETFs to stake their holdings and pass yield to investors. Currently ETH ETFs cannot stake, making them yield-disadvantaged vs. direct holding.
- **When:** Q2-Q3 2026 (multiple pending applications from BlackRock, Fidelity)
- **Impact:** +20-30%. Staking approval would make ETH ETFs yield-bearing instruments (~2.9% APY), attracting massive institutional flows. This is the single biggest catalyst.
- **Probability:** Medium-High (65%) — pro-crypto SEC under Trump administration, but regulatory process takes time
- **Market expectation:** Market is skeptical after slow ETH ETF adoption relative to BTC ETFs
- **Our edge:** When BTC ETFs launched, the first-week flows exceeded all expectations. ETH staking approval would create a yield product that competes with bonds — a completely different investor base.

## Catalyst 3: Vitalik's L1 Scaling Pivot

- **What:** Vitalik publicly abandoned the "rollup-centric roadmap" (Feb 3, 2026), declaring L2s are "not scaling Ethereum." Refocusing on L1 throughput increases, ZK proofs, and blob-based execution.
- **When:** Feb 2026 → ongoing through 2026-2027
- **Impact:** +10-15% sentiment boost as narrative shifts. Long-term transformative.
- **Probability:** High (90%) — Vitalik has already published multiple detailed posts
- **Market expectation:** Market hasn't fully processed the implication: Ethereum L1 is becoming the execution layer again, not just settlement
- **Our edge:** This pivot addresses the #1 bear case (L2s extracting value from L1). If L1 recaptures activity, fee burn returns and ETH becomes deflationary again.

## Catalyst 4: Ethereum Foundation AI Strategy — "Trust Layer for AI"

- **What:** EF appointed AI lead (Davide Crapis), positioned Ethereum as coordination/verification layer for AI agents. ERC-8004 standard for onchain AI agents. Partnership with MetaMask, Coinbase, Google engineers.
- **When:** Active now, accelerating through 2026
- **Impact:** +10-20% if AI-agent narrative gains traction. Could redefine ETH as "AI infrastructure" alongside compute plays.
- **Probability:** Medium (50%) — depends on adoption velocity of AI agent frameworks
- **Market expectation:** Market views ETH as "old tech" vs. Solana/newer chains. AI angle is completely unpriced.
- **Our edge:** ETH has the deepest DeFi liquidity ($50B+ TVL) and smart contract ecosystem — the natural home for AI agents needing to transact.

## Catalyst 5: BitMine (BMNR) Treasury Strategy

- **What:** BitMine holds 4.47M ETH (3.71% of total supply), $6B+ staked. Planning MAVAN staking platform launch. Tom Lee-chaired, Strategy (MSTR)-style ETH treasury play.

- **Impact:** +5-10% supply squeeze effect, BUT also significant downside risk if forced to sell (currently 45% underwater, ~$7.4B unrealized losses)

- **Our edge:** If ETH recovers, BitMine becomes a massive reflexive bull. If not, it's a ticking bomb.
