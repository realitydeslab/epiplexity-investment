# ETH Leader Profile 👤

**Date:** March 9, 2026

---

## Vitalik Buterin — Co-Founder & Spiritual Leader

### Background
- Co-founded Ethereum at age 19 (proposed 2013, launched 2015)
- Thiel Fellowship recipient, dropped out of University of Waterloo
- Published hundreds of technical posts, EIPs, and research papers
- One of the most influential figures in all of crypto and blockchain

### Recent Activity & Strategic Direction

**Bold L1 Scaling Pivot (Feb 2026):**
- Published major blog post (Feb 27) outlining new vision for L1 scaling
- Explicitly said the rollup-centric roadmap "no longer makes sense"
- Focusing on: parallel block processing, gas repricing, ZK proofs, blob-based execution
- Called out L2s directly: "If you create a 10000 TPS EVM where its connection to L1 is mediated by a multisig bridge, then you are not scaling Ethereum"

**"You Are Not Scaling Ethereum" Post (Feb 3, 2026):**
- Delivered blunt reality check to L2 ecosystem
- Argued many L2s are not meeting decentralization/security standards
- Some L2s "not able or willing" to move beyond Stage 1
- Suggested L2s should focus on specialized value (privacy, speed, app-specific)

**AI Trust Layer Vision:**
- Supports EF's positioning of Ethereum as trust/coordination layer for AI
- EF appointed Davide Crapis as AI lead (Sept 2025)
- Ethereum as verification layer in an "increasingly AI-mediated world"

### ⚠️ Insider Selling — THE RED FLAG

**Vitalik sold ~20,000 ETH (~$40M) in early 2026.**

This was flagged by blockchain analytics firm Lookonchain and highlighted in the Culper Research short report (Mar 5, 2026). Culper stated: "Vitalik is selling, while bulls like Tom Lee are clueless as to ETH's new reality. We're with Vitalik."

**Context matters:**
- Vitalik has periodically sold ETH throughout Ethereum's history, often for charitable donations (Gitcoin grants, MIRI, etc.)
- He has historically sold near local tops
- The $40M represents a small fraction of his estimated holdings
- However, selling during a -59% drawdown from ATH is unusual and bearish signaling

### Key Hires / Departures (Last 6 Months)

- **Davide Crapis** — appointed AI lead at Ethereum Foundation (Sept 2025)
- **Dankrad Feist** — longtime ETH developer left EF to join Tempo (Oct 2025)
- **Aya Miyaguchi** — continues as EF President
- **Privacy officers** — new appointments to coordinate privacy projects across ecosystem (Oct 2025)
- **EF published "8 factions" report** (Sept 2025) — internal analysis warning that short-term pragmatism is eclipsing founding vision

### Tom Lee — BitMine (BMNR) Chairman

- Former Fundstrat Global Advisors co-founder, well-known crypto bull
- Chairs BitMine, the largest corporate ETH treasury holder (4.47M ETH)
- Publicly bullish on ETH, citing rising transaction counts and active addresses
- **Currently 45% underwater on ETH position** ($7.4B unrealized losses)
- Under attack from Culper Research short report
- Claims annualized staking revenue of $172M, scaling to $253M

### Assessment

**Vitalik:** Still the most capable technical leader in crypto. His pivot to L1 scaling is strategically sound and addresses the core bear case. BUT his selling is a warning sign.

**Tom Lee / BitMine:** Conviction bet, but dangerously leveraged to ETH price. If ETH doesn't recover, BitMine becomes a forced seller of 3.71% of total ETH supply — potentially catastrophic.

**Net:** Mixed signals. The technical vision is strong. The on-chain behavior of the founder is bearish. The largest institutional buyer is underwater.
