**Date:** March 9, 2026

## Revenue / Fee Growth — DECELERATING (Severely)

### Transaction Fees (Network Revenue)

| Period | Est. Daily Fees | Trend |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| Pre-Fusaka (Nov 2025) | ~$5-10M/day | Baseline |
| Post-Fusaka (Jan 2026) | ~$0.5-1M/day | -90% collapse |
| Current (Mar 2026) | Near zero (0.0 Gwei gas) | Floor |

**What happened:** The Fusaka upgrade (Dec 2025) introduced PeerDAS and phased blob expansion, massively increasing available blockspace. Combined with L2 migration, this cratered L1 fees by ~90%.

**Implication:** Ethereum's "ultrasound money" thesis (fee burn > issuance = deflationary) is currently broken. Supply growth is ~0.00%/year but was supposed to be negative.

### Supply Dynamics

- **Total supply:** 121,516,578 ETH
- **Annual supply growth:** ~0.00% (near zero — issuance roughly equals negligible burn)
- **Pre-Fusaka:** Supply was mildly deflationary in high-activity periods
- **Post-Fusaka:** Supply is now flat to mildly inflationary (issuance > burn)

### Transaction Count & Active Addresses

- Transaction counts have risen post-Fusaka due to cheaper gas
- **BUT Culper Research argues** significant portion is address poisoning attacks (spam/scam transactions), not genuine activity
- Active address growth is real but quality is questionable

Staking yield has dropped significantly as fees component approaches zero. Validators now earn almost purely from issuance rewards, not tips.

### DeFi TVL

- Ethereum remains the dominant DeFi chain by TVL
- Estimated $50B+ TVL on L1 (down from $100B+ peak)
- L2 TVL growing but mostly Base, Arbitrum, Optimism
- L2 growth is BEARISH for ETH L1 fees (activity migrating away)

### Growth Verdict: ⚠️ MIXED

**Bearish signals:**
- Fees collapsed 90% post-Fusaka
- Deflationary thesis broken
- Staking yields declining
- ETH/BTC at multi-year lows
- Vitalik selling

**Bullish signals:**
- Transaction counts rising (cheap gas = more usage)
- Staking participation stable/growing
- L1 scaling pivot could recapture fee revenue
- Supply remains near-zero growth (not inflationary)
- Price at same level as 12 months ago despite hitting ATH in between

**Growth is NOT accelerating.** Ethereum is in a transition phase. The old model (fee burn) is broken, the new model (L1 scaling + AI trust layer) hasn't materialized yet. This is the trough.
