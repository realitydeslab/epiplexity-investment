# RKLB Investment Thesis — Rocket Lab Corporation

*Updated: March 8, 2026*

## The Thesis in One Sentence
Rocket Lab is transforming from a small-rocket launcher into a vertically integrated space systems prime, with Neutron's Q4 2026 first launch as the binary catalyst that could reprice the stock 50%+ higher.

## The Catalyst
**Neutron first launch (Q4 2026)** is the defining event. Success opens a $10B+ medium-lift market currently dominated by SpaceX's Falcon 9. But even if Neutron slips, the underlying business — $1.85B backlog, 57% Q1 revenue growth guidance, $816M SDA defense contract — provides a strong floor.

## The Trade
- **Entry:** $70.08 (current), or $65-67 on pullback to recent support
- **Bull target:** $120 (BofA PT) — Neutron launches, defense contracts pile up, SpaceX IPO lifts comps → **+71%**
- **Base target:** $90 (Simply Wall St fair value) — partial catalyst realization, earnings beats → **+28%**
- **Bear target:** $45 — Neutron delayed to 2028, growth decelerates → **-36%**
- **Stop-loss:** $55 (below 200 DMA)
- **Position size:** 3-5% of portfolio

## The Risk
1. **Neutron execution** — rocket development is binary. Tank failure in January shows it's not easy.
2. **Valuation** — $38B market cap on -$198M GAAP net loss. Trading on faith in future profitability.
3. **Insider selling** — CEO Beck sold $141M in December 2025. Rational but not confidence-inspiring.
4. **SpaceX competition** — Starship could make Neutron's economics obsolete before it even flies.
5. **Macro** — High beta (2.20) means RKLB amplifies any market downturn.

## The Verdict: **BUY on dips, HOLD at current levels**

The risk/reward at $70 is moderately attractive. The ideal entry is $65 or below (near 50 DMA support). The $1.85B backlog and 57% growth guidance provide downside protection. Neutron is the asymmetric upside.

**Key insight:** Don't think of this as a rocket stock. Think of it as a defense prime contractor in its early innings with aerospace-level backlog visibility and tech-stock growth rates. That's the mispricing.
