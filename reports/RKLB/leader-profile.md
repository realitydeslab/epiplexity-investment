# RKLB Leader Profile — Sir Peter Beck

*Updated: March 8, 2026*

## CEO: Sir Peter Beck (Founder)
- **Background:** Self-taught rocket engineer from New Zealand. Founded Rocket Lab in 2006. Knighted in 2024 for services to aerospace.
- **Track record:** Built Electron from scratch into the world's 3rd most frequently launched orbital rocket (behind Falcon 9 and Long March). Took company public via SPAC in 2021.
- **Execution credibility:** 21 successful launches in 2025 (100% success rate for the year). Grew revenue from $245M (2023) → $436M (2024) → $602M (2025). Consistently beats guidance.
- **Vision:** Vertically integrated space company — launch + spacecraft + components. Not just a rocket company.
