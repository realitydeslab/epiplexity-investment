# RKLB Learnings & Intelligence

*Updated: March 8, 2026*

## What This Tells Us About the Sector

1. **Space is becoming a defense industry.** The $816M SDA contract signals that satellite manufacturing and launch services are now core national security infrastructure. The days of space being a "science project" are over.

2. **Vertical integration wins.** SpaceX proved it. Rocket Lab is the only other company pursuing full-stack space (launch + spacecraft + components). Everyone else is a components supplier or a single-service provider.

3. **The medium-lift market is the prize.** Small-lift (Electron) is a niche. Heavy-lift (Starship) may be overkill for most missions. Medium-lift (Neutron, Falcon 9 class) is the sweet spot for constellations, defense, and commercial.

## Adjacent Plays
- **Mynaric (MYNA)** — laser communication terminals for satellite constellations (including SDA)
- **Kratos Defense (KTOS)** — autonomous drones + satellite ground systems
- **L3Harris (LHX)** — space sensors and ISR
- **Redwire (RDW)** — space infrastructure and manufacturing
- **AST SpaceMobile (ASTS)** — direct-to-cell satellites (potential Rocket Lab launch customer)

## Contrarian Angle — What's the Market Missing?

1. **Space Systems is the real business.** Everyone focuses on rockets, but Space Systems is 60% of revenue and growing faster. Rocket Lab is becoming a spacecraft manufacturer that also launches.

2. **The backlog math.** $1.85B backlog on $602M revenue = 3+ years of revenue visibility. This is aerospace-level backlog-to-revenue ratio, yet RKLB trades like a speculative growth stock, not a defense prime.

3. **Neutron failure might not matter that much.** Even without Neutron, Rocket Lab has a $38B market cap on $602M revenue growing 40%+. The Space Systems + Electron business alone could justify $50-60/share. Neutron is optionality, not the base case.

4. **Peter Beck's selling is rational.** After 550%+ stock appreciation, a founder taking $141M off the table is normal portfolio management, not a bearish signal. He still owns $62M in stock.
