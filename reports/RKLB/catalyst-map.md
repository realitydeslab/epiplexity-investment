# RKLB Catalyst Map — Rocket Lab Corporation

*Updated: March 8, 2026*

## 1. Neutron First Launch (Q4 2026)
- **What:** First flight of Neutron medium-lift reusable rocket — Rocket Lab's gateway to the $10B+ medium/heavy launch market
- **When:** Q4 2026 (delayed from mid-2026 after January 2026 tank rupture during qualification testing)
- **Impact:** +30-50% on success. This is THE binary catalyst. Neutron opens a $10B+ TAM vs Electron's ~$500M niche.
- **Probability:** Medium. Tank failure in Jan 2026 was a setback but management says on track for Q4.
- **Market expectation:** Consensus expects delays — market has partially priced in a slip to 2027. Successful Q4 2026 launch would be a positive surprise.
- **Our edge:** Market too focused on delay headline. Rocket Lab has 95%+ Electron success rate (81 launches, ~248 satellites). Tank rupture was during ground testing (better than in-flight). Engineering track record is best-in-class outside SpaceX.

## 2. $816M Space Development Agency Contract Execution (Throughout 2026)
- **What:** Design and manufacture 18 satellites for SDA Tranche 3 Transport Layer — missile defense constellation
- **When:** Revenue recognition ramps through 2026-2027
- **Impact:** +10-15%. Validates Rocket Lab as prime defense contractor. Opens door to multi-billion dollar follow-ons.
- **Probability:** High. Contract signed and funded.
- **Market expectation:** Partially priced in. Margin profile and follow-on potential underappreciated.
- **Our edge:** Transforms RKLB from "small rocket company" to "integrated space systems prime." $1.85B backlog = 3x annual revenue.

## 3. Q1 2026 Earnings (Late April/Early May 2026)
- **What:** Guidance of $185M-$200M revenue, representing 57% YoY growth at midpoint
- **When:** Late April 2026
- **Impact:** +10-15% on beat, -10% on miss
- **Probability:** High for beat. Rocket Lab consistently beats guidance.
- **Market expectation:** Consensus ~$190M. $200M+ print would be very bullish.
- **Our edge:** Backlog conversion math supports higher-than-guided revenue.

## 4. Additional Defense/Government Contract Wins (H1-H2 2026)
- **What:** Follow-on SDA contracts, NSSL certification, DIU hypersonic test contracts
- **When:** Rolling throughout 2026
- **Impact:** +5-15% per major contract
- **Probability:** Medium-High. Geost acquisition positioned RKLB for payload capabilities needed for national security missions.
- **Market expectation:** Some government work priced in, but pipeline larger than consensus.

## 5. Electron Launch Cadence Acceleration (2026)
- **What:** Targeting 25+ Electron launches in 2026 (vs 21 in 2025)
- **When:** Throughout 2026
- **Impact:** +5-10%. Each launch = high-margin revenue ($7.5M+ per launch)
- **Probability:** High. Main risk is customer payload readiness.

## 6. SpaceX IPO / Valuation Event (2026)
- **What:** SpaceX reportedly planning $30B+ raise at $1.5T valuation — creates public comp for space companies
- **When:** Potentially H2 2026
- **Impact:** +10-20% via multiple expansion. RKLB is the only pure-play public comparable.
- **Probability:** Medium. Depends on market conditions.

## 7. Index Inclusion Potential
- **What:** $38B+ market cap makes RKLB eligible for S&P 500 if profitability criteria met
- **When:** 2026-2027 if GAAP profitability achieved
- **Impact:** +5-10% from index fund buying
- **Probability:** Low for 2026 (still GAAP unprofitable)
