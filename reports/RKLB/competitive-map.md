# RKLB Competitive Map — Rocket Lab Corporation

*Updated: March 8, 2026*

## Direct Competitors

### SpaceX (Private)
- **Overlap:** Falcon 9 dominates the medium-lift market Neutron targets
- **Advantage over RKLB:** Proven reusability, Starship in testing, massive launch cadence (100+ flights/year)
- **RKLB advantage:** Public/investable, integrated space systems (not just launch), smaller dedicated missions, US government needs second provider
- **Key dynamic:** US government actively wants a SpaceX alternative for national security — RKLB is the leading candidate

### Blue Origin (Private, Bezos-backed)
- **Overlap:** New Glenn medium-heavy lift rocket
- **Status:** First New Glenn launch in 2025 (partial success). Still ramping.
- **RKLB advantage:** More flight heritage, integrated spacecraft capabilities, faster cadence

### Firefly Aerospace (Recently IPO'd)
- **Overlap:** Alpha rocket (small-lift), MLV medium-lift in development
- **Status:** Earlier stage, fewer launches, less backlog
- **RKLB advantage:** 10x more flight heritage, proven at scale

### United Launch Alliance (Boeing/Lockheed JV)
- **Overlap:** Vulcan Centaur (medium-heavy), government launches
- **Status:** Operational but expensive, limited cadence
- **RKLB advantage:** Cost efficiency, vertical integration, growth trajectory

## Who Benefits from the Same Catalysts?
- **AJRD / Aerojet Rocketdyne** (L3Harris) — rocket propulsion
- **LHX / L3Harris** — defense space systems
- **LDOS / Leidos** — defense/intel satellite programs
- **KTOS / Kratos** — autonomous systems, defense tech
- **BA / Starliner** — if commercial crew demand rises

## Who Loses if RKLB Wins?
- **ULA** — loses government launch monopoly
- **Arianespace (private)** — European launch market share erodes
- **Virgin Orbit** — already bankrupt; small launch market is winner-take-most

## Pair Trade Opportunities
1. **Long RKLB / Short ULA exposure (via LMT/BA)** — if Neutron succeeds, it takes share from Vulcan
2. **Long RKLB + Long KTOS** — defense space + autonomous systems double play
3. **Long RKLB / Short SPCE (Virgin Galactic)** — real space company vs tourism play

## RKLB's Moat
1. **Vertical integration:** Only company besides SpaceX that builds rockets + spacecraft + components
2. **Flight heritage:** 81 Electron launches, 95%+ success rate
3. **Government relationships:** SDA, DARPA, DIU, NRO contracts
4. **Electron reuse:** Recovering and reusing Electron first stages (cost advantage)
5. **Neutron optionality:** If it works, TAM expands 10x+
