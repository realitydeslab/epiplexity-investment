- Q4 2025: $1.85B (+73% YoY)

## Launch Cadence (Electron)
- 2022: 9 launches
- 2023: 10 launches
- 2024: 16 launches
- 2025: 21 launches (100% success rate)
- 2026 target: 25+ launches

## Guidance vs Actual History
Rocket Lab consistently beats guidance:
- Q4 2025: Guided $165-175M, delivered $180M (+3-9% beat)
- Pattern of sandbagging guidance then beating — builds analyst confidence

## Segment Mix
- **Space Systems:** Now majority of revenue (~60%), growing faster, but lower margins
- **Launch Services:** ~40%, higher margins (~50%+ gross margin), Electron cadence driving this
- Q1 2026 margin guidance: slight decline due to Space Systems mix shift

## Unit Economics
- Electron launch: ~$7.5M per launch, improving margins through reuse/cadence
- Space Systems: Project-based, improving as scale increases
- **GAAP net loss:** $198.2M in 2025 (still unprofitable)
- **Non-GAAP gross margin target:** 44% (improving)
- **Path to profitability:** EBITDA breakeven possible in 2026-2027 as Neutron R&D peaks and launch margins scale
- **Cash position:** Strong liquidity (quick ratio 2.83, current ratio 4.08, debt-to-equity 0.10)

## Key Metric: Revenue Per Launch
Growing from ~$6M/launch historically to $7.5M+ as pricing power increases and satellite integration adds value per mission.
