## Leadership Assessment

**BULLISH.** The team is executing methodically on a clear regulatory pathway. Ben-Maimon has navigated FDA interactions skillfully — getting skin FXN accepted as a surrogate endpoint through the START pilot program is a significant regulatory achievement. The lean team of 65 employees and heavy insider ownership create strong alignment. Deerfield's continued involvement as anchor investor is a strong quality signal.

**Risk:** This is a one-drug company. If Ben-Maimon or key clinical leaders depart, the BLA timeline could slip.
