## Friedreich's Ataxia Treatment Landscape

| Company | Drug | Mechanism | Status | Price | Limitations |
|---------|------|-----------|--------|-------|-------------|

### Nomlabofusp's Competitive Advantages

1. **First frataxin replacement therapy:** Directly replaces the missing protein (root cause treatment), unlike Skyclarys which treats downstream effects
2. **100% biomarker response:** Every patient in OLE achieved carrier-level skin FXN at 6 months — unprecedented efficacy signal
3. **BTD designation:** Accelerated regulatory pathway that competitors lack for FA
4. **Both adults and children:** FDA accepted for all ages, while Skyclarys is limited to ≥16 years
5. **Complementary, not competitive:** Could potentially be used alongside Skyclarys (different mechanisms)

### Competitive Vulnerabilities

1. **Gene therapy threat:** Lexeo's one-time gene therapy could theoretically "cure" FA if successful, making chronic daily injections less attractive
2. **Daily subcutaneous injection:** Patient compliance burden vs. oral (Skyclarys) or one-time (gene therapy)
3. **Surrogate endpoint risk:** Approval based on skin FXN as surrogate means a confirmatory Phase 3 will still be required post-approval

## Who Benefits from SAME Catalysts?

Companies that benefit if FA frataxin replacement is validated:

- **Lexeo Therapeutics (LXEO):** Validation that increasing frataxin helps FA patients validates their gene therapy approach
- **Biogen (BIIB):** Expanded FA treatment market draws attention to the space, potentially boosting Skyclarys sales as first-line
- **CRISPR Therapeutics (CRSP):** Gene editing approaches for FA would benefit from precedent

## Who Loses if LRMR Wins?

- **Biogen/Skyclarys:** Nomlabofusp as a root-cause treatment could capture majority market share as preferred therapy
- **PTC Therapeutics:** Their frataxin inducer becomes less compelling if a direct replacement works better
- **Design Therapeutics:** Early-stage programs lose urgency if nomlabofusp is approved

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### Long LRMR / Short BIIB (FA market share shift)
- Rationale: If nomlabofusp approved, it displaces Skyclarys as preferred treatment
- Risk: Biogen is massive and diversified; FA is a small portion of revenue
- **Not recommended** — Biogen has too many other catalysts

### Long LRMR / Long LXEO (FA validation basket)
- Rationale: Both benefit from validation that frataxin restoration treats FA
- Risk: Gene therapy could ultimately make protein replacement obsolete
- **Consider:** Equal-weight basket for FA sector exposure
