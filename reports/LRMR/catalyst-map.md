## 🎯 Catalyst #1: BLA Submission (June 2026)

- **What:** Biologics License Application submission to FDA for nomlabofusp in Friedreich's Ataxia (FA), seeking accelerated approval using skin frataxin (FXN) concentration as a novel surrogate endpoint
- **When:** June 2026 (company reiterated timeline on Feb 24, 2026)
- **Impact:** +30-50% on filing acceptance. BLA submission validates the surrogate endpoint strategy and de-risks the regulatory path. Would be only the second FA treatment ever.
- **Probability:** HIGH — FDA granted Breakthrough Therapy Designation (BTD) on Feb 24, 2026 and explicitly stated openness to skin FXN as surrogate. START pilot program alignment is strong.
- **Market Expectation:** Most analysts already model BLA submission in H1 2026. Consensus target $16.29 assumes filing proceeds.
- **Our Edge:** Market is underappreciating the BTD + accelerated approval pathway. BTD grants priority review (6-month clock vs. 10-month standard), so PDUFA could land by Dec 2026/Q1 2027 — much sooner than most retail investors realize.

## 🎯 Catalyst #2: FDA Priority Review / PDUFA Date (Q4 2026–Q1 2027)

- **What:** If BLA is accepted with BTD priority review, FDA action date would be ~6 months post-filing = Dec 2026 to Feb 2027
- **When:** Q4 2026 – Q1 2027
- **Impact:** +100-200% on approval. Approval would make nomlabofusp the FIRST frataxin replacement therapy for FA, a disease with only one approved treatment (Skyclarys).
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH — BTD designation historically correlates with ~80%+ approval rates. Skin FXN normalization data is compelling (100% of participants achieved carrier-level FXN).
- **Market Expectation:** Market is pricing significant uncertainty — $480M mcap for a drug with $1-2B peak sales potential suggests only ~25-40% probability of approval is priced in.
- **Our Edge:** The surrogate endpoint (skin FXN) showed 100% response rate in the open-label study. The FDA's willingness to accept this as a surrogate is unusually accommodating for a rare disease program.

## 🎯 Catalyst #3: Ongoing Open-Label Study Data Updates (Q2-Q3 2026)

- **What:** Additional long-term efficacy and safety data from the Phase 2 OLE study, including adolescent cohort data and 18+ month follow-up
- **When:** Mid-2026 (likely at medical conferences or company updates)
- **Impact:** +15-25% on positive data; -30-40% on safety signals or efficacy plateau
- **Probability:** HIGH for continued positive data — 65 participants dosed across all studies, 39 in OLE, 14 on treatment 6+ months, 8 for over 1 year. Consistent directional improvement on mFARS, FARS-ADL, 9-HPT, and MFIS.
- **Market Expectation:** Incremental positive data expected but updates on functional endpoints (mFARS improvement) are key.
- **Our Edge:** The shift from skin FXN (biomarker) to functional clinical endpoints over time will strengthen the approval case and could surprise on the upside.
