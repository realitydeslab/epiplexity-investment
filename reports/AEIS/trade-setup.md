### What Kills the Trade?
1. **Data center capex pause** — if hyperscalers pull back on AI infrastructure spending
2. **Margin disappointment** — if 40% gross margin target proves elusive (cost inflation, pricing pressure)
3. **Semi equipment double-dip** — if WFE spending declines again
4. **Macro recession** — high beta means AEIS falls harder in a downturn
5. **China tariff escalation** — AEIS has Asia manufacturing exposure

### Correlation Risk
- Highly correlated with semiconductor equipment sector (ASML, AMAT, LRCX)
- Correlated with AI infrastructure spending (NVDA capex cycle)
- If you already hold heavy semi equipment exposure, AEIS adds concentration risk
- Diversification benefit: data center power is a different end market than traditional semi
