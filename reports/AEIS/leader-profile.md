# AEIS — Leader Profile 👤

**CEO: Steve Kelley** | President & CEO since March 2021

---

## Background
- **Education:** BS Chemical Engineering, MIT; JD, Santa Clara University
- **Previous roles:**
  - CEO, Amkor Technology (major semiconductor packaging company) — led financial transformation with strong revenue growth and efficiency gains
  - Executive roles at Cree, Texas Instruments, Philips Semiconductors (NXP)
- **Industry experience:** 30+ years in semiconductor industry
- **Hired:** March 2021 — brought in specifically to transform Advanced Energy

## Leadership Assessment

**Strengths:**
- **Transformation track record:** Successfully led Amkor through financial turnaround — relevant experience for AEIS's pivot toward data center power
- **Semiconductor DNA:** Deep understanding of the customer base (semi equipment makers are AEIS's core market)
- **Built the team:** Brought in several executives from Amkor/TI ecosystem (COO Eduardo Bernal from NXP, CCO John Donaghey from Amkor) — cohesive leadership team
- **Strategic pivot:** Under Kelley, AEIS has successfully diversified from pure semi equipment toward data center computing, telecom, and networking power solutions
- **40% margin vision:** Set ambitious but achievable gross margin target, demonstrating focus on profitability not just revenue

**Concerns:**
- **Not a founder** — hired CEO, which means less personal skin in the game
- **Modest insider ownership:** 1.05% insider ownership is low
- **Net insider selling:** -6.5% insider transaction ratio (net sellers), though this often reflects equity compensation vesting rather than bearish signals for non-founder CEOs

## Key Executive Team
- **Paul Oldham** — CFO since May 2018, 30 years finance experience, former CFO at ESI (led multi-year transformation)
- **Eduardo Bernal** — COO since Sept 2021, 30+ years semiconductor ops, former SVP at NXP (12,000 employees), Singapore-based overseeing Thailand expansion
- **John Donaghey** — CCO since July 2021, 30 years electronics manufacturing, TI and Amkor background
- **Emdrem Tan** — EVP System Power (promoted Aug 2024), 40+ years semi industry, oversees data center computing, telecom & networking product groups — **KEY person for the data center growth thesis**

## Insider Activity
- **Insider ownership:** 1.05%
- **Net insider transactions:** -6.5% (net selling)
- **Assessment:** Moderately negative signal, but context matters — equity comp vesting creates mechanical selling. No large discretionary insider buys visible, which is a neutral-to-slightly-negative indicator. Would prefer to see CEO buying in open market.

## Public Statements
- Management has publicly discussed:
  - "Data Center Surge" as a growth driver
  - 40% gross margin goal (up from 37.7%)
  - Thailand capacity plans for margin improvement
  - Jim Cramer recently highlighted AEIS, saying "I want you to wait till it's in the 280s, 290s, and then pull the trigger" (Mar 8, 2026)

## Verdict
**B+ Leader** — Kelley is a competent transformation CEO with the right background for this moment. He's executing well on the data center pivot and margin expansion. The team is cohesive and experienced. Main knock: modest insider ownership and net selling. Not a visionary founder, but a strong operator — which is exactly what AEIS needs right now.
