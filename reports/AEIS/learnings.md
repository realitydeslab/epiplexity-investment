## What Does This Tell Us About the Sector?

### The AI Power Bottleneck Is Real
AEIS's 21% revenue growth and margin expansion tells us something important: **the AI data center buildout is creating massive demand for power infrastructure, and this demand is filtering down the supply chain to specialized power conversion companies.** This isn't just an NVIDIA story — every GPU rack needs precision power delivery.

### Power Is the New Bandwidth
In the AI infrastructure stack, the bottleneck is shifting:
1. **2023-2024:** GPU supply (NVDA Blackwell allocation)
2. **2024-2025:** Networking (AVGO, ANET)
3. **2025-2026:** Power delivery and cooling (AEIS, VRT, CEG)
4. **2026+:** Physical space and grid capacity

AEIS is positioned at the emerging bottleneck. When TSMC or NVIDIA talk about supply constraints, it creates urgency for power infrastructure companies.

### Semi Equipment + Data Center = Unique Dual Exposure
AEIS is one of few companies that benefits from BOTH the semiconductor equipment recovery AND the AI data center buildout. This dual exposure reduces cyclical risk while offering two independent growth drivers.

## Contrarian Angle — What's the Market Missing?

### The Good Contrarian Signal
1. **Insider selling** — The -6.5% net insider transaction ratio suggests insiders are taking profits. Could mean they see limited near-term upside... or just equity comp vesting.
2. **5.93% short float** — Non-trivial shorts suggest some believe the stock is overvalued at current levels.
3. **Analyst PT disconnect** — Average PT of $278 is BELOW the current price ($293). Yet consensus is "Strong Buy." This suggests PTs haven't caught up with the recent rally — upgrades likely coming.

### What the Market May Be Missing
1. **Power density escalation:** Next-gen GPU racks (NVIDIA Blackwell/Vera Rubin, AMD MI400) draw significantly more power than current gen. AEIS's revenue per data center could increase 50-100% as power density rises.
2. **48V power architecture transition:** The industry shift from 12V to 48V data center power architectures benefits AEIS's product lineup. This is a multi-year tailwind.
3. **OCP design wins:** AEIS's OCP-compliant power shelves position them as a standard supplier to hyperscalers. Design wins are sticky and recurring.
