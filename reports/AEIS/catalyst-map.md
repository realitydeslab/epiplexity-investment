# AEIS — Catalyst Map 🎯

**Advanced Energy Industries** | NASDAQ: AEIS | $293.04 | Mar 9, 2026

---

## Catalyst 1: AI Data Center Power Delivery Supercycle
- **What:** Advanced Energy is a critical supplier of precision power conversion for AI data centers. Every GPU rack needs power shelves and DC-DC converters — AEIS makes them. Company expanding ultra-efficient non-isolated bus converters and OCP-compliant power shelves specifically for hyperscaler AI clusters.
- **When:** Ongoing through 2026-2027; each quarterly earnings (May, Aug, Nov) will show acceleration
- **Impact:** +15-25% on strong data center revenue beats. Data center computing is their fastest-growing segment.
- **Probability:** HIGH — AI capex cycle confirmed by every hyperscaler (MSFT $80B, META $65B, GOOGL $75B, AMZN $100B 2025 capex plans)
- **Market Expectation:** Consensus models ~22% revenue growth in 2026. Street is catching up but may still underestimate the power delivery bottleneck.
- **Our Edge:** Power delivery is a less-covered bottleneck than GPUs or networking. Most investors focus on NVDA/AVGO — AEIS is the "picks and shovels" of the power layer. 40% gross margin target suggests pricing power the Street hasn't fully modeled.

## Catalyst 2: 40% Gross Margin Target
- **What:** Management has publicly stated a goal of reaching 40% gross margins, up from current 37.7%. This would be driven by: (1) Thailand factory ramp with lower costs, (2) higher-margin data center product mix, (3) operating leverage on growing revenue base.
- **When:** Gradual through FY2026; could hit 39-40% by Q3-Q4 2026
- **Impact:** +10-15% on margin expansion beats. Every 100bp of gross margin improvement = ~$18M additional gross profit at $1.8B revenue run rate.
- **Probability:** HIGH — Thailand factory already producing at record margins; product mix shifting toward higher-ASP data center solutions.
- **Market Expectation:** Street models ~38% gross margin for FY2026. 40% would be a meaningful upside surprise.
- **Our Edge:** Thailand capacity ramp is tangible and already showing results. Not a future promise — it's executing now.

## Catalyst 3: Semiconductor Equipment Recovery
- **What:** AEIS's legacy plasma power business serves semiconductor fab equipment (etch, deposition). After a 2023-2024 cyclical downturn, the semi equipment cycle is recovering with leading-edge investments (TSMC Arizona, Intel Foundry, Samsung Taylor).
- **When:** Q1-Q3 2026 as fab equipment orders flow through
- **Impact:** +5-10% — provides additional revenue tailwind on top of data center growth
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH — WFE spending recovering but pace uncertain; China export controls add complexity
- **Market Expectation:** Consensus expects gradual semi recovery. Upside if recovery is faster than expected.
- **Our Edge:** AEIS has dual exposure — both semi equipment AND data center. Most semi equipment companies don't have the data center kicker.

## Catalyst 4: Thailand Manufacturing Capacity Expansion
- **What:** AEIS is ramping a Thailand factory for system power products, which provides lower manufacturing costs and higher margins compared to legacy facilities. The company has discussed expanding capacity for data center power products.
- **When:** Capacity additions through 2026; contributing to earnings by mid-2026
- **Impact:** +5-8% — margin improvement and capacity to meet surging data center demand
- **Probability:** HIGH — factory is operational and delivering results
- **Market Expectation:** Partially priced in, but magnitude of margin benefit may surprise
- **Our Edge:** Thailand capacity is a structural cost advantage that compounds over time.

## Catalyst 5: EPS Acceleration / Multiple Expansion
- **What:** FY2026 consensus EPS of $8.77 represents +128% YoY growth from $3.84. If AEIS delivers above consensus (they beat Q4 by 8.81%), the stock could re-rate from current 33x forward PE toward 35-40x as growth credentials strengthen.
- **When:** Each quarterly earnings report (May, Aug, Nov 2026, Feb 2027)
- **Impact:** +20-30% if multiple expands from 33x to 38-40x forward PE on higher EPS
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — execution needs to continue; macro/tariff risks exist
- **Market Expectation:** $8.77 EPS consensus for FY2026. Beat-and-raise cycle could push toward $9-10.
- **Our Edge:** Company has historically beaten estimates — Q4 2025 beat by 8.81% on EPS.
