**Advanced Energy Industries** | NASDAQ: AEIS | $293.04 | Mar 9, 2026

## The One-Liner
**AEIS is the precision power conversion backbone of AI data centers, riding a dual-cyclical recovery (semi equipment + data center) with accelerating margins toward a new earnings peak in FY2026.**

---

## The Catalyst
AI data centers need exponentially more power, and AEIS makes the precision power conversion systems (PSUs, DC-DC converters, power shelves) that sit inside every GPU rack. With hyperscalers spending $300B+ on AI infrastructure in 2025-2026, AEIS is a critical — and under-covered — supplier. The 40% gross margin target (from 37.7%) and Thailand factory ramp provide earnings upside beyond revenue growth.
