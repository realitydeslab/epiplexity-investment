Europe's defense spending supercycle is structural, not cyclical. NATO members are racing from 1.7% to 3.5% of GDP on defense — that's a 100% increase over 5-7 years. BAE Systems, as Europe's largest defense contractor with a record **£83.6B backlog** (~3x annual revenue), is the primary beneficiary. Key 12-month catalysts: H1 2026 results (Aug) with likely guidance upgrade, AUKUS submarine milestones, ReArm Europe contract awards, and Saudi Arabia orders. 3. **Best backlog visibility** — £83.6B record, growing every quarter
4. **Zero beta (-0.06)** — genuine portfolio diversifier, uncorrelated to equity markets
5. **US exposure** — BAE Systems Inc. is a top-6 US defense supplier
6. **Sole-source programs** — submarines (Dreadnought, SSN-AUKUS), Typhoon support = no competition
7. **Income + growth** — 1.58% yield AND 12%+ profit growth
