# BAE Systems (BAESY) — Leader Profile 👤

*Research Date: 2026-03-09*

---

## CEO: Charles Woodburn

- **Tenure:** CEO since July 2017 (8+ years)
- **Background:** Engineering degree from Cambridge, MBA from Erasmus Rotterdam. Previously COO of BAE Systems (2016-2017), CEO of Expro Group (oilfield services). Spent 20 years at Shell in various leadership roles globally.
- **Style:** Operational excellence focus. Disciplined capital allocator. Not a flashy communicator — steady hand.
- **Track record as CEO:**
  - Share price from ~GBX 600 (2017) to GBX 2,234 (+272%)
  - Revenue from ~£18B to £28.3B (+57%)
  - Operating profit from ~£1.8B to £3.32B (+84%)
  - Rebuilt order backlog from ~£40B to record £83.6B
  - Navigated Brexit uncertainty, COVID, and geopolitical upheaval
  - Completed Ball Aerospace acquisition ($5.6B, 2024) — diversified into space
  - Maintained dividend growth throughout tenure

## Assessment: Right Leader for This Moment?
**YES.** Woodburn's operational focus is exactly what's needed to execute on the backlog conversion. He's proven at scaling, managing multi-country programs (Typhoon, F-35), and navigating government relationships. He's not a visionary — but BAE doesn't need vision right now. It needs execution on a massive, growing backlog.

The Ball Aerospace acquisition shows strategic thinking — adding space and intelligence capabilities ahead of the AI-defense convergence.
