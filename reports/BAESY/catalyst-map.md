# BAE Systems (BAESY) — Catalyst Map 🎯

*Research Date: 2026-03-09 | ADR Price: $121.38 | London (BA.L): GBX 2,234*

---

## Catalyst 1: European ReArm Spending Surge — NATO 3.5% GDP Target
- **What:** NATO members committing to 3.5% GDP defense spending (up from 2%). EU "ReArm Europe" plan with €800B+ in defense investment. UK pledged to increase to 2.5% GDP by 2027, with pressure to go higher. Germany exempted defense from debt brake, approved €500B infrastructure fund.
- **When:** Throughout 2026 — budget announcements Q2-Q3, spending ramp H2 2026 into 2027
- **Impact:** +15-25% revenue acceleration potential over 2-3 years. BAE is the largest European defense contractor.
- **Probability:** HIGH — political consensus across Europe, bipartisan support
- **Market expectation:** Partially priced in (stock +345% over 5 years), but spending commitments keep growing
- **Our edge:** Market underestimates the *duration* of the spending cycle. This isn't a one-year bump — it's a structural 5-10 year rearmament. BAE's £83.6B backlog provides visibility, but new orders from ReArm Europe are incremental.

## Catalyst 2: AUKUS Submarine Program Acceleration
- **What:** SSN-AUKUS nuclear attack submarines — BAE Systems is the prime contractor for UK build at Barrow-in-Furness. Australia will build 8 boats under license. This is a multi-decade, £100B+ program.
- **When:** First steel cut expected 2026. Australian funding commitment milestones Q2-Q3 2026. Next progress update likely at BAE H1 results (July/August 2026).
- **Impact:** +5-10% on program visibility alone. Signals decades of secured revenue.
- **Probability:** HIGH — treaty-level commitment between UK, US, Australia
- **Market expectation:** Known but underappreciated in scale. The spend curve steepens from 2026.
- **Our edge:** Market prices BAE as a defense company, not a nuclear submarine monopolist. Barrow is the only UK nuclear sub facility.

## Catalyst 3: GCAP (Global Combat Air Programme) Milestones
- **What:** UK-Italy-Japan 6th-gen fighter program. BAE Systems is UK lead contractor. Designed to replace Typhoon by 2035.
- **When:** Formal development contract decisions expected 2026. Concept design phase advancing through 2026.
- **Impact:** +3-5% on sentiment. Long-term revenue driver (£30B+ program value).
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH — government committed, but pace could slow
- **Market expectation:** Priced as a long-term option, not near-term revenue
- **Our edge:** Development funding accelerating faster than expected as geopolitical urgency mounts

## Catalyst 4: UK Defense Budget Increase
- **What:** UK committed to 2.5% GDP defense spending by 2027. Calls from within government to move to 3% or higher given current threats. Spring Statement (March 26, 2026) and Strategic Defence Review updates could signal acceleration.
- **When:** March 26 Spring Statement; Q2-Q3 budget details
- **Impact:** +5-8% on UK-exposed revenue (Air, Maritime segments). BAE is the #1 UK defense supplier.
- **Probability:** HIGH — cross-party support, US pressure for NATO burden-sharing
- **Market expectation:** 2.5% by 2027 is consensus. Anything above or accelerated = upside surprise.
- **Our edge:** B-1 bomber deployments to RAF Fairford (March 2026) signal heightened readiness → faster procurement

## Catalyst 5: H1 2026 Results & Updated Guidance
- **What:** Half-year results expected July/August 2026. BAE has a track record of guidance beats.
- **When:** Late July / early August 2026
- **Impact:** +5-10% if they raise full-year guidance (as they did in FY2025)
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH — backlog conversion + new orders support beat
- **Market expectation:** Consensus FY2026 EPS ~$3.82-$4.43 depending on analyst
- **Our edge:** Erste Group estimates $4.43 EPS for FY2026, 16% above consensus $3.82. If BAE delivers toward the high end, stock re-rates.

## Catalyst 6: US Defense Spending (BAE Systems Inc.)
- **What:** BAE's US subsidiary is one of the 6 largest DoD suppliers. US defense budget >$800B. Key programs: Bradley replacement (AMPV), electronic warfare (F-35 electronics), naval ship repair.
- **When:** FY2027 US defense budget details Q2 2026; contract awards throughout year
- **Impact:** +3-5% on US segment growth
- **Probability:** HIGH — US defense spending bipartisan priority
- **Market expectation:** Steady growth baked in
- **Our edge:** BAE US is less visible to UK-focused analysts. Electronic warfare/cyber growing fastest.

## Catalyst 7: Saudi Arabia & Middle East Orders
- **What:** Typhoon replacement/upgrade discussions. Saudi Arabia is BAE's third-largest market. Potential for new major contracts as Saudi modernizes military.
- **When:** Ongoing negotiations; potential announcements Q2-Q4 2026
- **Impact:** +3-8% on major contract win
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — geopolitical complexity, but commercial interest strong
- **Market expectation:** Not priced in; treated as option value
- **Our edge:** UK-Saudi defense relationship deepening; post-Yemen, political obstacles reducing

## Catalyst 8: Denmark Simulation Training Contract (Just Announced)
- **What:** Danish Army selected BAE Systems for simulation training upgrade. Part of broader Nordic/Baltic defense buildup.
- **When:** Announced March 9, 2026 (today)
- **Impact:** +0.5% individually, but signals broadening European customer base
- **Probability:** CONFIRMED — contract awarded
- **Market expectation:** Not yet priced in
- **Our edge:** Signals BAE expanding beyond traditional UK/US/Saudi markets into Nordic defense expansion
