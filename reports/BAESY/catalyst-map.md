# BAE Systems (BAESY) — Catalyst Map 🎯

*Research Date: 2026-03-09 | Price: $121.92 | Market Cap: $91.2B*

## 1. European ReArm Spending Supercycle (Ongoing → Q4 2026)

- **What:** EU ReArm Europe initiative committing €800B+ in defense spending. Germany exempted defense from debt brake, approved €500B infrastructure fund. NATO members racing to 3.5% GDP targets. UK committed to 2.5% GDP by 2027.
- **When:** Spending acceleration through all of 2026, major contract awards Q2-Q4 2026
- **Impact:** +15-25% revenue uplift over 2-3 years; could add £5-8B to backlog annually
- **Probability:** HIGH — already legislated in multiple countries
- **Market expectation:** Market expects moderate growth; consensus EPS $3.82 for FY2026 may be conservative
- **Our edge:** BAE is Europe's largest defense contractor but trades at a discount to Rheinmetall (37x P/E) and Saab (48x P/E). Market underprices BAE's diversified exposure across UK, US, EU, Saudi, Australia

## 2. AUKUS Submarine Program Acceleration (H1-H2 2026)

- **What:** AUKUS pact commits Australia to nuclear-powered SSN-AUKUS submarines built by BAE at Barrow-in-Furness. Australia investing A$368B+ over 30 years. First steel cut expected 2026.
- **When:** Major milestone announcements expected H2 2026
- **Impact:** Multi-decade revenue stream worth £100B+; each milestone announcement = 3-5% stock catalyst
- **Probability:** HIGH — treaty-level commitment between US/UK/Australia
- **Market expectation:** Known but timeline risk underappreciated
- **Our edge:** BAE is the ONLY builder for UK submarine programs. Zero competition. Revenue visibility extends to 2060s

## 3. GCAP 6th-Gen Fighter Program (2026 milestones)

- **What:** Global Combat Air Programme (UK/Japan/Italy) — next-gen stealth fighter to replace Eurofighter/F-2. BAE leads the UK industrial share.
- **When:** Program design review milestones throughout 2026; first flight target 2035
- **Impact:** Estimated £100B+ program; design phase worth £2-3B in near-term contracts
- **Probability:** HIGH — three-nation government commitment, organization GIGO established Dec 2023
- **Market expectation:** Long-dated; market assigns minimal near-term value
- **Our edge:** Near-term design contract awards in 2026 are under-modeled by analysts

## 4. H1 2026 Results / Upgraded Guidance (Aug 2026)

- **What:** BAE interim results likely to show continued order intake acceleration from ReArm Europe. Potential guidance upgrade.
- **When:** August 2026 (H1 results)
- **Impact:** Guidance raise could drive 5-10% re-rating
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH — Q4 2025 EBITDA already +43.4% YoY, FCF +59.3%
- **Market expectation:** Consensus at $3.82 EPS for FY2026; Erste Group sees $4.43
- **Our edge:** Record £83.6B backlog provides exceptional visibility; street estimates look conservative

## 5. Saudi Arabia Mega-Contracts (H1-H2 2026)

- **What:** Saudi Vision 2030 and regional security spending. BAE is Saudi Arabia's largest defense supplier (Typhoon, Hawk trainer, armored vehicles). New order potential from Saudi's increased defense budget.
- **When:** Ongoing throughout 2026
- **Impact:** Individual contracts can be £5-10B; +5-8% on announcements
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — geopolitical dependencies, but relationship is deep (40+ year partnership)
- **Market expectation:** Saudi exposure viewed as both opportunity and ESG risk
- **Our edge:** ESG concerns create artificial discount; Saudi spending is accelerating, not slowing

## 6. Dreadnought-Class Submarine Progress (2026)

- **What:** UK's nuclear deterrent replacement program — four Dreadnought-class submarines. BAE is sole builder. First boat (HMS Dreadnought) scheduled for sea trials by 2030.
- **When:** Construction milestones throughout 2026
- **Impact:** £31B+ program; steady revenue contributor with margin expansion as production matures
- **Probability:** HIGH — national security priority, ring-fenced funding
- **Market expectation:** Baked into base case but execution milestones could positively surprise

- **Impact:** $150-200M in annual synergies; margin accretive
- **Probability:** HIGH — integration reportedly on track
- **Market expectation:** Partially modeled; upside from faster realization
