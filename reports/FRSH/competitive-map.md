## Who Loses if Freshworks Wins?

2. **AI-native startups** — if pure AI agents (like Intercom Fin, Ada) replace traditional helpdesks
3. **Salesforce** — if mid-market enterprises "graduate" to CRM/NOW without stopping at FRSH

## Competitive Moat Assessment

**Moat strength: NARROW** (2/5)

**Strengths:**
- 84.96% gross margin (best in class)
- 30.26% insider ownership (aligned)
- "Uncomplicated" positioning resonates with mid-market buyers who don't want CRM/NOW complexity
- Growing AI capabilities (Freddy) at price points enterprises can't get elsewhere
- $632M net cash — financial strength to invest and acquire

**Weaknesses:**
- No true lock-in — switching costs are moderate, not high
- SMB customer base has higher churn than enterprise
- Brand recognition weaker than CRM/NOW/HUBS
- Dependent on continuing to deliver value vs. AI-native alternatives

**The contrarian case:** The market is pricing FRSH as if it has ZERO moat. But 84.96% gross margins, 28% FCF margins, and growing revenue don't happen without something working. The "uncomplicated" positioning IS the moat — mid-market companies need capable-but-simple tools, and FRSH delivers.
