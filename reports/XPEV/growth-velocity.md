## Gross Margin Trajectory — IMPROVING RAPIDLY

| Period | Gross Margin |
|--------|-------------|
| FY2023 | 1.47% 😱 |
| Q4 2024 | 14.44% |
| Q1 2025 | 15.56% |
| Q2 2025 | 17.33% |
| Q3 2025 | 20.14% 🚀 |

**Gross margin went from 1.5% to 20% in two years.** This is an extraordinary improvement driven by:
1. Scale leverage on fixed manufacturing costs
2. Higher ASP models (X9, P7+)
3. Supply chain cost optimization
4. MONA M03 BOM cost discipline

At 20% gross margin, XPeng is approaching Li Auto territory (~22%) and well above NIO (~10-13%).
