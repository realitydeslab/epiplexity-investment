## ANKTIVA Differentiation
- **Mechanism:** IL-15 superagonist — activates NK cells and CD8+ T cells differently from PD-1/PD-L1 checkpoint inhibitors
- **Combination potential:** ANKTIVA + BCG is additive, not competitive with checkpoint inhibitors
- **First-mover in BCG-unresponsive:** Only approved immunotherapy specifically for BCG-unresponsive NMIBC CIS

## Who Benefits from the Same Catalysts?

- **BCG manufacturers:** Merck KGaA (BCG supply) — more ANKTIVA use = more BCG use
