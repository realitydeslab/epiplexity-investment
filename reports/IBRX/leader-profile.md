# IBRX Leader Profile — Patrick Soon-Shiong

## The Leader
- **Name:** Patrick Soon-Shiong, MD
- **Role:** Executive Chairman, Global Chief Medical & Technology Officer
- **Age:** 73 (born July 29, 1952)
- **Net Worth:** $16.6B (Bloomberg/Forbes 2026)

- **Transplant surgery:** Pioneered pancreatic islet cell transplant techniques
- **Academic:** Adjunct professor UCLA Surgery, visiting professor Imperial College London and Dartmouth
- **Serial entrepreneur:** Built multiple biotech companies from concept to commercialization
