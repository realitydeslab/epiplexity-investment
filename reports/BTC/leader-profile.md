## Bitcoin's "CEO": Decentralized Leadership

Bitcoin has no CEO — that's the point. But it has key figures whose actions matter enormously for price:

### Satoshi Nakamoto (Creator)
- Pseudonymous, identity unknown, ~1.1M BTC in untouched wallets
- Not active since 2010. Coins haven't moved. This is BULLISH — no dump risk from founder
- The "Satoshi wallet" moving would be the single most bearish event possible

### Michael Saylor / Strategy (Largest Corporate Holder)
- **Role:** De facto Bitcoin spokesperson and largest corporate buyer
- **Holdings:** 738,731 BTC ($56.04B cost, avg price $75,862)
- **Recent Action:** Just bought 17,994 BTC ($1.28B) at avg $70,946 — BUYING THE DIP
- **Financing Innovation:** STRC preferred stock (11.50% dividend) funded 30% of latest purchase. This is financial engineering genius — converting yield-seeking institutional capital into BTC accumulation.
- **Conviction Signal:** 🟢 EXTREME. Strategy is buying at scale during extreme fear. Saylor has never sold a single Bitcoin.
- **Risk:** If BTC drops below Strategy's avg cost ($75,862) significantly and stays there, margin/debt pressure could force liquidation. Current price ($69K) is below avg cost.

### Larry Fink / BlackRock (Largest ETF Issuer)
- **Role:** World's largest asset manager, runs iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT)
- **Stance:** Called Bitcoin "digital gold" and compared it to early internet. Pro-BTC.
- **Institutional Signal:** BlackRock's entry validated Bitcoin for pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, and RIAs.

### Donald Trump (US President)
- **Role:** Most pro-crypto US president in history
- **Actions:**
  - Signed Strategic Bitcoin Reserve executive order (March 2025)
  - Cyber strategy explicitly supports cryptocurrency security
  - Appointed pro-crypto regulators across SEC, CFTC, OCC
  - Family involved in crypto (World Liberty Financial, Eric Trump)
- **Conviction Signal:** 🟢 HIGH. Trump's political brand is now tied to crypto success.

### Key Bitcoin Core Developers
- Active, no drama. Protocol is stable. Taproot/Ordinals/BRC-20 innovations continuing.
- No contentious hard forks planned — stability is a feature.
