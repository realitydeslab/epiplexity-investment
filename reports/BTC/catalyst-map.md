## Catalyst 1: Iran War Resolution 🔥
- **What:** US-Iran conflict ends or reaches ceasefire. Trump stated operations "very far ahead" of 4-5 week timeline. Oil dropped from $120→$85/barrel in 24 hours on Trump's comments.
- **When:** Days to weeks (March 2026)
- **Impact:** +10-20% ($76K-$83K). War resolution = risk-on rally across equities and crypto. Oil normalization reduces inflation fears, potentially restoring Fed rate cut expectations.
- **Probability:** HIGH (Trump signaling imminent resolution)
- **Market Expectation:** Markets pricing in 50/50 quick resolution. Already bounced 2.4% on March 9 comments.
- **Our Edge:** The EXTREME FEAR (index at 8) means any positive resolution will trigger a disproportionate relief rally. Short positions are elevated.

## Catalyst 2: GENIUS Act (Stablecoin Legislation) 📜
- **What:** First comprehensive US stablecoin regulation. US Treasury already acknowledging legitimate privacy uses. Congressional report on implementation released.
- **When:** Q1-Q2 2026 (active progress now)
- **Impact:** +5-10% ($72K-$76K). Regulatory clarity brings institutional capital off the sidelines. Stablecoin legislation validates the entire crypto ecosystem.
- **Probability:** HIGH (bipartisan support, Trump admin pro-crypto)
- **Market Expectation:** Expected to pass but timing uncertain. Already partially priced.
- **Our Edge:** The GENIUS Act's passage creates a domino effect — stablecoin clarity enables broader market structure legislation, unlocking a multi-quarter regulatory tailwind.

## Catalyst 3: Digital Asset Market Clarity Act ⚖️
- **What:** Comprehensive crypto market structure bill defining SEC vs CFTC jurisdiction. Former CFTC Chair Giancarlo says banks need it more than crypto firms.
- **When:** Q2-Q3 2026
- **Impact:** +10-15% ($76K-$79K). Removes the #1 barrier to institutional adoption — regulatory uncertainty.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH (gaining momentum, but Congress moves slowly)
- **Market Expectation:** Consensus doubts passage in 2026. Low expectations.
- **Our Edge:** If this passes alongside GENIUS Act, it creates a regulatory supercycle. Markets are underpricing the speed of the current pro-crypto legislative push.

## Catalyst 4: Fed Rate Cuts Resume 📉
- **What:** Federal Reserve resumes rate cutting cycle. Currently delayed by Iran war oil inflation. Once war resolves, inflation pressure eases, enabling 2-3 cuts in H2 2026.
- **When:** Q3-Q4 2026 (contingent on war resolution + oil normalization)
- **Impact:** +15-25% ($79K-$86K). Rate cuts = weaker dollar = BTC historically surges. Each 25bp cut has averaged 5-8% BTC moves in previous cycles.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM (dependent on Iran resolution and oil)
- **Market Expectation:** Market has largely given up on 2026 rate cuts. Very low expectations.
- **Our Edge:** If Iran war ends quickly (as Trump suggests), the inflation spike reverses fast, reopening the rate cut window. Market is offsides on this.

## Catalyst 6: Post-Halving Cycle Dynamics ⚡
- **What:** Bitcoin halved in April 2024. Historically, BTC peaks 12-18 months post-halving. The Oct 2025 ATH of $126K aligned with the 18-month mark. Previous cycles (2013, 2017, 2021) saw corrections of 30-50% followed by secondary highs or new ATHs.
- **When:** Already in correction phase. Secondary rally potential H2 2026.
- **Impact:** Historical pattern suggests retest of ATH ($126K) or secondary high ($100K-$140K) is plausible.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM (cycles are not guaranteed to repeat, but rhyme)
- **Market Expectation:** Many believe the cycle top is in. Extreme fear suggests capitulation.
- **Our Edge:** At 8 on the Fear & Greed Index, sentiment is at levels historically associated with major bottoms, not continued declines.

## Catalyst 7: Strategic Bitcoin Reserve Expansion 🇺🇸
- **What:** Trump signed the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve executive order in March 2025. The reserve exists but the question is: will the US ACTIVELY BUY more beyond seized assets? Congressional pressure to expand purchases.
- **When:** Q2-Q4 2026 (any expansion announcement is a catalyst)
- **Impact:** +20-40% ($83K-$97K). A single announcement of US government active BTC purchases would be the most bullish event in Bitcoin history.
- **Probability:** LOW-MEDIUM (executive order exists, but active purchasing requires Congressional approval)
- **Market Expectation:** Market has largely forgotten about the strategic reserve after initial hype. Priced out.
- **Our Edge:** The infrastructure is in place. Any budget surplus or defense savings from Iran could be redirected. Under-monitored risk.
