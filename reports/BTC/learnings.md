Bitcoin's fundamentals have never been stronger — 194 public companies, 738K BTC held by one company alone, sovereign buyers, US Strategic Reserve, bipartisan legislation. Yet the Fear & Greed Index is at 8. This is a textbook dislocation: fundamentals improving while sentiment collapses due to macro fear (Iran war, oil, delayed rate cuts).

### 2. The Treasury Company Playbook is Going Parabolic
Strategy's STRC preferred stock innovation is creating a BTC accumulation machine that converts yield-seeking institutional capital into permanent Bitcoin demand. This model — issue preferred equity, buy BTC, repeat — is being copied by dozens of companies. 194 public companies now hold BTC. This number was ~80 in early 2024. The structural demand this creates is unprecedented.

### 3. Geopolitics is the New Crypto Risk Factor
Bitcoin used to move on exchange hacks and regulation. Now it moves on oil prices, Iran wars, and Fed policy. This is a sign of maturation — BTC is now a macro asset, not a niche speculation. The correlation with traditional markets (~0.6-0.7 with Nasdaq) makes it harder to use as a diversifier but easier for institutional adoption.

### 4. The Stablecoin Infrastructure Boom Benefits BTC
USDC, PayPal USD, and stablecoin legislation are building the crypto on-ramps at scale. Every insurance company using stablecoins, every bank offering crypto custody, every tokenized stock platform — these all expand the user base that eventually discovers BTC. The stablecoin → BTC pipeline is real and underappreciated.
