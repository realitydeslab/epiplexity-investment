## Growth Acceleration Analysis

### AI-Powered Business — ACCELERATING ✅
- FY2025: RMB 40.0B (+48% YoY), up from RMB 27.0B in FY2024
- Now 43% of Baidu General Business revenue (Q4), up from 26% in FY2024
- **This is the story: AI is eating Baidu from the inside, replacing legacy search**

### AI Cloud Infra — ACCELERATING ✅
- FY2025: RMB 19.8B (+34% YoY)
- Q3→Q4: RMB 4.2B → 5.8B (+38% sequential!)
- Computing power subscription: +143% YoY in Q4, triple-digit for full year
- **Strongest growth vector. Enterprise demand for domestic AI compute exploding**

### AI Applications — FLAT ⚠️
- FY2025: RMB 10.2B (+5% YoY) — modest
- Need to see monetization of 200M ERNIE MAU users

### AI-native Marketing Services — EXPLODING ✅
- FY2025: RMB 9.8B (+301% YoY!) — from RMB 2.4B in FY2024
- New AI-powered ad formats replacing traditional search ads

### Apollo Go — ACCELERATING ✅
- Q4 2025: 3.4M fully driverless rides (+200% YoY)
- FY2025: 10M+ fully driverless rides
- Cumulative: 20M rides by Feb 2026
- Weekly peak: 300K rides
- 300M km autonomous driving, 190M km fully driverless
- International expansion: 5+ countries in 2026

### Legacy Search — DECLINING ⬇️
- Traditional search/marketing revenue under pressure from short video (Douyin), WeChat search
- This is the drag on overall revenue flatness despite AI segments growing 48%

## Guidance vs. Actual
- Q4 2025: Beat EPS by $0.16 ($1.55 vs $1.39 est), beat revenue by $59M
- Pattern: Baidu has been consistently beating estimates in recent quarters
- Management guiding for continued AI Cloud acceleration and Apollo Go expansion

## Unit Economics
- Non-GAAP operating income Q4: RMB 3.0B (9% margin)
- FY2025 Non-GAAP OI: RMB 15.0B (12% margin)
- Non-GAAP OI grew 28% QoQ in Q4 — **profitability accelerating**
- Operating cash flow turned positive H2 2025: RMB 3.9B (vs negative H1)
- Digital humans costs fell to 1/3 of prior quarter — improving unit economics

## Path to Re-Rating
Baidu's problem isn't growth — AI segments are growing 48%. The problem is legacy search is shrinking, masking the underlying AI transition. As AI crosses 50% of revenue (likely mid-2026), the narrative shifts from "dying search engine" to "China AI platform." That's when multiple expansion happens.

**Verdict: Growth is ACCELERATING in all the right places (AI Cloud +34%, Apollo Go +200%, AI Marketing +301%). Legacy drag is shrinking as a % of total. The crossover point is near.**

*Sources: Baidu Q4 2025 earnings release (Feb 26, 2026), IR presentation*
