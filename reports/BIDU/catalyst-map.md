# BIDU Catalyst Map — Baidu Inc.

*Research date: March 8, 2026 | Price: ~$119*

---

## 1. Kunlunxin AI Chip IPO (HK) 🔥
- **What:** Spin-off of Baidu's AI chip subsidiary Kunlunxin onto Hong Kong Stock Exchange Main Board
- **When:** Filed Jan 1, 2026. IPO expected H2 2026 or early 2027
- **Impact:** +30-50%. Analysts (Jefferies, CICC, Macquarie) estimate Kunlunxin post-IPO value at $16-23B. Baidu owns ~59%, implying $9.4-13.6B value for Baidu's stake alone — vs. Baidu's entire market cap of ~$42B
- **Probability:** High — filing submitted, China Mobile invested, regulatory momentum positive
- **Market expectation:** Market partially pricing in — stock jumped 15% on filing day to $150, but has since pulled back to $119, suggesting skepticism on timeline/valuation
- **Our edge:** The private round valued Kunlunxin at only $3B; institutional estimates of $16-23B post-IPO suggest massive re-rating. Kunlunxin ranked #3 in China AI accelerator chips (IDC 2024), revenue doubling to ~$1.4B in 2026 (Macquarie). Over 50% of revenue now from external customers

## 2. $5B Share Buyback Program
- **What:** New $5B repurchase authorization (announced Feb 2026), effective through Dec 2028
- **When:** Ongoing through 2028, likely front-loaded
- **Impact:** +10-15%. $5B is ~12% of market cap
- **Probability:** High — board authorized, cash position of RMB 294.1B (~$40B) supports it
- **Market expectation:** Modestly priced in; execution pace matters
- **Our edge:** Combined with first-ever dividend policy, signals management confidence in undervaluation

## 3. Apollo Go International Expansion + Uber/Lyft Partnerships
- **What:** Apollo Go robotaxi expanding globally — South Korea (Feb 2026), Uber/Lyft for London/Germany (2026), Dubai (Feb 2026), Swiss public transport
- **When:** Rolling through 2026
- **Impact:** +15-25% if international traction proves. 300K weekly peak rides, 3.4M fully driverless rides Q4 (+200% YoY). Approaching Waymo scale (20M cumulative rides each)
- **Probability:** Medium-High — partnerships signed, permits being secured
- **Market expectation:** Market gives near-zero value to Apollo Go
- **Our edge:** Apollo Go is free optionality. If a single major international market achieves scale, forces re-rating

## 4. AI Cloud Infra Acceleration
- **What:** AI Cloud Infra revenue RMB 19.8B FY2025 (+34% YoY), computing power subscription +143% YoY in Q4. Total cloud+AI = RMB 30B
- **When:** Q1-Q4 2026 earnings (next: ~May 2026)
- **Impact:** +10-20% on continued acceleration
- **Probability:** High — enterprise AI adoption in China accelerating
- **Market expectation:** Consensus expects moderate growth; triple-digit subscription growth not fully modeled
- **Our edge:** China's AI compute demand exploding due to export controls. Baidu has only full-stack AI platform (chips + framework + models + cloud) in China

## 5. ERNIE Model + Consumer AI Scale
- **What:** ERNIE Assistant 200M MAU (Dec 2025); Baidu app ~700M MAU; AI search API +110% QoQ; 1M+ apps on Miaoda/MeDo
- **When:** Ongoing quarterly
- **Impact:** +5-10%. Monetization pathway developing
- **Probability:** High
- **Market expectation:** Market wants revenue proof, not user numbers
- **Our edge:** 200M MAU puts ERNIE among top 3 AI assistants globally

## 6. First-Ever Dividend (by Dec 2026)
- **What:** First-ever dividend policy announced, payment by year-end 2026
- **When:** By Dec 2026
- **Impact:** +5-10%. Attracts income/value investors
- **Probability:** High — board committed
- **Our edge:** At ~8x earnings, adding a dividend makes it attractive to new investor classes

## 7. Digital Humans Business (+110% YoY)
- **What:** Revenue +110% YoY, virtual hosts +200%, production costs fell to 1/3
- **When:** Quarterly through 2026
- **Impact:** +3-5%. Niche but high-margin
- **Probability:** Medium-High
