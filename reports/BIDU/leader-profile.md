# BIDU Leader Profile — Robin Li (Yanhong Li)

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

---

## CEO: Robin Li (李彦宏)
- **Role:** Co-Founder, Chairman & CEO since 2000
- **Background:** Invented RankDex (early hyperlink analysis patent predating Google PageRank). SUNY Buffalo MS, Peking University BS
- **Ownership:** 521.4M shares, 60.2% voting power (13G/A filing, Aug 2025). Supervoting structure gives Robin Li ironclad control
- **Tenure:** 26 years as CEO. One of the longest-serving tech founder-CEOs globally

## Leadership Assessment
- **Right person for AI pivot?** YES. Robin Li made the bold early bet on AI starting 2017, invested billions in autonomous driving (Apollo), AI cloud, and ERNIE models before ChatGPT made AI mainstream. He personally drove the Kunlunxin chip development
- **Criticism:** Search core business has been declining as China's internet shifts to apps/short video. Some argue he was too slow to diversify from search. Stock is ~60% below 2021 highs
- **Counter:** AI Cloud growing 34% YoY, Apollo Go at Waymo scale, Kunlunxin spinoff unlocks hidden value. The AI transition is actually working

## CFO: Haijian He
- Driving shareholder returns: $5B buyback + first dividend. Operating cash flow turned positive H2 2025 (RMB 3.9B combined)
- Kunlunxin spinoff architect

## Key Executives
- **Dou Shen:** EVP & President, Baidu AI Cloud Group — driving enterprise AI growth
- **Rong Luo:** EVP, Baidu Mobile Ecosystem Group — managing search-to-AI transition

## Insider Activity
- **Robin Li:** No recent open-market purchases or sales reported. 60.2% voting control means he IS the company. His ~59% stake in Kunlunxin through Baidu is his biggest bet
- **Institutional moves:** Barclays raised PT from $100→$147 (Jan 2026). Jefferies hiked to $181 on Kunlunxin filing. Tiger Securities noted stock "increasingly driven by long-term AI potential"
- **Strong Buy consensus:** 27 buy / 4 hold / 1 sell among analysts

## Key Hires/Departures
- No major departures reported in last 6 months — leadership team stable
- Kunlunxin team being built out for independent operation post-IPO

## Public Statements on Near-Term Outlook
Robin Li (Q4 2025 call): "2025 marked a pivotal year as AI became the new core of Baidu. As our AI-first strategy takes clear shape, we're confident in our ability to create lasting value in the AI era."

CFO He: Highlighted four key achievements — AI-powered business at 43% of revenue, sequential growth acceleration, cash flow turnaround, and Kunlunxin/shareholder returns
