# BIDU Structured Intelligence — Learnings

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

---

## What This Tells Us About the Sector

1. **China AI is real and accelerating despite sanctions.** Export controls haven't killed China AI — they've redirected it. Baidu's AI Cloud grew 34%, computing power subscriptions +143%. Domestic chip development (Kunlunxin) is becoming commercially viable. The market underestimates China's AI capability because of geopolitical bias.

2. **Robotaxi is going international.** Both Waymo and Apollo Go hit 20M rides. The next frontier is international — Apollo Go's Uber/Lyft partnerships suggest robotaxi tech is becoming a licensable platform, not just a local service. This model (tech licensing + partnerships) is more scalable than fleet ownership.

3. **Spinoffs unlock value in conglomerates.** Baidu's entire market cap ($42B) is roughly equal to its cash ($40B), meaning the market values all its businesses at near-zero. The Kunlunxin spinoff forces the market to price the AI chip business explicitly. More China tech spinoffs will follow (Alibaba already did this with Cainiao, Cloud).

## Contrarian Angle — What's the Market Missing?

The market treats Baidu as a "dying search engine with AI hopes." The reality:
- AI is already 43% of revenue and growing 48% YoY
- Cash = market cap (you're getting all AI businesses for free)
- Kunlunxin alone could be worth 30-50% of current Baidu market cap
- Apollo Go is at Waymo scale but valued at zero
- $5B buyback + first dividend = management agreeing the stock is cheap

**The market is missing the crossover:** When AI passes 50% of revenue (likely mid-2026), Baidu reclassifies from "search company" to "AI company." That's a 2-3x multiple expansion opportunity.
