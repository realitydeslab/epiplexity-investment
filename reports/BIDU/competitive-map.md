# BIDU Competitive Map — Baidu Inc.

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

---

## Direct Competitors

### AI Cloud / Models
| Company | AI Cloud Strategy | Threat Level |
|---------|------------------|-------------|
| **Alibaba (BABA/9988.HK)** | Qwen models, Alibaba Cloud — largest China cloud provider | HIGH. Bigger cloud market share, but Baidu has deeper AI stack |
| **Tencent (0700.HK)** | Hunyuan AI, WeChat integration | MEDIUM. Different moat (social), less pure AI cloud focus |
| **ByteDance (private)** | Doubao models, massive compute capacity | HIGH. Not investable but biggest competitive threat |
| **Huawei** | Ascend chips + MindSpore + cloud | HIGH. Government-backed, competing on AI infra + chips |

### Autonomous Driving
| Company | AV Strategy | Comparison |
|---------|------------|------------|
| **Waymo (GOOGL)** | US robotaxi leader, 20M+ rides | Peer. Similar scale, different geographies |
| **Tesla (TSLA)** | FSD, planned robotaxi | Behind Baidu in fully driverless deployment |
| **Pony.ai (PONY)** | China + Toyota partnership | Smaller scale, but listed competitor |
| **WeRide (WRD)** | Multi-vehicle L4 autonomy | Smaller, but expanding internationally |

### AI Chips
| Company | Position | Comparison |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| **Cambricon (688256.SH)** | China AI chip #2 | Kunlunxin has larger deployments |
| **Moore Threads** | China GPU startup | Less mature than Kunlunxin |

## Who Benefits From Same Catalysts
- **China AI compute demand surge:** Alibaba Cloud, Biren, SMIC (foundry), SenseTime
- **Robotaxi expansion:** Sensor makers (Hesai, Sunny Optical), EV platforms (Geely — Apollo Go vehicle partner)
- **Kunlunxin IPO success:** Other China chip IPO candidates gain confidence

## Who Loses If Baidu Wins
- **Huawei Ascend** loses cloud AI share to Kunlunxin/Baidu Cloud
- **Traditional ad platforms** (Tencent Ads, ByteDance) if AI-native marketing takes share
- **Traditional ride-hailing (Didi)** as Apollo Go robotaxi scales and cuts costs

## Pair Trade Opportunities
1. **Long BIDU / Short BABA:** If Kunlunxin IPO is the catalyst — Baidu has more binary upside from the spinoff; Alibaba lacks equivalent value-unlock event
2. **Long BIDU / Short PONY:** Both in China AV, but Baidu has vastly larger scale + diversified AI business
3. **Long BIDU + Long SMIC (0981.HK):** China AI compute beneficiary basket — Kunlunxin chips fabbed domestically

## Unique Moat
Baidu is the ONLY company in China with a complete AI stack:
- **Chips:** Kunlunxin (custom AI accelerators)
- **Framework:** PaddlePaddle (China's largest deep learning framework)
- **Models:** ERNIE (200M MAU)
- **Cloud:** AI Cloud Infra (RMB 20B revenue)
- **Application:** Apollo Go (robotaxi), Digital Humans, AI Marketing
- **Search/Distribution:** 700M MAU Baidu app

No other China tech company has this vertical integration. Alibaba is strong on cloud but lacks chips and AV. Tencent is strong on apps but lacks cloud scale. Huawei competes on chips+cloud but lacks consumer apps.

*Sources: IDC, Baidu IR, industry analysis*
