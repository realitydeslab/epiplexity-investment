# AXON — Leader Profile 👤

## Rick Smith — Founder & CEO
- **Tenure:** Co-founded 1993 (as AIR TASER). CEO for 30+ years.
- **Background:** Harvard MBA. Pivoted from TASER-only to full public safety tech platform.
- **Vision:** "Make the bullet obsolete" → now "make the world safer through technology"
- **Execution:** Exceptional. Renamed company, built Evidence.com, launched body cameras as loss-leader → SaaS, acquired Fusus/Dedrone/Sky-Hero, launched Draft One AI.
- **Insider ownership:** 5.22% (~$2.4B)
- **Recent insider transactions:** Net selling -2.45% (typical founder diversification, not alarming)

- Key driver of international expansion
