# AXON — Catalyst Map 🎯

**Axon Enterprise (NASDAQ: AXON) | Price: $574.01 (Mar 6, 2026)**

---

## Catalyst 1: AI Bookings Acceleration — $750M+ and Growing
- **What:** Axon's AI suite (Draft One, Axon Assistant, Axon Records AI) hit $750M in cumulative AI bookings as of Morgan Stanley conference (Mar 5, 2026). Draft One alone adopted by hundreds of agencies.
- **When:** Ongoing through 2026; next earnings (Q1 2026) in May
- **Impact:** +15-25%. AI revenue is high-margin SaaS, every incremental AI seat increases LTV/CAC
- **Probability:** High — already confirmed $750M bookings
- **Market expectation:** Analysts model ~31% revenue growth for FY2026 ($3.65B consensus)
- **Our edge:** AI adoption in law enforcement is early innings. Draft One solves a visceral pain (40% of officer time on reports). Once adopted, switching costs are extreme — evidence chain-of-custody makes it nearly impossible to leave Axon's ecosystem.

## Catalyst 2: Q1-Q4 2026 Earnings Beats
- **What:** Axon consistently beats estimates. Q4 2025: revenue $797M (+38.5% YoY), smashing $756M consensus. EPS beat by 34.5%. Stock surged 16% after hours.
- **When:** Q1 ~May 2026, Q2 ~Aug, Q3 ~Nov
- **Impact:** +10-15% per beat; cumulative if consistent
- **Probability:** High — guidance implies $3.65B+ revenue (31%+ growth)
- **Market expectation:** Consensus EPS $7.93 for FY2026
- **Our edge:** Forward P/E of 54.5x on $10.53 next-year EPS is reasonable for 30%+ growth compounder

## Catalyst 3: TASER 10 Cycle + International Expansion
- **What:** TASER 10, the most significant hardware upgrade in a decade. 10-probe design with 45-foot range. Connected devices grew significantly in Q4 2025.
- **When:** Full-year 2026 as replacement cycle continues
- **Impact:** +10%. Hardware is the gateway drug to the SaaS ecosystem
- **Probability:** High — replacement cycle is multi-year
- **Our edge:** International markets (only ~25% of revenue) are massively underpenetrated

## Catalyst 4: Drone & Counter-Drone Programs
- **What:** Axon acquired Sky-Hero (indoor drones) and Dedrone (counter-drone). Drone-as-first-responder (DFR) programs expanding.
- **When:** Throughout 2026; FAA BVLOS waivers expanding
- **Impact:** +10-15% if drone programs scale
- **Probability:** Medium-High
- **Our edge:** Integrated platform — drones feed into Evidence.com, creating network effects no competitor can match

## Catalyst 5: Federal & Military Contract Expansion
- **What:** Growing federal business. Body cameras for federal agencies, potential DoD applications.
- **When:** Federal budget cycle, Q2-Q3 2026
- **Impact:** +5-10%
- **Probability:** Medium — political environment favorable (law & order emphasis)
- **Our edge:** Bipartisan support; efficiency mandates benefit Axon (AI = fewer admin hours)
