# AXON — Competitive Map 🗺️

## Direct Competitors
- **Motorola Solutions (MSI):** Primary rival. Body cameras, command center software. Larger ($73B) but AI products behind Axon. No Draft One equivalent.
- **Genetec:** Video/evidence management. Private. Mainly physical security.
- **Mark43:** Cloud RMS. VC-backed, much smaller. Axon Records + Draft One pulling ahead.

## Same-Catalyst Beneficiaries
- Motorola Solutions (MSI) — public safety spending
- Palantir (PLTR) — government AI adoption
- CrowdStrike (CRWD) — law enforcement cybersecurity
- Kratos (KTOS) — drone/counter-drone overlap

## Who Loses If Axon Wins?
- Mark43 and standalone RMS vendors
- Legacy paper-based processes (biggest "competitor")
- Small body camera vendors

## Axon's Moat: WIDE
1. Ecosystem lock-in (hardware + cloud + AI + drones)
2. Data moat (millions of hours of police video)
3. Trust moat (evidence chain-of-custody)
4. Regulatory moat (compliance/certifications)
5. Network effects (more agencies → better AI → more agencies)

Sources: Finviz, company reports
