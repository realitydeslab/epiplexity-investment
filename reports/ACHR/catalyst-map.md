# ACHR — Catalyst Map 🎯

**Archer Aviation Inc.** | NYSE: ACHR | $6.26 (Mar 6, 2026)
**Time Horizon:** 12 months (through March 2027)

---

## Catalyst 1: FAA Type Certification of Midnight eVTOL

- **What:** FAA issuing Type Certificate for the Midnight aircraft — the single most important milestone in Archer's history. Enables commercial passenger flights.
- **When:** Expected mid-to-late 2026 (company guided "on track" for 2026, originally targeting H2 2025 but pushed)
- **Impact:** +50-100%. Certification de-risks the entire business and converts ~$6B order book into executable contracts.
- **Probability:** Medium. FAA has been methodical; Archer completed piloted transition flights. But FAA timelines historically slip.
- **Market expectation:** Most analysts assume certification in 2026 but price in delay risk.
- **Our edge:** If Archer achieves certification ahead of consensus (Q2 vs Q3/Q4), stock reprices sharply before competitors.

## Catalyst 2: First Commercial Revenue Flights

- **What:** Launch of commercial air taxi operations in select corridors (NYC, LA, or UAE).
- **When:** H2 2026 (post-certification), meaningful ramp Q4 2026 / Q1 2027.
- **Impact:** +30-50%. Validates unit economics.
- **Probability:** Medium-Low. Depends on Catalyst 1 + infrastructure readiness.
- **Market expectation:** Consensus projects $37.7M revenue in 2026.

## Catalyst 3: United Airlines & Stellantis Partnership Conversions

- **What:** United Airlines invested and pre-ordered. Stellantis partnership for high-volume manufacturing. LOI to firm order conversions.
- **When:** Throughout 2026 as certification approaches
- **Impact:** +20-30% per major conversion
- **Probability:** High.

## Catalyst 4: Defense / Government Contracts

- **What:** DoD contracts for military eVTOL (medevac, logistics, ISR). AFWERX programs.
- **When:** Rolling through 2026
- **Impact:** +10-20%. Diversifies away from commercial certification risk.
- **Probability:** Medium.

## Catalyst 5: International Expansion — UAE & India

- **What:** Agreements for UAE and India operations. UAE may launch before US.
- **When:** Q2-Q4 2026
- **Impact:** +15-25%.
- **Probability:** Medium-High (UAE), Medium (India).

## Catalyst 6: Earnings / Cash Burn

- **What:** Quarterly cash position vs. burn rate. $1.03B net cash, ~$130M/quarter burn = runway into early 2028.
- **When:** Q1 (~May), Q2 (~Aug), Q3 (~Nov) 2026
- **Impact:** Negative if burn accelerates. Positive if declining.
