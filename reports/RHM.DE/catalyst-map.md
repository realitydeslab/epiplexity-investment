# Rheinmetall (RHM.DE) — Catalyst Map

*Research Date: 2026-03-07*

## 1. Q4 2025 Full-Year Results — March 11, 2026 🔥

- **What:** Full-year 2025 defense results release (automotive now discontinued). First official numbers after pre-close guidance.
- **When:** March 11, 2026 (4 days away)
- **Impact:** ±10-15%. If margins come in above 19% or revenue beats the 30-35% growth guide, stock reprices higher. If "cautious" guidance repeats, another 5-8% selloff possible.
- **Probability:** High (event is certain; beat probability moderate)
- **Market expectation:** Defense revenue ~€10.5-11B (30-35% growth on ~€8B 2024 base), margin 18.5-19%.
- **Our edge:** CEO Papperger bought shares on Feb 5 dip to ~€1,520. Insiders buying before earnings = confidence signal.

## 2. German €67B Defense Order Wave — Q1-Q4 2026

- **What:** Major German defense procurement orders totaling €67B expected over next 4 quarters. Includes Arminius Boxer contract (€12.5B fixed to 2030, €25B option to 2035).
- **When:** Rolling through 2026, announcements expected quarterly
- **Impact:** Each major contract announcement = +3-5% pop. Full pipeline realization would fundamentally reprice the company.
- **Probability:** High — orders are politically committed, budgets approved
- **Market expectation:** Market knows the pipeline but treats execution as uncertain
- **Our edge:** Germany approved a 45% defense budget increase for 2026 ($129B). This isn't aspiration — it's budgeted.

## 3. Germany €500B Infrastructure Fund (SVIK) Acceleration — H2 2026

- **What:** €500B over 12 years for infrastructure + defense. 2026 expected to see "clear acceleration" of disbursements after slow 2025.
- **When:** H2 2026 ramp
- **Impact:** +5-10% as spending flows into Rheinmetall-adjacent defense/dual-use programs
- **Probability:** High — constitutional amendment already passed

## 4. NATO 3-3.5% GDP Target Adoption — Throughout 2026

- **What:** European NATO members moving toward 3-3.5% GDP defense spending (up from 2% target). If achieved = €500B+ annual combined spending. Rheinmetall management expects 20-25% market share of European military spend.
- **When:** Policy announcements throughout 2026, budget commitments by year-end
- **Impact:** +15-25% if concrete multi-year commitments materialize
- **Probability:** Medium-High

## 5. €80B International Order Pipeline Realization — 2026

- **When:** Contract announcements rolling through 2026

- **Probability:** Medium
