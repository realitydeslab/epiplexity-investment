# HOOD — Leader Profile 👤

## CEO: Vladimir Tenev

- **Role:** Co-founder & CEO since 2020 (co-CEO 2013-2020)
- **Age:** ~37 (born 1987, Bulgaria → moved to US age 5)
- **Background:** Stanford math/physics, UCLA PhD dropout → built HFT platforms for financial institutions in NYC
- **Equity:** 5.2% economic interest, **24% voting power** (dual-class structure)
- **Co-founder:** Baiju Bhatt — 7.8% equity, **40% voting power**, now Chief Creative Officer

### Leadership Assessment
- **Visionary:** Tenev has consistently expanded Robinhood's scope from commission-free trading → crypto → options → prediction markets → banking → credit cards → wealth management → international. The "financial super app" vision is being executed methodically.
- **Product velocity:** In 2024-2025 alone: launched UK brokerage, EU crypto, wealth management, Platinum credit card, prediction markets, futures, short selling, Ventures Fund. This is extraordinary product cadence for a 2,900-person company.
- **Acquisition discipline:** Strategic M&A (Bitstamp $200M, TradePMR $300M, WonderFi $180M, X1 $95M, Pluto Capital) — all targeted at expanding capabilities, not revenue buying.
- **Political savvy:** Publicly supported Trump's universal baby accounts proposal (March 2026), positioning Robinhood as politically neutral/accessible.
- **Risk:** Tenev navigated the 2021 GameStop crisis and regulatory investigations. History of controversial decisions but has matured as a leader.

### Insider Activity
- **Net insider transactions:** -5.64% (recent quarter) — **net selling**
- **Insider ownership:** 12.97-15.3% total
- **Founders' voting control:** 64% combined (Tenev 24% + Bhatt 40%) — founders control the company regardless of share sales
- **Assessment:** Insider selling is concerning but typical for founders of high-growth companies taking partial profits. The dual-class voting structure means founders are fully committed to long-term direction. No insider buying signals detected.

### Key Hires & Departures (Last 6 Months)
- TradePMR team integration (wealth management expertise)
- Bitstamp team integration (institutional crypto, European operations)
- No major executive departures reported
- Company maintained 2,900 headcount — disciplined hiring despite revenue growth

### Public Statements on Near-Term Outlook
- **Citizens JMP Conference (March 2, 2026):** Targeted 20%+ annualized growth in net deposits. Emphasized four pillars: trading, investing, spending, saving. Called prediction markets and private markets "promising opportunities."
- **Q4 2025 Earnings Call (Feb 10, 2026):** Highlighted $324B AUC (+70% YoY platform assets), 80%+ non-crypto revenue diversification, 3B monthly event contracts.
- **Product vision:** "Financial super app" — consolidating all financial services into one platform. Robinhood wants to be the Charles Schwab for millennials + Gen Z.

### Founder Grade: **A-**
Tenev is executing a bold, coherent vision with remarkable product velocity. The main concern is insider selling, but the dual-class structure ensures founder alignment. He's the right CEO for this stage of Robinhood's evolution — the question is whether he can maintain execution as the company scales into wealth management and international markets.
