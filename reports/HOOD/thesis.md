# HOOD — Investment Thesis 📋

## One-Sentence Thesis
Robinhood is evolving from a meme-stock trading app into the first true financial super app, with prediction markets, international expansion, and banking creating multiple new revenue vectors — available at a 48% discount from all-time highs.

---

## The Catalyst
**Prediction markets are the next asset class.** Robinhood processes 3 billion event contracts per month. This revenue line barely existed 18 months ago and is already 11% of quarterly revenue (~$140M/quarter). If prediction markets achieve even a fraction of options-market adoption, this becomes a multi-billion dollar annual business. No other mainstream platform has this exposure.

**The "financial super app" is being built.** In the past 18 months: Bitstamp acquisition ($200M), TradePMR ($300M for wealth management), WonderFi ($180M for Canadian crypto), $695 Platinum credit card, Robinhood Ventures Fund, EU expansion. Product velocity is extraordinary for a 2,900-person company.

## The Risk
1. **Rate cuts crush NII** — 32% of revenue is net interest income. Rate cuts pressure this directly.
2. **PFOF regulatory ban** — payment for order flow under regulatory scrutiny. A ban would destroy the business model.
3. **Prediction market regulation** — CFTC/SEC could restrict or ban event contracts.
4. **Crypto winter** — 17% of revenue is crypto. A sustained downturn hurts.
5. **Beta death** — 2.48 beta means a 20% market decline becomes a 50% HOOD decline.
6. **Insider selling** — net -5.64% insider transactions. Founders are selling shares.

## The Verdict
**BUY on pullback. Current levels are attractive.**

The -48% correction from ATH has created a compelling entry point for a company executing one of the most impressive transformations in fintech history. Revenue is growing 25-30%, operating margins are expanding, new product lines are launching, and the valuation (28x forward PE, 1.4x PEG) is reasonable for the growth rate.

The prediction market business is the underappreciated catalyst. At 3B monthly contracts and growing, this could be worth $5-10B as a standalone business within 3 years.

**Key risk to manage:** The 2.48 beta means this position will be volatile. Size at 3-5% of portfolio, use a stop-loss, and be prepared for -20% drawdowns even in the base case.

**Conviction level: 7/10** — Strong fundamentals and product execution, but high beta and regulatory risk demand disciplined position sizing.
