## Growth Velocity Verdict: **DECELERATING FROM HYPERGROWTH TO STRONG GROWTH**

Revenue growth is normalizing from 50-100% to 25-30% — but this is at a $4.5B revenue base. The margin story is more impressive than the top-line: operating margins expanding from -90% to +51% means every incremental revenue dollar is highly profitable. This is the transition from "growth at all costs" to "profitable compounder."

The question for the next 12 months: Can prediction markets, international expansion, and banking products maintain 25-30% growth, or does it decelerate further to 15-20%?
