# HOOD — Competitive Map 🗺️

## Direct Competitors

### 1. Coinbase (COIN) — $52.76B market cap
- **Overlap:** Crypto trading, crypto wallets, crypto regulation
- **Robinhood advantage:** Broader platform (equities + options + futures + prediction markets + banking). Lower fees. Better mobile UX for retail.
- **Coinbase advantage:** Deeper institutional crypto (Coinbase Prime), more crypto assets listed, Base L2 chain, staking revenue.
- **Verdict:** Robinhood is a superset of Coinbase's retail business. If crypto + traditional finance converge, Robinhood wins.

### 2. Charles Schwab (SCHW) — $165.37B market cap
- **Overlap:** Retail brokerage, wealth management, banking
- **Robinhood advantage:** Modern tech, prediction markets, crypto, younger demographic, mobile-first.
- **Schwab advantage:** Scale ($10T+ client assets), advisor network, brand trust, full banking license, wider product set (mutual funds, bonds).
- **Verdict:** Different customer bases today. Robinhood is winning the next generation. The real question is whether Robinhood can scale into wealth management (via TradePMR) to compete with Schwab's advisor business.

### 3. Interactive Brokers (IBKR) — $30.06B market cap
- **Overlap:** Active trading, options, futures, international
- **Robinhood advantage:** Better UX, prediction markets, crypto integration, broader retail appeal.
- **IBKR advantage:** Professional-grade tools, lower margin rates, wider international access, more asset classes.
- **Verdict:** IBKR targets professional traders, Robinhood targets retail. Minimal direct competition.

### 4. Kalshi / Polymarket — Private
- **Overlap:** Prediction markets / event contracts
- **Robinhood advantage:** 27M funded customers already on platform, regulated CFTC approval, integrated with brokerage (trade stocks AND predictions in same app).
- **Competitor advantage:** Kalshi is pure-play prediction market (more contract variety), Polymarket has crypto-native appeal and international access.
- **Verdict:** Robinhood's distribution advantage is massive. 3B monthly event contracts suggests they're already dominant in regulated US prediction markets.

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### Long HOOD / Short COIN
- **Thesis:** Robinhood is a superset of Coinbase's retail business. If crypto + traditional finance converge, Robinhood wins.
- **Risk:** If crypto goes niche/decentralized, COIN's institutional business survives while HOOD's crypto revenue fades.

### Long HOOD / Short AXP
- **Thesis:** Robinhood's Platinum card targets Amex's core franchise. Generational shift in premium card spending.
- **Risk:** Amex's brand loyalty and card benefits ecosystem are deeply entrenched. This is a very long-term thesis.

### Long HOOD + Long SCHW (Barbell)
- **Thesis:** Own both ends of retail finance — the next generation (HOOD) and the established base (SCHW).
- **Risk:** Rate sensitivity on both names; broad market selloff hits both.

## Competitive Moat Assessment

| Moat Factor | Strength | Notes |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| **Network effects** | Medium-High | 27M customers, social features, community |
| **Switching costs** | Medium | Assets, account history, tax records create friction |
| **Brand** | High | "Robinhood" brand is iconic for retail investing |
| **Data advantage** | High | Prediction market data, trading patterns, 3B monthly contracts |
| **Regulatory** | Medium | CFTC approval for event contracts, MiFID/MICA licenses |
| **Cost advantage** | High | 95% gross margins, 2,900 employees, tech-native |
| **Product breadth** | High | Only platform with stocks + options + crypto + futures + predictions + banking + credit cards |

**Verdict:** Robinhood's moat is the breadth of the platform combined with retail brand dominance. No single competitor offers the full stack. The risk is that specialized competitors (COIN for crypto, Schwab for wealth management, Kalshi for predictions) are better at individual verticals.
