# VERA — Catalyst Map 🎯

## Stock: Vera Therapeutics (VERA) | Price: $39.07 (Mar 6, 2026)

---

### Catalyst 1: FDA PDUFA Decision — Atacicept for IgA Nephropathy
- **What:** FDA approval decision for atacicept (subcutaneous TACI-Fc fusion protein) for treatment of IgA nephropathy (IgAN), a leading cause of kidney failure
- **When:** July 7, 2026 (PDUFA date)
- **Impact:** +100-150% on approval; -50-60% on rejection (binary event)
- **Probability:** HIGH (70-75%) — Breakthrough Therapy designation, strong Phase 3 data, dual BAFF/APRIL mechanism is differentiated
- **Market expectation:** Consensus expects approval. 9/10 analysts rate Strong Buy/Buy. Average PT $80.56 implies market is pricing ~50% probability of approval at current levels
- **Our edge:** The stock has pulled back from ~$49 in Jan to ~$39, creating better risk/reward. The market may be underpricing approval probability given Breakthrough Therapy status and the unmet need in IgAN

### Catalyst 2: Phase 3 ORIGIN Trial Topline Data (IgAN)
- **What:** Confirmatory Phase 3 trial results for atacicept in IgAN
- **When:** Data expected H1 2026, prior to PDUFA
- **Impact:** +20-40% on strong data; -30% on weak data
- **Probability:** HIGH — Phase 2 ORIGIN data was robust; Phase 3 is confirmatory
- **Market expectation:** Strong results expected based on Phase 2
- **Our edge:** Positive Phase 3 data de-risks the PDUFA binary event significantly

### Catalyst 3: Expansion Indications — FSGS, MCD, PMN
- **What:** Clinical progress in focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (FSGS), minimal change disease (MCD), and primary membranous nephropathy (PMN)
- **When:** Ongoing through 2026, data readouts possible H2 2026
- **Impact:** +15-25% per positive readout (expanding TAM significantly)
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — earlier stage, but mechanism supports multi-indication potential
- **Market expectation:** Viewed as pipeline optionality, not priced in at current levels
- **Our edge:** If IgAN is approved, these indications leverage same commercial infrastructure

### Catalyst 4: VT-109 (Next-Gen BAFF/APRIL Inhibitor) Phase 2 Progress
- **What:** VT-109, a novel dual-inhibitor BCMA molecule for B-cell autoimmune diseases
- **When:** Phase 2 ongoing, data expected late 2026
- **Impact:** +10-20% on positive early data
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — still early
- **Market expectation:** Largely not in estimates
- **Our edge:** Pipeline depth reduces single-asset risk

### Catalyst 5: Commercial Launch Preparation / Partnership Potential
- **What:** If approved July 2026, commercial launch in H2 2026. Also potential for partnership/acquisition given peak sales estimates of $3-5B for IgAN alone
- **When:** Q3-Q4 2026
- **Impact:** Acquisition premium could be 30-100% over pre-approval price
- **Probability:** MEDIUM for partnership/acquisition (multiple large pharma interested in nephrology)
- **Market expectation:** Some M&A premium priced in
- **Our edge:** With only $2.8B market cap, the company is digestible for any large pharma. IgAN is a $5B+ market

### Catalyst 6: Index Inclusion Potential
- **What:** If stock re-rates post-approval, could qualify for S&P SmallCap 600 or Russell additions
- **When:** Q3-Q4 2026
- **Impact:** +5-10% from passive fund buying
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — contingent on approval and market cap growth
- **Market expectation:** Not priced in
