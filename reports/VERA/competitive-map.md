# VERA — Competitive Map 🗺️

## IgA Nephropathy Treatment Landscape

### The Market
- **IgAN prevalence:** ~130K diagnosed US patients, ~500K globally
- **TAM:** $5-8B globally at peak
- **Current SOC:** Supportive therapy (ACE inhibitors, ARBs), immunosuppression

### Direct Competitors

| Company | Drug | Mechanism | Stage |
|---------|------|-----------|-------|
| **Vera (VERA)** | Atacicept | Dual BAFF/APRIL inhibitor | NDA filed, PDUFA July 2026 |
| **Calliditas (CALT)** | Nefecon | Targeted corticosteroid | **Approved** (2023) |
| **Novartis (Chinook)** | Atrasentan | Endothelin receptor antagonist | Phase 3 |
| **Otsuka** | BION-1301 | Anti-APRIL mAb | Phase 2 |
| **Travere (TVTX)** | Sparsentan | Dual endothelin/angiotensin | **Approved** (2023) |

### VERA's Edge
1. **Dual mechanism** — blocks BOTH BAFF and APRIL (competitors target one)
2. **Subcutaneous self-administration** — convenient home injection
3. **Breakthrough Therapy designation** — strong efficacy signal
4. **Best-in-class proteinuria reduction** in Phase 2

### VERA's Risks
1. Nefecon has first-mover advantage
2. Novartis entering aggressively via Chinook acquisition
3. Multiple drugs could compress pricing
