## Catalyst 1: SkyWater Technology Acquisition Closing ($1.8B)
- **What:** IonQ agreed to acquire SkyWater Technology for $1.8B (Jan 26, 2026), creating the ONLY vertically integrated full-stack quantum platform — from chip fabrication to cloud delivery. SkyWater brings US-based semiconductor manufacturing (Minnesota fab + Indiana advanced packaging), ITAR-compliant production, and DOD trusted foundry status.
- **When:** Expected Q2–Q3 2026 (regulatory/shareholder approval timeline)
- **Impact:** +15-25%. Vertical integration narrative is powerful. Transforms IonQ from asset-light quantum software into a vertically integrated "quantum TSMC." Massive dilution risk — $1.8B all-stock deal adds ~50M+ shares at current price.
- **Probability:** HIGH — both boards approved, limited antitrust concerns (different markets)
- **Market Expectation:** Priced in as mixed — some see empire-building, others see strategic genius
- **Our Edge:** SkyWater's existing DOD relationships + IonQ Federal = government quantum monopoly. The DOD "Department of War Technology" hire (Katie Arrington as CIO, Jan 2026) signals IonQ is playing for defense contracts that require US-made chips.

## Catalyst 2: Oxford Ionics Acquisition Closing (~$1.1B)
- **What:** Acquisition of British quantum startup Oxford Ionics (agreed June 2025). Brings trapped-ion chip fabrication IP using standard CMOS processes — potentially 10-100x cost reduction for quantum processors.
- **When:** Expected H1 2026 completion
- **Impact:** +10-15% on close. The CMOS-compatible trapped-ion approach could be the key to scalable quantum manufacturing.
- **Probability:** HIGH — agreed and in progress
- **Market Expectation:** Largely priced in from June 2025 announcement
- **Our Edge:** Oxford Ionics' CMOS approach + SkyWater's fab = IonQ could mass-produce quantum chips at semiconductor-industry economics by 2028

## Catalyst 3: Remaining Performance Obligations (RPO) Conversion
- **What:** RPO surged to $370M from $77M YoY — a 380% increase. This is contracted future revenue waiting to be recognized. Q1 2026 guidance of $20-23M suggests flat/modest sequential decline from Q4's $61.9M, but full-year consensus is $229M (+76% YoY).
- **When:** Throughout 2026, with quarterly earnings as checkpoints (Q1 ~May, Q2 ~Aug, Q3 ~Nov)
- **Impact:** If organic growth exceeds 80% (management's claimed 2025 organic rate), stock re-rates +20-30%
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — concerns about acquisition-inflated revenue vs. organic
- **Market Expectation:** Consensus $229M for 2026. Bears say guidance is "flat" excluding acquisitions.
- **Our Edge:** The $370M RPO backlog is real contracted revenue. Even if execution is lumpy, it provides a floor.

## Catalyst 4: Government/Defense Quantum Contracts
- **What:** IonQ selected for Missile Defense Agency SHIELD IDIQ contract (Feb 2026). US-Italy quantum deployment for civil protection. IonQ Federal subsidiary with Robert Cardillo (ex-NGA director) as Executive Chair. Katie Arrington (DOW Technology veteran) hired as CIO.
- **When:** Contract awards throughout 2026; potential classified contracts not yet public
- **Impact:** +10-20% per major contract win. Government quantum spending is early but accelerating.
- **Probability:** HIGH — IonQ is positioning as the US government's preferred quantum vendor
- **Market Expectation:** Not significantly priced in. Most analysts focus on commercial cloud revenue.
- **Our Edge:** Defense quantum is a blue ocean. IonQ's ITAR-compliant fab (via SkyWater) + cleared personnel + existing contracts create an insurmountable moat for competitors.

## Catalyst 5: Quantum Networking / QKD Revenue Ramp
- **What:** IonQ delivered one of Europe's largest operational QKD networks (Feb 2026). Acquired ID Quantique (quantum-safe crypto, May 2025), Skyloom (space comms, Jan 2026), Qubitekk (quantum networking, Nov 2024). Quantum networking could be the FIRST killer app for quantum technology.
- **When:** Revenue ramp H2 2026 into 2027
- **Impact:** +15-25%. Creates entirely new revenue stream. Post-quantum cryptography deadline (NIST standards) drives urgency.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH — real product deployments happening now
- **Market Expectation:** Largely overlooked by analysts focused on quantum computing
- **Our Edge:** IonQ is the ONLY company with end-to-end quantum networking capability: quantum memories + photonic interconnects + satellite links + QKD

## Catalyst 6: 100-Qubit System Delivery (South Korea KISTI)
- **What:** Agreement finalized Dec 2025 to deliver 100-qubit quantum system to Korea Institute of Science and Technology Information (KISTI). This would be one of the most powerful deployed quantum systems.
- **When:** Expected delivery 2026
- **Impact:** +5-10%. Validates international commercial traction.
- **Probability:** HIGH — contract finalized
- **Market Expectation:** Known but not a major focus
- **Our Edge:** International government contracts (South Korea, Japan AIST, QuantumBasel, Slovakia, Italy) diversify beyond US dependence

## Catalyst 7: Quantum Computing Market Reclassification
- **What:** As quantum computing moves from "science project" to "enterprise tool," analyst coverage and institutional ownership could increase dramatically. IonQ is the largest pure-play publicly traded quantum computing company.
- **When:** Gradual throughout 2026; could accelerate if Google/IBM announce quantum advantage milestones
- **Impact:** +20-40% if quantum sector gets reclassified from "speculative" to "enterprise tech"
- **Probability:** LOW-MEDIUM — depends on broader quantum ecosystem progress
- **Market Expectation:** Not priced in. Current P/S of ~98x reflects growth but also extreme speculation.
- **Our Edge:** If one quantum company breaks through, IonQ benefits most as the largest pure-play

## Catalyst 8: Capella Space / Space-Based Quantum
- **What:** Acquired Capella Space (SAR satellite imaging) for $311M in IonQ stock (July 2025). Combined with Skyloom (optical inter-satellite links), IonQ is building space-based quantum networking infrastructure.
- **When:** 2026-2027 for initial capability demonstrations
- **Impact:** +5-10% on milestone achievements
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — ambitious but early stage
- **Market Expectation:** Most analysts are confused by this acquisition ("what does satellite imaging have to do with quantum?")
- **Our Edge:** Space-based QKD is the ultimate secure communications play. Governments will pay massive premiums for unhackable satellite links.
