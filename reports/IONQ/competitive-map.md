## IonQ's Competitive Advantages

1. **Only vertically integrated quantum company** (after SkyWater closes): chip design → fabrication → systems → cloud → applications
2. **Trapped-ion architecture benefits:** Higher qubit fidelity, longer coherence times, all-to-all connectivity (no need for physical qubit adjacency)
3. **Revenue leadership among pure-plays:** $130M TTM vs. Rigetti's ~$7.5M and D-Wave's ~$11M
4. **Multi-cloud distribution:** Available on AWS Braket, Azure Quantum, Google Cloud — every hyperscaler
5. **Government moat:** ITAR-compliant manufacturing, DOD trusted foundry (via SkyWater), former intelligence community leaders on team
6. **Quantum networking portfolio:** Only company with end-to-end quantum networking (QKD, satellite, photonic interconnects)

## Architecture Risk: Trapped Ion vs. Superconducting

This is the existential question for IonQ:

**Trapped-ion advantages:** Higher fidelity, longer coherence, full connectivity, room-temperature operation possible
**Trapped-ion disadvantages:** Slower gate speeds, harder to scale past ~100 qubits (traditionally), laser complexity
**Superconducting advantages:** Faster gates, easier to manufacture at scale (CMOS-compatible), more R&D investment globally
**Superconducting disadvantages:** Requires millikelvin cooling, limited connectivity, higher error rates

**Oxford Ionics acquisition is critical:** Their CMOS-compatible trapped-ion approach could solve the scaling problem, making trapped-ion competitive with superconducting on manufacturability while retaining quality advantages.
