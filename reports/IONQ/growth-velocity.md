## Verdict: MASSIVELY ACCELERATING — But Acquisition-Driven

The headline numbers are stunning: 202% annual growth, 428% Q4 YoY. But context matters enormously:
- **Q1 2025 was nearly flat YoY** (-0.2%). This was the last "organic-only" quarter.
- **Acquisitions started contributing from Q2 2025** (ID Quantique closed May 2025)
- **Q3-Q4 include Oxford Ionics, Capella Space, Vector Atomic, Lightsynq**
- Management claims 80% organic growth in 2025 → implies ~$77M organic vs $130M total
- SA analysts skeptical: "guidance for 2026 shows flat excluding acquisitions"

**Critical Flag:** Q1 2026 guidance of $20-23M is a MASSIVE sequential decline from Q4's $61.9M. This suggests:
1. Q4 was lumpy (large contract recognitions)
2. Revenue is highly seasonal/project-based
3. The $370M RPO backlog converts unevenly

- At $229M consensus 2026 revenue, RPO covers ~1.6 years of revenue
- International contracts (South Korea, Japan, Europe) provide geographic diversification

## Unit Economics

| Metric | Q4 2025 | Q4 2024 | Trend |
|--------|---------|---------|-------|
| Gross Margin | 29.6% | 56.9% | ⬇️ DETERIORATING |
| Operating Margin | -369% | -662% | ⬆️ Improving (less negative) |
| FCF Margin | -135% | -367% | ⬆️ Improving |

**Gross margin compression is concerning:** 29.6% in Q4 2025 vs 56.9% in Q4 2024. This likely reflects:
- Lower-margin acquired businesses (ID Quantique, SkyWater-related)
- Hardware delivery costs (international quantum system deployments)
- Mix shift from pure cloud access to bundled hardware+software+services

Annual gross margin: 40.4% (2025) vs 52.2% (2024) vs 63.2% (2023) — steady decline.

## Path to Profitability

- **Current:** Deeply unprofitable. FY 2025 net loss $510M (EPS -$1.82)
- **2026 forecast:** EPS -$1.12 (improving)
- **2027 forecast:** EPS -$0.94
- **Best-case analyst:** EPS +$0.19 in 2026 (highly optimistic outlier)
- **Realistic breakeven:** 2028-2029 at earliest, if acquisition integration goes well
