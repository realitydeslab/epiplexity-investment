# IONQ — Leader Profile 👤

**CEO:** Niccolò de Masi | **Since:** February 26, 2025 | **Also:** Chairman (since Aug 2025)

---

## Background

- **Education:** Physics, University of Cambridge (Cavendish Laboratory) — relevant: a physicist leading a quantum company
- **Prior roles:** CEO of Monstermob Group PLC, CEO of Glu Mobile (mobile gaming, grew significantly), co-founded dMY Technology Group (SPAC firm that took IonQ public in 2021)
- **Board experience:** Served on 12+ public company boards
- **IonQ connection:** On IonQ board since 2021, intimately familiar with the company before becoming CEO

## Assessment: Is He The Right Leader?

**STRENGTHS:**
- **Dealmaker DNA:** Closed 7+ acquisitions in 12 months as CEO — SkyWater ($1.8B), Oxford Ionics ($1.1B), ID Quantique, Capella Space ($311M), Skyloom, Lightsynq, Vector Atomic, Seed Innovations, Qubitekk. This is an aggressive roll-up strategy reminiscent of Broadcom under Hock Tan.
- **Capital markets expertise:** SPAC veteran, knows how to use stock as currency (most deals are all-stock)
- **Physics background:** Can actually evaluate the technology, unlike many tech CEOs
- **Government positioning:** Hired Katie Arrington (DOW Technology veteran) as CIO, installed Robert Cardillo (ex-NGA director) at IonQ Federal. Clearly building a defense moat.
- **Visionary narrative:** Calls quantum networking "the future of the internet" (Fox Business, Sept 2025). Sees IonQ as a platform company, not just a quantum computer maker.

**CONCERNS:**
- **Empire builder risk:** 7+ acquisitions in 12 months is aggressive. Integration risk is enormous. Shares outstanding grew 31.6% YoY (280M → 367M diluted by Q4 2025).
- **No organic growth proof:** Seeking Alpha criticism that Q4's 428% YoY surge was acquisition-driven, not organic. Management claims 80% organic growth in 2025, but guidance shows "flat" growth ex-acquisitions.
- **Personal selling:** De Masi proposed sale of 17,690 shares at $52.69 in Dec 2025 (~$932K), and sold 16,120 shares at $44.40 in Sep 2025 (~$716K). Not massive relative to his ~1.18M share position but selling, not buying.

## Verdict

De Masi is an aggressive, vision-driven dealmaker with relevant physics credentials. The acquisition spree is either brilliant vertical integration or reckless empire-building — won't know for 18-24 months. The heavy insider selling is a yellow flag, though most appears to be pre-planned (10b5-1) rather than panic selling. Teuber's consistent buying is a modest positive signal.

**Grade: B+** — Right strategy, but execution risk is extremely high. Watch integration closely.
