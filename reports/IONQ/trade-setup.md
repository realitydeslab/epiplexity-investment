## Risk Management

### Stop-Loss
- **Technical stop:** $30.00 (-16.4%) — below recent trading range
- **Fundamental stop:** $25.00 (-30.3%) — below Q1 2025 lows adjusted for dilution

### Position Sizing
- **Recommended:** 2-3% of portfolio MAX
- **Rationale:** High-conviction theme (quantum computing) but extreme valuation (98x P/S), deep losses, and acquisition integration risk make this a speculative position

### What Kills the Trade?

1. **Q1 2026 revenue misses the $20-23M guidance** — would confirm organic growth is stalling
2. **SkyWater acquisition falls through** — regulatory block or shareholder vote failure
3. **Gross margins fall below 25%** — would signal acquired businesses are dilutive
4. **Key insider selling accelerates** — de Masi or board selling beyond 10b5-1 plans
5. **Google/IBM quantum superiority announcement** — validates quantum but with different architecture, potentially commoditizing trapped-ion approach
6. **Broader quantum sector selloff** — if Rigetti/D-Wave collapse, sector sentiment drags all names

### Correlation Risk
- Highly correlated with quantum computing peers (RGTI, QBTS, QUBT)
- Moderately correlated with speculative tech/growth (high-multiple unprofitable tech)
- Negatively correlated with interest rates (higher rates → multiple compression)
- Don't pair with other quantum names — they all move together

## Entry Strategy

**Best approach: Scale in**
1. **Starter position** at current $35-36 (1% of portfolio)
2. **Add on Q1 2026 earnings** if organic growth shows promise (May 2026)
3. **Full position** after SkyWater closes and integration begins

**Avoid:** Buying a full position before Q1 earnings. The $20-23M guidance creates a potential negative surprise if it comes in at $20M or below.
