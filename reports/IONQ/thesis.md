**One-page summary** | $35.87 | March 9, 2026

## The Catalyst

IonQ is executing the most aggressive acquisition strategy in quantum computing history — 9+ acquisitions in 14 months — to build the **only vertically integrated quantum platform company**. The $1.8B SkyWater acquisition (closing Q2-Q3 2026) creates a US-based, ITAR-compliant quantum chip foundry. Combined with Oxford Ionics' CMOS-compatible trapped-ion technology, IonQ aims to mass-produce quantum processors at semiconductor-industry economics by 2028.

The near-term catalyst is whether this roll-up can prove organic growth. Q4 2025 revenue of $61.9M (+428% YoY) was stunning, but heavily acquisition-driven. Q1 2026 guidance of $20-23M represents a massive sequential decline. The next 2 quarters will determine if IonQ is building a platform or buying revenue.

1. Organic growth is actually flat → stock revisits $25
2. Gross margin compression accelerates below 25% → acquisitions are dilutive
3. SkyWater acquisition fails → strategic thesis collapses
4. Quantum winter sentiment → sector-wide selloff
5. CEO is empire-building, not value-creating → insiders keep selling

**What protects you:**
- $2.39B cash ($6.52/share) — years of runway
- $370M RPO — contracted future revenue
- 12 analyst coverage, 9 bullish, avg PT $67.67 (+89%)
- Only pure-play quantum computing company at scale

## The Verdict

**SPECULATIVE BUY — Scale In** | Epiplexity Score: **72/100**

IonQ is the most interesting company in quantum computing and possibly the most dangerous investment. The upside is enormous if quantum computing crosses the commercialization threshold in 2026-2028 — IonQ's vertical integration and government positioning would make it the NVIDIA of quantum. The downside is significant if the acquisitions prove to be empire-building rather than value creation.

The 98x P/S ratio means you're paying for success. The 57% decline from ATH means the market has serious doubts. The right approach is a small, scaling position that bets on the thesis while limiting downside.

**Key conviction driver:** The $370M RPO backlog is real contracted revenue. Even if execution is lumpy, IonQ has a visible revenue path that most quantum companies don't.

**Key concern:** Gross margins falling from 63% → 30% in two years. If IonQ is becoming a low-margin hardware company rather than a high-margin platform company, the valuation premium evaporates.
