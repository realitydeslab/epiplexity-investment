## Revenue Model

Venice operates a **hybrid model:**

1. **Free Tier:** $0 — 10 text prompts/day, 15 image prompts/day. Funnel for conversion
2. **Pro Tier:** $18/month ($12.60 annually) — unlimited text, 1000 images/day, advanced models
3. **API Pay-as-you-go:** Per-token pricing competitive with OpenAI/Anthropic (some models cheaper)
4. **VVV Staking (Diem):** Stake VVV for pro-rata share of inference capacity at zero marginal cost

**Revenue estimate (speculative):**
- If 5% of 1M users are Pro subscribers: 50,000 × $18/mo = $900K/month = **$10.8M ARR**
- API revenue likely additive — Venice serves OpenAI-compatible endpoints
- Total estimated revenue: **$12–20M ARR** (conservative estimate)

This is notable for a crypto AI project. Most competitors have near-zero revenue.
