# VVV (Venice Token) — Leader Profile 👤

**Research Date:** 2026-03-09

## Erik Voorhees — Founder & CEO

### Background
- **Born:** 1984, Danbury, Connecticut
- **Education:** University of Puget Sound
- **Residence:** Panama (since 2013)
- **Age:** 41–42

### Career Track Record
- **BitInstant** (2012) — Director of Marketing. Early Bitcoin exchange (alongside Charlie Shrem)
- **SatoshiDice** (2012) — Co-founded Bitcoin gambling site; sold for $11.5M in BTC (2013). At peak, accounted for ~50% of all Bitcoin transaction volume
- **ShapeShift** (2014–2021) — Founded non-custodial crypto exchange in Switzerland. Raised venture funding. Eventually decentralized the protocol and handed governance to DAO. Pioneer in "instant exchange" concept
- **Venice AI** (2024–present) — Founded May 2024. Pivoted his crypto expertise into AI x privacy

### Assessment
Erik Voorhees is a **crypto OG** — one of the earliest Bitcoin advocates, with a 13+ year track record in the space. He's a libertarian ideologue who genuinely believes in privacy and decentralization, not just as marketing angles. This is important because Venice's positioning is authentically aligned with his decades-long mission.

**Strengths:**
- Deep crypto native credibility. His name alone brings attention and trust in the crypto community
- Serial founder who has built, scaled, and exited successfully
- Ideological conviction — he'll stay the course on privacy/uncensored AI even when it's unpopular
- Public speaker, thought leader — regularly featured in major crypto media

**Risks:**
- SEC settlement history ($50K in 2014 for unregistered securities with SatoshiDice). Minor but worth noting
- Libertarian positioning may limit enterprise/institutional adoption
- ShapeShift's DAO transition was messy — some view it as abandoning the project
- No deep AI/ML technical background (compensated by strong team)

### Co-Founder: Teana Baker-Taylor
- Less public profile than Voorhees
- Brings operational and business development expertise
- Important for day-to-day execution while Voorhees handles vision and community

### Insider Activity
- **Token distribution:** 35% of 100M initial supply to Venice.ai company — team is heavily aligned
- **No presale** — unusual and bullish. Team didn't extract value pre-launch
- **Emission:** 8M VVV annually — ~7% dilution declining over time
- Company treasury holdings are locked/vesting (specific schedule not publicly detailed)

### Key Hires / Team
- Venice has scaled to support 1M+ users with apparently lean team
- Integration of 40+ AI models (including Claude, GPT-5, Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, etc.) shows strong engineering
- OpenAI-compatible API indicates solid technical execution

### Public Statements
- Voorhees consistently frames Venice as "the anti-ChatGPT" — private, uncensored, permissionless
- Strong public advocacy for AI agents needing sovereign, censorship-resistant inference
- Active on X (@ErikVoorhees) with substantial crypto following

## Verdict: 7.5/10

Strong founder-market fit. Erik Voorhees is the right person to lead a privacy-first, crypto-native AI platform. His crypto credibility is unmatched. The risk is limited AI technical depth, but Venice's rapid model integration suggests the engineering team is capable. The no-presale tokenomics signal genuine alignment with holders.
