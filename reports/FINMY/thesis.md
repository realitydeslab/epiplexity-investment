# Investment Thesis — Leonardo SpA (FINMY / LDO.MI)

*Updated: March 8, 2026*

---

## The Catalyst

Leonardo is Europe's most diversified defense company, positioned at the intersection of three converging forces:

1. **European rearmament super-cycle** — NATO spending commitments being codified into law across the continent
2. **Strategic transformation** — CEO Cingolani pivoting from hardware to integrated security technology (cyber, space, AI, digital)
3. **Catalyst-dense 12 months** — UK helicopter contract, Saudi Arabia JV/GCAP entry, Italian Lynx IFV orders, potential index inclusion

The FY2025 results (+11% revenue, +18% operating profit, +15% new orders) confirm the inflection point. Morningstar calls it exactly that.

## The Trade

- **Entry:** €58.62 (near all-time highs, 5% below Morningstar FV of €62.20)
- **Bull target:** €80 (+37%) if Saudi JV + GCAP + margin expansion catalysts hit
- **Base target:** €68 (+16%) on continued execution and partial catalyst realization
- **Bear target:** €48 (-18%) on geopolitical détente + catalyst misses
- **Stop-loss:** €48
- **Position size:** 3-5% of portfolio

**$10K invested → 12-month range: $8,200 (bear) to $13,670 (bull)**

## The Risk

- **Valuation:** P/E of 32x isn't cheap. Priced for double-digit growth continuation. Any deceleration = multiple compression.
- **Near ATH:** Buying strength, not weakness. Requires conviction in catalysts.
- **Geopolitical:** A genuine peace deal could deflate the defense premium (though structural spending is already legislated).
- **Execution:** Transformation from hardware to tech is a multi-year journey. Cingolani has ~3 years of track record.
- **Currency:** ADR holders face EUR/USD risk.

## The Verdict

**BUY — Accumulate on pullbacks.**

Leonardo is the best-positioned European defense company for the next 12 months. It has:
- ✅ Accelerating growth with improving margins
- ✅ 5+ identifiable catalysts in the next 12 months
- ✅ Government backing providing a floor
- ✅ A CEO who understands technology, not just hardware
- ✅ Dual exposure to European AND US defense spending (via DRS)

The main risk is valuation — you're buying near ATH at 32x P/E. But the catalyst density justifies it. This isn't a "hope and hold" — it's a catalyst-driven re-rating thesis.

**Preferred entry:** Scale in — 50% now, 50% on pullback to €52-54.
**Primary listing:** LDO.MI (Milan) for better liquidity. FINMY ADR for US investors.
