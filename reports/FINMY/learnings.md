# Learnings & Intelligence — Leonardo SpA (FINMY / LDO.MI)

*Updated: March 8, 2026*

## What Does Leonardo Tell Us About the Sector?

1. **European defense is a multi-year structural cycle, not a trade.** NATO spending commitments are being codified into law (Germany's constitutional amendment, UK 2.5% GDP floor). This isn't reversible in 1-2 years.

2. **The market is transitioning from "defense hardware" to "defense technology."** Companies that combine platforms with electronics, cyber, and AI will command higher multiples. Leonardo's pivot under Cingolani is the template.

3. **European defense consolidation is accelerating.** JVs (LRMV, MBDA) and cross-border programs (GCAP) are creating a "European defense champion" tier. Leonardo, Rheinmetall, BAE, and Thales are the Big 4.

4. **Saudi Arabia is the next major defense market.** GCAP Saudi entry + Leonardo aerostructures JV signal a pivot of European defense toward Gulf customers. This is a secular trend as US defense relationships become more complex.

## Adjacent Plays

- **Hensoldt (HNS.DE):** Leonardo owns 22.8%. German sensor/optronics pure-play. Direct beneficiary of German defense spending boom. Smaller, higher-growth, more volatile.
- **Leonardo DRS (DRS):** US-listed, 72.3% owned. Pure US defense electronics. Under-covered.
- **MBDA (private, 25% Leonardo):** European missile champion. Benefits from same rearmament cycle. Not investable directly but contributes to Leonardo earnings.
- **Indra Sistemas (IDR.MC):** Spanish defense electronics, smaller-cap European defense play.

## Contrarian Angle — What Is the Market Missing?

1. **Sum-of-parts undervaluation:** DRS alone (~$4B market cap at 72.3% ownership = ~$2.9B to Leonardo) + Hensoldt stake (~€2B at market) + MBDA value + Telespazio + core business. Sum-of-parts may exceed current market cap.

2. **Margin expansion catalyst:** Aerostructures JV could unlock €200-300M in annual losses. That flows straight to the bottom line — potentially a 1-2% margin jump overnight.

3. **European defense ETF flows:** New defense-focused ETFs launching in 2026. Leonardo is a top holding in most European defense indices. Passive inflows will provide structural demand.

4. **Italian "national champion" premium:** Italy will protect and promote Leonardo more aggressively as European defense becomes politically important. Government support = reduced downside risk.

## What Could Go Wrong — The Steelman Bear Case

1. **Peace dividend:** A genuine Russia-Ukraine peace deal could deflate defense enthusiasm. But: even with peace, structural spending commitments are already legislated.

2. **Execution risk on transformation:** Shifting from hardware to tech is hard. Legacy culture, union constraints, and Italian state bureaucracy are real obstacles.

3. **GCAP cost overruns / delays:** Multi-national defense programs notoriously go over budget and behind schedule. Tempest/GCAP has already faced Japan disagreements.

4. **Italian fiscal constraints:** Italy's debt/GDP (~140%) could eventually force spending discipline, affecting domestic orders. But defense is currently politically untouchable.
