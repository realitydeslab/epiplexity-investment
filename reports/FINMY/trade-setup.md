## Risk Management

- **Stop-loss:** €48 (-18%) — below major technical support
- **Position sizing:** 3-5% of portfolio. European defense is a strong theme but single-stock risk exists.
- **What kills the trade:**
  1. Surprise peace deal / détente that reduces defense urgency
  2. Italian government forced sale of shares (fiscal pressure)
  3. GCAP program cancellation or restructuring
  4. Currency risk (EUR/USD) for ADR holders
- **Correlation risk:** Highly correlated to other European defense (Rheinmetall, BAE, Thales). If the sector sells off, Leonardo goes with it.

## ADR vs. Milan Listing

- **Prefer LDO.MI** if you have access to European exchanges — better liquidity, tighter spreads
- **FINMY ADR** is OTC Pink Sheets — wider spreads, lower volume. Suitable for US investors who can't access Milan.
- **Currency exposure:** ADR holders have EUR/USD exposure. EUR strength = additional return; EUR weakness = headwind.
