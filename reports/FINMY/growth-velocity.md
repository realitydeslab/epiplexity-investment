## Growth Acceleration Analysis

**Revenue growth: ACCELERATING**
- FY2023 → FY2024: ~+8%
- FY2024 → FY2025: +11%
- FY2025 → FY2026E: +10-12%
- Growth rate has increased from mid-single-digit to double-digit. European defense budgets provide structural tailwind.

**New orders growth: +15% YoY in FY2025**
- Order backlog expanding — critical for future revenue visibility
- Book-to-bill ratio >1.0x, meaning orders exceed revenue = growing backlog

**Operating profit: +18% YoY in FY2025**
- Operating leverage kicking in — profit growing faster than revenue
- Margin expansion from ~7.1% to ~7.7% (2024 → 2025)
- Target: 8-9% EBIT margin medium-term as portfolio mix shifts to electronics/cyber

## User/Customer Growth

- **Geographic expansion:** Strengthening Middle East presence (Saudi JV), maintaining UK (helicopter contract), growing US via DRS
- **Sector expansion:** From defense-only to broader security, cyber, space
- **Contract wins:** 15% increase in new orders signals accelerating customer acquisition

## Unit Economics

- **Improving:** Higher-margin electronics/cyber growing as share of revenue
- **Aerostructures drag:** Still loss-making but JV should restructure this
- **Cash conversion:** Free cash flow €649M in Q3 2025 (+68.25% YoY). Debt reduction underway.
