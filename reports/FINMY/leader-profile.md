# Leader Profile — Leonardo SpA (FINMY / LDO.MI)

*Updated: March 8, 2026*

## CEO: Roberto Cingolani

- **Appointed:** March 2023
- **Background:** Physicist by training. Former Italian Minister for Ecological Transition (2021-2022) under Draghi government. Previously Scientific Director at Italian Institute of Technology (IIT) for 16 years, turning it into a world-class robotics/AI lab. Senior VP at Leonardo before ministerial role.
- **Edge:** Rare combination of deep tech (AI, robotics, nanotechnology) + government connections + defense industry experience. His appointment signaled Leonardo's strategic pivot from hardware to technology/digital.
- **Vision:** Transforming Leonardo from a traditional defense hardware company into an integrated security technology group. Focus on cyber, space, AI-enabled defense systems, and digital platforms.
- **Execution:** FY2025 results confirm the strategy is working — revenue +11%, operating profit +18%, new orders +15%. Morningstar analyst calls it an "inflection point."

## Chairman: Stefano Pontecorvo

- Former NATO Senior Civilian Representative in Afghanistan. Deep diplomatic/defense network.

## Key Hires/Departures (Last 12 Months)

- Cingolani has been restructuring the C-suite toward digital/tech backgrounds
- Leonardo DRS leadership stable under William Lynn III (former US Deputy Secretary of Defense)
- Hensoldt stake (22.8%) maintained — strategic sensor/optronics partnership

## Insider Activity

- **Italian Government (30.2% owner):** No indication of reducing stake. Government actively supportive of Leonardo as national champion.
- **CEO compensation:** Tied to long-term incentive plan linked to revenue/margin targets, aligning interests with shareholders.
- **No significant insider selling reported.** Government stake provides a floor.

## Public Statements on Near-Term Outlook

- Cingolani at FY2025 results: Called European rearmament a "generational opportunity" for Leonardo
- Flagged aerostructures JV as "coming in the next months"
- Emphasized shift to "security and not just defense" — broader addressable market
- Noted improved contract economics and capital discipline
- Guided for continued double-digit growth in 2026

## Leadership Assessment

**Grade: A-**

Cingolani is the right leader for this moment. His tech background (rare in European defense CEOs) positions Leonardo to capture the higher-margin digital/cyber segment. Government connections de-risk regulatory/political dimensions. The FY2025 results validate execution. Main risk: he's been CEO only ~3 years — long-term track record still building.
