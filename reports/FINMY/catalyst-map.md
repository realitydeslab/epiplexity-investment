# Catalyst Map — Leonardo SpA (FINMY / LDO.MI)

*Updated: March 8, 2026*

## 1. European Defense Rearmament Super-Cycle
- **What:** NATO allies accelerating toward 3.5% GDP defense spending targets. Germany's €500B+ infrastructure/defense fund. EU defense coordination expanding.
- **When:** Ongoing throughout 2026, with key budget decisions Q2-Q3 2026
- **Impact:** +15-25% revenue uplift over 2-3 years. Stock could re-rate P/E from ~32x to 35-40x (+10-25% share price)
- **Probability:** HIGH — already underway, bipartisan consensus across Europe
- **Market expectation:** Partially priced (stock +17% YTD, +73% from 52-week low). But spending is structural, not cyclical.
- **Our edge:** Market underestimates the duration. Leonardo's diversified portfolio (helicopters, electronics, cyber, space) captures more budget lines than pure-play defense.

## 2. Saudi Arabia Joint Venture (Aerostructures)
- **What:** JV accord to restructure loss-making aerostructures business, potentially with Saudi partners
- **When:** Expected by mid-2026 (CEO indicated "coming months" in March 2026)
- **Impact:** Could eliminate €200-300M annual drag. If announced with favorable terms: +5-8% stock move
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH — CEO publicly flagged as imminent
- **Market expectation:** Aware but skeptical on execution
- **Our edge:** If deal includes Saudi defense procurement commitments, it becomes a revenue catalyst not just margin fix.

## 3. GCAP (Global Combat Air Programme) — 6th Gen Fighter
- **What:** UK-Italy-Japan trilateral next-gen fighter. Leonardo leads Italian work share. Potential Saudi Arabia entry.
- **When:** Key design phase decisions Q2-Q3 2026. Saudi entry decision could come by June 2026.
- **Impact:** €100B+ lifecycle program. Leonardo's share €15-20B over 20 years. Near-term: +3-5% on milestones.
- **Probability:** HIGH for continuation, MEDIUM for Saudi entry by H1 2026
- **Market expectation:** Treated as "long-term." Acceleration or Saudi entry = positive surprise.
- **Our edge:** Saudi entry would be transformational for order backlog visibility.

## 4. UK £1bn+ Helicopter Contract
- **What:** Rachel Reeves signing off £1bn Leonardo helicopter contract for UK military
- **When:** Imminent — reported week of March 1, 2026 (FT)
- **Impact:** ~€1.2B order intake. +2-3% on formal announcement.
- **Probability:** HIGH
- **Market expectation:** Partially expected, not fully priced until formal contract.
- **Our edge:** Validates UK positioning. UK defense spending rising to 2.5%+ GDP.

## 5. Rheinmetall JV (LRMV) — Lynx IFV Orders
- **What:** LRMV (50/50 JV) competing for 1,050+ Lynx IFV orders for Italian Army
- **When:** Decision expected H1-H2 2026
- **Impact:** Contract €3-5B. Leonardo's 50% = €1.5-2.5B. +3-5% on win.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH — LRMV is preferred bidder
- **Market expectation:** Known pipeline, timing uncertain.
- **Our edge:** European land rearmament accelerating faster than expected.

## 6. Leonardo DRS (US Subsidiary) Growth
- **What:** 72.3%-owned US defense electronics subsidiary. US defense AI/EW spending.
- **When:** Ongoing quarterly earnings
- **Impact:** Hidden value driver at ~$4B market cap. Sum-of-parts re-rating. +2-4% on beats.
- **Probability:** HIGH
- **Market expectation:** Underappreciated by European investors.
- **Our edge:** Double-dipping on both European AND US defense spending.

## 7. Cyber & Digital Transformation
- **What:** Shift to higher-margin cyber, digital identity, integrated security. NATO contracts.
- **When:** Revenue recognition accelerating through 2026
- **Impact:** Margin expansion from ~7% to 8-9% EBIT. P/E expansion. +5-10% if visible H1 2026.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH — Morningstar confirms "inflection point"
- **Market expectation:** "Show me" mode.
- **Our edge:** Market still values as old-school hardware defense.
