# SOFI — Growth Velocity 📈

**Verdict: ACCELERATING on revenue, margins, and members. Decelerating slightly on YoY growth rate (from 38% to 30% guided), but absolute dollar growth is increasing.**

## Unit Economics

- **Average borrower income:** Personal loans $158K, Student loans $149K (FICO 746/765)
- **Personal loan charge-off rate:** 2.80% (up 20bps QoQ — modest deterioration, but manageable given income levels)
- **Student loan charge-off rate:** 0.76% (excellent)
- **Deposit costs:** Bank charter gives SoFi access to low-cost deposits (~$28B+ in total deposits), funding loans at ~4% vs. 7-8% for non-bank lenders
- **CAC declining** as existing members cross-buy (40% cross-buy rate)

**Bottom Line:** Growth is ACCELERATING on absolute metrics (revenue dollars, members, products) while growth rates remain remarkably high at 30%+. Margin expansion is adding fuel. This is textbook compounding.
