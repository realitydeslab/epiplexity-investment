# SOFI — Investment Thesis (1-Page)

## The Catalyst
SoFi Technologies is a digital banking super-app that just crossed $1B quarterly revenue, growing 37% YoY with 13.7M members (+35%) and GAAP profitability for 9 consecutive quarters. CEO Anthony Noto bought $1M of stock at $17.88 on March 2, 2026 — his first major open-market purchase since the stock was in single digits (it subsequently 4x'd). The stock is down 29% YTD despite record fundamentals, creating a classic sentiment-driven dislocation.

## The Trade
**Entry:** $17-19 (scaling in around CEO's buy price of $17.88)  
**Base target:** $26 (+44%) — consensus analyst PT  
**Bull target:** $32 (+78%) — S&P 500 inclusion + guidance beats  
**Stop-loss:** $14.50 (-19%)  
**Time horizon:** 12 months  
**Position size:** 3-5% of portfolio (moderate conviction, high beta)

## The Edge
1. **CEO signal:** Noto's insider buys have 100% hit rate historically. He doesn't buy casually.
2. **Growth at a discount:** 30x forward P/E for a company growing revenue 30%+ and EPS 54% is cheap by any growth framework. Rule of 40 score at 68 is elite.
3. **Galileo hidden value:** The BaaS platform (150M+ accounts) is valued at ~$0 by the market. Standalone worth $5-10B.
4. **Bank charter moat:** Only major neobank with a full national charter. This gives SoFi a 300-400bp funding cost advantage over non-bank competitors.
5. **S&P 500 inclusion optionality:** Not priced in. Would trigger $2-3B in forced passive buying.

## The Risk
1. **Credit quality:** Personal loan charge-offs at 2.80% — manageable but trending up. A recession could spike this to 4%+ and force provisions.
2. **High beta (2.12):** Stock amplifies market moves. Broad market selloff drags SOFI regardless of fundamentals.
3. **Capital raise overhang:** $1.5B December raise created dilution concerns. Need time to demonstrate deployment returns.
4. **Valuation compression:** If growth narrative breaks (guidance cut), stock could de-rate to 15-20x forward P/E ($9-12).

## The Verdict
**BUY — High conviction contrarian entry**

The setup is textbook: record fundamentals + CEO buying + technical oversold + sentiment capitulation. SoFi is transitioning from "fintech startup" to "diversified financial institution," and the market hasn't caught up. The -29% YTD decline is noise; the 35% revenue growth and 54% EPS growth trajectory is signal. Follow the CEO's money.
