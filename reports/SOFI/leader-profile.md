## Is He the Right Person?

**Verdict: YES — Strong operator with execution track record**

Anthony Noto is one of the most credentialed fintech CEOs in the market. His career trajectory — Goldman Sachs partner → NFL COO → Twitter CFO → SoFi CEO — demonstrates versatility and ambition. Since taking the helm in 2018, he has:

1. **Secured the bank charter** (2022) — the single most important strategic decision, giving SoFi cost-of-funding advantage over neobank competitors
2. **Acquired Galileo** (2020, $1.2B) — transformed SoFi from a lending company into a financial infrastructure platform
3. **Acquired Technisys** (2022) — added Cyberbank Core, enabling BaaS capabilities
4. **Scaled profitably** — from $621M revenue (2021) to $3.6B (FY2025), achieving 9 consecutive quarters of GAAP profitability
5. **Grew members from ~1M to 13.7M** — 35% YoY growth sustained

His Wall Street background (Goldman Sachs) means he understands capital markets, M&A, and institutional investors. His tech background (Twitter) means he understands platform scaling. Rare combination.
