# TTD — The Trade Desk: Investment Thesis

*Last Updated: March 8, 2026*

## The Catalyst
CEO Jeff Green just made the loudest insider buy in ad tech history — $148M of personal capital deployed at $23-25/share after a 55% decline. Simultaneously, OpenAI is in early talks to partner with TTD for ChatGPT ad monetization. The Ventura ecosystem is embedding TTD into CTV operating systems. Multiple growth catalysts are converging at historically cheap valuations.

## The Trade
**Entry:** $25-30 range (current ~$29, near CEO purchase zone)
**Position:** 3-5% of portfolio, dollar-cost average over 4-6 weeks
**Time Horizon:** 12 months
**Base Target:** $40-45 (+38-55%)
**Bull Target:** $55-60 (+90-107%)

## The Edge
1. **Insider conviction at scale:** $148M CEO buy is an informational signal the market hasn't fully digested
2. **Growth trough, not structural decline:** Ex-political spending, Q4 was +19%. Guidance is sandbagged.
3. **Valuation reset:** 33x P/E and 4x P/S — cheapest TTD has been since its early public days
4. **OpenAI optionality:** ChatGPT ad partnership would unlock an entirely new high-intent channel
5. **CTV/Ventura:** Long-term moat building that makes TTD infrastructure, not just a DSP

## The Risk
1. **Growth continues decelerating** below 10% — would break the thesis
2. **Amazon DSP** takes meaningful share of open internet programmatic
3. **OpenAI chooses a walled garden** partner instead (Google, Amazon)
4. **Macro worsens** — ad budgets get slashed, TTD's cyclical exposure hurts
5. **Take rate compression** from 20% to 19% pressures profitability

## The Verdict
**BUY on weakness.** TTD is a high-quality business in a cyclical trough with the strongest insider buy signal on our entire watchlist. The risk/reward at $25-30 is asymmetric: downside is capped near $20 (hard stop), upside is $45-60 on growth reacceleration. The CEO put $148M on the line — that's not a man worried about the next quarter.
