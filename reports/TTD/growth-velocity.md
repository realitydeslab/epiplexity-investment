# TTD — The Trade Desk: Growth Velocity 📈

*Last Updated: March 8, 2026*

## Revenue Growth — Quarterly Trajectory

| Quarter | Revenue | YoY Growth | QoQ Trend |
|---------|---------|------------|-----------|
| Q1 2025 | $616M | +25% | Strong |
| Q2 2025 | ~$585M | +19% | Decelerating |
| Q3 2025 | ~$628M | +18% | Decelerating |
| Q4 2025 | $847M | +14% | Decelerating |
| **Q1 2026 Guide** | **≥$678M** | **~10%** | **Trough?** |

**Full Year 2025:** $2.9 billion (+18% YoY from $2.4B in 2024)

## The Deceleration Story
- Growth has slowed from 25% → 19% → 18% → 14% → ~10% guided
- Key headwind: CPG and automotive ad verticals pulled back (Loop Capital estimates -5+ pts impact)
- Political spending absence creates a difficult comp (2024 was an election year)
- Amazon DSP competition is real — Amazon's first-party data advantage pressures TTD in retail

## But Is This the Trough?
**Signs of potential reacceleration:**
- Ex-political spending, Q4 growth was ~19% — the underlying business is healthier than 14% suggests
- "At least $678M" is sandbagged guidance — TTD has historically beaten guidance
- Analyst consensus models 18.5% FY2026 revenue growth, implying back-half acceleration
- OpenAI partnership could add an entirely new channel
- Ventura ecosystem creates new programmatic inventory

## Profitability & Margins

| Metric | Q4 2025 | YoY Change |
|--------|---------|------------|
| Adj. EBITDA | $400M | 47.3% margin (beat $376M est) |
| Operating Margin | 30.3% | Up from 26.4% |
| Free Cash Flow Margin | 33.3% | Up from 21% prior Q |
| EPS | $0.59 | Beat $0.58 consensus |

**Key insight:** Margins are EXPANDING even as growth slows. This is a company becoming more profitable, not deteriorating.

## Customer Metrics
- Customer retention: >95% for 12th consecutive year
- This is exceptional for ad tech — clients are not leaving TTD

## Guidance vs. Actual History
- TTD consistently beats its own "at least" guidance
- Q4 2025: Guided $840M, delivered $847M
- Pattern suggests Q1 2026 will likely beat $678M guide

## Unit Economics
- Take rate stable (~20%, some analysts model decline to 19% in 2026)
- Gross spend on platform growing
- Revenue per customer increasing

## Path to Growth Reacceleration
1. **Political spending normalization** — cleaner comps from Q2 2026 onward
2. **Macro recovery** — CPG/auto verticals recover with rate cuts
3. **CTV/Ventura** — new inventory opens up
4. **OpenAI + AI channels** — entirely new ad surfaces
5. **Retail media** — programmatic retail media outside Amazon

## Verdict
Growth is decelerating but this looks like a **cyclical trough**, not a structural decline. Margins are strong, customers are sticky, and multiple catalysts could reaccelerate growth in H2 2026. The question is timing.

*Sources: TTD Q4 2025 earnings release, 247 Wall St, Financhle, Yahoo Finance*
