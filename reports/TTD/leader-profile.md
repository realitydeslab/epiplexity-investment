# TTD — The Trade Desk: Leader Profile 👤

*Last Updated: March 8, 2026*

## Jeff Green — Co-Founder & CEO

### Background
- Co-founded The Trade Desk in 2009, has been CEO since inception
- Previously co-founded AdECN, one of the first online ad exchanges (acquired by Microsoft)
- Deep domain expertise in programmatic advertising — 20+ years in ad tech
- Known for long-term strategic vision and willingness to make contrarian bets

### The $148M Signal
- **March 2-4, 2026:** Green purchased 6 million shares at $23.49-$25.08 per share, totaling ~$148 million
- This is one of the largest CEO insider purchases in US tech history
- Stock surged 18% on the disclosure (March 5, 2026)
- **Significance:** This is personal money, not options or grants. When a founder-CEO bets $148M of his own capital, he's signaling extreme conviction in the company's near-term prospects.

### Recent Public Statements
- Q4 2025 earnings call (Feb 25, 2026): "I believe our best days are ahead"
- "I don't think there's any company in our industry that's better positioned to take advantage of advances in AI"
- Notably bullish tone despite growth deceleration, suggesting he sees catalysts the market doesn't

### Insider Activity (Broader)
- Green historically has been a net seller at higher prices — this is his first major BUY
- The reversal from seller to massive buyer at 55% off highs is an extremely strong signal
- $500M buyback authorization by the board reinforces management confidence

### Key Hires/Departures
- Some executive turnover during 2025 noted by analysts as a concern
- However, 95%+ customer retention for 12 consecutive years suggests operational stability
- Ventura ecosystem launch demonstrates continued product innovation capability

### Assessment
- **Rating: A+** — The single strongest insider buy signal we've seen across the entire watchlist
- Green has the clearest view of the business pipeline and chose to deploy $148M at these levels
- He's the right leader: founder-led, domain expert, long-term thinker
- The insider buy alone makes this worth serious investigation
