# TTD — The Trade Desk: Competitive Map 🗺️

*Last Updated: March 8, 2026*

## The Programmatic Advertising Landscape

Global ad spend expected to surpass $1 trillion in 2026, with programmatic accounting for ~90% of global display ad spending.

## Direct Competitors

### 1. Google DV360 (GOOGL)
- **Position:** Largest DSP by market share. Part of Google's walled garden.
- **Advantage:** Unmatched first-party data (Search, YouTube, Gmail), massive scale
- **Weakness:** Conflicts of interest (Google is both buyer and seller of ads). Antitrust pressure.
- **If TTD wins:** Google faces pressure from regulators; independent DSPs gain share
- **If TTD loses:** Google's dominance continues unchallenged

### 2. Amazon DSP (AMZN)
- **Position:** Fastest-growing DSP, powered by purchase intent data
- **Advantage:** First-party shopping data is the holy grail for CPG/retail advertisers
- **Weakness:** Walled garden; limited transparency; primarily e-commerce focused
- **Threat Level:** HIGH — Amazon is THE biggest competitive threat to TTD, especially in retail media
- **If TTD wins:** Open internet programmatic stays relevant alongside walled gardens

### 3. Meta Ads (META)
- **Position:** Dominant in social advertising
- **Advantage:** Unmatched social graph, AI-powered ad targeting
- **Weakness:** Not a true DSP; limited to Meta's surfaces
- **Overlap with TTD:** Limited. Different channels, different buyers

### 4. AppLovin (APP)
- **Position:** AI-powered mobile ad tech
- **Advantage:** AXON engine for mobile game/app advertising
- **Weakness:** Narrow mobile focus; questions about long-term moat
- **Epiplexity Score:** 82 (already researched)

## Who Loses if TTD Wins?
- **Google DV360** — loses open internet share to an independent alternative
- **Smaller DSPs** (StackAdapt, MediaMath) — TTD consolidates the independent DSP market
- **Linear TV ad sales teams** — CTV shift accelerates their decline

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### Long TTD / Short ROKU
- **Thesis:** TTD is the infrastructure layer; ROKU is a device/OS layer with margin pressure
- If CTV advertising grows, TTD captures more value than ROKU

### Long TTD / Short APP (hedged)
- **Thesis:** Both are independent ad tech, but TTD has broader CTV/retail media moat while APP is mobile-concentrated
- Use if you want pure contrarian ad tech exposure with a hedge

## TTD's Moat Assessment
1. **Independence** — Only major DSP not owned by a media/tech giant
2. **95%+ retention** — Clients don't leave
3. **Ventura** — Embedding into CTV OS layer creates switching costs
4. **Unified ID 2.0** — TTD-led identity solution for post-cookie world
5. **Koa AI** — Proprietary optimization engine

**Moat Durability:** Medium-High. Amazon DSP is the biggest threat, but TTD's independence is a structural advantage for brands that don't want to buy ads from their competitor.
