# NUVL Competitive Map — Nuvalent

*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

## ROS1+ NSCLC Landscape

| Drug | Company | Limitations |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Crizotinib (Xalkori) | Pfizer | 1st-gen, limited CNS, resistance |
| Entrectinib (Rozlytrek) | Roche | TRK/ROS1 — CNS adverse events |
| Repotrectinib (Augtyro) | Bristol-Myers | ROS1/TRK — same TRK liability |

**Zidesamtinib edge:** ROS1-selective (no TRK toxicity), overcomes G2032R mutation, brain-penetrant. Would be first ROS1-selective inhibitor if approved.

## ALK+ NSCLC (Neladalkib)
Competes with lorlatinib (Pfizer), alectinib (Roche), brigatinib (Takeda). Must show CNS safety/resistance superiority.

## Who Benefits from Same Catalysts
- Guardant Health (GH), Natera (NTRA) — companion diagnostics
- Precision oncology CROs

## Who Loses
- Roche (entrectinib), BMS (repotrectinib)
