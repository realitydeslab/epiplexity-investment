# NUVL Catalyst Map — Nuvalent

*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

## 🎯 Key Catalysts (Next 12 Months)

### 1. FDA PDUFA — Zidesamtinib (NVL-520) for ROS1+ NSCLC
- **What:** FDA decision on zidesamtinib NDA for ROS1-positive non-small cell lung cancer (2nd-line+). First ROS1-selective inhibitor designed to overcome resistance mutations including the G2032R solvent front mutation
- **When:** September 18, 2026 (PDUFA date)
- **Impact:** +50-80% on approval; -40-60% on CRL/rejection
- **Probability:** HIGH (70-75%). Breakthrough Therapy designation, strong Phase 2 ARROS-1 data, unmet need, Priority Review likely
- **Market expectation:** Consensus expects approval — 15/15 analysts rate Buy/Strong Buy. Priced at ~50-60% probability given current $99 vs $135 average PT
- **Our edge:** Market may be underpricing the approval probability. Breakthrough Therapy drugs have ~85% historical approval rate. The ROS1-selective mechanism is differentiated from crizotinib/entrectinib which have off-target TRK issues

### 2. Neladalkib (NVL-655) — ALK-Positive NSCLC Phase 3 Updates
- **What:** ALKOVE-1 Phase 1/2 data updates + Phase 3 trial enrollment progress for ALK+ NSCLC (larger market than ROS1)
- **When:** Q2-Q3 2026 data updates
- **Impact:** +15-25% on strong data; this is the bigger long-term value driver (ALK+ NSCLC is ~5% of NSCLC vs 1-3% for ROS1)
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH (60-65%)
- **Market expectation:** Seen as pipeline optionality, not fully priced in
- **Our edge:** If neladalkib shows superiority vs lorlatinib in CNS outcomes, the peak sales ceiling expands dramatically

### 3. NVL-330 (HER2-Selective) Phase 1 Data
- **What:** First clinical data from HER2ex20 program
- **When:** H2 2026 (likely ESMO)
- **Impact:** +10-20% on proof-of-concept data
- **Probability:** MEDIUM
- **Market expectation:** Not meaningfully priced in — pure pipeline upside

### 4. Commercial Partnerships / Ex-US Licensing
- **What:** Potential licensing deals for ex-US commercialization
- **When:** Q2-Q3 2026
- **Impact:** +5-15%
- **Probability:** MEDIUM

### 5. Earnings / Cash Updates
- **What:** Q1 (May) and Q2 (Aug) earnings
- **When:** May, August 2026
- **Impact:** ±5%
