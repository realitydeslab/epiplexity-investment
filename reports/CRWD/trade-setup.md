## Risk Management

### Stop-Loss
- **Tight:** $380 (-12.5%) — below 50 DMA and recent support
- **Wide:** $340 (-21.7%) — below Q1 2026 consolidation range

### Position Sizing
- **Recommended:** 3-5% of portfolio
- **Rationale:** High-conviction cybersecurity leader with premium valuation. Size modestly to account for valuation risk; add on pullbacks to $380-400.

### What Kills the Trade?
1. **Revenue deceleration below 20%** — would signal structural slowdown, not cyclical
2. **Charlotte AI fails to monetize** — if AI remains cost center rather than revenue driver, the narrative collapses
3. **Major customer breach or second global outage** — July 2024 outage precedent makes this existential risk
4. **Microsoft Defender enterprise wins** — bundling threat is real for SMB/mid-market
5. **Insider selling acceleration** — if Kurtz or senior leadership materially increase selling

### Correlation Risk
- Highly correlated to tech/software sentiment (Nasdaq-100 member)
- If broader SaaS selloff continues, CRWD gets dragged down regardless of fundamentals
- Positively correlated to geopolitical tension (benefits from cyber threats)
- Partially correlated to defense spending sentiment
