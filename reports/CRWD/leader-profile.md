# CRWD Leader Profile — George Kurtz

**Research Date:** March 9, 2026

---

## CEO: George Kurtz (Co-Founder, CEO & President)

### Background
- **Tenure:** Co-founded CrowdStrike in 2011; CEO since inception (~15 years)
- **Prior:** CTO of McAfee (2009-2012), CEO of Foundstone (acquired by McAfee 2004)
- **Education:** Accounting/finance background; CPA
- **Known for:** Cybersecurity industry veteran, pioneer of cloud-native endpoint security
- **Public profile:** Regular presence at Fal.Con, RSAC, and government security conferences
- **Compensation:** Among highest-paid cybersecurity CEOs

### Leadership Assessment
- **RIGHT person for this phase?** YES — Kurtz understands both the technical and business sides. He built the Falcon platform from scratch, navigated the July 2024 global outage crisis (Blue Screen of Death incident), and steered recovery. His ability to retain customers through that crisis demonstrated genuine trust.
- **Execution track record:** Exceptional. Took CrowdStrike from startup to $4.81B revenue, $5.25B ARR, and S&P 500 membership. Fastest cybersecurity company to $1B+ net new ARR.
- **Strategic vision:** Correctly pivoted to platform consolidation (Falcon Flex) and agentic AI (Charlotte AI) before competitors. Understands that AI is an accelerant, not a threat.
- **Risk:** Has been CEO for 15 years — succession planning is important but not urgent.

### CTO: Dmitri Alperovitch (Co-Founder)
- Left CrowdStrike in 2020 to found Silverado Policy Accelerator (think tank)
- No longer involved in day-to-day operations
- Current CTO role filled internally

### Key Observations
- **No panic selling:** No unusual insider selling around Q4 FY2026 earnings
- **No significant insider buying:** Unlike some competitor CEOs (e.g., SoFi, TTD), no large open-market insider buys recently — this is a slight negative signal, but understandable given Kurtz's existing large position
- **Institutional ownership:** 72.73% — strong institutional support

## Leadership Verdict

**Score: 8.5/10**

George Kurtz is among the top cybersecurity CEOs globally. He built the company, navigated the worst crisis in its history (July 2024 outage), and emerged stronger. The Charlotte AI pivot is strategically sound. Main concern is the lack of insider buying (unlike peers where CEOs are buying aggressively), but the existing $3.67B stake provides alignment. Employee efficiency improvements are a positive sign of operational maturity.
