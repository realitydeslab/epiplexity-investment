## Growth Velocity Verdict

**Score: 7.5/10**

Growth is decelerating from hyper-growth (66%→22%) as expected for a $5B revenue company, BUT the Q4 acceleration and FY2027 reacceleration guidance are bullish signals. The $1.01B net new ARR record, 57% Q4 FCF growth, and improving employee productivity show the business engine is healthy. The key question is whether Charlotte AI can sustain 24%+ growth or if it reverts to high-teens. Consensus expects reacceleration to 24.3% in FY2027 — if achieved, this is a growth re-rating story.
