# BYD Company (1211.HK) — The Thesis 📄

*Research date: March 9, 2026*

## The Bet

BYD is the world's largest NEV maker (4.28M vehicles sold in 2025), with unmatched vertical integration — they make their own batteries, chips, electric motors, and now autonomous driving software. The bet: **the 2nd-gen blade battery + flash charging + global expansion reignite growth after a temporary 6-month sales slump, and the stock re-rates from a temporary bottom.**

## The Bull Case

This is a ~CNY 950B revenue company growing 20%+ annually, with gross margins improving to ~16%, now trading at ~37x trailing P/E — down 38% from its May 2025 high of HK$159.27. The 2nd-gen blade battery launched March 5, 2026 is a game-changer: 10% to 97% charge in 9 minutes, 5% higher energy density, lifetime warranty on cells. Ten models launching with this tech in Q2 2026. BYD is building 20,000 flash-charging stations by year-end. Exports crossed 100K/month for 4 consecutive months — up 50% YoY even as domestic sales slump. The Hungary factory opens in 2026, giving tariff-free EU access. God's Eye autonomous driving is rolling out across the lineup. FY2025 annual results drop this month — likely a record.

## The Bear Case

Six consecutive months of domestic sales decline. February 2026 sales: 190K units, down 41% YoY. The 5% NEV purchase tax in 2026 is a real headwind. Battery raw material costs are rising, forcing price adjustments on new-gen models. The EU anti-subsidy tariff investigation could result in 20-40% duties. Competition is savage — Xiaomi SU7 is eating into BYD's premium push, Huawei's smart driving ecosystem is arguably ahead, and the Chinese market is in financial warfare mode (7-year loan plans = desperation). At 37x P/E, BYD is expensive relative to legacy auto peers (Toyota 13x, VW 8x). Warren Buffett has been steadily trimming his position since 2022.

## The Risk

1. **Sales recovery fails** — if 2nd-gen battery doesn't reignite demand, China market stays saturated
2. **EU tariffs** — could lock BYD out of its biggest growth market
3. **Margin compression** — rising raw materials + price wars + 7-year financing subsidies
4. **Geopolitical** — Western governments restricting Chinese EVs
5. **Competition** — Xiaomi, Huawei, Tesla all competing aggressively

## The Verdict

**High-conviction buy on this pullback.** BYD at HK$98 is 38% below its May 2025 high. The 2nd-gen blade battery is the most significant product launch since the original blade battery in 2020. Global expansion provides structural growth beyond China's saturated market. The sales slump is cyclical (purchase tax transition, Chinese New Year) not structural. Q2 2026 should show a meaningful recovery.

## One-Line Thesis

> **BYD is the Toyota of the EV era — vertically integrated, relentlessly cost-competitive, now arming itself with breakthrough battery tech and a global manufacturing footprint — buy the 6-month dip.**
