# BYD Company (1211.HK) — Leader Profile 👤

*Research date: March 9, 2026*

## Wang Chuanfu (王传福) — Chairman & President since founding (1995)

### Background
- **Born:** February 15, 1966, Wuhu, Anhui Province, China (age 60)
- **Education:** BS Chemistry, Central South University of Technology (1987); MS Physical Chemistry, Beijing Non-Ferrous Metal Research Institute (1990)
- **Career:** Entire career at BYD — founded the company in 1995 with CNY 2.5M borrowed from relatives
- **Net worth:** ~$30B+ (2026 estimates), one of China's richest
- **Stake:** ~22% economic interest through BYD International
- **Background:** Orphaned young, raised by elder siblings. Self-made in every sense

### Track Record

**The numbers are staggering:**
- Revenue: ~CNY 2B (2003 IPO) → CNY 950B+ (2025) — 475x growth
- From battery manufacturer to world's largest NEV maker
- 4.28M vehicles sold in 2025 (surpassing Volkswagen Group in total NEVs)
- Blade battery (2020) — redefined EV safety standards
- DM-i hybrid technology — made PHEVs viable at ICE prices
- Vertical integration: makes own batteries, chips, motors, IGBT modules, e-platforms
- Successfully navigated Buffett investment (2008, $230M), subsequent partial exit without stock collapse

**What makes him exceptional:**
- Engineer-CEO who personally reviews technical specs and drives product decisions
- "Battery + EV + semiconductor" trilateral strategy was conceived 20+ years ago — and executed
- Survived 2019 near-death (stock at all-time lows, critics calling BYD a "zombie company")
- Pivoted from pure EV to hybrid when market demanded it — pragmatic, not ideological
- March 5, 2026: personally presented 2nd-gen blade battery at launch event

**What he's NOT:**
- Not a charismatic public speaker (compared to Elon Musk or Lei Jun)
- Not a global brand builder yet — BYD's international marketing is still maturing
- Not a delegator — extremely hands-on, which could limit global scaling

### Vision Articulation

Wang communicates through **products and technology demonstrations** rather than narrative storytelling. His March 5 launch event was technically precise — charging speeds, cold-weather performance, safety tests — but lacked the showmanship of a Tesla event. This is a feature in China (substance > style) but a weakness globally.

His "Flash-charging China / Flash-charging Planet" vision is ambitious and clearly articulated: **solve range anxiety permanently, everywhere.**

### Insider Activity

- **Founder stake:** ~22% economic interest — significant skin in the game
- **No significant insider selling** in recent years (unlike Buffett's gradual trim)
- **Berkshire Hathaway** has reduced from 20.04% (2008) to <5% by late 2025 — but this is Buffett's portfolio rebalancing, not a BYD bear signal
- **Interpretation:** Wang's alignment with shareholders is strong. He IS the company

### Leadership Team

- **Wang Chuanfu** — Chairman, President, and de facto chief engineer
- **Lian Yubo** — Executive VP, oversees automotive business; Wang's right hand for operations
- **Li Ke** — VP, oversees tech R&D; key figure in semiconductor and ADAS development
- **Li Yunfei** — GM Branding & PR; public-facing spokesperson, handled 2nd-gen battery communications
- **He Long** — Oversees overseas business expansion; led Thailand, Hungary factory projects
- **Zhao Changjiang** — Head of Denza (premium brand); previously ran Ocean lineup
- **R&D army:** 90,000+ engineers — largest automotive R&D workforce in the world

**Bench depth: Strong but concentrated.** Wang is irreplaceable in the near term. The team is deep in engineering but thinner in global brand management and autonomous driving.

### Succession Risk

**High-medium.** Wang Chuanfu IS BYD — founder, visionary, technical decision-maker. At 60, he's active and healthy but has not publicly named a successor. The company's culture is deeply tied to his engineering-first philosophy. A transition would be disruptive but not fatal given the institutional depth of 90K+ R&D engineers.

### Red Flags

- **6 consecutive months of sales decline** — the current slump is real, even if cyclical
- **Buffett trimming** — not a sell signal per se, but notable when the world's most famous investor reduces a position
- **Margin pressure** — raw material costs rising, 7-year financing plans are margin-negative
- **Autonomous driving gap** — behind Huawei (ADS 3.0) and Tesla (FSD) in perception and possibly reality
- **Brand perception globally** — still fighting "cheap Chinese" stigma in Europe and Americas
- **Single-person dependency** — Wang's health/departure would be a major event

### The Verdict

**Wang Chuanfu is one of the most impressive founder-CEOs alive.** From an orphan in rural Anhui to building the world's largest EV company with full vertical integration. His engineering depth, strategic patience (planned EVs 25+ years ago), and execution track record are extraordinary. The gap is in global brand building and autonomous driving. But at 60, he has years left, and the machine he's built is increasingly self-sustaining.

**Rating: 8.5/10**
