# UAMY — Catalyst Map 🎯

**United States Antimony Corporation (UAMY)**
*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## Catalyst 1: NYSE Uplisting (March 11, 2026) ⭐

- **What:** UAMY moves from NYSE American to the main NYSE exchange
- **When:** March 11, 2026 (2 days away)
- **Impact:** +10-20% potential. NYSE listing attracts institutional investors who can't or won't buy NYSE American stocks. Opens the door to index inclusion, ETF buying, and larger fund participation.
- **Probability:** HIGH (already approved and announced March 6)
- **Market expectation:** Partially priced in — stock ran from ~$5 to $9+ anticipating this. But the actual institutional buying wave hasn't started yet.
- **Our edge:** Small-cap uplisting to NYSE with 22.72% short interest creates potential for a squeeze as institutional buyers enter and shorts cover.

## Catalyst 2: $245M DLA Sole-Source Contract Execution (Ongoing through 2026-2027)

- **What:** IDIQ sole-source contract with Defense Logistics Agency for antimony metal ingots to replenish U.S. stockpile. First $10M order announced Sept 30, 2025. This is a multi-year revenue stream.
- **When:** Orders ongoing throughout 2026. Expect $30-50M+ in DLA deliveries in 2026.
- **Impact:** +30-50% if order flow accelerates. This single contract transforms UAMY from a $15M/year company to potentially $50M+ from DLA alone.
- **Probability:** HIGH — contract awarded, first order delivered, sole-source means no competition.
- **Market expectation:** Market expects ~$39M revenue in FY2025, $125M in FY2026 — DLA is a key pillar.
- **Our edge:** Sole-source means pricing power. As China's antimony export ban persists, DLA urgency increases. Additional orders likely accelerate.

## Catalyst 3: $27M Critical Minerals Funding — DoW Initiative (March 5, 2026)

- **What:** Selected for Strategic Antimony Supply Chain Expansion under Department of the Interior/DoW initiative. $27M in funding for supply chain expansion.
- **When:** Announced March 5, 2026. Funds to deploy over 2026-2027.
- **Impact:** +15-25%. Government funding de-risks expansion capex and validates USAC as the go-to domestic antimony processor.
- **Probability:** HIGH (already awarded)
- **Market expectation:** Priced in partially. The bigger impact is what this unlocks — expanded processing capacity = more revenue capacity.
- **Our edge:** This is non-dilutive government funding. It builds capacity without equity raises.

## Catalyst 4: Fostung Tungsten Deposit Technical Report (March 3, 2026)

- **What:** Completed technical report on Fostung Tungsten Deposit in Ontario, Canada. Potentially a $4.6B tungsten resource. Tungsten is another critical mineral facing Chinese supply dominance.
- **When:** Report completed March 3, 2026. Development timeline = 2-4 years for production.
- **Impact:** +50-100% if resource estimate confirms major tonnage. Tungsten adds a second critical mineral leg to the UAMY story.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — early stage. Technical report ≠ production. Permitting, development, and capex ahead.
- **Market expectation:** Market hasn't fully valued tungsten optionality. Current $1.34B market cap is mostly antimony.
- **Our edge:** If Fostung confirms even half of the $4.6B resource value, tungsten alone could be worth more than current market cap. But this is 2-4 year development.

## Catalyst 5: Americas Gold & Silver JV — Hydromet Processing (Feb 10, 2026)

- **What:** New joint venture with Americas Gold & Silver Corporation to build a hydromet processing facility. This diversifies processing capabilities beyond pyrometallurgy.
- **When:** Announced Feb 10, 2026. Facility build-out likely 12-18 months.
- **Impact:** +10-20%. Expands processing capacity and capabilities. Enables processing of different antimony feed types.
- **Probability:** HIGH (JV announced, both parties committed)
- **Market expectation:** Partially priced in from Feb announcement.
- **Our edge:** Hydromet processing opens new antimony feedstock sources that competitors can't process.

## Catalyst 6: China Antimony Export Ban Continuation/Escalation

- **What:** China banned export of antimony (and other critical minerals) to the US in December 2024. China controls ~80% of global antimony supply. Any escalation makes UAMY more critical.
- **When:** Ongoing. Potential escalation in H1-H2 2026 amid US-China trade tensions.
- **Impact:** +25-50% on escalation. UAMY is literally the only significant antimony smelter in the US.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH — China unlikely to reverse ban given geopolitical tensions.
- **Market expectation:** Baked into the thesis but not into a further escalation scenario.
- **Our edge:** Any new China critical minerals restrictions = immediate UAMY re-pricing.

## Catalyst 7: Stibnite Hill Mining Ramp-Up (Montana)

- **What:** Resumed mining at Stibnite Hill in Montana using new "cut and cover" surface mining method (started Oct 2025). High-grade antimony ore — profitable at small scale without major capex.
- **When:** Ongoing ramp-up through 2026. Daily volumes still small (~239 tons to-date as of early 2026).
- **Impact:** +10-15%. Domestic supply reduces import dependence and improves margins (own-mined vs. purchased feedstock).
- **Probability:** HIGH — already operating, proven method.
- **Market expectation:** Not heavily featured in analyst models yet.
- **Our edge:** Vertical integration from mine to finished product. Highest margin antimony in the US.

## Catalyst 8: Critical Minerals Flotation Facility Acquisition (Jan 20, 2026)

- **What:** Acquired a critical minerals flotation facility. Expands milling/processing capacity.
- **When:** Completed Jan 20, 2026. Integration through H1 2026.
- **Impact:** +5-10%. Incremental capacity expansion.
- **Probability:** HIGH (completed)
- **Market expectation:** Factored in.

## Catalyst 9: FY2025 Earnings Report

- **What:** First full year of the DLA contract and China ban tailwinds. Revenue expected ~$39M (up 164% YoY). Still likely unprofitable (EPS -$0.02).
- **When:** Expected March-April 2026 (10-K filing, last year filed Feb 2025)
- **Impact:** +/-15%. Revenue beat matters more than EPS given growth stage.
- **Probability:** HIGH for a revenue beat given Q3 2025 TTM already at $33.1M.
- **Market expectation:** $39.4M revenue consensus.
- **Our edge:** TTM revenue trajectory suggests potential beat.

## Catalyst 10: Russell Index Inclusion (June 2026)

- **What:** With a $1.3B+ market cap and NYSE listing, UAMY may qualify for Russell 2000/3000 index inclusion at the June 2026 reconstitution.
- **When:** Russell preliminary lists in June; additions effective late June 2026.
- **Impact:** +15-30%. Forced index buying from passive funds. Creates sustained demand.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — depends on market cap threshold at measurement date (May).
- **Market expectation:** Not widely discussed yet.
- **Our edge:** Under-the-radar for most investors. Index inclusion could be the biggest catalyst for sustained re-rating.
