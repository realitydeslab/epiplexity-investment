## The Catalyst

UAMY is the **only significant antimony smelter in the United States**, and China just banned antimony exports to the US in December 2024. This created an overnight monopoly for a 62-person company founded in 1968. The US government responded with a $245M sole-source DLA contract and $27M in critical minerals funding. The company uplists to NYSE on March 11, 2026.

## The Trade

**Entry:** $8.50-$9.50 (current range)
**Target:** $12.50 (base), $18.00 (bull with squeeze)
**Stop:** $6.50
**Position size:** 2-3% of portfolio
**Time horizon:** 6-12 months

**Key setup:** 22.72% short float + NYSE uplisting + potential Russell inclusion in June 2026 = technical squeeze potential layered on top of fundamental growth.

Revenue is accelerating at 200%+ YoY. Consensus expects $39M in FY2025 (from $15M in FY2024), jumping to $126M in FY2026. First profitability expected in 2026 (EPS $0.20).

## The Risk

This is a **$1.3B company with 62 employees trading at 40x sales**. The market is pricing in perfect execution of an 8x revenue ramp. Last quarter missed revenue by -32%. Dilution is ongoing (11.45% YoY). If China lifts the antimony ban, or if UAMY stumbles on execution, the stock could easily halve.

The story is bigger than the operation. CEO Evans is aggressively promotional (Bloomberg, FOX appearances) — great for momentum, risky for sustainability.

## The Verdict

**BUY with tight risk management** — but this is a trade, not an investment.
