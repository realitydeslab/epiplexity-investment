## The Antimony Landscape

Antimony is a niche critical mineral market. Global production is ~140,000 metric tons/year, with China controlling ~80% of supply. The December 2024 Chinese export ban reshaped the market overnight.

## Who Benefits from the SAME Catalysts?

Companies that benefit from China critical minerals bans and US onshoring:
1. **MP Materials (MP)** — rare earth onshoring
2. **Albemarle (ALB)** — lithium
3. **Livent/Arcadium (ALTM)** — lithium
4. **Americas Gold & Silver (USAS)** — JV partner with UAMY
5. **BWX Technologies (BWXT)** — nuclear/defense critical minerals
6. **Centrus Energy (LEU)** — nuclear fuel onshoring

## Who LOSES if UAMY Wins?

1. **Chinese antimony exporters** — already blocked, losing US market share permanently
2. **Companies dependent on cheap Chinese antimony** — flame retardant manufacturers, ammunition makers who relied on cheap imports now face higher US-sourced costs
3. **Perpetua Resources (PPTA)** — if UAMY scales fast enough, Perpetua's antimony optionality becomes less urgent to investors

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### Long UAMY / Short PPTA
- **Thesis:** UAMY is producing NOW, PPTA is years away. Both are "US antimony" plays but UAMY has revenue and contracts while PPTA has a mine permit fight.
- **Risk:** PPTA has a larger potential resource.

### Long UAMY / Long USAS
- **Thesis:** Americas Gold & Silver is UAMY's JV partner. If the hydromet facility succeeds, both benefit. USAS is the picks-and-shovels play alongside UAMY.
- **Correlation:** Both benefit from antimony/critical minerals momentum.

### Long UAMY / Long LEU
- **Thesis:** Both are "only US producer" of critical minerals (UAMY = antimony, LEU = HALEU uranium). Both have DLA/DOE government contracts. Same macro thesis: US critical mineral onshoring.
- **Portfolio construction:** Critical minerals basket approach.
