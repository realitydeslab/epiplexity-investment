# UAMY — Leader Profile 👤

**United States Antimony Corporation**
*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## CEO: Gary C. Evans

- **Role:** Chairman & CEO
- **Background:** Texas oil & gas veteran who pivoted to critical minerals. Evans has been building UAMY's profile aggressively since China's antimony export ban in December 2024.
- **Media savvy:** Featured on Bloomberg Television's Businessweek Daily (Feb 10, 2026) and FOX News/FOX Business — unusual for a company this size. Shows aggressive promotional strategy.
- **Key moves under Evans:**
  - Secured $245M DLA sole-source contract (2025)
  - Acquired critical minerals flotation facility (Jan 2026)
  - Announced Americas Gold & Silver JV for hydromet processing (Feb 2026)
  - Completed Fostung tungsten deposit technical report (Mar 2026)
  - Pushed through NYSE uplisting from NYSE American (Mar 2026)
  - Secured $27M DoW critical minerals funding (Mar 2026)
  - Strengthened board with Wall Street expertise

## Insider Activity

- **Insider ownership:** 12.93% — healthy alignment with shareholders
- **Recent insider transactions:** Net positive (+0.56% insider trans metric on Finviz)
- **Board strengthening:** Company has explicitly noted adding "seasoned board members with Wall Street expertise" — signaling preparation for institutional investor engagement post-NYSE uplisting

## Key Hires / Developments

- **Peter Mejstrick:** Consulting geologist leading the Stibnite Hill mining revival in Montana. Developed the innovative "cut and cover" surface mining method.
- **Board additions:** Multiple new board members with capital markets backgrounds — positioning for larger institutional engagement.

## Public Statements & Strategy

Evans has been extremely vocal about:
1. **National security narrative:** Positioning UAMY as essential to US defense supply chain
2. **China dependence risk:** Leveraging the December 2024 China antimony export ban
3. **Vertical integration:** Building mine-to-market capability (Stibnite Hill → smelter → finished products)
4. **Tungsten diversification:** Fostung deposit adds a second critical mineral leg
5. **Responded forcefully** to "inaccurate and misleading Reuters article" (Jan 29) — shows management willing to defend narrative

## Assessment

**Strengths:**
- Aggressive capital allocation — JVs, acquisitions, and mining restarts simultaneously
- Media strategy is world-class for a $1.3B company
- Government relationships strong (DLA, DoW contracts)
- Moving fast on multiple fronts

**Concerns:**
- Company has 62 employees — can they execute on all these initiatives simultaneously?
- No "proven reserves" or "probable reserves" as SEC defines them for zeolite; likely similar situation for Stibnite Hill
- Revenue scaling from $15M to $125M+ requires significant operational buildout
- Shares outstanding growing (11.45% YoY dilution) — will they need to raise more equity?

**Verdict:** Evans is the right CEO for this moment — a promotional, aggressive leader who can attract government contracts and investor attention. But execution risk is real with so many irons in the fire and only 62 employees. The story is bigger than the operation right now.
