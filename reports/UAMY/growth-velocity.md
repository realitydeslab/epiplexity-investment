## Profitability Path

### Gross Margin Trajectory
| Quarter | Gross Margin |
|---------|-------------|
| Q1 2025 | 33.88% |
| Q2 2025 | 26.96% |
| Q3 2025 | 23.14% |

Gross margins improved dramatically from -38.48% in FY2023 to 23.21% in FY2024 and 25.78% TTM. The Q1 2025 margin spike (33.88%) suggests margin expansion is possible as scale improves and higher-margin own-mined ore enters the mix.

### Path to Profitability
- **FY2024:** Net loss -$1.74M (-11.58% profit margin)
- **FY2025E:** EPS -$0.02 (still slightly unprofitable)
- **FY2026E:** EPS +$0.20 (consensus expects profitability!)
- **Forward P/E:** 48.96x on FY2026 estimates
- **Breakeven expected:** H2 2025 or Q1 2026 based on trajectory

### Operating Expense Concern
- SG&A surged to $6.35M in Q3 2025 (from $1.22M/Q in 2024) — hiring/expansion costs
- Total OpEx of $7.87M in Q3 2025 wiped out a strong $2.01M gross profit
- Key question: Can SG&A normalize as a % of revenue? At $126M FY2026 revenue, even at current $25M/yr SG&A run rate, operating leverage would be massive
