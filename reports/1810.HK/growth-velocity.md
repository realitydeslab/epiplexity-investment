### EV Delivery Trajectory: EXPLOSIVE

| Period | Deliveries | YoY / Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| 2024 Full Year | 139,471 | First year of deliveries |
| 2025 Full Year | 411,837 | **+195%** YoY |
| Jan 2026 | 39,002 | +70.3% YoY (but -22% MoM due to SU7 transition) |
| Feb 2026 | ~20,000+ | CNY holiday + SU7 ended production |
| 2026 Target | 550,000 | **+33.6%** YoY |

**Critical Context:** Jan-Feb 2026 numbers are artificially depressed by:
1. First-gen SU7 production ended (cratered to 1,133 units in Jan)
2. Chinese New Year holiday (Feb 15-23)
3. New SU7 hasn't launched yet (April)

YU7 alone delivered 37,869 in Jan = nearly 38K/month run rate from ONE model. Once new SU7 adds back 15-20K/month, run rate jumps to 53-58K/month → 550K+ annual pace achievable.

### Profitability: INFLECTING

| Metric (Q3 2025) | Value | YoY Change |
|-------------------|-------|-----------|
| Net Income | CNY 12.27B | **+129.26%** |
| Net Profit Margin | 10.85% | **+87.39%** improvement |
| EBITDA | CNY 12.96B | **+60.97%** |

**Key Insight:** Operating leverage is kicking in. Revenue growing +22%, expenses growing +22%, but net income growing +129%. This means fixed costs are being absorbed and EV losses are narrowing dramatically.

### Smartphone Business: STABLE + PREMIUM SHIFT

- World's #3 smartphone vendor (behind Apple, Samsung)
- Pushing premium with Xiaomi 17 / 17 Ultra (Leica cameras)
- India: 6X QLED TV growth, 32% premium tablet market share
- Premium strategy = higher ASP = better margins
- **Risk:** Memory chip shortage pushing up component costs (Barclays concern)

### IoT & Lifestyle: STEADY GROWTH

- HyperOS ecosystem: 600M+ monthly active users
- Smart home, wearables, TVs, tablets
- Cross-selling with EV owners (ecosystem lock-in)
