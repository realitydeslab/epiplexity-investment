## Xiaomi's Competitive Moats

1. **Ecosystem Lock-In:** 600M+ HyperOS users across phones, TVs, appliances, cars, wearables. No other EV maker has this depth of user integration.
2. **Price-Performance:** DNA of "flagship specs at mid-range price." Applied to EVs: SU7 at ~¥215K vs. Tesla Model 3 at ~¥250K.
3. **Speed of Execution:** 3 years from EV announcement to 600K deliveries. Faster than any competitor.
4. **Brand Power in China:** Massive consumer mindshare, Lei Jun's personal brand, viral social media presence.
5. **Vertical Integration Path:** Own chips (CNY 50B commitment), own robots, own AI models, own manufacturing.

## Xiaomi's Competitive Weaknesses

1. **No International EV Sales:** China-only for now. Global expansion years away.
2. **Memory Dependency:** Smartphones vulnerable to DRAM/NAND pricing cycles.
3. **EV Not Yet Profitable:** Still investing in EV infrastructure and capacity.
4. **Recall Risk:** 370K SU7 door handle issue unresolved.
5. **Geopolitical:** Chinese company = US/EU sanction risk.
