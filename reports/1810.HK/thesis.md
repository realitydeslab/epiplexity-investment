# Investment Thesis — Xiaomi (1810.HK)
*Epiplexity Investment Research | March 9, 2026*

---

## THE CATALYST

Xiaomi is in a **planned model transition trough** that has driven the stock 45% below its 52-week high. The next-gen SU7 launches in **April 2026**, restoring the dual-model delivery engine (SU7 + YU7) that should push monthly deliveries from ~20-39K back to 55-65K. Simultaneously, the **YU7 GT** (990hp, ~¥500K) launches H1, and the **YU9 EREV SUV** enters China's largest EV segment in H2. All three launches happen against a backdrop of memory chip costs peaking and AI platform monetization beginning.

## THE TRADE

**Buy at HK$32-34** (current levels). Target **HK$42 base case (+25%)** with **HK$55 bull case (+63%)**. Stop-loss at **HK$28 (-17%)**.

P/E of 17.7x for a company growing earnings 129% YoY, with CNY 110B cash, a humanoid robot already deployed in production, and 600M+ AI agent users via HyperOS. This is a hardware multiple for what is becoming a platform company.

## THE RISK

1. **SU7 recall** of 370K units (door handle safety) could cost CNY 1-2B
2. **Memory chip shortage** persists or worsens, crushing smartphone margins
3. **New SU7 launch underwhelms** — weak orders or quality issues
4. **China macro deterioration** reduces consumer spending
5. **Geopolitical escalation** impacts Chinese tech

## THE VERDICT

**BUY. Strong conviction contrarian entry.**

This is one of the most asymmetric setups in the Epiplexity watchlist. Every temporary headwind is priced in simultaneously at the exact moment the company approaches its highest-catalyst quarter (new SU7 launch, earnings, YU7 GT). 

The market assigns zero value to:
- Humanoid robotics (deployed in factory before Tesla Optimus)
- AI agent platform (600M+ users)
- Custom chip initiative (CNY 50B commitment)

Management is buyying back stock. Lei Jun has a proven track record of under-promising and over-delivering. The 550K delivery target is deliberately conservative.

**Position size:** 3-5% of portfolio  
**Time horizon:** 12 months  
**Expected return:** +25% (base), +63% (bull)
