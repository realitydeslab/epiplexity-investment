# Catalyst Map — Xiaomi (1810.HK)
*Epiplexity Investment Research | March 9, 2026*

---

## 1. Next-Gen SU7 Sedan Launch (April 2026)
- **What:** Updated SU7 with significant smart driving & hardware upgrades. First-gen SU7 ended production Feb 2026 (381K+ cumulative units). Pre-orders started Jan 7.
- **When:** April 2026
- **Impact:** +10-15%. The SU7 was ~63% of 2025 deliveries. Current sales cratered to 1,133 units in Jan (model transition). Re-launch restores dual-model delivery volume.
- **Probability:** High (already in pre-production, confirmed by Lei Jun)
- **Market Expectation:** Priced in at base case. Market worried about transition gap.
- **Our Edge:** The transition dip creates an artificially low Q1 delivery number. Q2-Q3 snapback will look like dramatic acceleration. Market may under-price the ramp speed given Xiaomi's history (SU7 went 0→140K in 9 months).

## 2. YU7 GT Performance SUV Launch (H1 2026)
- **What:** 990hp dual-motor SUV, 300km/h top speed, 0-100 in ~2 seconds. Appeared in MIIT regulatory catalog Feb 2026. Pricing ~¥450-500K ($65-73K).
- **When:** H1 2026 (likely May-June)
- **Impact:** +5-8%. Opens the premium performance segment. Higher ASP = better margins.
- **Probability:** High (regulatory filing complete)
- **Market Expectation:** Some awareness but not fully modeled into estimates.
- **Our Edge:** YU7 GT competes directly with Tesla Model X Performance and BMW iX M60 at much lower prices. Performance halo effect lifts entire brand perception.

## 3. YU9 Full-Size Extended-Range SUV (H2 2026)
- **What:** Xiaomi's first extended-range vehicle (EREV). 7-seat SUV codenamed "Kunlun," 70+ kWh battery, 400-500km pure electric range. Competes with Li Auto L9.
- **When:** H2 2026
- **Impact:** +8-12%. EREV addresses range anxiety — TAM expansion into a massive segment Li Auto proved profitable.
- **Probability:** Medium-High (spy shots circulating, part of confirmed 4-model 2026 roadmap)
- **Market Expectation:** Not priced in. Analysts focused on BEV portfolio only.
- **Our Edge:** EREV is the fastest-growing EV segment in China. Li Auto proved 20%+ margins. Xiaomi entering this segment is a potential margin inflection.

## 4. Q4 2025 Earnings Release (Late March 2026)
- **What:** Full-year 2025 results. Q3 showed CNY 113.12B revenue (+22.3% YoY), net income CNY 12.27B (+129% YoY). Q4 should show 50,212 Dec delivery record.
- **When:** Late March 2026 (typically ~Mar 18-25)
- **Impact:** +5-10% on beat, -5-8% on miss. Key metrics: EV segment gross margin, smartphone ASP trend, 2026 guidance.
- **Probability:** Medium-High for beat (Q4 typically strong, Dec was record EV month)
- **Market Expectation:** Consensus expects continued growth but worries about memory chip cost headwinds.
- **Our Edge:** EV deliveries ramped aggressively in Q4 (Oct: ~43K, Nov: ~47K, Dec: 50.2K). Even with memory costs, operating leverage should surprise.

## 5. "Xiaomi miclaw" AI Agent Platform Expansion
- **What:** Xiaomi released mobile AI agent product "Xiaomi miclaw" (March 6, 2026). Stock rose on release. Integrates across HyperOS ecosystem — phones, TVs, cars, IoT devices.
- **When:** Ongoing rollout through 2026
- **Impact:** +3-5% per major milestone. Re-rates Xiaomi from hardware to AI platform company.
- **Probability:** Medium (product launched, execution key)
- **Market Expectation:** Low expectations. Market treats Xiaomi as hardware company.
- **Our Edge:** Xiaomi has 600M+ monthly HyperOS active users — one of the largest captive AI distribution platforms globally. AI monetization of this base is wildly undervalued.

## 6. Humanoid Robot Factory Deployment Scale-Up
- **What:** Xiaomi deployed humanoid robots in EV factory (March 2, 2026). Using VLA model "Xiaomi-Robotics-0" (4.7B parameters). 90.2% success rate, met 76-second cycle time.
- **When:** Scaling through 2026. Lei Jun: "large numbers in factories within 5 years"
- **Impact:** +5-10% on major milestones. Narrative re-rating from EV maker to robotics/AI company.
- **Probability:** Medium (early stage, but real factory deployment — ahead of Tesla Optimus in actual production use)
- **Market Expectation:** Zero. No analysts model robotics value.
- **Our Edge:** Xiaomi has actual production robot deployment before Tesla. Market cap gives zero value to this optionality worth potentially tens of billions.

## 7. Global EV Expansion (Europe Entry)
- **What:** Vision GT supercar concept unveiled at MWC Barcelona (Feb 28). First Chinese brand in Gran Turismo project. Signals European market entry preparation.
- **When:** Announcements through 2026, likely sales start 2027
- **Impact:** +5-8% on formal market entry announcement. TAM multiplier.
- **Probability:** Medium (concept phase, regulatory pathway unclear)
- **Market Expectation:** Not priced in for 2026. Market assumes China-only.
- **Our Edge:** Xiaomi's MWC presence and brand partnerships suggest 2027 Europe launch. Early announcement could re-rate.

## 8. HK$2.5B Share Buyback Execution
- **What:** Announced Jan 23, 2026. Already bought back shares in Jan. Signals management confidence.
- **When:** Ongoing through 2026
- **Impact:** +2-3% floor support. EPS accretion.
- **Probability:** High (already executing)
- **Market Expectation:** Partially priced in.
- **Our Edge:** Buyback at current 52-week low levels is extremely accretive. Management buying their own stock at the bottom.

## 9. Memory Chip Shortage Resolution / Pricing Peak
- **What:** Memory (DRAM/NAND) shortage driving up smartphone component costs. Barclays cut PT to HK$30 on this concern.
- **When:** H2 2026 expected easing
- **Impact:** +8-12% if costs normalize. Margin expansion story.
- **Probability:** Medium (supply additions from Samsung/SK Hynix expected H2)
- **Market Expectation:** Consensus bearish on memory costs through 2026.
- **Our Edge:** Memory shortages tend to be cyclical. Peak pessimism = best entry. When costs normalize, margin snapback will be sharp.

## 10. 550,000 Vehicle Delivery Target Achievement
- **What:** 2026 target of 550K deliveries vs. 411,837 in 2025 (+34% growth).
- **When:** Full year 2026, tracked monthly
- **Impact:** Each monthly beat: +2-3%. Full year beat: +10-15%.
- **Probability:** Medium. Citi noted market "disappointed" by "modest" 550K target.
- **Market Expectation:** Market thinks 550K is conservative (bullish) but worried about model transition gap (bearish). Net: skeptical.
- **Our Edge:** Jan (39K) + Feb (~20K) = ~59K = 10.7% of target in 2 months with SU7 basically at zero. Once new SU7 ramps in April, monthly run rate should jump to 55-65K. Target achievable and likely beatable.
