# Leader Profile — Xiaomi (1810.HK)
*Epiplexity Investment Research | March 9, 2026*

---

## Lei Jun — Founder, Chairman & CEO

### Background
- **Age:** ~56 (born 1969)
- **Education:** Wuhan University, Computer Science
- **Track record:**
  - Founded Xiaomi in 2010 at age 40 — "late bloomer" founder
  - Previously CEO of Kingsoft (Chinese software company)
  - Co-founded Joyo.com, sold to Amazon for $75M in 2004
  - Angel investor in numerous Chinese tech startups
  - Called "China's Steve Jobs" — known for product obsession and showmanship

### Leadership Assessment: 9/10

**Strengths:**
- **Visionary product sense.** Took Xiaomi from zero to world's #3 smartphone brand. Then pivoted to EVs — delivered first car 3 years after announcement, reaching 600K cumulative deliveries in 22 months. This speed is unprecedented.
- **Execution machine.** SU7 went from launch to 140K deliveries in 9 months. YU7 became China's #1 selling car by retail in January 2026.
- **Personally drives the narrative.** Lei Jun is Xiaomi's chief product officer AND chief marketing officer. His livestreams generate massive buzz. He personally announced the 600K delivery milestone, humanoid robot deployment, and Vision GT.
- **Long-term thinker.** Committed CNY 50B over 10 years to chip development. Invested in humanoid robotics. Built AI agent platform. These are 5-10 year bets made from a position of strength.
- **Frugal culture.** Xiaomi's DNA is "same quality, lower price." This discipline enables competitive pricing while maintaining margins.

**Risks:**
- **Spreading too thin.** Smartphones + IoT + EVs + AI + chips + robotics is an enormous scope. Execution risk is real.
- **Single leader dependency.** Lei Jun IS Xiaomi. Succession planning unclear.
- **China regulatory risk.** As CEO of a major Chinese tech company, subject to political winds.

### Insider Activity

**Share Buyback:**
- HK$2.5B buyback program announced Jan 23, 2026
- Already executing: HK$152M purchased on Jan 13 alone (4M shares)
- Regular buyback pattern in recent years — management consistently buys dips
- **Signal:** Management confident stock is undervalued at current levels

**Lei Jun Personal Holdings:**
- Remains one of Xiaomi's largest individual shareholders
- No reported insider selling
- Historically has increased stake during downturns

### Key Hires / Departures (Last 6 Months)

- **EV Division:** No major departures reported. Team appears stable. Li Tianyuan (EV chief designer) actively prominent at MWC Vision GT unveiling.
- **AI/Robotics:** Expanded team for Xiaomi-Robotics-0 VLA model development. Hiring aggressively in embodied AI.
- **Semiconductor Division:** Committed CNY 50B over 10 years starting 2025. Building internal chip team — strategic long-term play to reduce DRAM/component dependency.
- **India:** Sudhin Mathur (COO Xiaomi India) actively pushing premium strategy. Team stability in key market.

### Public Statements on Near-Term Outlook

- **Jan 3, 2026 (livestream):** Set 550K vehicle delivery target for 2026. Tone was confident but measured.
- **Feb 10, 2026 (Weibo):** Announced end of first-gen SU7 production. Framed as evolution, not retreat.
- **Feb 13, 2026:** Celebrated 600K cumulative EV delivery milestone.
- **Mar 2, 2026 (WeChat):** Predicted "large numbers of humanoid robots in Xiaomi factories within 5 years."
- **General tone:** Optimistic but realistic. Not over-promising. Has a track record of under-promising and over-delivering (SU7 initial target was 100K, delivered 140K in 2024).

### Verdict

Lei Jun is one of the best operators in Chinese tech. His execution on the EV pivot alone — from announcement to 600K deliveries in under 5 years — is extraordinary. He's now making parallel bets on AI agents, humanoid robotics, and custom chips that could each be worth more than the EV business long-term. The buyback signals conviction. The risk is scope creep, but Lei Jun has consistently proven skeptics wrong.
