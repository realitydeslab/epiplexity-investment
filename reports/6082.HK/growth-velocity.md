### Revenue Context
- HK$404M revenue for a company with HK$76.7B market cap = **~190x Price/Sales**
- This is extreme, even for a speculative semiconductor play
- Revenue is coming from BR100/BR104 sales to state-owned computing centers (China Mobile, China Telecom)
- The modified BR100 (de-tuned to comply with pre-Oct-2023 sanctions thresholds) is the primary commercial product

### Is Growth Accelerating?
- **Pre-IPO disclosure limited** — no quarterly breakdown publicly available yet
- FY2025 full results (expected Mar-Apr 2026) will be the first real look
- Revenue of ~US$52M suggests early-stage commercialization
- For comparison: 
  - Huawei's Ascend chip division is estimated at several billion USD
  - Moore Threads (private) is estimated at <$100M revenue
  - Biren is in the same league as Moore Threads but smaller than Huawei

### Customer Pipeline
- **Confirmed customers:** China Mobile, China Telecom intelligent computing centers
- **Probable pipeline:** Government AI projects mandated to use domestic chips, universities, research institutes
- **Growth driver:** China's "compute power plan" — building 10+ national AI compute hubs with domestic chips

### Path to Profitability
- **Current state:** Massively unprofitable (net margin -631%)
- **Cash position:** Strong post-IPO (~HK$5.58B raised + existing cash)
- **Burn rate:** ~HK$1B+ per year based on EBITDA losses
- **Path to breakeven:** 3-5+ years minimum. Requires:
  1. Viable manufacturing (SMIC or alternative foundry)
  2. Next-gen chip (BR200) to command higher ASPs
  3. Government procurement at scale
  4. Software ecosystem development (CUDA alternative)
- **Profitability estimate:** Not before 2029-2030 at earliest

### Unit Economics
- Gross margin of 47.72% is actually **decent for a fabless semiconductor company** — suggests the chips themselves are viable products
- The problem is scale (low revenue) and massive R&D/SG&A overhead
- If revenue can scale 3-5x while maintaining margins, the path to operating leverage exists

### Guidance vs. Actuals
- No public guidance as newly listed company
- FY2025 results will establish the baseline

### Growth Velocity Verdict
⚠️ **Too early to call.** Revenue of HK$404M is tiny relative to valuation. Growth rate unknown until FY2025 disclosure. The 47.72% gross margin is a positive signal that the product works commercially. But this is a pre-revenue bet on China's AI self-sufficiency, not a growth story you can model with confidence.

**Growth Velocity Score: 3/10** — Insufficient data, pre-scale revenue, no quarterly trajectory visible.
