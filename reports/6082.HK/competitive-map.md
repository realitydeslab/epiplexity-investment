*Research Date: 2026-03-09*

## China Domestic AI GPU Landscape

### Direct Competitors (China AI Chips)

| Company | Ticker | Product | Advantage | Disadvantage |
|---------|--------|---------|-----------|--------------|
| **Huawei Ascend** | Private (within Huawei) | Ascend 910B/910C/950 | Largest ecosystem, CANN software stack, government backing | US sanctions, SMIC-dependent, not publicly investable separately |
| **Moore Threads** | Private | MTT S4000 | Strong on inference, growing enterprise adoption | Private, smaller scale than Biren |
| **Lisuan Tech** | Private | — | Focused on RISC-V-based AI accelerators | Very early stage |
| **Cambricon** | 688256.SS | MLU370/590 | Listed on Shanghai STAR, government AI compute wins | Smaller revenue, narrow product line |
| **Iluvatar CoreX** | Private | BI-V150 | GPU + inference accelerator | Limited commercial deployment |

### Key Insight: Huawei is the 800-lb Gorilla
- Huawei Ascend is the dominant domestic AI chip with **90%+ of China's government AI compute orders**
- DeepSeek trained on Huawei Ascend chips, not Biren
- Biren is fighting for the remaining 10-20% alongside Moore Threads and Cambricon
- **Biren's differentiation:** General-purpose GPU architecture (closer to NVIDIA's model) vs. Huawei's ASIC-style accelerator

### International Comparison

| Company | Ticker | Why They Matter |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| **NVIDIA** | NVDA | The benchmark. Every China chip company is trying to replicate what NVIDIA does. US sanctions create the demand for Biren's existence. |
| **AMD** | AMD | MI300X as inference alternative. If AMD can sell (legally) into China, Biren loses market share. |
| **SMIC** | 0981.HK | Biren's most likely manufacturing partner. SMIC's 3nm tape-out in 2026 is directly enabling for Biren's next-gen chip. |

- **Huawei Ascend** (partially) — zero-sum competition for government orders within China

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### Long Biren / Short SenseTime
- Both are speculative China AI plays with massive losses
- Biren has hardware (harder to replicate), SenseTime has software (easier to displace)
- If you're bullish on China AI infra, hardware > software

### Long SMIC / Express View on Biren
- SMIC benefits from Biren's success without the single-company risk
- SMIC serves all domestic chip designers, not just Biren
- Better risk-adjusted way to play the China AI chip thesis

### Basket Trade: China AI Chip Stack
- Long: SMIC (0981.HK) + Biren (6082.HK) + Alibaba (9988.HK)
- This gives foundry + chip designer + AI platform exposure
- Hedged against single-company risk

## Competitive Verdict
Biren is a **second-tier player** in China's AI chip market, behind Huawei by a wide margin. Its value proposition is being a general-purpose GPU alternative (closer to NVIDIA architecture) vs. Huawei's more specialized approach. The best way to play the China AI chip thesis is through **SMIC** (the pick-and-shovel play) rather than betting on which chip designer wins.
