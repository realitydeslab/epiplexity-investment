## The Risk

1. **AI execution failure** — Hunyuan stays behind Doubao/Qwen. Yuanbao retention (30.1%) doesn't improve. WeChat AI agent delayed.
2. **China regulation** — New VAT on internet firms. Gaming restrictions return.
3. **Macro** — China GDP slowdown, property crisis spillover, US-China tensions.
4. **Competition** — ByteDance's Doubao entrenches as the default AI app in China.

## The Verdict

**BUY — Contrarian entry on sentiment dislocation.**

Tencent is trading at maximum narrative pessimism ("AI laggard," -$173B rout) while fundamentals are at maximum strength (record profits, 14% revenue growth, HK$80B+ annual buybacks, 10-year low share count). Goldman Sachs target HK$770 implies +48% upside.

The key insight: Tencent doesn't need to win the standalone AI chatbot race. It needs to make AI native to WeChat — the most used app on Earth. That's a distribution advantage no competitor can match. When the narrative shifts from "Tencent is behind in AI" to "Tencent is putting AI in front of 1.3 billion people," the re-rating will be significant.
