## Capital Expenditure

- **Q1 2025 Capex:** RMB23B (mainly AI infrastructure)
- **Trend:** Significantly ramping AI investment — data centers, GPU procurement (in talks with Samsung/SK Hynix)
- **Assessment:** Capex increase is necessary and well-funded from strong cash generation

## Path to Higher Growth

The key question is whether AI can reaccelerate Tencent from 13-15% revenue growth to 20%+. The levers:
1. **AI-enhanced ad targeting** — already contributing, further upside from Video Accounts
2. **WeChat AI agent platform** — potentially opens entirely new revenue streams
3. **Mini Program AI ecosystem** — lower Apple commissions (15%) enable new monetization
4. **Cloud AI** — enterprise adoption of Hunyuan
5. **Gaming AI** — extending lifecycle of existing titles + AI-native new titles

## Growth Velocity Score: 7.5/10

Strong and steady growth for a mega-cap, with acceleration from 8% (2024) to 14%+ (2025). AI has potential to be incremental but Tencent is 3rd in China's AI chatbot race.
