## Direct Competitors

### ByteDance (Private)
- **Advantage:** Doubao is dominant in consumer AI. Volcano Engine winning cloud AI contracts.
- **Weakness:** Not publicly traded. No gaming revenue comparable to Tencent.
- **Tencent's edge:** WeChat ecosystem lock-in, gaming moat, public market access

### Alibaba (9988.HK / BABA)
- **Advantage:** Qwen is leading open-source LLM. Alibaba Cloud is #1 in China.
- **Weakness:** E-commerce core under pressure. Qwen retention is worst among major apps.
- **Tencent's edge:** Higher user engagement via social + gaming. Better retention metrics.

### Baidu (BIDU / 9888.HK)
- **Advantage:** First-mover with Ernie Bot. Apollo autonomous driving.
- **Weakness:** Core search business declining. AI chatbot losing market share.
- **Tencent's edge:** Diversified revenue, much larger ecosystem.

## Who Loses if Tencent Wins?

- **ByteDance:** If WeChat AI agent succeeds, Doubao's standalone app strategy is challenged
- **Baidu:** Further AI market share loss
- **Independent AI startups:** WeChat platform play could crowd them out

## Pair Trade Opportunities

1. **Long Tencent / Short Baidu:** Tencent has broader moat; Baidu is losing relevance
2. **Long Tencent / Long Alibaba:** Both benefit from China AI rerating, but different risk profiles
3. **Long Tencent vs. Long Meta:** Pure "China AI discount" convergence trade

## Competitive Position Score: 8/10

Dominant ecosystem, proven monetization, but trailing in AI chatbot race. The distribution advantage (WeChat 1.3B MAU) is the key differentiator that could flip the narrative.
