# Learnings & Intelligence — RENK Group AG (RENK.DE)

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## What This Tells Us About the Sector

1. **European defense is a structural multi-year supercycle, not a trade.** €108B German defense budget, NATO 3.5% GDP target, and €500B Sondervermögen mean 5-10 years of elevated spending. This isn't a cyclical blip.

2. **Supply chain bottlenecks = pricing power.** Renk's monopoly on NATO heavy vehicle transmissions means it sets prices. Order backlogs at 5x revenue prove demand exceeds supply.

3. **The "boring" defense suppliers outperform.** While markets obsess over jets and missiles, the drivetrain/propulsion layer (Renk) and ammunition/vehicles layer (Rheinmetall) deliver the highest growth because they're the actual bottleneck.

4. **KNDS IPO will re-rate the entire European defense chain.** A €20B KNDS valuation creates a new benchmark that makes every supplier look cheap.

## Contrarian Angle — What's the Market Missing?

1. **RENK America is being valued at zero.** RAM posted >$550M order intake but the market prices Renk as a European story. US defense exposure deserves separate valuation.

2. **The €200M deferral is actually bullish.** Market sold off 6% on March 5 for "weak Q4 orders" — but these orders are confirmed for 2026. The dip is a gift.

3. **Renk's monopoly position means it can raise prices.** Defense inflation is real. Renk doesn't need volume growth if it can push through 5-10% annual price increases on captive customers.

4. **Marine & Industry segment hiding in plain sight.** Naval spending is accelerating globally (submarine programs, frigate modernization). M&I grew 15.3% and margins expanded to 11.9% — this segment could inflect higher.

## Risk Factors to Monitor

1. **German export restrictions** — CEO flagged uncertainty. If Scholz/Merz government restricts Leopard component exports, it directly hits Renk.
2. **EUR/USD movements** — RAM revenues in USD, reported in EUR. Strong EUR hurts.
3. **Defense budget delays** — Political changes could slow procurement.
4. **Multiple compression** — Defense sector is consensus long. Any rotation out of defense hits all names.
5. **Industrial segment weakness** — Slide Bearings growing only 2.5%. If industrial recession deepens, drag increases.
