# Leader Profile — RENK Group AG (RENK.DE)

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## CEO: Dr. Alexander Sagel

- **Background:** Former COO of Renk Group. Appointed CEO after Susanne Wiegand stepped down (personal reasons) in late 2024/early 2025.
- **Style:** Operational executive — deep knowledge of manufacturing, production scaling, and defense logistics. Not a flashy communicator but an executor.
- **Key quote (March 5, 2026):** "Our strategy of placing the focus firmly on defense technologies is paying off – we are seeing the highest revenue, order intake and order backlog in company history."
- **Iran/ME statement (CNBC March 5):** Explicitly flagged Iran conflict as potential demand driver for Middle East defense exports — shows strategic awareness of geopolitical optionality.
- **Assessment:** Right person for this phase. Renk needs operational scaling (modular production in Augsburg, Poland expansion, RAM growth) more than it needs a visionary. Sagel is a production/ops guy delivering on execution.

## CFO: Anja Mänz-Siebje

- Highlighted US tariffs, weak industrial performance, export embargoes, and FX effects as headwinds that were overcome
- Proposed 38% dividend increase — signals confidence in cash generation
- "Impressive proof of our successful performance on the capital market" — MDAX inclusion narrative

## Insider Activity

- **KNDS** (25.1% holder): Increased stake from 6.7% to 25.1% in February 2025, paying above IPO price of €15/share. This is the strongest possible insider signal — Renk's largest customer betting hundreds of millions on the stock.
- **Mixed insider transactions:** Some selling in May 2025 (likely post-lockup), buying in November 2025 and late 2024. Pattern shows buying on dips.
- **Key signal:** KNDS's 25.1% stake is effectively a massive insider buy from the company's most important customer. No better alignment exists.

## Key Personnel Changes

- CEO transition from Wiegand to Sagel was smooth, no operational disruption
- No other significant departures flagged

## Board/Governance

- German AG governance structure (Executive Board + Supervisory Board)
- KNDS representation on supervisory board provides customer-board alignment
- IPO was February 2024 — still relatively new as public company

## Assessment

**Score: 8/10**

Strong operational leadership at exactly the right moment. KNDS's massive stake provides unique customer-owner alignment that no peer has. The CEO transition was clean. Main risk: Sagel hasn't been tested through a downturn as CEO.

*Sources: Renk press release March 5, 2026; CNBC March 5, 2026; TradingView/Reuters Feb 2025; InsiderScreener*
