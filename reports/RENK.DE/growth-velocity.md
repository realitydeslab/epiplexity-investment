# Growth Velocity — RENK Group AG (RENK.DE)

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## Revenue Growth — ACCELERATING

| Period | Revenue (€M) | YoY Growth |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| FY2023 | ~€950 (est.) | — |
| FY2024 | €1,141 | ~20% |
| FY2025 | €1,366 | +19.8% |
| FY2026E (guidance) | >€1,500 | ~10%+ |

- Growth is **sustaining at ~20% YoY** — remarkable for an industrial manufacturer
- Defense revenue growing faster at **+24% YoY** (€1.02B, 74% of total)
- FY2026 guidance of >€1.5B implies ~10% floor growth — management historically conservative (beats guidance)

## Segment Growth — VMS Leading

| Segment | FY2025 Rev | Growth | EBIT Margin |
|---------|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Vehicle Mobility Solutions | €872M | +24.8% | 20.4% |
| Marine & Industry | €380M | +15.3% | 11.9% |
| Slide Bearings | €128M | +2.5% | 17.9% |

- **VMS is the engine** — 64% of revenue, highest growth, highest margins
- M&I marine business strong, offsetting weak industrial demand
- Slide Bearings resilient despite "very weak industry environment"

## Order Momentum — STRONG

| Metric | FY2024 | FY2025 | Change |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Order Intake | €1,442M | €1,571M | +9.0% |
| Order Backlog | €4,960M | €6,680M | +34.7% |
| Book-to-Bill | 1.3x | 1.2x | — |
| RAM Order Intake | — | >$550M | — |

- **€6.68B backlog = ~4.9x annual revenue** — extraordinary visibility
- B2B >1.0x means backlog still growing
- €200M of orders deferred to 2026 — would have been 1.4x without deferral
- 45.2% jump in defense order intake (9M 2025 vs 9M 2024 per Simply Wall St)

## Profitability — EXPANDING

| Metric | FY2024 | FY2025 | Change |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Adj. EBIT | €189M | €230M | +21.7% |
| Adj. EBIT Margin | 16.6% | 16.9% | +0.3pp |
| FY2026E EBIT | — | €255-285M | +11-24% |

- EBIT growing **faster than revenue** — positive operating leverage
- Margins expanding despite headwinds (tariffs, FX, export restrictions)
- VMS segment at 20.4% margin — best-in-class for defense drivetrain

## Guidance vs. Actuals

- FY2025 EBIT came at "upper end of forecast range" — management under-promises, over-delivers
- FY2026 EBIT guidance midpoint (~€270M) is ~2% below analyst consensus — conservative again
- Pattern: expect beat-and-raise quarters

## Path to Higher Margins

- Modular production concept in Augsburg fully operational Q3 2025 — "initial efficiency gains" already visible
- Poland facilities will lower cost basis for Eastern European orders
- Defense mix shift (74% → likely 78%+) increases overall margin (VMS has highest margins)

## Growth Velocity Assessment

**Verdict: ACCELERATING fundamentals, temporarily conservative guidance**

- Revenue growth sustaining ~20% for an industrial company is exceptional
- Backlog growing faster than revenue (+35% vs +20%) = acceleration ahead
- Margins expanding = positive operating leverage proven
- The "deceleration" to ~10% in FY2026 guidance is management conservatism, not real slowdown

**Growth Score: 9/10**

*Sources: Renk FY2025 press release; Reuters; Capital.com; Simply Wall St*
