# Investment Thesis — RENK Group AG (RENK.DE)

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

---

## The Catalyst

**RENK is a monopoly supplier of military drivetrain systems riding the largest European defense spending wave since the Cold War, with a KNDS IPO catalyst in summer 2026 that will force institutional revaluation of the entire supply chain.**

## The Trade

- **Entry:** €62.19 (current) or scale-in on pullback to €55-58
- **12-month base target:** €72 (+16%)
- **12-month bull target:** €85 (+37%)
- **Stop-loss:** €52 (-16%)
- **Position size:** 3-5% of portfolio

## The Edge

1. **Monopoly moat** — sole manufacturer of Leopard 2 transmissions, no substitute exists
2. **€6.68B backlog** — 4.9x annual revenue, multi-year visibility unmatched in sector
3. **KNDS catalyst** — customer owns 25.1%, IPO at ~€20B creates revaluation benchmark
4. **Hidden US growth** — RENK America ($550M+ orders) priced at zero by European-focused analysts
5. **Management under-promises** — FY2025 at "upper end," FY2026 guidance 2% below consensus = beat setup

## The Risk

1. **German export restrictions** — CEO flagged uncertainty, could restrict Leopard component exports
2. **Crowded trade** — European defense is consensus long, vulnerable to rotation
3. **Near ATH entry** — €62 is near 52-week high of €62.25
4. **Single customer concentration** — KNDS/Bundeswehr are dominant customers
5. **Currency** — EUR/USD fluctuations impact RAM earnings translation

## The Verdict

**BUY — High Conviction**

RENK is a rare monopoly business in a structurally growing market with a defined near-term catalyst (KNDS IPO). The €6.68B backlog provides unparalleled earnings visibility. Management's conservative guidance creates a beat-and-raise setup. The 6% March 5 selloff on order deferrals (not cancellations) was irrational and partially reversed.

Key question: Is the defense supercycle priced in? At ~27x EBIT with 20%+ growth and monopoly pricing power, we'd argue no — this would be 40x+ in the US market. European discount = opportunity.
