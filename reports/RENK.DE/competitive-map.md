# Competitive Map — RENK Group AG (RENK.DE)

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## Direct Competitors

### Drivetrain / Transmission
- **RENK has near-monopoly** on heavy military vehicle transmissions in NATO
- No direct Western competitor manufactures equivalent high-torque military transmissions
- **L3Harris (LHX):** Some power/drivetrain but not at Renk's scale for heavy armor
- **Allison Transmission (ALSN):** US military transmissions, but different platforms. More of a complementary player than competitor.
- **Chinese alternatives:** NORINCO produces own drivetrain for Chinese tanks, but not a market competitor

### Who Loses if Renk Wins?
- **Non-Western defense suppliers** — as NATO countries buy Western, Russian/Chinese equipment loses market share
- **Industrial conglomerates with defense sidelines** — Renk's pure-play defense focus attracts premium multiples away from diversified names

## Renk's Competitive Moat

1. **Sole-source supplier:** Only maker of Leopard 2 transmissions. Customers cannot switch.
2. **Installed base lock-in:** Every Leopard 2, many Puma IFVs, and naval vessels need Renk parts for maintenance. Recurring revenue.
3. **Decades of qualification:** Military drivetrain qualification takes 5-10+ years. No new entrant can replicate quickly.
4. **KNDS ownership:** 25.1% owner is also largest customer. Perfect alignment.
5. **MDAX membership:** Index inclusion provides passive buying floor.
