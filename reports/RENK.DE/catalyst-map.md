# Catalyst Map — RENK Group AG (RENK.DE / R3NK)

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## 1. KNDS IPO & German Government Stake (Summer 2026)

- **What:** KNDS (Krauss-Maffei Wegmann / Nexter — Leopard 2 maker) expected to IPO summer 2026. German government acquiring 25.1% Sperrminorität (blocking minority) before listing. KNDS already holds 25.1% of Renk.
- **When:** Summer 2026 (June-July estimated)
- **Impact:** +15-25%. KNDS IPO at ~€20B valuation creates a reference benchmark that makes Renk look cheap. Government stake = long-term program certainty for Leopard 2/MGCS.
- **Probability:** High
- **Market expectation:** Partially priced. Market knows KNDS is coming but hasn't fully modeled the valuation re-rating.
- **Our edge:** KNDS IPO forces institutional revaluation of the entire European defense supply chain. Renk as 25.1% KNDS-owned critical supplier becomes indirect play on €20B KNDS.

## 2. €200M+ Deferred Orders Converted (H1 2026)

- **What:** ~€200M of defense orders from naval and R&D programs deferred from Q4 2025 into 2026. CEO confirmed "these programmes are not lost; they are shifted."
- **When:** H1 2026
- **Impact:** +5-8%.
- **Probability:** High
- **Market expectation:** Analysts aware but flagging as overhang. Conversion removes bear talking point.
- **Our edge:** Market punished stock 6% on March 5 partly for this deferral. When orders land, positive catalyst on depressed expectations.

## 3. Germany €108B+ Defense Budget & European Rearmament

- **What:** Germany's approved 2026 defense budget ~€108B. European NATO push toward 3.5% GDP. €500B+ Sondervermögen for infrastructure.
- **When:** Ongoing through 2026, major procurement decisions Q2-Q3
- **Impact:** +10-20% over 12 months
- **Probability:** Very High — already legislated, bipartisan support
- **Market expectation:** Partially priced. Market underestimates multi-year duration and acceleration.
- **Our edge:** Renk is the ONLY manufacturer of transmissions for Leopard 2 and many NATO platforms. No competition = pricing power.

## 4. Poland Expansion & New Facilities

- **What:** Renk opening new production/service facilities in Poland. Poland is Europe's fastest-growing defense spender (~4% GDP).
- **When:** 2026 buildout and initial operations
- **Impact:** +5-10%
- **Probability:** High — already announced
- **Our edge:** Poland's €45B+ Leopard acquisition program feeds directly into Renk transmissions.

## 5. RENK America (RAM) — $550M+ Order Intake

- **What:** RAM posted >$550M order intake in 2025. US defense spending elevated.
- **When:** Ongoing, major US defense budget decisions Q2-Q3
- **Impact:** +5-10%
- **Probability:** Medium-High
- **Our edge:** RAM growing >25% creates second growth engine; most analysts still view Renk as European pure-play.

## 6. Iran/Middle East Conflict Demand

- **What:** CEO explicitly noted Iran war could drive "increasing demand" in Middle East.
- **When:** Unpredictable, elevated risk through 2026
- **Impact:** +5-15% on escalation
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Our edge:** Renk drivetrain systems already deployed by ME militaries. Not priced.

## 7. MDAX Momentum / Potential DAX Promotion

- **What:** Renk joined MDAX March 2025. Potential DAX promotion if market cap grows.
- **When:** H2 2026 index review
- **Impact:** +3-5% from passive flows
- **Probability:** Medium

## 8. Dividend Growth (+38% YoY)

- **What:** Proposed €0.58/share (+38% YoY), AGM June 10, 2026.
- **When:** June 10, 2026
- **Impact:** +2-3%
- **Probability:** Very High
