# Competitive Map — SMIC (0981.HK) 🗺️

*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## SMIC's Position

**World's #3 foundry by revenue** (behind TSMC and Samsung, as of Q3 2025). China's #1 foundry by a wide margin. Only Chinese foundry capable of 7nm and 5nm production.

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### Pair 1: Long SMIC / Short GlobalFoundries
- **Thesis:** SMIC gains share at mature nodes through aggressive pricing (28nm down 40%). GFS's mature-node business is commoditizing.
- **Risk:** GFS has US/European customers with regulatory preference for non-China manufacturing.

### Pair 2: Long SMIC / Short Samsung (Foundry Exposure)
- **Thesis:** Both are foundry underdogs to TSMC, but SMIC has a captive domestic market (90% China) while Samsung's foundry division is losing money and customers.
- **Risk:** Samsung is a conglomerate — foundry is a small part. Pair is imprecise.

### Pair 3: Long SMIC + Long AMEC (Package Deal)
- **Thesis:** Pairs the foundry with its domestic equipment supplier. Both benefit from China chip self-sufficiency. AMEC's 5nm etching equipment is becoming SMIC's critical enabler.
- **Risk:** Both exposed to same sanctions risk.

### Pair 4: Long SMIC / Long Hua Hong (China Foundry Basket)
- **Thesis:** #1 and #2 Chinese foundries, both benefiting from consolidation wave and localization demand. Reduces single-stock risk.
- **Risk:** Hua Hong is smaller and less advanced. More of a mature-node play.

## Competitive Moats

**SMIC's moats (unusual for a foundry):**
1. **Sanctions-created captive market** — Chinese fabless companies MUST use SMIC for advanced nodes. There's literally no alternative within China. This is a "negative moat" turned positive.
2. **Government backing** — Unlimited state funding, subsidies, tax breaks. SMIC's $8.1B capex would be impossible on operating cash flows alone.
3. **Liang Mong Song's engineering team** — Developed 28nm → 7nm → 5nm in record time. Institutional knowledge that can't be replicated quickly.
4. **Scale** — 1.059M wafers/month. Economies of scale in mature nodes make SMIC the low-cost producer globally for 28nm.

**Vulnerabilities:**
1. **Technology ceiling** — Without EUV, pushing below 5nm becomes exponentially harder. Each node requires more multi-patterning passes, increasing cost and reducing yield.
2. **Equipment dependency** — Even DUV equipment maintenance depends on foreign suppliers. A maintenance ban would be devastating.
3. **Talent concentration** — Too much depends on Liang Mong Song personally.
4. **Geopolitical binary** — The entire thesis can be wiped out by a single policy decision in Washington or a military confrontation over Taiwan.
