# Catalyst Map — SMIC (0981.HK) 🎯

*Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation*
*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## Catalyst #1: 5nm Ramp to Volume Production
- **What:** SMIC achieved 5nm (N+3) in late 2025 using DUV multi-patterning. Huawei's Kirin 9030 SoC is being manufactured on this node. China aims to boost 7nm/5nm output from ~20,000 wafers/month to 100,000 in 1-2 years.
- **When:** Q2-Q4 2026 (progressive ramp throughout year)
- **Impact:** +25-40% on stock. 5nm volume production would dramatically re-rate SMIC as a credible advanced-node foundry, narrowing the perceived gap with TSMC. Revenue mix shift to higher-ASP advanced nodes.
- **Probability:** Medium-High. Production is confirmed; the question is yield improvement (currently ~33% vs TSMC) and volume scaling.
- **Market expectation:** Consensus expects gradual ramp, skeptical on yields and cost economics. Most analysts model minimal advanced-node revenue contribution in 2026.
- **Our edge:** Reports indicate yield has improved from <30% to ~40% and targets 60%. Each 10pp yield improvement dramatically changes unit economics. The national strategic imperative means government subsidies offset the cost gap.

**Source:** TrendForce (Feb 25, 2026); Granitefirm (March 2025); Wccftech teardown research (Dec 2025)

---

## Catalyst #2: Huawei Ascend 950 Series Launch
- **What:** Huawei plans to launch Ascend 950PR and 950DT AI chips in 2026, manufactured by SMIC. These feature Huawei's self-developed HBM, stronger vector performance, and 2.5x interconnect bandwidth boost. This is the backbone of China's domestic AI compute infrastructure.
- **When:** H1-H2 2026 (multiple chipset launches throughout year)
- **Impact:** +15-25%. Validates SMIC's advanced process for AI workloads. Creates recurring high-volume demand at advanced nodes.
- **Probability:** High. Huawei has no alternative foundry for advanced nodes in China. The chips are in development.
- **Market expectation:** Market knows Huawei depends on SMIC but underestimates the volume potential of Ascend 950 given China's massive AI infrastructure buildout.
- **Our edge:** China's AI compute demand is exploding post-DeepSeek. Every major Chinese tech company is rushing to build AI infrastructure, and ALL of it flows through SMIC for advanced chips.

**Source:** TrendForce (Feb 25, 2026) citing Nikkei and Mydrivers

---

## Catalyst #3: 3nm Tape-Out
- **What:** SMIC has started R&D on 3nm using GAA surround gate technology and two-dimensional materials. Expected tape-out in 2026. Separately, 3nm carbon-based wafers using carbon nanotubes have completed lab verification and are being adapted to SMIC's production line.
- **When:** H2 2026 (tape-out, not volume production)
- **Impact:** +20-30%. Even a tape-out announcement would be massive for sentiment — proving China can push past 5nm without EUV.
- **Probability:** Medium. Tape-out ≠ production. Technical risk is real. But Huawei/SMIC already have 3nm DUV patents filed.
- **Market expectation:** Market gives near-zero probability to 3nm in 2026. Any announcement would be a massive surprise.
- **Our edge:** Tom's Hardware reported the patent filings. If SMIC follows the pattern (7nm achieved 2023, 5nm achieved 2025), 3nm tape-out in 2026 follows the ~1-year cadence.

**Source:** Tom's Hardware; Granitefirm; SMIC/Huawei patent filings

---

## Catalyst #4: ASP Increases (~10%) + Revenue Acceleration
- **What:** SMIC reportedly implementing ~10% price hikes on mature nodes driven by AI-related demand. Q4 2025 already showed ASP increase of 3.8% QoQ. With China accounting for ~90% of revenue and domestic substitution accelerating, pricing power is strengthening.
- **When:** Q1-Q2 2026 (already in progress)
- **Impact:** +10-15%. Direct margin expansion once depreciation headwinds stabilize.
- **Probability:** High. Already being reported and implemented.
- **Market expectation:** Partially priced in. Market focused on margin compression from depreciation, missing the pricing improvement.
- **Our edge:** The 10% price hike is happening against a backdrop of 30% depreciation increase in 2026. Market sees the margin squeeze but misses that H2 2026 revenue acceleration from price + volume could outpace depreciation.

**Source:** TrendForce (Jan 27, 2026); SMIC Q4 2025 earnings call

---

## Catalyst #5: "Build China's ASML" National Initiative
- **What:** China's leading semiconductor executives are calling for a nationwide push to build a domestic alternative to ASML. This represents the next phase of China's chip self-sufficiency drive, with SMIC as the primary beneficiary of domestically-produced advanced equipment.
- **When:** Ongoing throughout 2026 (announced March 2026)
- **Impact:** +10-20% (sentiment/re-rating). Signals government commitment to unlimited support for SMIC's mission.
- **Probability:** High that initiative continues; Medium that it produces near-term equipment breakthroughs.
- **Market expectation:** Seen as aspirational. Market discounts ability to replicate ASML in meaningful timeframe.
- **Our edge:** Even partial success in domestic equipment reduces SMIC's dependence on sanctions-restricted supply chains. The initiative signals that Beijing will throw unlimited resources at the problem.

**Source:** Benzinga, SCMP (March 6, 2026)

---

## Catalyst #6: Capacity Expansion (40K 12-inch Wafers)
- **What:** SMIC plans to add ~40,000 12-inch equivalent wafers of monthly capacity by end of 2026 (added 50,000 in 2025). 2026 capex guided flat at ~$8.1B. Total monthly capacity already at 1.059M 8-inch equivalents.
- **When:** Throughout 2026, fully realized by Q4
- **Impact:** +5-10%. Volume growth drives revenue even with flat pricing.
- **Probability:** Very High. Capex is committed, equipment procurement underway.
- **Market expectation:** Fully expected. The concern is that new capacity drives depreciation faster than revenue.
- **Our edge:** The mix shift to 12-inch (higher ASP) is underappreciated. Each 12-inch wafer generates ~2.5x the revenue of an 8-inch equivalent.

**Source:** SMIC Q4 2025 earnings call (Feb 10, 2026); Reuters (Feb 11, 2026)

---

## Catalyst #7: China Chip Consolidation
- **What:** SMIC and Hua Hong are consolidating ownership of subsidiaries (SMIC acquiring non-wholly-owned subs, Hua Hong acquiring 97.5% of Huali). This reduces minority interests, simplifies structure, and potentially creates a national champion foundry ecosystem.
- **When:** H1 2026 (already in progress)
- **Impact:** +5-10%. Cleaner structure, better earnings capture, potential for further sector consolidation.
- **Probability:** High. Already announced and executing.
- **Market expectation:** Viewed positively but not as a major catalyst.
- **Our edge:** Consolidation positions SMIC as THE undisputed domestic champion, creating a quasi-monopoly premium in Chinese advanced chip manufacturing.

**Source:** Benzinga (Jan 2026); SCMP (Jan 2026); TrendForce (Jan 2, 2026)

---

## Catalyst #8: Potential Sanctions Escalation (RISK/CATALYST)
- **What:** US could tighten export controls further, potentially targeting DUV equipment maintenance or expanding SMIC's entity list restrictions. Conversely, any geopolitical thaw could ease restrictions.
- **When:** Unpredictable; ongoing risk throughout 2026
- **Impact:** ±20-30%. Tightening = massive selloff. Easing = massive rally.
- **Probability:** Escalation: Medium. Easing: Low. Status quo most likely.
- **Market expectation:** Market prices in current restrictions but not further escalation.
- **Our edge:** The sanctions have paradoxically become SMIC's moat — they've driven ALL domestic Chinese chip demand to SMIC. Further sanctions would hurt short-term but accelerate the "build China's ASML" push long-term.
