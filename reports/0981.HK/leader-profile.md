# Leader Profile — SMIC (0981.HK) 👤

*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## Co-CEOs: Zhao Haijun & Liang Mong Song

SMIC has an unusual dual-CEO structure that reflects an internal tension:

### Zhao Haijun — Co-CEO (Commercially Pragmatic)
- **Background:** Long foundry industry career. Favors developing commercially viable products using mature and established technology nodes.
- **Philosophy:** Build revenue and profitability on nodes where SMIC can compete on cost and scale (28nm, 14nm, specialty). Pragmatic about what's achievable under sanctions.
- **Recent:** Led Q4 2025 earnings call. Highlighted $8.1B capex, 40K new 12-inch wafer capacity for 2026, warned about 30% depreciation increase.
- **Assessment:** The steady hand. Good at execution, capacity planning, and customer management. Right person for the commercial side.

### Liang Mong Song — Co-CEO (Technology Warrior)
- **Background:** Ex-TSMC, ex-Samsung. Led SMIC's team of 2,000 engineers to develop process nodes from 28nm to 7nm in record time. This would have taken other companies 10+ years.
- **Philosophy:** Aggressive leading-edge strategy. Push to 5nm, 3nm despite sanctions. "Technology is the moat."
- **Key achievement:** Improved 14nm yield from 3% to 95% in 298 days. Developed 7nm without EUV (2023), then 5nm (2025).
- **Tension:** Was "at odds" with Zhao over strategy (FT report). Nearly resigned in late 2020 when Chiang Shangyi was appointed vice-chairman without his knowledge. Called himself "astonished and puzzled" in leaked resignation letter.
- **Assessment:** Irreplaceable technical genius. SMIC's entire advanced-node capability exists because of him. His departure would be catastrophic for the stock. Reports suggest SMIC persuaded him to stay.

### Chiang Shangyi — Vice-Chairman (The Bridge)
- **Background:** Former head of R&D at TSMC for 9 years (retired 2006). Led TSMC's development from 0.25μm to 16nm. Age 74.
- **Appointment:** Executive director and vice-chairman, strategic committee member. Compensation: $670K/year + incentives.
- **Role:** Expected to tilt SMIC toward a more "pragmatic and balanced" development approach. Brings TSMC-level strategic thinking.
- **Assessment:** Stabilizing force. But his appointment triggered boardroom drama that remains a risk factor.

### Liu Xunfeng — Chairman
- Provides government liaison and strategic oversight.

## Insider Activity

- **No significant open-market insider buying/selling detected** in recent public filings. As a state-influenced entity, insider trading is less relevant — management compensation is modest compared to Western peers.
- **Northbound funds (mainland China investors)** have been aggressively buying SMIC shares through Stock Connect. March 9, 2026: Record HKD 37.2B northbound flow into HK stocks, with SMIC among top buys.
- **Bullish block trade:** 138K shares at HKD 61.0 on March 9, 2026 (~HKD 8.4M turnover), signaling institutional conviction.

---

## Key Personnel Changes (Last 6 Months)

- **Chiang Shangyi appointment** as vice-chairman (most significant). Signals shift toward balanced strategy between leading-edge and commercial viability.
- **Liang Mong Song retention** — reportedly nearly resigned but was persuaded to stay. His continued presence is critical.
- **No other major departures reported.**

---

## Management Assessment: SCORE 7/10

**Strengths:**
- Liang Mong Song is a once-in-a-generation technical leader
- Zhao Haijun is a competent commercial operator
- Government backing provides unlimited strategic support
- Chiang adds TSMC-caliber strategic thinking

**Weaknesses:**
- Dual-CEO tension is a real governance risk
- Liang's potential departure remains a tail risk
- State influence means priorities may not always align with shareholder returns
- No clear succession plan for Liang (age ~70)

**The Question:** Can the Zhao-Liang tension be managed productively, or will it blow up? Chiang's appointment suggests Beijing is trying to impose stability. For now, both CEOs remain. Watch for any signals of Liang's departure — that would be a sell signal.
