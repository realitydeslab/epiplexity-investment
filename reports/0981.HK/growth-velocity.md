## Profitability Trajectory

### Gross Margin

| Quarter | Gross Margin | Trend |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| Q1 2025 | ~19.8% (est.) | Recovery from trough |
| Q2 2025 | ~20.5% (est.) | Improving |
| Q3 2025 | 22.0% | **Peak** |
| Q4 2025 | 19.2% | Decline (depreciation, yield issues) |
| Q1 2026E | 18-20% | Guided range |
| FY2025 | 21.0% | +3.0pp YoY |

**Key headwind:** Depreciation expected to rise ~30% YoY in 2026 as new fabs come online. This is the main margin compressor.

**Key tailwind:** 10% ASP increases on mature nodes, plus mix shift to higher-ASP 12-inch and advanced-node wafers.

**H2 2026 outlook:** Margins likely trough in H1 2026 and recover in H2 as volume absorption of fixed costs improves and ASP increases take effect. Consensus: FY2026 gross margin ~19-21%.

## Guidance vs. Actual History

SMIC management tends to guide conservatively and beat:

- **2025 capex:** Guided "roughly flat" at start of year, actual was $8.1B (higher than originally expected due to strong demand)
- **Revenue:** Consistently guided "flat" QoQ for Q1, then delivered above-consensus for full year
- **Pattern:** Under-promise, over-deliver. Expect 2026 guidance to prove conservative.

---

## Customer/Demand Pipeline

- **China domestic = ~90% of revenue.** Localization wave is structural and multi-year.
- **Consumer electronics:** 47% of Q4 revenue, growing 10% QoQ. Driven by stockpiling ahead of potential sanctions escalation.
- **AI demand:** Huawei Ascend chips, Cambricon AI processors, plus dozens of smaller Chinese AI chip designers — all captive SMIC customers.
- **Memory cycle pressure:** Rising memory prices squeezing some customer budgets, leading to delays in non-AI chip orders. This is the main near-term demand headwind.

## Growth Velocity Score: 7.5/10

**Accelerating:**
- Full-year revenue growth rate (14.7% → 16.2%)
- Net profit growth (+39.1%)
- Utilization (85.5% → 93.5%)
- ASP trajectory (pricing power emerging)
- Advanced node contribution (5nm coming online)

**Decelerating:**
- QoQ revenue growth slowing (seasonal + base effect)
- Gross margins under pressure from depreciation
- Memory cycle headwinds on non-AI customer demand
- Q1 2026 flat guidance muted

**Net assessment:** Growth is solid and fundamentally driven by structural localization. The margin squeeze from depreciation is real but temporary (H1 2026 trough, H2 recovery). Revenue growth should reaccelerate in H2 2026 as 5nm ramp and price hikes take effect.
