## Risk Management

### Stop-Loss
- **Hard stop:** HKD 48.00 (-21%). Below this, the thesis is broken (likely sanctions escalation or leadership crisis).
- **Soft stop:** HKD 52.00 (-14.5%). Review position if breached; may just be broader market weakness.

### Position Sizing
- **Recommended:** 3-5% of portfolio. This is high-conviction but high-risk due to geopolitical binary.
- **Max:** 7% for aggressive portfolios with China allocation.
- **Rationale:** The asymmetry is favorable (56% upside vs 31% downside), but the tail risk from sanctions is impossible to hedge.

### What Kills the Trade?
1. **Liang Mong Song departure** — SMIC loses its technology edge. Immediate sell.
2. **US sanctions on DUV maintenance/spare parts** — Would cripple production. Immediate sell.
3. **Gross margin below 16% for 2 consecutive quarters** — Indicates structural, not cyclical, problem.
4. **China economic crisis / property spiral** — Broader systemic risk.
5. **Taiwan Strait military escalation** — Existential risk for all China tech.

### Correlation Risk
- Highly correlated with: HK tech stocks (HSI Tech), China semiconductor ETFs, Huawei-linked names
- If broader China/HK market sells off 15%+, SMIC will likely sell off more (high beta ~1.5-2.0x)
- NOT correlated with US semiconductor cycle (different customer base, different drivers)
