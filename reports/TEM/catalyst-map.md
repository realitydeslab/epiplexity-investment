# Tempus AI (TEM) — Catalyst Map 🎯

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## Catalyst 1: ASP Expansion via xT CDx Migration
- **What:** Migration from LDT (~$2,900 list) to FDA-approved xT CDx companion diagnostic (~$4,500 list), driving blended ASP from $1,640 → $2,200+
- **When:** Rolling through 2026–2027, meaningful impact by Q3 2026
- **Impact:** +35% revenue uplift on existing oncology volumes (~$200/test near-term). Could add $200M+ annualized revenue at current volumes
- **Probability:** High — FDA-approved CDx already in market, migration is operational
- **Market expectation:** Consensus models gradual ASP improvement; 25% guide implies conservative uplift
- **Our edge:** ASP expansion is mechanical — each CDx conversion is pure margin. Beat potential underappreciated

## Catalyst 2: xF Liquid Biopsy FDA Clearance
- **What:** FDA 510(k) clearance for xF liquid biopsy panel, enabling broader cfDNA testing
- **When:** Expected H1–H2 2026
- **Impact:** ~$230/test ASP uplift. Opens new monitoring/recurrence volume. 10-15% stock move on clearance
- **Probability:** Medium-High
- **Market expectation:** Partially priced; most analysts model xF contribution starting 2027
- **Our edge:** Earlier clearance pulls forward revenue

## Catalyst 3: MRD (Minimal Residual Disease) Ramp
- **What:** MRD monitoring scaling from 4,700 tests/quarter (Q4 2025) toward 10,000+/quarter
- **When:** Inflection throughout 2026, especially H2
- **Impact:** Recurring revenue stream — patients tested quarterly. TAM enormous. Could be $200M+ business by 2028
- **Probability:** High — 56% QoQ growth demonstrating product-market fit
- **Market expectation:** Early stage, models don't capture trajectory
- **Our edge:** 700PB multimodal data library = best MRD algorithm training data
