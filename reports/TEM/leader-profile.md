# Tempus AI (TEM) — Leader Profile 👤

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## Eric Lefkofsky — Founder & CEO

### Background
- **Serial entrepreneur:** Co-founded Groupon (GRPN), took it public in 2011. Built multiple billion-dollar companies
- **Founded Tempus in 2015** after his wife was diagnosed with cancer — mission-driven origin
- **Vision:** Build the world's largest library of clinical and molecular data, make it useful via AI
- **Track record:** Scaled Tempus from zero to $1.27B revenue in ~9 years. IPO'd June 2024

### Execution Assessment
- **Revenue execution:** Delivered 83% YoY growth in 2025 — well above initial guidance
- **Profitability:** Hit positive Adj. EBITDA milestone ahead of the "10-year birthday" target
- **M&A:** Ambry Genetics (hereditary testing) and Paige (AI digital pathology) acquisitions integrated successfully, expanding TAM
- **Fundraising:** $759.7M cash on balance sheet — no near-term dilution risk
- **Strategic partnerships:** AstraZeneca ($200M), Merck (multi-year), and numerous pharma data licensing deals

### Insider Activity ⚠️
- **Feb 19, 2026:** Eric Lefkofsky **SOLD** 166,250 shares at $58.87 = **$9.79M**
- Post-sale ownership: 8,908,283 shares (~$524M at sale price)
- This represents only a **1.83% reduction** in his position
- **Context:** CEO holds >$500M in stock. Selling $10M is routine liquidity, not a bearish signal. Still massively overweight in TEM
- No other notable insider sales or buys in recent weeks

### Key Hires & Organizational Changes
- **Ryan Fukushima** — CEO of Data & Apps division. Leading the Merck collaboration and pharma data licensing expansion
- **Paige team integration** — AI pathology expertise absorbed post-acquisition
- Partnerships with **NYU Langone Health** and **Northwestern Medicine** announced Q4 2025 for clinical collaboration

### Public Statements on Near-Term Outlook
- *"The strength of our unit growth in diagnostics along with the accelerating growth of our data business is proof that we are unique in this space"* — Eric Lefkofsky, Q4 2025 earnings
- Guided to **25% topline growth over next 3 years** — confident, not hyperaggressive
- Plans to reinvest majority of incremental gross profit into platform expansion while generating "meaningful adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow"

### Verdict
Lefkofsky is the right CEO for this phase. Serial entrepreneur with proven scale-up DNA. The insider sale is immaterial relative to his position. The key risk is whether a tech-founder CEO can navigate the complex healthcare regulatory landscape long-term — but hiring domain experts (Fukushima, clinical partnerships) mitigates this.
