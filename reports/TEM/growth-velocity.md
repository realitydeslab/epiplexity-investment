# Tempus AI (TEM) — Growth Velocity 📈

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## Revenue Trajectory (Last 4 Quarters)

| Quarter | Revenue | YoY Growth | QoQ Growth |
|---------|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Q1 2025 | $255.6M | ~75% | — |
| Q2 2025 | $289.4M | ~80% | +13.2% |
| Q3 2025 | $334.2M | +84.7% | +15.5% |
| Q4 2025 | $367.2M | +83.0% | +9.9% |
| **FY 2025** | **$1.27B** | **+83% YoY** | — |

### Growth Assessment: ACCELERATING profitability, DECELERATING (but still strong) topline

- Revenue growth was 83% YoY for the full year — exceptional for a $1B+ company
- Organic growth (ex-Ambry acquisition) was 33.5% in Q4 — the real underlying rate
- 2026 guide of $1.59B implies 25% growth — significant deceleration from 83%, BUT this is conservative given management's beat history
- **The real story is PROFIT acceleration:** Adj. EBITDA went from -$104.7M (2024) → -$7.4M (2025) → +$65M guided (2026)

## Unit Economics

| Metric | Q4 2024 | Q4 2025 | Change |
|--------|---------|---------|--------|
| Non-GAAP Gross Margin | 59.6% | 64.7% | +510 bps |
| Diagnostics Gross Margin | 49.6% | 62.2% | +1,260 bps |
| Data & Apps Gross Margin | ~70% | 74.5% | +~450 bps |
| Oncology ASP | ~$1,270 | $1,640 | +29% |
| Adj. EBITDA | -$7.8M | +$12.9M | +$20.7M |

**Unit economics are dramatically improving.** Diagnostics margin expansion from 49.6% → 62.2% in one year is exceptional. This is the operating leverage thesis playing out.

## Path to GAAP Profitability
- FY 2025 GAAP Net Loss: ~-$220M (includes ~$200M SBC)
- FY 2026 Adj. EBITDA guide: $65M, SBC ~$200M, D&A ~$120M, interest ~$60M
- **GAAP profitability likely by H2 2027** if SBC as % of revenue declines and EBITDA continues expanding
- Cash: $759.7M — no dilution needed, can self-fund for 3+ years

## Verdict: Growth is STRONG but TRANSITIONING
The 83% → 25% revenue growth deceleration looks scary but is misleading. Organic growth is ~33%. The real inflection is **margin expansion** — Tempus is becoming a profit engine. MRD + ASP expansion + data licensing provide multiple vectors for upside surprises.

*Sources: Tempus Q4 2025 earnings release (Feb 24, 2026), Q3 2025 CEO letter, Fintool analysis*
