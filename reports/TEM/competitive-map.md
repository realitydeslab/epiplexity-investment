# Tempus AI (TEM) — Competitive Map 🗺️

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## Direct Competitors

### Guardant Health (GH) — Liquid Biopsy Leader
- **Revenue:** ~$965-970M (2025), +31% YoY
- **Moat:** FDA-approved Guardant360, Shield screening (colorectal), liquid biopsy pioneer
- **Overlap:** Both compete in genomic testing for oncology
- **Difference:** Guardant is pure liquid biopsy; Tempus has tissue-based NGS + data platform + AI
- **Edge vs Tempus:** Stronger in liquid biopsy/MRD today
- **Weakness vs Tempus:** No data licensing business, no AI pathology, smaller multimodal dataset

### Exact Sciences (EXAS) — Cancer Screening
- **Revenue:** ~$2.8B (2025)
- **Moat:** Cologuard (colorectal screening), Oncotype DX (breast cancer recurrence)
- **Overlap:** Cancer diagnostics broadly
- **Difference:** Focused on screening; Tempus on precision treatment selection
- **Threat level:** Low — different market segments

### Natera (NTRA) — MRD & Reproductive
- **Revenue:** ~$1.7B (2025)
- **Moat:** Signatera MRD test, reproductive genetics
- **Overlap:** MRD monitoring — direct competitor to Tempus MRD
- **Edge vs Tempus:** More established in MRD with clinical evidence
- **Weakness vs Tempus:** No comprehensive data platform, no pharma data licensing

### Foundation Medicine (Roche) — Tissue NGS
- **Overlap:** Comprehensive genomic profiling
- **Edge:** Roche backing, established payer relationships
- **Weakness:** Private subsidiary, less innovation velocity, no AI data platform

## Who Benefits From the Same Catalysts?
- **MRD expansion:** Natera (NTRA), Guardant (GH) — rising tide lifts all boats in MRD
- **ASP improvement:** All genomic testing companies benefit from better reimbursement
- **AI in healthcare:** Veracyte (VCYT), Schrodinger (SDGR) — adjacent AI-health plays
- **Pharma data demand:** Flatiron (Roche), Veeva Systems (VEEV) — clinical data platforms

## Who Loses If Tempus Wins?
- **Foundation Medicine** — if Tempus's CDx migration captures oncologist mindshare
- **Smaller lab testing companies** — Tempus's scale advantage increases with volume
- **Legacy pathology labs** — Paige AI digital pathology disrupts traditional histopathology

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### Long TEM / Short EXAS
- **Thesis:** Precision treatment selection (Tempus) growing faster than legacy screening (Exact Sciences)
- **Risk:** EXAS has proven products with recurring revenue

### Long TEM / Long GH (Basket)
- **Thesis:** Both win from genomic testing expansion. Tempus for data + tissue, Guardant for liquid biopsy
- **Diversifies:** MRD competitive risk between them

## Tempus's Unique Position
No other company combines:
1. ✅ Largest clinical + molecular data library (700PB)
2. ✅ High-volume diagnostics (generating proprietary data)
3. ✅ AI-powered digital pathology (Paige)
4. ✅ Pharma data licensing ($1.1B contract value)
5. ✅ GPU infrastructure for healthcare AI

This "flywheel" — diagnostics generate data, data trains AI, AI improves diagnostics, pharma pays for data — is genuinely unique and creates compounding competitive advantage.
