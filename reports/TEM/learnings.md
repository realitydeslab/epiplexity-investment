# Tempus AI (TEM) — Structured Intelligence 🧠

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## Sector Signal: AI Healthcare Data is the New Oil

Tempus validates a broader thesis: **the most valuable asset in AI healthcare isn't the model — it's the data**. Every diagnostic test generates proprietary training data. Every pharma partnership validates the data's worth. This is the "data flywheel" thesis that applies to:

- **Genomics broadly:** Companies with large patient datasets will command premium valuations
- **Pharma R&D:** Drug developers will pay increasing premiums for real-world evidence and multimodal datasets
- **Healthcare AI:** The winners aren't the model builders — they're the data generators

## Contrarian Angle: What's the Market Missing?

1. **The 25% guide is sandbagged.** Management guided 25% but has beaten every quarter. Organic growth is 33.5%. MRD is not yet material in numbers. Real 2026 growth could be 30-35%

2. **Data licensing is massively undervalued.** $100M/quarter at 74.5% gross margin with 126% NRR and >$1.1B backlog. This business alone could be worth $5-8B on a SaaS multiple

3. **MRD is a sleeping giant.** 4,700 tests/quarter is nothing. If every cancer patient in remission gets quarterly MRD testing, this is a $5B+ TAM. Tempus has the data advantage to win here

4. **The Paige acquisition was brilliant, not dilutive.** Digital pathology + genomics creates a multimodal dataset no one else has. The market priced this as "another money-losing acquisition." It's actually the moat

5. **Beta of 5.22 creates opportunity.** In a broad market sell-off, TEM gets killed regardless of fundamentals. This creates entry points for informed buyers

## What Could Go Wrong (Bearish Contrarian)

1. **GAAP profitability is still far away.** $200M SBC, $120M D&A, $60M interest expense. Even with $65M EBITDA, GAAP loss will be ~$300M+
2. **Lefkofsky is a "good times" entrepreneur.** Groupon parallels are uncomfortable — hype-driven scaling that eventually hit a wall
3. **Healthcare reimbursement is fragile.** Policy changes under any administration could compress genomic testing payments
4. **Competition from big tech.** If Google Health or Amazon Health build similar data platforms with broader datasets...
