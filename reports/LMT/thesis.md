## The Catalyst

**Lockheed Martin sits at the center of three simultaneous tailwinds that haven't converged like this since the Cold War:**

1. **Active U.S. military operations against Iran** — creating immediate munitions demand and a 4x production commitment from the Trump administration
2. **European rearmament super-cycle** — NATO 3.5% GDP targets driving F-35 orders and missile defense purchases
3. **EPS normalization** — FY2025 was depressed by one-time charges; FY2026 consensus expects +40% EPS growth to $30.19

LMT's monopoly position as the sole F-35 manufacturer (1,000+ jets in service) creates an unassailable moat. Every F-35 sold locks a country into decades of LMT sustainment contracts. The Pentagon just signaled a $12B boost for F-35 sustainment alone.

## The Risk

1. **Iran peace deal** — the biggest risk. A sudden de-escalation would remove the war premium and send defense stocks down 10-15% across the board.
2. **Goldman Sachs is a notable bear** ($517 PT, Strong Sell) — they see the stock as overextended after +50% in 12 months.
3. **Analysts are behind** — average PT of $576 is 14% below current price, meaning the stock has outrun consensus.
4. **Margins** — operating margins at 10.3% vs historical 12-13%. Recovery is expected but not guaranteed.

## The Verdict

**CAUTIOUS BUY.** LMT is a quality compounder with active conflict catalysts, but it's priced near perfection at all-time highs. The risk/reward is asymmetric: the bear case (-18%) is shallower than the bull case (+26%), and the dividend provides downside cushion.

**Best entry strategy:** Scale in. Buy half now, add on any pullback to $620-640 (which could come on any Iran de-escalation headlines).

**Who should own this:**

- Dividend growth investors (22 years of consecutive raises)

**Who should NOT own this:**
