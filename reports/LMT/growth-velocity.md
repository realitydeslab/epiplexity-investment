# LMT Growth Velocity — Lockheed Martin

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## 📈 Is Growth Accelerating?

**YES — revenue growth is clearly accelerating, and EPS is set for a major bounce.**

## Revenue Growth (Quarterly YoY)

| Quarter | Revenue | YoY Growth |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Q1 2025 | $17.96B | +4.5% |
| Q2 2025 | $18.16B | +0.2% |
| Q3 2025 | $18.61B | +8.8% |
| Q4 2025 | $20.32B | **+9.1%** |

**Trend: ACCELERATING.** Q4 2025 was the strongest growth quarter since Q1 2024 (+13.7%). Revenue hit $20.3B — first $20B+ quarter ever.

## Annual Revenue Trajectory

| Year | Revenue | Growth |
|------|---------|--------|
| FY2022 | $65.98B | -1.6% |
| FY2023 | $67.57B | +2.4% |
| FY2024 | $71.04B | +5.1% |
| FY2025 | $75.05B | +5.6% |
| FY2026E | $79.86B | **+6.4%** |
| FY2027E | $83.81B | +5.0% |

**Multi-year acceleration from -1.6% → +6.4%.** Analyst consensus expects continued mid-single-digit growth, but Iran conflict + European rearm could push above consensus.

## EPS Trajectory

| Year | EPS (Diluted) | Growth |
|------|---------------|--------|
| FY2023 | $27.55 | +27.2% |
| FY2024 | $22.31 | -19.0% |
| FY2025 | $21.49 | -3.7% |
| FY2026E | $30.19 | **+40.5%** |
| FY2027E | $32.28 | +6.9% |

**EPS story:** FY2024-2025 were depressed by one-time charges (especially Q2 2025 EPS of $1.46 — margin compression). FY2026 is a **normalization + growth** year. The 40% jump is partly mean reversion to the $27+ baseline.

## Quarterly EPS Volatility

| Quarter | EPS | Notes |
|---------|-----|-------|
| Q1 2025 | $7.28 | Strong |
| Q2 2025 | $1.46 | **One-time charges** |
| Q3 2025 | $6.95 | Recovery |
| Q4 2025 | $5.80 | Solid, +161% YoY |

Q2 2025 was the outlier dragging FY2025 down. Q4 2025 EPS of $5.80 vs Q4 2024's $2.22 = massive recovery.

## Free Cash Flow

| Year | FCF | Growth |
|------|-----|--------|
| FY2023 | $6.23B | +1.6% |
| FY2024 | $5.29B | -15.1% |
| FY2025 | $6.91B | **+30.7%** |

**FCF inflection is real.** $6.9B gives management firepower for buybacks, dividends, and M&A.

## Margins

| Metric | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Gross Margin | 12.6% | 9.8% | 10.2% |
| Operating Margin | 12.6% | 9.9% | 10.3% |
| FCF Margin | 9.2% | 7.4% | 9.2% |

Margins compressed in FY2024-2025 due to F-35 TR3 tech refresh costs and supply chain headwinds. Recovery underway but not yet back to FY2023 levels.

## Guidance vs. Actual History

LMT management tends to guide conservatively and beat modestly. Q4 2025 revenue of $20.3B beat estimates. Pattern suggests FY2026 guidance will prove conservative if Iran conflict sustains.

## Backlog / Pipeline

- LMT backlog was ~$166B at end of FY2025 (2.2x annual revenue)
- Backlog provides exceptional revenue visibility
- F-35 sustainment contracts + European orders growing backlog further

## Profitability Path

LMT is already highly profitable. Key watch: margin recovery toward 12%+ operating margin (from current ~10.3%). If margins normalize AND revenue grows 6%+, EPS upside is significant.

## Verdict

**Growth is accelerating on revenue, FCF is surging, and EPS is set for a 40% bounce.** The Iran conflict and European rearmament provide incremental upside to consensus numbers. Margin recovery is the swing factor — if it happens, EPS could exceed $32 in FY2026.
