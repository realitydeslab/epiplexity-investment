# LMT Catalyst Map — Lockheed Martin

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## 🎯 12-Month Catalysts

### 1. Iran Conflict / Middle East Escalation (ACTIVE — March 2026+)
- **What:** U.S. military operations against Iran underway. Trump met defense executives March 6, 2026 — announced plans to **quadruple production** of "exquisite class" weaponry. LMT hit all-time highs.
- **When:** Ongoing through 2026, with escalation/de-escalation cycles
- **Impact:** +10-20% on sustained conflict (munitions drawdown → replenishment orders). Already partially priced: stock up ~50% in 12 months.
- **Probability:** HIGH (conflict is active)
- **Market expectation:** Sustained defense spending increases
- **Our edge:** Replenishment cycles last 2-3 years AFTER conflict ends. Even peace = multi-year orders to refill stockpiles.

### 2. Q1 2026 Earnings (April 21, 2026)
- **What:** First earnings of 2026. Key: 2026 guidance update, backlog growth, margin trajectory
- **When:** April 21, 2026
- **Impact:** ±5-8%. Consensus EPS: ~$30.19 FY2026 (vs $21.49 FY2025) = **40% EPS growth expected**
- **Probability:** HIGH for beat (Q4 2025 showed 9.1% revenue growth)
- **Market expectation:** Revenue ~$79.9B FY2026, EPS $30.19
- **Our edge:** FY2025 EPS depressed by one-time Q2 charges ($1.46 EPS). "40% growth" is partly mean reversion, not pure acceleration.

### 3. F-35 Sustainment $12B Boost + New Contracts
- **What:** Pentagon signaled **$12 billion boost for F-35 sustainment**. New F-35 contracts awarded March 6. F-35 Lot 18-19 deliveries ramping.
- **When:** Contracts throughout 2026
- **Impact:** +5-10% incremental revenue
- **Probability:** HIGH (contracts already awarded)
- **Market expectation:** Priced as steady-state
- **Our edge:** F-35 sustainment is recurring, high-margin. 1,000+ installed jet base = annuity stream.

### 4. 4x Weapons Production Commitment
- **What:** LMT committed to quadruple output of some weapons after Trump WH meeting
- **When:** Announced March 6-7, 2026; ramp through 2026-2027
- **Impact:** +10-15% MFC segment revenue (~$13B/year base)
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH (commitment made; funding depends on appropriations)
- **Market expectation:** Announcement priced; execution skepticism
- **Our edge:** JAVELIN, PAC-3, HIMARS demand far exceeds supply. Seller's market.

### 5. European Rearmament / NATO 3.5% GDP
- **What:** NATO allies raising defense budgets. European orders for F-35, missile defense.
- **When:** Orders flowing throughout 2026
- **Impact:** +5-8% international revenue growth
- **Probability:** HIGH (political commitment locked in)
- **Market expectation:** Moderate growth priced
- **Our edge:** LMT is ONLY F-35 manufacturer. Every European F-35 = monopoly revenue.

### 6. Space Segment / Artemis
- **What:** NASA eyeing Boeing-Lockheed components for Moon mission. Classified program spending rising.
- **When:** H2 2026
- **Impact:** +3-5% segment growth
- **Probability:** MEDIUM

- **Our edge:** Classified spending rising faster than unclassified.

### 7. Share Buyback Acceleration
- **What:** FCF surged to $6.9B FY2025 (+31%). Shares declining ~2.4%/year.
- **When:** Ongoing
- **Impact:** +2-3% EPS accretion
- **Probability:** HIGH

### 8. Autonomous Systems / AI
- **What:** STAR.OS framework, AI Fight Club, distributed teaming for autonomous drones
- **When:** Contract wins throughout 2026
- **Impact:** Re-rating potential
- **Probability:** MEDIUM
