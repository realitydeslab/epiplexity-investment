2. **Defense budget sequestration** — politically unlikely but possible
3. **F-35 program cancellation/reduction** — extremely unlikely but fatal
4. **Margin deterioration continues** — if operating margins fall below 9%, earnings estimates get cut

### Correlation Risk
- LMT is positively correlated with: RTX, NOC, GD, LHX (all defense)
- LMT is NEGATIVELY correlated with broader market during crises (defense = safe haven)
- Iran conflict risk: if war ends, ALL defense stocks sell off simultaneously
