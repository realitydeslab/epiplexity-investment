# LMT Learnings — Lockheed Martin

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## 🧠 Structured Intelligence

### What Does This Tell Us About the Sector?

1. **Defense is in a multi-year super-cycle.** This isn't a one-quarter trade. NATO 3.5% GDP targets, Iran conflict, Ukraine restocking, and Pacific deterrence against China create a structural demand increase through at least 2030.

2. **Munitions are the bottleneck.** Trump's 4x production commitment highlights that the U.S. industrial base cannot produce weapons fast enough. This gives pricing power to LMT, RTX, and missile makers.

3. **Defense stocks have re-rated permanently.** LMT at 22x forward PE (vs historical 15-18x) reflects the market pricing a longer, higher growth cycle. The question isn't "will they re-rate?" — it's "is the re-rating justified?"

4. **The defense-AI convergence is real but early.** LMT's STAR.OS, AI Fight Club, and autonomous systems are strategic investments. The stock hasn't gotten an "AI premium" yet, but if a major AI defense contract materializes, the narrative could shift.

### Contrarian Angle — What's the Market Missing?

**BULL contrarian:** The market is pricing LMT as a "steady defense grower" at 22x PE. It's NOT pricing the possibility of:
- F-35 production ABOVE 180/year (currently ~150)
- Missile production scaling 4x creating a $5-10B incremental revenue opportunity
- AI defense contracts worth $1B+ annually within 3 years
- International revenue growing from 27% to 35%+ of sales

**BEAR contrarian:** Goldman Sachs (PT $517, Strong Sell) argues:
- Stock has run too far, too fast (+50% in 12 months)
- Defense budgets could face political headwinds if administration changes
- Margin compression is structural, not cyclical
- The "war premium" will evaporate
