# LMT Leader Profile — Lockheed Martin

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## CEO: Jim Taiclet

- **Tenure:** CEO since June 2020, Chairman since March 2021
- **Background:** Former CEO of American Tower Corp (cell tower REIT, grew market cap from $3B to $100B+). West Point graduate, F-15 fighter pilot, Harvard MBA.
- **Execution track record:** Strong. Pivoted LMT toward 21st Century Security (network-centric warfare), AI, and hypersonics. Revenue grew from $65.4B (2020) to $75.0B (2025).
- **Leadership style:** Strategic, M&A-oriented, tech-forward for a defense CEO. Pushed digital transformation across all segments.

### Right Person for This Moment?
**YES.** Taiclet's telecom infrastructure background is uniquely suited to LMT's pivot toward networked, AI-enabled defense. His relationships with tech companies (5G/AI partnerships) differentiate LMT from pure legacy defense peers. His White House meeting on March 6, 2026 — committing to 4x weapons production — shows willingness to lean into the current political/military moment.
