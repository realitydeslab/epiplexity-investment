## The REAL Growth Story: AI Revenue Mix Shift

- **Enterprise AI Revenue:** $100M+ ARR, growing **50% YoY**
- **Subscription Revenue:** Up 12% (vast majority of total revenue)
- **AI as % of Revenue:** ~8-9% and growing rapidly
- **Trajectory:** If AI revenue sustains 50% growth → $150M+ by end FY2026 → 12%+ of total revenue

This is a classic "growth within growth" story. Total growth decelerates, but the fastest-growing segment (AI) is taking over the mix. By FY2027, AI could be 15-20% of revenue growing 40-50%.
