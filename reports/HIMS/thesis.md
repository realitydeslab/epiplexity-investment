# HIMS — Investment Thesis (1-Page)

**Hims & Hers Health (NYSE: HIMS) | $22.16 | March 9, 2026**

---

## The Catalyst

Novo Nordisk dropped its patent lawsuit and agreed to let HIMS sell branded Wegovy and Ozempic on its platform. This transforms HIMS from a legally contested compounded GLP-1 provider into an authorized distributor of the world's most in-demand weight-loss drugs. Stock surged 40% on the news (March 9, 2026).

## The Trade

HIMS trades at 2.15x TTM sales with 59% revenue growth, $128M net income, and 39% short float. The Novo deal eliminates the existential legal overhang and creates a massive revenue upside catalyst. Consensus FY2026 revenue of $2.8B (+19%) was set BEFORE the Novo deal — likely needs 25-35% revision upward. At $22, you're buying a $5B company growing 25%+ with a pharmaceutical distribution deal that didn't exist 24 hours ago.

**Entry:** $20-22 (post-gap consolidation) or scale in 1/3 now, 1/3 at $19, 1/3 on Q1 earnings.

## The Risk

1. **Margin compression** — Branded GLP-1 margins (30-40%) are much lower than compounded (80%+). Revenue grows but profits may not.
2. **Debt** — $972M from Eucalyptus acquisition. Interest expense drags on FCF.
3. **Short squeeze reversal** — 40% gap could reverse if deal terms disappoint.
4. **Gross margin** — Already fell from 82% → 74% in FY2025. Could decline further with branded drugs.

## The Verdict

**BUY — Epiplexity Score: 80/100**

The Novo Nordisk deal is a structural game-changer. HIMS went from being sued to being a distribution partner for the world's largest GLP-1 maker. The 39% short float creates squeeze dynamics. The $1.15B Eucalyptus acquisition adds international optionality. The base business (sexual health, hair loss, mental health) alone justifies $18-20/share.

**The real question:** Does branded GLP-1 distribution generate enough margin to matter, or is this a "revenue vanity, profit sanity" situation? We lean bullish — even if margins are lower, the TAM is 10x larger, and the legal risk premium that compressed the stock from $70 to $14 is now gone.
