# HIMS — Leader Profile 👤

**CEO: Andrew Dudum (Co-Founder)**
**Research Date:** March 9, 2026

---

## The Leader

**Andrew Dudum** has been CEO since co-founding Hims & Hers in 2017. He previously founded Atomic, a venture studio, and has a background in consumer technology and venture capital.

### Execution Track Record

- Built HIMS from zero to $2.35B revenue in 7 years
- Achieved consistent 60-90%+ annual revenue growth for 5 consecutive years
- Navigated the company to profitability in 2024 ($126M net income) and sustained it in 2025 ($128M)
- Executed the $1.15B Eucalyptus acquisition — HIMS' first major international expansion
- **Most critically:** Negotiated the Novo Nordisk partnership from a position of legal weakness (being sued) into a distribution deal. This is masterful dealmaking — turning an existential threat into the company's biggest growth catalyst.

### Leadership Style

Dudum is a consumer-brand builder, not a pharma executive. This matters: HIMS' moat is brand + DTC + technology, not clinical IP. He runs HIMS more like a consumer tech company than a healthcare company.

### Key Strengths
- Consumer brand instinct — built a healthcare brand that doesn't feel like healthcare
- Aggressive expansion while maintaining profitability
- Willingness to take legal and regulatory risks (compounding GLP-1s before having Novo's blessing)
- Good at M&A timing (Eucalyptus when international telehealth was still undervalued)

### Key Risks
- Not a pharma/healthcare lifer — may underestimate regulatory complexity in international markets
- Heavy reliance on GLP-1 narrative could mean over-concentration
- $972M long-term debt from Eucalyptus is the largest leverage HIMS has ever carried

## Insider Activity

### Recent Insider Transactions
- **Insider ownership:** 9.16% (significant founder alignment)
- **Recent insider transactions:** Net -3.19% (modest selling)
- **Institutional ownership:** 81.09% (heavy institutional backing)
- **Institutional transactions:** -8.08% (some institutional selling in the downturn)

### Reading the Insiders

The insider selling occurred during the $30-70 run-up in 2025 — normal profit-taking. What matters more:
1. **Dudum still holds 9%+** — his wealth is overwhelmingly tied to HIMS stock
2. No insider panic selling during the GLP-1 lawsuit period ($70→$14)
3. Institutions were selling into the uncertainty — those positions will be re-established after the Novo deal

### Key Management Changes
- The Eucalyptus acquisition brought international management talent
- R&D spending jumped from $78.8M (2024) to $149.3M (2025) — 90% increase — signaling investment in technology platform
- Employee count at 2,442 — lean for a $2.35B revenue company ($961K revenue per employee)

## CEO Verdict

**Rating: B+**

Andrew Dudum turned an existential legal crisis into a transformative partnership. That alone earns high marks. The concern is the debt load from Eucalyptus and whether he can execute international expansion as well as he executed US growth. The man builds brands — the question is whether that translates across cultures and regulatory regimes.
