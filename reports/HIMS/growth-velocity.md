**Verdict:** Revenue growth is technically decelerating from 90%+ to 59%, but at $2.35B scale this is expected. The critical question: does the Novo Nordisk deal RE-ACCELERATE growth in FY2026?

If HIMS captures even 2-3% of the US GLP-1 market ($35B+ by 2027), that's $700M-$1B incremental revenue.

## Profitability Trajectory

| Metric | FY 2023 | FY 2024 | FY 2025 |
|--------|---------|---------|---------|
| Revenue | $872M | $1,477M | $2,348M |
| Gross Margin | 82.0% | 79.5% | 73.8% |
| Operating Margin | -3.4% | 4.2% | 4.5% |
| Net Income | -$23.5M | $126.0M | $128.4M |
| EPS (diluted) | -$0.11 | $0.53 | $0.51 |
| FCF | $56.3M | $209.4M | $74.0M |

### Key Observations:

1. **Gross margin compression:** 82% → 74% — this is the cost of switching from high-margin compounded drugs to lower-margin branded pharmaceuticals. Selling Wegovy/Ozempic at retail will have 30-40% gross margins vs. 80%+ for compounded products. **This is the bear case within the bull story.**

2. **Operating margin expanding:** -3.4% → 4.5% — leverage on SG&A despite gross margin pressure.

3. **FCF collapsed:** $209M → $74M — driven by the $1.15B Eucalyptus acquisition and associated debt.

4. **EPS flat:** $0.53 → $0.51 despite 59% revenue growth — reflects margin mix shift and Eucalyptus integration costs.

## Growth Velocity Verdict

**DECELERATING organically, but the Novo deal creates a potential RE-ACCELERATION catalyst.**

The base business is still growing 20-30% at scale. Adding branded GLP-1 distribution could push total growth to 25-35% in FY2026. The trade-off: lower gross margins on branded drugs vs. much larger TAM and eliminated legal risk.
