# SenseTime (0020.HK) — Leader Profile 👤

*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## CEO: Dr. Xu Li (徐立)

- **Role:** Chairman of the Board & CEO
- **Background:** PhD in Computer Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong. Joined SenseTime as co-founder. Became CEO after the passing of founding Chairman Tang Xiao'ou in December 2023.
- **Strengths:** Deep technical understanding of AI; led the company's pivot from computer vision to generative AI; architect of the "1+X" restructuring strategy
- **Communication style:** Candid about financials — publicly stated "we would be profitable immediately" if spin-offs are completed. Clear on strategic direction.
- **Key quote (Aug 2025):** "The speed of narrowing losses will only accelerate in the second half of this year."

## Co-Founders & Key Executives

- **Wang Xiaogang** — Co-founder, Executive Director, also Chairman of Ace Robotics (the embodied AI spin-off). Leading the robotics/world model strategy.
- **Lin Dahua** — Co-founder, Chief Scientist. Associate Professor at CUHK. Driving the multimodal AI / computer vision technical strategy. Key architect of the "Google of China" analogy for SenseTime's full-stack approach.
- **Yang Fan** — Co-founder. Expects computing power capacity to grow triple-digit percentage annually over next 2 years.

## Founding Story & Culture

- Founded 2014 in Hong Kong by CUHK computer science professors. Academic DNA.
- Late founder Tang Xiao'ou (passed Dec 2023) was a pioneer in computer vision and facial recognition. His wife established HKD 20M charity foundation in his name.
- Company culture transitioning from "professor-led" to "startup-led" via "Re-CoFound" initiative — encouraging executives to become "re co-founders" of independent business units.

## Insider Activity — RED FLAG ⚠️

- **July 2025:** SenseTime conducted a HKD 2.5B share placement at a 6% discount to the closing price. This is DILUTIVE. ~8.4% share count increase YoY.
- **No major insider BUYING reported.** The placement was for corporate purposes (GenAI expansion), not personal insider conviction.
- Share dilution has been persistent: 8.44% increase in FY2024, 3.56% in FY2023.
- The placement signals the company still needs external capital to fund operations — it's not yet self-sustaining.

## Assessment

**CEO Execution Score: 7/10**

Xu Li has executed well on the GenAI pivot — revenue from GenAI went from near-zero to 77% of total in ~2 years. Loss narrowing is real. The 1+X strategy is smart: keep the valuable core, spin off capital-intensive non-core businesses.

However:
- Still no profitability after 12 years
- Continued share dilution
- R&D spend (CNY 4.1B) exceeds total revenue (CNY 3.77B) — this is unsustainable
- No insider BUYING — management isn't putting their own money in

The spin-off strategy is the make-or-break moment. If Xu Li delivers on making the core profitable via spin-offs in 2026, he proves himself. If spin-offs stall and losses persist, patience will run out.

*Sources: SCMP interviews (Aug 2025, Jan 2026), ACN Newswire, SenseTime official*
