# SenseTime (0020.HK) — Structured Intelligence 🧠

*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## What This Tells Us About the Sector

1. **China's GenAI market is real and growing fast.** SenseTime's 73% GenAI revenue growth in 1H2025 isn't isolated — it reflects a genuine enterprise adoption wave driven by government policy ("AI Plus") and competitive pressure among Chinese firms to adopt AI.

2. **The "pure-play AI" business model is still unproven.** SenseTime is the largest pure-play AI company in China and it STILL loses money after 12 years. This tells us that AI software companies without distribution (unlike big tech) struggle to monetize at scale. The R&D cost of staying competitive in AI models is astronomical.

3. **Compute infrastructure is the real moat.** SenseTime's 25,000 PFLOPS of compute is a genuine barrier to entry. In China, where access to NVIDIA's latest chips is restricted by US sanctions, having domestic compute at scale is a strategic asset that only a handful of companies possess.

4. **The embodied AI wave is starting.** SenseTime's Ace Robotics spin-off, combined with moves by Tencent, Xiaomi, UBTech, and others into robotics/world models, signals that the next phase of AI investment is about the physical world, not just chatbots.

## Contrarian Angle — What's the Market Missing?

**The market prices SenseTime as a perpetual loss-maker.** Analyst targets (HKD 1.83) are BELOW the current price, and consensus expects losses through 2027. But:

1. The "1+X" spin-off strategy could flip the profitability narrative overnight. If Smart Auto and Healthcare are IPO'd as separate entities, the remaining "1" core (GenAI + CV) may already be near breakeven.
2. The DeepSeek effect lowered AI compute costs globally, but SenseTime BENEFITS — it can train more models for less money on its existing infrastructure, while selling compute to others.
3. SenseTime is becoming a "picks and shovels" play for Chinese AI — selling compute infrastructure, model APIs, and enterprise agents — not just building models. This is a more sustainable business model than competing on model benchmarks alone.

**The contrarian bet:** SenseTime is the only publicly-traded, pure-play AI infrastructure + model + application company in China. If China's AI market grows as fast as the US market has, SenseTime is dramatically undervalued relative to its position in the ecosystem.

## Risk Intelligence

- **US sanctions escalation** — The #1 existential risk. Any tightening of chip export controls to China could cut SenseTime off from the compute supply chain. Monitor US-China trade relations closely.
- **Share dilution** — Management has diluted at 3-8% per year. Another placement in 2026 is possible if spin-off IPOs don't raise enough capital.
- **Competition from DeepSeek** — DeepSeek's open-source models and extreme cost efficiency could commoditize the model layer, making SenseTime's model business less valuable.
