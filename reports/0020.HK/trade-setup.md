## Key Risk Factors

1. **US Entity List / Sanctions** — SenseTime has been on the US sanctions list since 2019. This limits access to cutting-edge NVIDIA chips and Western partnerships. Any escalation is a risk.
2. **Persistent Losses** — Net loss of CNY 3.3B TTM. R&D exceeds revenue.
3. **Dilution** — 8.4% share count increase in 2024. HKD 2.5B placement in July 2025.
4. **Gross Margin Compression** — Declining from 70%+ to 38.5% as business shifts to infra-heavy GenAI.
5. **Competition** — DeepSeek, Baidu (Ernie), Alibaba (Qwen), ByteDance (Doubao), Zhipu AI all competing in Chinese GenAI market.
6. **Geopolitical** — Taiwan tensions, US-China tech decoupling

## Risk Management

- **Stop-loss:** HKD 1.80 (-22%) — below analyst consensus and key support
- **Position sizing:** 2-3% of portfolio max — this is HIGH RISK (loss-making, sanctions risk, dilution)
