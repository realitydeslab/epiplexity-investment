# SenseTime (0020.HK) — Catalyst Map 🎯

*Research Date: March 9, 2026 | Price: HKD 2.30 | Market Cap: HKD 91B (~USD 11.7B)*

---

## Catalyst 1: FY2025 Annual Results (IMMINENT)

- **What:** Full-year 2025 earnings release. 1H2025 showed 36% revenue growth and 50% adjusted loss reduction. 2H2025 expected to be even stronger per CEO Xu Li's guidance ("speed of loss reduction in second half will not be low").
- **When:** Late March 2026 (HK-listed companies report by end of March)
- **Impact:** +15-25% if full-year shows continued GenAI acceleration and further loss narrowing; -10-15% if gross margins compress further
- **Probability:** HIGH — 1H momentum was clear, China AI Plus initiative boosts spending
- **Market expectation:** Consensus expects ~CNY 4.5-5.0B FY2025 revenue, modest loss improvement
- **Our edge:** Market may underestimate the acceleration — 1H was +64.55% vs prior year half. If 2H matches 1H's CNY 2.36B, full year hits CNY 4.7B+. GenAI now 77% of revenue and growing 73% — the traditional AI drag is mostly washed out.

## Catalyst 2: SenseNova V7 / Next Model Release

- **What:** SenseTime has been on a V5→V6→V6.5 cadence with major model releases at Tech Day (April) and WAIC (July). V6.5 already claimed parity with Gemini 2.5 Pro and Claude 4 Sonnet. V7 or V6.5+ expected at April 2026 Tech Day.
- **When:** Q2 2026 (April Tech Day, July WAIC)
- **Impact:** +10-20% on model launch announcements (V6 launch in Apr 2025 caused +36% single-day surge; V6.5 at WAIC 2025 also moved stock)
- **Probability:** HIGH — consistent annual cadence
- **Market expectation:** Incremental improvements expected
- **Our edge:** SenseTime's interleaved image-text thought chain technology is genuinely differentiated. If V7 shows autonomous agent capability or embodied AI breakthroughs, it could trigger rerating.

## Catalyst 3: X-Business Spin-offs / IPOs

- **What:** CEO Xu Li explicitly stated SenseTime would be "profitable immediately" if X businesses (Smart Auto, Healthcare, Robotics, Retail) are taken off balance sheet. Ace Robotics already launched separately. More spin-offs expected in 2026.
- **When:** Q2-Q4 2026
- **Impact:** +20-40% — this is the single biggest catalyst. A healthcare or auto IPO would crystallize hidden value and instantly make the core business profitable.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH — CEO committed publicly, Ace Robotics already carved out
- **Market expectation:** Market prices in continued losses; spin-offs not priced
- **Our edge:** The market still values SenseTime as a money-losing AI company. If spin-offs happen, the core GenAI business could show profitability, triggering a massive rerating.

## Catalyst 4: China "AI Plus" National Initiative

- **What:** China's State Council declared "AI Plus" as a key policy priority. Government procurement of domestic AI solutions accelerating. SenseTime is a national champion in AI.
- **When:** Throughout 2026 (ongoing policy implementation)
- **Impact:** +10-15% from sustained demand; government contracts tend to be large and multi-year
- **Probability:** HIGH — policy already announced and being implemented
- **Market expectation:** Broadly known but not fully priced into SenseTime specifically
- **Our edge:** SenseTime's partnership with Cambricon for domestic chip self-reliance + its compute infrastructure (25,000 petaflops) makes it a primary beneficiary of the "AI self-reliance" narrative.

## Catalyst 5: Embodied Intelligence / Robotics Expansion

- **What:** Ace Robotics (SenseTime-backed) launched Kairos 3.0 world model + robotic dogs. SenseTime's computer vision heritage gives it natural advantage in embodied AI.
- **When:** Product launches Q2-Q3 2026
- **Impact:** +10-20% — robotics is the next hot theme after LLMs
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — technology ready but commercial scale uncertain
- **Market expectation:** Market views SenseTime as an AI model company, not a robotics play
- **Our edge:** Kairos 3.0 is open-source, creating ecosystem effects. Partnership with Cambricon for domestic inference chips positions SenseTime as the "NVIDIA of Chinese embodied AI."

## Catalyst 6: MSCI / Index Inclusion

- **What:** MSCI recently tilted toward Chinese tech/AI stocks, adding semiconductor and AI companies. SenseTime at HKD 91B market cap is in range for index inclusion.
- **When:** May or November 2026 MSCI rebalance
- **Impact:** +5-10% from passive fund inflows
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — depends on liquidity and free-float requirements
- **Market expectation:** Not priced in
- **Our edge:** The trend toward adding Chinese AI stocks to indices is clear — SenseTime is the largest pure-play AI company in China.

## Catalyst 7: Profitability Inflection

- **What:** Adjusted loss narrowed 50% in 1H2025. Operating cash outflow narrowed 82%. CEO says "speed of loss narrowing will accelerate." Potential for first-ever quarterly profit in Q3/Q4 2026.
- **When:** H2 2026
- **Impact:** +25-50% — first profit for a company that's lost money every year since IPO would be transformative
- **Probability:** MEDIUM — depends on R&D discipline and spin-off execution
- **Market expectation:** Consensus doesn't expect profitability until 2027-2028
- **Our edge:** CEO's public commitment + spin-off strategy could pull this forward. If X businesses are spun off, core is already near breakeven.

## Catalyst 8: Raccoon AI Agent Scaling

- **What:** Raccoon (SenseTime's enterprise AI agent) surpassed 10M users and 3M for productivity tool. Education and finance editions deployed across 500+ institutions.
- **When:** Usage metrics at each earnings report; enterprise deals through 2026
- **Impact:** +5-10% per quarter as user metrics beat expectations
- **Probability:** HIGH — already on a steep adoption curve
- **Market expectation:** Modest expectations for enterprise AI monetization
- **Our edge:** Raccoon's data analysis capability claimed to match Claude 4 Opus while significantly outperforming o3. If enterprise ARPU improves, revenue could accelerate faster than modeled.
