# SenseTime (0020.HK) — Investment Thesis

*Research Date: March 9, 2026 | Price: HKD 2.30 | Market Cap: HKD 91B (~USD 11.7B)*

---

## The Thesis in One Sentence

SenseTime is China's largest pure-play AI company, trading at the cusp of a profitability inflection via spin-offs, with 73% GenAI revenue growth and 8 major catalysts in the next 12 months — but US sanctions risk, persistent losses, and share dilution make this a high-risk, high-conviction play.

## The Catalyst

**FY2025 annual results (late March 2026)** are imminent and could show full-year revenue of CNY 4.7B+ with GenAI now comprising 77% of total revenue, growing 73% YoY. CEO Xu Li has publicly committed that loss narrowing will "accelerate" in 2H2025. A strong earnings beat could trigger analyst upgrades and push the stock toward HKD 3.00.

The BIGGER catalyst: **X-business spin-offs.** If SenseTime successfully IPOs its Smart Auto or Healthcare divisions in 2026, the core GenAI + CV business could show immediate profitability. This would transform the investment narrative from "perpetual money-loser" to "profitable AI platform."

## The Trade

- **Entry:** HKD 2.30 (current) or HKD 2.00 on pullback
- **Target:** HKD 3.00-4.00 (base-to-bull, 12 months)
- **Stop:** HKD 1.80 (-22%)
- **Position size:** 2-3% of portfolio maximum

## The Risk

1. **Sanctions escalation** — existential if US tightens chip export controls further
2. **Perpetual losses** — R&D (CNY 4.1B) exceeds total revenue. No profit after 12 years.
3. **Dilution** — 8.4% share count increase in FY2024, HKD 2.5B placement in July 2025
4. **Competition** — DeepSeek, ByteDance, Alibaba all better-funded
5. **Margin compression** — Gross margins fell from 70% → 38.5% as business shifts to infra

## The Verdict

**SPECULATIVE BUY with strict risk management.**

This is NOT a safe position. It's a bet on three things:
1. China's AI market continues to grow rapidly (HIGH confidence)
2. SenseTime maintains its position as the leading pure-play beneficiary (MEDIUM confidence)
3. Management executes on spin-offs to unlock profitability (MEDIUM confidence)

If all three hit, HKD 4.00+ is achievable (74% upside). If any one fails significantly, HKD 1.50 is realistic (-35% downside). The risk/reward is favorable for a small position, but this is not a core holding — it's a catalyst trade.
