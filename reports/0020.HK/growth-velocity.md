## Gross Margin Concern ⚠️

| Year | Gross Margin |
|------|-------------|
| FY2020 | 70.6% |
| FY2021 | 69.7% |
| FY2022 | 66.8% |
| FY2023 | 44.1% |
| FY2024 | 42.9% |
| 1H2025 | 38.5% |

**Gross margins are declining** — from 70%+ (pure software) to 38.5% (hardware/infra-heavy). This is because GenAI revenue includes data center infrastructure and compute services, which carry lower margins than pure CV software. This is a structural shift, not a temporary issue.

## Unit Economics

- R&D: CNY 4.1B (FY2024) — still exceeds total revenue of CNY 3.77B
- R&D intensity: ~109% of revenue (extreme, though typical for pre-profit AI labs)
- SGA: CNY 2.1B (FY2024) — 56% of revenue
- **Total opex: ~165% of revenue** — still far from operating leverage
- Need revenue to roughly double to ~CNY 7-8B to reach operating breakeven at current cost structure

## Verdict

Growth is genuinely accelerating, driven by GenAI adoption in China. The inflection from declining revenue to 64.6% half-on-half growth is real. But the path to profitability requires either: (1) spin-offs to remove loss-making segments, or (2) massive revenue scaling to absorb the CNY 6.3B opex burden. Both are possible in 2026 but neither is certain.

**Growth Score: 8/10** (accelerating revenue, but margin compression and massive R&D create profitability uncertainty)
