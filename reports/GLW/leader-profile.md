# GLW (Corning) — Leader Profile 👤

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## CEO: Wendell P. Weeks

- **Tenure:** CEO since 2005 (~21 years). Chairman since 2007.
- **Background:** Joined Corning in 1983. Rose through telecom and optical divisions. Led company through 2001 fiber bust recovery, pivoted to Gorilla Glass/display, now pivoting to AI infrastructure.
- **Track record:** Navigated multiple technology cycles. Launched Springboard plan in 2023 and delivered 20% margin target one year early.
- **Public statements:** "We have transformed Corning's financial profile. We now have a highly profitable launch point for future growth." (Q4 2025 call)

## Insider Activity — RED FLAG ⚠️

**CEO Wendell Weeks sold 137,514 shares on Feb 26, 2026:**
- Sale price: ~$155.37/share
- Total value: **$21.37 million**
- Represents **15.48% decrease** in his direct holdings
- Remaining: 750,585 shares (~$116.6M at sale price)

**Assessment:** This is a significant insider sale near all-time highs. While CEOs often sell for diversification/tax reasons, the size (15% of holdings) and timing (after 50%+ YTD run) warrants caution. It suggests even the CEO thinks the stock may be ahead of itself in the near term.

## Leadership Assessment

**Strengths:**
- Weeks is a 40+ year Corning veteran who deeply understands the technology
- Proven ability to reinvent the company across cycles (telecom → display → mobile → AI)
- Springboard execution ahead of schedule demonstrates operational competence
- Long-tenured team provides stability

**Concerns:**

- Heavy insider selling at highs signals near-term caution

**Verdict:** Right leader for execution. The insider selling is a timing signal, not a thesis breaker — but it means we should be patient on entry.
