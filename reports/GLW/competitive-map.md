## Who Loses if Corning Wins?

- **CommScope** — Already struggling; if Corning locks up hyperscaler deals, CommScope loses share
- **Prysmian** — Corning's specialty fiber for AI is higher-margin and more differentiated than Prysmian's commodity cable
- **Copper cable manufacturers** — AI data centers switching from copper to fiber at accelerating rates

## Corning's Moat

1. **170+ years of glass/materials science IP** — impossible to replicate
2. **Specialty fiber for AI** — not commodity fiber; requires specific optical properties for high-bandwidth AI workloads
3. **Switching costs** — hyperscalers design data centers around Corning specs
4. **Scale** — largest specialty glass manufacturer globally
5. **Vertical integration** — from raw materials to finished optical cable
6. **Apple relationship** — Gorilla Glass is default for premium smartphones

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### Long GLW / Short COMM
- Corning captures AI fiber spend; CommScope continues to lose share
- CommScope has balance sheet issues; Corning is financially strong

### Long GLW + CIEN (Basket)
- Both benefit from fiber buildout — Corning sells the fiber, Ciena sells the optical networking equipment
- Correlated to same catalyst (AI data center buildout) but different risk profiles

### GLW as AI Infrastructure Hedge
- More defensive than NVDA (lower beta, dividend, diversified revenue)
- If AI capex shifts from compute to connectivity, GLW benefits disproportionately
