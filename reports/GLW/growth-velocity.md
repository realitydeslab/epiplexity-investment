# GLW (Corning) — Growth Velocity 📈

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

## Revenue Trajectory

| Period | Revenue | YoY Growth | Notes |
|--------|---------|------------|-------|
| FY 2024 | $13.1B | — | Pre-Springboard baseline |
| FY 2025 | $15.6B | +19% | Springboard in full effect |
| Q4 2025 | $4.2B | +20% QoQ implied | Beat estimates ($4.412B vs $4.396B expected) |
| Q1 2026 (guide) | $4.2-4.3B | +15% YoY | Continued acceleration |

**Verdict: ACCELERATING.** Revenue grew 19% full-year and management guided 15% YoY for Q1 — but the Optical Communications segment is growing at 24%+ and enterprise optical at 58%. The blended number understates the AI-driven acceleration.

**4 consecutive quarterly earnings beats in 2025.** Management achieved 20% operating margin target one year early.

## Guidance vs. Actual History

Corning has consistently beat or met guidance under the Springboard plan:
- Set $8B incremental sales target → upgraded to $11B
- Set 20% margin target for 2026 → achieved in 2025
- Pattern: **Under-promise, over-deliver**

## Enterprise Optical — The Growth Engine

- Enterprise optical sales jumped **58% in Q3 2025** from Gen AI adoption
- Q4 Optical Communications revenue up **24% YoY** to $1.7B
- Meta $6B deal validates multi-year demand runway
- India facility in H2 2026 adds capacity for continued growth

## Path to $7 EPS by 2030

Wall Street consensus: EPS trajectory from $2.52 (2025) → $3.08 (2026) → $7.01 (2030)
- Requires ~22% CAGR in earnings
- Achievable if Optical Communications maintains 20%+ growth and margins expand
- Risk: Display segment drag, auto weakness, FX headwinds

## Unit Economics

- Fiber optic is a **high-margin, recurring** business — data centers need ongoing cable runs
- Gorilla Glass has **high switching costs** — Apple, Samsung locked in
- Specialty glass manufacturing has **extreme barriers to entry** — decades of process knowledge
