# GLW (Corning) — Investment Thesis 📄

*Research date: March 8, 2026*

---

## The One-Liner

Corning is the monopoly fiber optic supplier for AI data centers, riding a multi-year $100B+ infrastructure buildout — but the stock ran 50% in 2 months and needs to consolidate.

## The Catalyst

**Meta's $6B fiber deal is just the beginning.** Every hyperscaler (Google, Amazon, Microsoft) needs 5x more optical connectivity for AI workloads. Corning is the only company that can supply specialty fiber at scale. Additional deals worth $2-5B each are in the pipeline but NOT in guidance.

## The Trade

- **Entry:** $105-115 on pullback (current ~$123 is chasing)
- **Target:** $150-180 over 12 months
- **Stop:** $100
- **Position:** 3-5% of portfolio
- **Scale-in approach:** 1/3 positions at three levels ($123, $110, $100)

## The Numbers

- FY2025 revenue: $15.6B (+19% YoY)
- FY2025 net income: $1.6B (+215% YoY)
- Optical Communications: $1.7B/quarter, growing 24% YoY
- Enterprise optical: +58% in Q3 2025
- Forward P/E: 42x on $3.08 consensus EPS
- Springboard target: $11B incremental sales by 2028

## The Risk

1. **Valuation** — 42x forward P/E prices in perfection. Any miss punishes hard.
2. **CEO selling** — Weeks sold $21.4M (15% of holdings) at $155 in late Feb. Smart money signal.
3. **AI capex cycle risk** — If hyperscalers pause spending, Corning is collateral damage.
4. **Concentration** — Meta deal is single-customer risk. Optical is 40% of revenue.

## The Verdict

**BUY on pullback. WAIT at current levels.**

Corning is a fantastic AI infrastructure play with genuine monopoly characteristics in specialty fiber. The thesis is strong: irreplaceable technology, massive demand runway, proven management execution. But the stock has gotten ahead of itself. Patient entry at $105-115 offers 35-60% upside with manageable downside risk. Chasing at $123+ after CEO selling and a 50% YTD run is poor risk/reward.
