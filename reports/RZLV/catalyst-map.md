# RZLV Catalyst Map — Rezolve AI PLC
*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

## 1. Reward Loyalty Acquisition Integration ($230M Deal) 🔥
- **What:** Rezolve acquired Reward Loyalty UK for $230M (announced Feb 2026), adding fintech banking/loyalty capabilities to its AI commerce platform. This is transformational — adds real recurring revenue, financial services vertical, and cross-sell into banking customers.
- **When:** Integration Q1-Q2 2026, revenue contribution from Q2 2026 onward
- **Impact:** +30-50% incremental revenue; if Reward contributes ~$100M+ annually, gets RZLV significantly closer to $350M 2026 guidance. Could drive 50-100% stock move on successful integration proof.
- **Probability:** High (deal is closed, not speculative)
- **Market expectation:** Skeptical — stock dropped ~7% on announcement (dilution fears, acquisition integration risk)
- **Our edge:** Market is underpricing the revenue acceleration. Reward brings real, contracted banking revenue. At $2.72 stock price, the market is pricing almost zero value for this acquisition.
