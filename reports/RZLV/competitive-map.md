# RZLV Competitive Map — Rezolve AI PLC
*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

## Competitive Landscape: AI-Powered Commerce

### Direct Competitors

**1. Salesforce (CRM) — Agentforce Platform**
- Market Cap: $184.5B | Revenue: ~$37B
- Thesis: Enterprise AI agents for commerce, CRM, and customer service
- Advantage over RZLV: 150,000+ enterprise customers, massive distribution, trusted brand
- RZLV advantage: Retail-specific LLM (brainpowa) vs. Salesforce's general-purpose approach; speed of deployment; lower cost
- Verdict: RZLV is a niche player competing for a sliver of CRM's TAM

**2. Shopify (SHOP) — AI Commerce Tools**
- Market Cap: ~$100B | Revenue: ~$8B
- Thesis: AI-powered merchant tools, Shopify Magic, Sidekick AI
- Advantage: Largest commerce platform, millions of merchants
- RZLV advantage: Enterprise-focused (top 500 retailers) vs. Shopify's SMB focus; AI-native architecture
- Verdict: Different market segments, minimal direct overlap

**3. Adobe Commerce (ADBE)**
- Market Cap: $116.5B | Revenue: ~$20B
- Thesis: AI-powered digital commerce (Sensei AI)
- Advantage: Enterprise installed base, creative tools integration
- RZLV advantage: Conversational AI focus vs. Adobe's workflow AI; purpose-built vs. bolted on
- Verdict: Adobe is the incumbent RZLV must displace in enterprise retail

**4. Dynamic Yield (acquired by Mastercard)**
- AI-powered personalization for retail
- Advantage: Mastercard distribution, proven at scale
- RZLV advantage: Full-stack (discovery→transaction→post-purchase) vs. Dynamic Yield's personalization-only

**5. Coveo (CVO.TO)**
- AI search and recommendations for enterprise commerce
- Market Cap: ~$1.5B | Revenue: ~$130M
- Most comparable public company to RZLV
- RZLV advantage: Conversational AI + transaction completion vs. Coveo's search focus

### Who Benefits from the SAME Catalysts?
If agentic commerce becomes real, these also win:
- **Shopify (SHOP)** — merchant-side agent infrastructure
- **Stripe** (private) — payment rails for AI agents
- **Salesforce (CRM)** — enterprise agent orchestration
- **Google (GOOGL)** — Shopping AI, search commerce
- **Microsoft (MSFT)** — Copilot commerce extensions

### Who Loses if RZLV Wins?
- **Traditional e-commerce search providers** (Algolia, Bloomreach, Coveo)
- **Legacy loyalty platforms** (now competing with RZLV+Reward combo)
- **Generic chatbot providers** (Intercom, Drift/Salesloft for commerce use cases)
- **Manual merchandising teams** at retailers

### Pair Trade Opportunities

**1. Long RZLV / Short AI (C3.ai)**
- Rationale: Both trade at extreme valuations to revenue, but RZLV has specific retail vertical focus and named customers, while C3.ai has struggled with enterprise adoption
- C3.ai market cap: $1.29B — comparable to RZLV
- Risk: C3.ai has more diversified customer base

**2. Long RZLV / Short FIVN (Five9)**
- Rationale: Both in AI-powered customer interaction. RZLV focused on commerce, FIVN on contact center. RZLV has higher growth ceiling but more risk.
- Five9 market cap: $1.38B — comparable
- Risk: FIVN has real revenue ($850M+) and is profitable

**3. Long RZLV as part of "agentic commerce basket"**
- Own RZLV + SHOP + CRM for diversified agentic commerce exposure
- RZLV provides the high-beta, high-upside component
