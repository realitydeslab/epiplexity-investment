# RZLV Leader Profile — Daniel M. Wagner
*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

## CEO: Daniel M. Wagner — Chairman & CEO

### Background
- Founded Rezolve AI in 2016
- Previously founded MAID in 1984, which listed on LSE (1994) and NASDAQ (1995) — making him one of the youngest public company CEOs at the time
- Serial entrepreneur with 40+ years in tech/digital commerce
- London-based, UK national
- Has been the face of RZLV — does media appearances (Schwab Network, CNBC) to pitch the $500M ARR vision

### Execution Assessment ⚠️
**Strengths:**
- Successfully navigated SPAC listing and maintained NASDAQ listing
- Secured Microsoft and Google distribution partnerships — non-trivial for a ~26-employee company
- Closed $250M oversubscribed financing from institutional investors
- Completed $230M Reward Loyalty acquisition — shows M&A capability
- Government of Singapore investment validates credibility
- Board includes former Visa CCO, former UK Ambassador to Japan — credible governance

**Concerns:**
- Revenue history is almost zero ($188K in FY2024) despite company existing since 2016 — 8 years with near-zero revenue raises execution questions
- Jim Cramer publicly noted RZLV is "still a long way from profitability" (Jan 2026)
- Massive SG&A spending ($128M in H2 2024 alone) vs. minimal revenue — cash burn is extreme
- The revenue guidance ($40M→$350M→$500M ARR) is extraordinary by any standard — requires near-flawless execution
- Only 26 employees at last count (growing to 61 per Finviz) — can this team manage a $350M revenue business?

### Insider Ownership
- **Insider ownership: 34.18%** — Very high, aligned with shareholders
- CEO Daniel Wagner holds significant stake
- No recent insider transactions noted (0.00% insider trans)
- Note: As an FPI (Foreign Private Issuer), RZLV is exempt from SEC insider filing requirements, making insider trading visibility limited

### Key Hires (Recent)
- **Crispin Lowery** (CRO) — ex-Microsoft Retail EMEA head, also ex-Google, Apple, Tesco. Critical hire for enterprise sales.
- **Sauvik Banerjjee** (President Global Professional Services & CDO) — ex-Accenture Global Commerce Head, founding CTO of Tata Digital. Brings enterprise delivery expertise.
- **Arthur Yao** (COO/CFO) — "Deputy CEO" role, strategic partnerships focus, global network
- **Justin King CBE** (Senior Advisor) — Former CEO of Sainsbury's (2004-2014), massive UK retail credibility
- **Christian Angermayer** (Senior Advisor) — Prominent tech/biotech investor

### Leadership Verdict
The leadership team is **surprisingly credible** for a company of this size. The CRO from Microsoft, the advisor who ran Sainsbury's, the Singapore government backing — these are not typical micro-cap hires. However, the gap between the team's pedigree and the company's revenue history (nearly $0 for 8 years) is the central risk. Either they've been building something real that's now scaling, or this is an elaborate story stock. The next 2-3 quarters of revenue prints will tell.

**Grade: B- (credible team, unproven execution at scale)**
