The 96% gross margin and enterprise contracts are real positive signals. But the extreme back-end loading (only $6.3M in H1 of a $40M year) and the historical near-zero revenue for 8 years are red flags.

**Key question: Does ARR = recognized revenue, or is there a massive gap between "contracted" and "delivered"?**
