# RZLV Investment Thesis — Rezolve AI PLC
*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## The Catalyst
Rezolve AI is a **London-based AI commerce company** ($1.08B market cap, $2.72 stock) positioned at the intersection of three converging megatrends: **conversational AI, agentic commerce, and fintech loyalty.** The $230M acquisition of Reward Loyalty (Feb 2026) transforms RZLV from a pure-play AI story into a commerce+fintech platform with real banking revenue. Management guides $350M FY2026 revenue and $500M ARR exit rate — consensus is $315M — at 3.4x forward P/S vs. 50-100x for comparable AI platforms.

**The 12-month catalyst:** FY2025 annual results (expected Q1 2026) proving the $40M revenue target was real, followed by combined quarterly prints showing Reward acquisition revenue contribution. If the first combined quarter shows $50M+, the stock re-rates to $5-8 quickly.

## The Trade
- **Entry:** $2.50-2.80 (scale in, 30% now / 40% on H2 revenue proof / 30% on FY2026 Q1 beat)
- **Bull target:** $8.00 (+194%) — FY2026 revenue hits $250M+, 4x forward P/S
- **Base target:** $4.50 (+65%) — FY2026 revenue $150-200M, gradual re-rating
- **Stop-loss:** $2.00 (-26%) — below support floor
- **Position size:** 1-2% of portfolio MAX (speculative)

## The Risk
This is a **binary stock.** Either:
- Revenue materializes → 100-300% upside (Palantir-like re-rating)
- Revenue doesn't → -60-80% downside (story stock collapse)

**Key risks:**
1. FY2025 revenue miss (need $35M in H2 alone)
2. Reward integration failure
3. Further dilution (shares already 2.4x'd in 1 year)
4. $164M SG&A vs. $5M revenue — extreme cash burn
5. CEO credibility gap (8 years of near-zero revenue before 2025)

## The Verdict
**SPECULATIVE BUY — with strict risk management.**

RZLV is the highest-risk, highest-reward play on our watchlist. The 7 analysts unanimously rate it Strong Buy with avg PT of $10 (+268%), the Microsoft/Google partnerships are real, the Reward acquisition adds substance, and the 34% insider ownership provides alignment. But the revenue history is essentially zero, the dilution has been severe, and the guidance is extraordinary to the point of incredulity.

**The right approach is small position, scaled entry, tight stop.** Wait for H2 2025 revenue proof before full conviction.

**Epiplexity Score: 76/100** — High conviction if revenue materializes, but too much execution risk for a higher score.
