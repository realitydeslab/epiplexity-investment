# ALDX — Leader Profile 👤

## Todd C. Brady, MD, PhD — President & CEO

### Background
- Co-founded Aldeyra Therapeutics (originally Aldexa Therapeutics, founded 2004)
- MD/PhD with deep expertise in ophthalmology and immunology
- Has led the company through entire reproxalap development cycle — NDA submission, CRL, resubmission
- Presented at Oppenheimer 36th Annual Healthcare Conference (Feb 25, 2026) — highlighted reproxalap ahead of March 16 PDUFA
- Ultra-lean operator: runs the entire company with **only 8 employees**

### Assessment
- **Strength:** Scientist-CEO who understands the science deeply. Has navigated a brutal FDA process (initial rejection → resubmission → PDUFA extension) without giving up. Secured AbbVie as commercialization partner — smart move for an 8-person company.
- **Weakness:** No commercial experience. If AbbVie doesn't exercise option, Aldeyra has zero infrastructure to launch. The 8-employee count is both a feature (low burn) and a risk (no bench depth).
- **Verdict:** RIGHT person for R&D phase. AbbVie partnership is the smart play for commercialization. If approved, Brady's job is done — AbbVie takes over.
