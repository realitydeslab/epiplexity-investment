## Pre-Revenue Biotech — Growth = Pipeline Progress

Aldeyra is a pre-revenue company. Traditional revenue growth metrics don't apply. Growth velocity here is measured by **clinical/regulatory progress** and **loss reduction** (operational efficiency).
