## The Catalyst
**FDA PDUFA decision on reproxalap for dry eye disease — March 16, 2026 (7 days).**

Reproxalap is a first-in-class RASP modulator — entirely novel mechanism of action in a $7-8B market dominated by aging immunosuppressants (Restasis going generic) and mediocre alternatives. The Chamber Phase 3 trial met endpoints with rapid, durable symptom relief. AbbVie (maker of Restasis) holds an option to commercialize, worth ~$200M+ in milestones.

## The Trade
- **Entry:** $5.26 (current price)
- **Upside target:** $9-15 on approval (71-185%)
- **Downside target:** $1.50-2.50 on rejection (-52-71%)
- **Position size:** 2-3% of portfolio MAX (binary event)
- **Timeframe:** 7 days to catalyst, 12 months to full thesis

## The Risk
The field trial **missed** its primary symptom endpoint. This is the elephant in the room. The FDA could issue another CRL. The PDUFA was already extended once (Dec 2025 → March 2026). Probability of approval: ~55-60%.

**What kills it:** CRL on March 16. No recovery for months. Stock to near-cash value ($0.91/share + pipeline optionality).
