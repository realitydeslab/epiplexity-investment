- **Post-approval:** Trail at $7.00 (allow for normal volatility)
- **Post-CRL:** Exit immediately at market open. Don't hope for recovery.

### Position Sizing
- **Max 2-3% of portfolio** — this is a pure binary biotech play
- Size for 100% loss possibility (CRL → stock to $1.50)

### What Kills the Trade
1. **CRL on March 16** — immediate -50-70% gap down. No recovery for months.

3. **Post-approval manufacturing/safety issues** — rare but possible

- If ALDX fails, other micro-cap biotech will feel sympathy selling
