# ALDX — Competitive Map 🗺️

## Dry Eye Disease (DED) Landscape

The DED market is ~$7-8B globally and growing, driven by screen time, aging demographics, and increasing diagnosis rates. Current treatments have significant limitations.

---

| Company | Drug | Mechanism | Status | Revenue |
|---------|------|-----------|--------|---------|
| **AbbVie/Allergan** | Restasis (cyclosporine) | Immunosuppressant | Marketed (going generic) | Peak ~$4B/yr, declining |
| **Bausch+Lomb** | Xiidra (lifitegrast) | LFA-1 antagonist | Marketed | ~$500M/yr |
| **Novartis** | Ikervis (cyclosporine) | Immunosuppressant | Marketed (EU) | ~$200M/yr |
| **Sun Pharma** | CEQUA (cyclosporine) | Immunosuppressant | Marketed | Growing |
| **Novaliq** | Vevye (perfluorohexyloctane) | Lubricant/anti-evaporative | Marketed (2023) | Early stage |
| **Tyrvaya** (Viatris) | Varenicline nasal spray | Cholinergic agonist | Marketed | Growing |
| **Aldeyra** | **Reproxalap** | **RASP modulator (NOVEL)** | **NDA (PDUFA Mar 16)** | **$0** |

### Reproxalap's Competitive Advantage
- **First-in-class RASP modulator** — entirely new mechanism targeting reactive aldehyde species
- Rapid onset of action (key differentiator vs. Restasis which takes weeks)
- Addresses both signs AND symptoms (most competitors address one or the other)
- Topical eye drop (familiar format for patients/doctors)
- AbbVie commercialization = instant access to largest ophthalmology salesforce globally

### Reproxalap's Competitive Weakness
- Field trial missed primary symptom endpoint (competitors have cleaner data packages)
- New mechanism = prescriber education needed
- Entering a market where Restasis generics are driving price pressure

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### 1. Long ALDX / Short BLCO (Bausch+Lomb)
- **Thesis:** If reproxalap approved, AbbVie's commercialization machine takes share from Xiidra
- **Risk:** BLCO is diversified beyond eye care; correlation imperfect

### 2. Long ALDX + Long ABBV
- **Thesis:** Both benefit from approval. ABBV gets revenue growth, ALDX gets milestones.
- **Risk:** If reproxalap fails, both suffer (ABBV minimally, ALDX catastrophically)

### 3. Long ALDX / Hedge with Puts
- **Thesis:** Binary event demands defined risk. Buy stock + buy puts at $3-4 strike.
- **Cost:** Options premium will be elevated pre-PDUFA
