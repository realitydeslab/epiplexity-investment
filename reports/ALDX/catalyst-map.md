# ALDX — Catalyst Map 🎯

## Aldeyra Therapeutics | March 9, 2026

---

### Catalyst #1: FDA PDUFA Decision — Reproxalap for Dry Eye Disease 🔴 CRITICAL
- **What:** FDA decision on reproxalap NDA (resubmission) for treatment of dry eye disease (DED). This is a first-in-class RASP (reactive aldehyde species) modulator — entirely novel mechanism of action.
- **When:** March 16, 2026 (7 DAYS AWAY). Extended from original PDUFA date in December 2025.
- **Impact:** +80-150% on approval ($9-13), -50-70% on CRL ($1.50-2.50). This is a BINARY event.
- **Probability:** Medium (55-60% approval). Chamber Phase 3 met symptom endpoints with rapid/durable relief. BUT field trial missed primary symptom endpoint — this is the core FDA concern. Resubmission suggests company addressed prior deficiencies.
- **Market expectation:** Market is pricing ~40-45% probability of approval based on current $5.26 price vs. $9.50 analyst PT. Street is cautious due to field trial miss.
- **Our edge:** The PDUFA extension (not rejection) is a positive signal — FDA requested additional info, didn't issue CRL outright. If FDA weighs Chamber trial success + field trial supportive signals + novel mechanism in unmet-need area, approval is more likely than market implies.

### Catalyst #2: AbbVie Option Exercise on Approval
- **What:** AbbVie holds an option agreement to commercialize reproxalap for both DED and allergic conjunctivitis. On FDA approval, AbbVie is expected to exercise this option, triggering ~$200M+ in upfront/milestone payments to Aldeyra.
- **When:** Immediately following FDA approval (March 16+)
- **Impact:** +30-50% incremental on top of approval move. Validates commercial potential. AbbVie (makers of Restasis) bringing reproxalap to market transforms Aldeyra's revenue trajectory.
- **Probability:** High (contingent on approval). AbbVie wouldn't maintain the option agreement without intent.
- **Market expectation:** Partially priced into analyst targets. But full milestone value likely underappreciated.
- **Our edge:** AbbVie as commercialization partner removes Aldeyra's biggest weakness — no salesforce, 8 employees. This is essentially an acquisition-in-waiting.

### Catalyst #3: Allergic Conjunctivitis NDA Expansion
- **What:** Reproxalap also at NDA stage for allergic conjunctivitis (also under AbbVie option). Approval for DED would fast-track this indication.
- **When:** Q2-Q3 2026 (likely 3-6 months post-DED approval)
- **Impact:** +15-25%. Expands TAM significantly.
- **Probability:** High if DED approved (same drug, similar mechanism).
- **Market expectation:** Not in most models yet.
- **Our edge:** Two indications from one NDA cycle = double the revenue opportunity.

### Catalyst #4: ADX-2191 Orphan Drug Pipeline Progress
- **What:** ADX-2191 (methotrexate intravitreal injection) for primary vitreoretinal lymphoma (PVRL) and retinitis pigmentosa (RP). Has FDA Orphan Drug designation + Fast Track + EMA Orphan designation.
- **When:** Ongoing clinical development through 2026
- **Impact:** +10-20% on positive data readouts. These are small-patient-population drugs with premium pricing potential.
- **Probability:** Medium. Early stage but multiple regulatory incentives.
- **Market expectation:** Zero value assigned by most models.
- **Our edge:** Orphan + Fast Track = accelerated pathway. Pipeline optionality is free at current valuation.

### Catalyst #5: RASP Platform Expansion into CNS/Systemic Diseases
- **What:** Nov 2025 announcement expanded RASP platform to CNS/neuroinflammatory diseases (ADX-248), plus oral formulations for atopic dermatitis and obesity/hypertriglyceridemia.
- **When:** Preclinical through 2026-2027
- **Impact:** +5-15% on positive preclinical data. Long-term platform value.
- **Probability:** Low-Medium. Very early stage.
- **Market expectation:** Zero value.
- **Our edge:** If reproxalap validates the RASP mechanism, the entire platform gets re-rated.

### Catalyst #6: Short Squeeze Potential

- **When:** March 16+ (triggered by approval)

- **Our edge:** Asymmetric — shorts are taking massive risk into a binary event with limited downside if they're wrong but catastrophic covering if approval hits.
