# Shift4 Payments (FOUR) — Leader Profile 👤

*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## Founder: Jared Isaacman

- **Role:** Founder (1999), former CEO. Now serving as **15th Administrator of NASA** (confirmed Dec 17, 2025).
- **Age:** 43 (born Feb 11, 1983)
- **Background:** High-school dropout turned billionaire. Founded Shift4 at age 16 in his parents' basement. Also founded Draken International (fighter jet training for US military). Commanded Inspiration4 (first all-civilian spaceflight) and Polaris Dawn (first private spacewalk).
- **Net worth:** ~$1.4B (May 2025)
- **Insider ownership:** ~29-35% of shares outstanding
- **Insider buying:** The watchlist thesis notes "$13.6M in share purchases" — Isaacman historically has been a buyer, not seller. His 35% insider ownership represents enormous skin-in-the-game even from NASA.

### Isaacman Assessment
- **Visionary founder** who built Shift4 from a basement to $4.2B revenue over 27 years
- **Musk ecosystem connections** — SpaceX astronaut, xAI partnership, Starlink payments processing
- Resigned CEO role upon NASA confirmation, but his ~35% stake means his interests remain deeply aligned
- The NASA role adds brand prestige but creates leadership transition risk
- Key question: Can Shift4 execute without its founder-CEO?

---

## Current CEO: Taylor Lauber

- **Appointed:** Effective upon Isaacman's NASA confirmation (late 2025)
- **Background:** Previously President/COO of Shift4. Deep operational experience.
- **Public appearances:** Spoke at ICR Conference (Jan 2026), NYSE interview (Feb 2026) — positioned as "growth through payments technology innovation"
- **Assessment:** Lauber is a known quantity internally. He ran operations while Isaacman was doing SpaceX missions. The transition appears smooth.
- **Risk:** Lauber lacks Isaacman's celebrity/dealmaker profile. M&A pace may slow.

---

## Key Governance Changes (Feb 2026)

1. **Single share class conversion** — Removed Isaacman's super-voting Class B/C stock. Now standard Class A only.
2. **Eliminated Tax Receivable Obligations** to founder — removed a structural overhang.
3. These moves signal: (a) Isaacman is fully stepping back from operational control, (b) the company is positioning for institutional-grade governance.
