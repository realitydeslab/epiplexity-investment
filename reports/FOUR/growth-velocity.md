## Path to Full Earnings Power

The $4.5B debt load is the bear case. Here's the math:
- **FY2026 EBITDA estimate:** ~$950M-1.0B (based on margin trends)
- **Interest expense run-rate:** ~$240M/year
- **Debt/EBITDA:** Currently 5.7x → targeting 3-4x within 2 years via EBITDA growth
- **FCF after interest:** Still ~$400-500M → can service debt AND delever

The company doesn't need to pay down debt aggressively — it just needs to grow into it.
