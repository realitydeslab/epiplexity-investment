## Competitive Position

### Shift4's Moat: Integrated Software + Payments

Shift4 isn't just a payment processor — it's an **integrated commerce platform** combining:
1. **SkyTab POS** — purpose-built for restaurants & hospitality
2. **Payment processing** — end-to-end, not just gateway
3. **Business intelligence** (Lighthouse analytics)
4. **Venue/stadium technology** (VenueNext acquisition)
5. **E-commerce** (Shift4Shop)
6. **International** (Finaro for cross-border, Global Blue for tax-free shopping)

### Why Shift4 Wins
- **Vertical integration:** Most competitors are either payment processors OR POS providers. Shift4 does both.
- **Enterprise focus:** While Square/Block targets SMBs, Shift4 targets large merchants — stadiums, hotel chains, restaurant groups. Higher volume, stickier contracts.
- **M&A machine:** Aggressive, strategic acquisitions to fill gaps (Finaro → Europe, VenueNext → venues, Worldline NA → enterprise merchants, Global Blue → international luxury).
