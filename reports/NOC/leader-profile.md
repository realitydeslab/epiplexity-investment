# NOC Leader Profile — Northrop Grumman

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## CEO: Kathy Warden

- **Title:** Chair, Chief Executive Officer and President
- **Tenure:** CEO since January 2019; Chair since 2020
- **Background:** Rose through Northrop after joining in 2008. Led Mission Systems before becoming CEO. Engineering and business background — unusual dual competence for defense CEO.
- **Track record:** Navigated B-21 program through development into production. Managed the B-2/B-21 margin transition crisis in 2023 (one-time charges). Company recovered strongly in 2024-2025.
- **Assessment:** Strong execution-focused leader. Right person for the B-21 production ramp, which is the defining challenge for the next 5 years. Has credibility with DoD and Congress.
