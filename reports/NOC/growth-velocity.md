# NOC Growth Velocity — Northrop Grumman

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## Revenue Growth: ACCELERATING ⬆️

### Quarterly Revenue (Last 4 Quarters)
| Quarter | Revenue | YoY Growth |
|---------|---------|------------|
| Q1 2025 | $9.47B | -6.56% |
| Q2 2025 | $10.35B | +1.30% |

**Clear acceleration pattern:** From -6.56% in Q1 to +9.60% in Q4. This is a meaningful inflection.

## Free Cash Flow: STRONG ACCELERATION ⬆️⬆️

| Year | FCF | Growth | FCF/Share |
|------|-----|--------|-----------|
| FY2022 | $1.47B | -31.9% | $9.42 |
| FY2023 | $2.10B | +43.3% | $13.82 |
| FY2024 | $2.62B | +24.8% | $17.79 |
| FY2025 | $3.31B | +26.2% | $23.00 |

Three consecutive years of 25%+ FCF growth. This is the most important metric — it funds buybacks, dividends, and debt reduction.

## Guidance vs. Actual

Northrop has consistently guided conservatively and beat:
- FY2024: Beat revenue and EPS guidance
- FY2025: Strong Q4 suggests likely beat on initial guidance

## Share Count Reduction

Steady ~2.4% annual reduction. 161M shares (FY2021) → 143M (Q4 2025). Aggressive buyback program funded by expanding FCF.

## Backlog

Northrop's total backlog exceeds $85B (estimated based on program wins). B-21 and Sentinel alone represent $150B+ in lifetime value. This is a revenue visibility story unmatched in defense.

## Verdict: Growth Accelerating

The headline FY2025 growth of 2.24% is misleading. Q4 at 9.6% shows the real trajectory. FCF is the star metric — 26% growth with decades of backlog visibility. Consensus FY2026 EPS of $28.27 looks beatable.
