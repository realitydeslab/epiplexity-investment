# NOC Catalyst Map — Northrop Grumman

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## 1. B-21 Raider Production Ramp 🎯

- **What:** Low-rate initial production (LRIP) scaling toward 100+ planned bombers. Flight testing ongoing with multiple airframes. Northrop explicitly states "accelerating B-21 Raider production." World's first sixth-generation aircraft.
- **When:** Throughout 2026; key milestones as flight test program expands and production lots awarded
- **Impact:** +10-15%. B-21 is the largest defense program by value ($80B+ lifetime). Each production milestone de-risks the single biggest revenue driver for the next decade.
- **Probability:** High — program is well past first flight and in active test/production
- **Market expectation:** Gradual ramp priced in; market underestimates pace of production acceleration
- **Our edge:** B-21 transition from development to production flips margin profile — production margins significantly higher than development losses that dragged 2023 results (-$393M operating loss in Q4 2023)

## 2. Q1 2026 Earnings (April 21, 2026) 📊

- **What:** First 2026 quarterly report. Q4 2025 showed strong 9.6% YoY revenue growth to $11.71B
- **When:** April 21, 2026
- **Impact:** +5-8% on beat, -5-8% on miss
- **Probability:** Medium-High — Q4 showed acceleration after sluggish Q1 2025 (-6.56% YoY)
- **Market expectation:** Consensus EPS ~$28.27 for FY2026 (slight decline YoY). Consensus revenue $44.3B (+5.6%)
- **Our edge:** Q4 2025 momentum (9.6% growth, FCF $3.24B) suggests 2026 guidance will be raised

## 3. European/NATO Defense Spending Surge 🌍

- **What:** NATO allies committing to 3.5%+ GDP defense targets. ReArm Europe initiative. Northrop sells Triton drones, IBCS, missile defense to NATO allies
- **When:** Ongoing; major contract announcements H1-H2 2026
- **Impact:** +8-12%. New international revenue streams
- **Probability:** High — European defense budgets approved
- **Market expectation:** Market focused on European primes; underweighting US primes' international exposure
- **Our edge:** IBCS (Integrated Battle Command System) is critical NATO interoperability — less covered than F-35

## 4. Sentinel (GBSD) ICBM Program 🚀

- **What:** Ground Based Strategic Deterrent replacing Minuteman III — $100B+ lifetime value
- **When:** Key design reviews throughout 2026
- **Impact:** +5-10%
- **Probability:** High — bipartisan nuclear modernization
- **Market expectation:** Stable program, priced as utility-like
- **Our edge:** 30-year revenue stream not captured in near-term P/E

## 5. Space Systems Expansion 🛰️

- **What:** Next-gen satellite constellations, Space Development Agency contracts, missile warning/tracking
- **When:** Contract awards throughout 2026
- **Impact:** +3-5% per major award
- **Probability:** Medium-High

## 6. Autonomous Systems & AI Integration 🤖

- **What:** MQ-4C Triton deployments, CCA subcontract potential, AI mission systems
- **When:** H1-H2 2026
- **Impact:** +3-5%
- **Probability:** Medium

## 7. FY2026 Defense Budget 📋

- **What:** Expected to exceed $900B with bipartisan support
- **When:** H1 2026
- **Impact:** +3-5% on favorable; risk on CR disruption
- **Probability:** High for increase

## 8. Share Buyback Acceleration 💰

- **What:** ~2.4% annual share reduction. FCF $3.3B in FY2025 (+26% YoY) enables aggressive returns
- **When:** Ongoing quarterly
- **Impact:** +2-3% EPS accretion
- **Probability:** High
