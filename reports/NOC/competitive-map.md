## Who Loses if NOC Wins?

- **Boeing:** Continued defense execution problems push work to Northrop
- **Legacy munitions makers:** Precision weapons + hypersonics shift spend toward advanced tech
- **Small defense contractors:** Consolidation around top 5 primes accelerates

## Pair Trade Opportunities

1. **Long NOC / Short BA** — B-21 execution vs Boeing defense mismanagement. Boeing's defense division has been bleeding; Northrop taking share.

2. **Long NOC + LMT basket** — Both are "must-own" for defense budgets. Different programs, similar macro tailwinds. Diversified defense exposure.

3. **Long NOC / Short consumer discretionary** — NOC's 0.01 beta means it's essentially a defensive asset. Pairs well against cyclical exposure in a portfolio.

## NOC's Moat

1. **Program lock-in:** B-21 and Sentinel are sole-source, decades-long programs. No competitive threat.
2. **Security clearances:** Classified work creates enormous barrier to entry
3. **Systems integration:** Ability to integrate across air, space, cyber is unique
4. **Nuclear certification:** Nuclear weapons handling certification limits competitors to a handful of companies
