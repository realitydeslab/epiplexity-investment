# NOC Structured Intelligence — Northrop Grumman

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## What This Tells Us About the Sector

1. **Defense is in a structural upcycle.** NOC's 69% 1-year gain isn't an anomaly — the entire defense sector is repricing for a world of permanent great-power competition. US defense budgets heading toward $1T. Europe rearming at WW2-pace urgency.

2. **Production > Development.** The 2023 margin collapse (B-21 development charges) and 2024-25 recovery illustrates the key defense investing rule: development programs destroy margins; production programs print cash. NOC is at the inflection point.

3. **FCF is the real metric.** Revenue growth looks modest (2.24% FY2025) but FCF grew 26%. Defense contractors' real value is in cash generation from locked-in programs. P/E understates the value; P/FCF tells the real story.

## Contrarian Angle

**What the market is missing:**

The average analyst PT ($675) is 10.7% BELOW the current price. This is unusual — it means the stock has outrun the sell-side. Two interpretations:
1. **Bullish:** Analysts are behind. Defense spending increases aren't yet fully modeled. PTs will ratchet up after Q1 2026 earnings (Apr 21). This happened with Rheinmetall — analysts chased the stock up.
2. **Bearish:** The easy money is made. The 69% 1-year run priced in the European rearmament thesis. Risk/reward less favorable at $756 vs. $450.

**Our view:** The structural thesis (B-21 production, Sentinel, European demand) has years to run. But entry at current levels requires patience and willingness to hold through potential 10-15% drawdowns.
