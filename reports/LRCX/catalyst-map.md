# LRCX Catalyst Map — Lam Research

*Research date: March 9, 2026 | Price: $211.15*

## 1. S&P 100 Index Addition (March 2026)

- **What:** LRCX added to S&P 100 alongside MU, AMAT, and GEV — recognition of semiconductor equipment's central role in the AI economy
- **When:** Effective week of March 6-9, 2026
- **Impact:** +3-5% from passive index fund rebalancing and increased institutional allocation. S&P 100 inclusion drives ~$2-4B in incremental passive buying
- **Probability:** HIGH (already announced and effective)
- **Market expectation:** Largely priced in after +5.93% move on March 9
- **Our edge:** The ongoing passive buying effect continues for 2-4 weeks post-inclusion. Second-order effect: more analyst coverage and options liquidity

## 2. Q3 FY2026 Earnings (April 22, 2026)

- **What:** Quarterly earnings report. Last quarter beat EPS by 8.48% and revenue by 2.13%. Consensus expects ~$1.35 EPS on ~$5.7B revenue
- **When:** April 22, 2026 (after market close)
- **Impact:** +10-15% on beat (history of strong positive reactions); -8-12% on miss
- **Probability:** HIGH for beat (8 consecutive quarters of beats, guidance typically conservative)
- **Market expectation:** Consensus at $1.35 EPS; street models $5.6-5.8B revenue
- **Our edge:** HBM capex acceleration from Samsung/SK Hynix/Micron not fully modeled yet. DRAM etch intensity increasing 30-40% with GAA transitions

## 3. HBM (High-Bandwidth Memory) Capex Supercycle

- **What:** HBM3E/HBM4 production ramp requires 2-3x more etch and deposition steps than conventional DRAM. Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron all expanding aggressively
- **When:** Ongoing through 2026-2027, accelerating each quarter
- **Impact:** Could add $2-3B incremental annual revenue, driving ~10-15% topline growth acceleration
- **Probability:** HIGH — HBM demand is confirmed by every hyperscaler capex guide
- **Market expectation:** Partially modeled but consensus underestimates the etch intensity increase
- **Our edge:** LRCX has ~50% market share in conductor etch, the critical step for HBM TSV (through-silicon via). Each HBM stack = more LRCX equipment per wafer

## 4. Gate-All-Around (GAA) Transistor Transition

- **What:** Samsung, TSMC, and Intel transitioning to GAA architecture (2nm and below) requiring fundamentally more etch steps per wafer
- **When:** H2 2026 for TSMC N2 volume ramp; Samsung 2nm in 2027
- **Impact:** 20-30% increase in etch steps per wafer = direct equipment revenue uplift
- **Probability:** HIGH — TSMC N2 is on track, first customer orders confirmed
- **Market expectation:** Consensus models gradual ramp; actual step-count increase may be higher
- **Our edge:** LRCX's Vantex and Sense.i platforms are specifically designed for GAA. They've won key design wins at TSMC/Samsung

## 5. Advanced Packaging (3D-IC, Chiplets, CoWoS)

- **What:** AI chip packaging (NVIDIA Blackwell, AMD MI400) requires new deposition and etch steps for advanced interconnects
- **When:** Continuous ramp through 2026
- **Impact:** $1-2B incremental TAM expansion for Lam's tools
- **Probability:** HIGH — every AI chip uses advanced packaging now
- **Market expectation:** Modeled as modest contributor
- **Our edge:** LRCX's SABRE and ALTUS product lines are the standards for advanced packaging deposition

## 6. China Revenue Risk/Catalyst

- **What:** US export restrictions could tighten further, impacting ~25-30% of Lam's revenue from China. Conversely, China accelerating domestic semi capex drives demand for allowed equipment
- **When:** Ongoing; potential new restrictions Q2-Q3 2026
- **Impact:** -10-15% if major restrictions; +5-10% if China domestic capex exceeds expectations
- **Probability:** MEDIUM for further restrictions
- **Market expectation:** China risk is well-known but exact impact uncertain
- **Our edge:** LRCX has been diversifying away; mature-node equipment for China is unrestricted and demand is surging

## 7. DRAM Capex Recovery

- **What:** DRAM pricing recovery and AI-driven demand (DDR5, LPDDR5X) driving memory fabs to expand
- **When:** H1-H2 2026
- **Impact:** Memory historically 30-35% of LRCX revenue; recovery adds $1-2B
- **Probability:** HIGH — Micron guiding record revenue, Samsung restarting investment
- **Market expectation:** Recovery partially priced; acceleration potential undermodeled

## 8. Share Buybacks

- **What:** LRCX has been buying back ~2.4% of shares annually, with $6.18B cash on balance sheet
- **When:** Continuous
- **Impact:** 2-3% annual EPS accretion from share count reduction
- **Probability:** HIGH — consistent buyback program
- **Market expectation:** Priced in as steady-state
