# LRCX Structured Intelligence — Learnings

*Research date: March 9, 2026*

## What This Tells Us About the Sector/Market

1. **AI capex is NOT slowing.** LRCX's 22%+ revenue growth and accelerating guidance tells us hyperscaler and memory fab spending is still accelerating, not peaking. The "AI capex bubble" narrative is premature.

2. **Memory is the next AI bottleneck.** HBM demand from NVIDIA Blackwell/Vera Rubin, AMD MI400, and custom ASICs is creating a structural shortage. Memory fabs MUST spend on Lam's tools to increase output. This is a multi-year driver.

3. **Semiconductor equipment = AI's picks and shovels.** While AI model companies come and go, every AI chip must be manufactured using Lam's equipment. This is the most durable layer of the AI stack.

4. **S&P 100 additions signal institutional recognition.** LRCX, MU, AMAT, and GEV being added to S&P 100 simultaneously shows that AI infrastructure has become blue-chip, not speculative.

5. **Margin expansion at scale.** Approaching 50% gross margins on $20B+ revenue is exceptional. This suggests Lam's pricing power is increasing — customers NEED their tools and will pay premium prices.

## Contrarian Angle — What's the Market Missing?

### Bull Contrarian:
- **Etch intensity growth is structural, not cyclical.** Every new node generation requires 15-25% more etch steps. The market prices LRCX as cyclical (hence 43x trailing P/E seems "high"), but the reality is that the trough of the next cycle will be MUCH higher than the trough of the last cycle ($14.9B FY2024 vs. the next trough likely $18-20B)
- **Services revenue is underappreciated.** Lam's installed base of >70,000 tools generates recurring service revenue that provides a floor during downturns
- **China "risk" may be China "opportunity."** Chinese fabs are accelerating domestic capex on mature nodes (28nm+), and Lam's equipment for these nodes is NOT restricted

### Bear Contrarian:
- **AI capex could plateau in late 2026.** If hyperscalers decide they've overbuilt, WFE spending could flatline in 2027
- **Valuation already rich.** At 43x trailing P/E and 30x forward, a lot of growth is priced in. Multiple compression from 43x to 30x trailing = -30% downside even with growing earnings
- **Iran conflict oil shock.** Rising oil prices and geopolitical uncertainty could compress tech multiples broadly
