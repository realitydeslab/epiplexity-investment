# LRCX Leader Profile — Timothy Archer, CEO

*Research date: March 9, 2026*

## CEO: Timothy M. Archer

- **Role:** President and CEO since January 2018
- **Background:** Joined Lam Research in 2012 as COO. Prior: 18 years at Novellus Systems (acquired by Lam in 2012). Deep semiconductor equipment engineering background
- **Education:** BS Chemical Engineering, University of Michigan
- **Tenure:** 8+ years as CEO, 14+ years at Lam
- **Compensation (FY2025):** ~$20M+ total (salary + stock awards)

### Leadership Assessment

**Strengths:**
- **Operational excellence:** Grew revenue from $9.7B (FY2018) to $20.5B TTM — doubled the business in 8 years
- **Margin expansion:** Gross margins from ~45% to nearly 50% under his watch
- **Capital allocation:** Consistent buybacks reducing share count by ~2.5% annually, steady dividend growth (~15%/year)
- **R&D focus:** R&D spend of $2.26B (11% of revenue) keeps LRCX at the technology frontier
- **Customer relationships:** Maintained and expanded key accounts at TSMC, Samsung, Intel, SK Hynix, Micron

**Concerns:**
- **Execution-focused, not visionary:** Archer is an operator, not a charismatic founder-CEO. This is fine for a capital equipment company where execution = value
- **China navigation:** Has managed the China restriction landscape well, diversifying revenue

### Insider Activity

- **Insider ownership:** 0.34% (modest for a $264B company)
- **Recent insider transactions:** Net -8.55% (typical planned selling from executive stock compensation vesting)
- **No significant open-market insider buying recently** — neutral signal
- **No unusual departures** from the C-suite in the last 6 months

### Key Leadership Team

- **Doug Bettinger** — EVP & CFO (since 2013, 12+ years). Excellent capital allocation track record
- **Sesha Varadarajan** — SVP, Global Products Group. Drives product roadmap for etch/dep systems

### Board Quality

- Board includes semi industry veterans from Intel, Applied Materials, and TSMC ecosystems
- Independent chairman structure

### Verdict

**B+ leadership.** Archer is a steady-hand operator who has delivered consistently. Not a "bet on the CEO" story like Tesla/NVIDIA, but exactly the kind of disciplined execution you want in a cyclical capital equipment business. The lack of insider buying is typical for semi equipment companies (executives sell programmatic RSU vests). No red flags.
