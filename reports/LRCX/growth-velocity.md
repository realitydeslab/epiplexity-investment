*Research date: March 9, 2026*

## Guidance vs. Actual (Beat History)

- **Last quarter:** Beat EPS by 8.48%, revenue by 2.13%
- **Pattern:** LRCX has beaten EPS estimates in 8 of the last 8 quarters
- **Management guidance style:** Conservative — consistently guides below what they deliver
- **FY2026 implied midpoint:** $22.8B revenue (consensus) vs. management likely guides to ~$22-23B

## Unit Economics

- **Revenue per employee:** ~$1.08M (improving from $955K in FY2024)
- **R&D as % of revenue:** 11% (consistent investment in next-gen tools)
- **ROIC:** 44.78% — outstanding capital efficiency
- **ROE:** 65.56% — exceptional return on equity

## Customer Concentration

LRCX sells to ~20 major semiconductor fabs globally:
- **TSMC:** ~20-25% of revenue (growing with N2/N3 ramp)
- **Samsung:** ~15-20% (HBM + foundry)
- **SK Hynix:** ~10-15% (HBM leader)
- **Micron:** ~10-12% (HBM + DRAM expansion)
- **Intel:** ~8-10% (recovering with Intel 18A)
- **China fabs (SMIC, etc.):** ~25-30% (risk factor)

## Order Backlog / Pipeline

While LRCX doesn't disclose formal backlog, semiconductor equipment companies report strong leading indicators:
- WFE (wafer fab equipment) market forecast at $100-110B for 2026 (+15-20% YoY)
- LRCX's TAM share growing due to etch intensity increases
- Memory capex recovery adds $2-3B to Lam's addressable market

## Profitability Status

**Highly profitable** — LRCX generates $6.2B in net income and $6.2B in FCF on $20.6B revenue. This is a cash machine. Not a "path to profitability" story — it's a "how much more profitable can they get" story.

## Growth Velocity Score: 9/10

Revenue accelerating, margins expanding, EPS growing 30%+, consistent beats, structural AI tailwinds. This is a high-quality cyclical growth story in the sweet spot of its cycle.
