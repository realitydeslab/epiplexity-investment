- **What:** FDA decision on sNDA for setmelanotide (IMCIVREE) in acquired hypothalamic obesity — the first-ever therapy for this condition
- **When:** March 20, 2026 (PDUFA date — 11 DAYS AWAY)
- **Impact:** +30-50% on approval; -25-35% on CRL
- **Probability:** High (70-75%). Phase 3 COMPASS trial showed -18.8% BMI reduction. Breakthrough Therapy Designation granted.
- **Market expectation:** Cautiously optimistic — price at $93.52 with $129 avg target. Not fully priced in.
- **Our edge:** BTD historically >80% approval rate. Zero approved therapies for aHO = massive unmet need. FDA already approved IMCIVREE for BBS/POMC/LEPR.
