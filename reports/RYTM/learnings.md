# RYTM Learnings & Intelligence 🧠

*Rhythm Pharmaceuticals — Last Updated: March 9, 2026*

## What This Tells Us About the Sector

### Rare Disease Pharma Is Thriving
- RYTM's trajectory ($3M→$190M revenue in 4 years) exemplifies the rare disease playbook: identify underserved populations, gain Breakthrough/Orphan designation, build specialty pharmacy infrastructure, expand indications
- The FDA has been consistently supportive of rare disease approvals — 5 of 6 BTD applications have been approved historically
- Rare disease premiums remain justified — 90% gross margins, limited competition, high switching costs

### Obesity Is the New Oncology
- The obesity market is the largest drug market opportunity since immuno-oncology
- GLP-1s dominate general obesity, but rare/genetic obesity is a distinct, untapped segment
- MC4R pathway is biologically validated but commercially unproven at scale

## Contrarian Angle: What's the Market Missing?

### The Bull Contrarian View
- The market is pricing RYTM like a single-product company ($6.4B on $190M revenue = 33x P/S)
- But if bivamelagon (oral) works, the TAM expands from rare obesity (~50K patients) to broader MC4R-related obesity (millions)
- An oral MC4R agonist in the GLP-1 era could be a differentiated complement — patients who can't tolerate GLP-1 side effects
- 2027 profitability is not priced in the stock — analysts expect $0.43 EPS but the stock trades as if profitability is years away

### The Bear Contrarian View
- 33x P/S is extreme even for a 58% grower
- GLP-1s could eventually get approved for hypothalamic obesity, eliminating RYTM's monopoly
- The rare obesity market may be smaller than modeled — patient identification remains the bottleneck
- Insider selling ahead of PDUFA is a yellow flag
