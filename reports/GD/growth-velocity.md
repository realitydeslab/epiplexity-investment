## Revenue Growth: DECELERATING but Still Strong

### Annual Revenue Trajectory
| Year | Revenue | YoY Growth |
|------|---------|------------|
| FY2021 | $38.47B | +1.4% |
| FY2022 | $39.41B | +2.4% |
| FY2023 | $42.27B | +7.3% |
| FY2024 | $47.72B | +12.9% |
| **FY2025** | **$52.55B** | **+10.1%** |
| FY2026E | $56.30B | +7.1% |
| FY2027E | $58.84B | +4.5% |

**Verdict:** Growth accelerated dramatically from FY2021 (1.4%) through FY2024 (12.9%), but is now decelerating. FY2025's 10.1% was strong but below FY2024. Consensus expects further deceleration to 7.1% in FY2026 and 4.5% in FY2027.

| Quarter | Revenue | YoY Growth | Trend |
|---------|---------|------------|-------|

**The deceleration is clear:** Q1's 13.9% growth was the cycle peak. Q4 dropped to 7.8%. The question is whether Iran conflict orders can re-accelerate this in 2026.

| FY2022 | $12.19 | +5.5% |

## Backlog / Pipeline

GD's total backlog is estimated at $90-95B (based on industry analysis). This represents ~1.8x annual revenue — providing exceptional visibility.

Key backlog components:
- **Columbia-class submarines:** $100B+ lifetime program (12 boats)
- **Virginia-class submarines:** Ongoing multi-year contracts
- **Stryker/Abrams modernization:** Multi-year IDIQ contracts
- **GDIT contracts:** $10B+ estimated backlog across government IT

**Book-to-bill ratio:** Likely >1.0x given defense spending environment, meaning backlog is growing.

## Growth Velocity Score: 7/10

**Positives:**
- Double-digit EPS growth sustained
- FCF recovery trajectory
- Massive backlog provides visibility
- Defense spending supercycle supports continued growth
- Share buybacks boost EPS ~2% annually

**Concerns:**
- Revenue growth decelerating (12.9% → 10.1% → 7.1%E)
- Gross margin compression continues
- Growth rate is solid but not exceptional for the premium valuation
- PEG of 2.46 suggests the stock is priced for more growth than consensus expects
