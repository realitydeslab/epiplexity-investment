## Revenue Growth: DECELERATING from strong base

### Annual Trajectory
| Year | Revenue | Growth |
|------|---------|--------|
| FY2021 | $38.47B | +1.4% |
| FY2022 | $39.41B | +2.4% |
| FY2023 | $42.27B | +7.3% |
| FY2024 | $47.72B | +12.9% ← Peak |
| FY2025 | $52.55B | +10.1% |
| FY2026E | $56.30B | +7.1% |
| FY2027E | $58.84B | +4.5% |

**Verdict:** Growth is decelerating from the FY2024 peak of 12.9%. However, the deceleration is gradual and from a high base — this is still WELL above GD's historical 2-3% growth rate pre-2023.

### Quarterly Trajectory (YoY Growth)
| Quarter | Revenue | Growth | EPS | EPS Growth |
|---------|---------|--------|-----|------------|
| Q1 2025 | $12.22B | +13.9% | $3.66 | +27.1% |

**⚠️ Q4 2025 Red Flag:** EPS growth collapsed to +0.5% despite 7.8% revenue growth. This suggests margin pressure, likely from higher-than-expected costs in Marine Systems and/or Gulfstream delivery mix. Profit margin was 7.95% in Q4 vs 8.61% in Q4 2024.

## Guidance vs. Actual History

GD consistently beats guidance:
- FY2024: Beat initial revenue guidance by ~$1B
- FY2025: Beat initial revenue guidance by ~4%
- Jefferies explicitly called FY2026 outlook "conservative" (Feb 2026)
- EPS surprise history: +1.37% last quarter

**Pattern:** Management sandags, then beats. This creates persistent upside surprise potential.

## Margin Analysis

| Metric | FY2021 | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Gross Margin | 16.66% | 16.80% | 15.78% | 15.43% | 15.13% |
| Operating Margin | 10.82% | 10.69% | 10.04% | 10.05% | 10.19% |
| Net Margin | 8.47% | 8.60% | 7.84% | 7.93% | 8.01% |

**⚠️ Gross margins declining** from 16.8% (FY2022) to 15.1% (FY2025). This is driven by product mix shift toward lower-margin Marine and Combat revenue. Offset by operating leverage (SGA declining as % of revenue).

## Path to Margin Expansion

1. **Marine Systems:** As Columbia-class moves from engineering to production, margins should improve (production work is higher-margin than development)
2. **Technologies:** GDIT mix shift toward AI/cyber higher-value work
3. **Aerospace:** G700/G800 are premium products with better margins than legacy models
4. **Interest expense:** Declining ($424M → $314M) as debt decreases, flowing directly to EPS

## Growth Velocity Score: 7/10

- FCF growth excellent (+24%)
- Margins stable with expansion potential
- Conservative guidance creates persistent upside surprise opportunity
- **Risk:** Q4 2025 EPS flatline needs to be a one-quarter blip, not a trend
