# General Dynamics (GD) — Catalyst Map 🎯

*Research date: March 9, 2026 | Price: $361.98*

---

## Catalyst 1: Q1 2026 Earnings (April 22, 2026)

- **What:** First earnings report of 2026. Analysts expect ~$3.68 EPS on ~$13.5B revenue. Key focus: backlog growth, GDIT AI contract wins, Marine Systems ramp
- **When:** April 22, 2026 (before market open)
- **Impact:** ±5-8% ($18-29/share). Beat could push to new ATH; miss on margins could retrace to $340
- **Probability:** High for beat — GD has a history of beating consensus (1.37% EPS surprise, 4.16% sales surprise last quarter)
- **Market expectation:** Moderate growth continuation, ~8% revenue growth
- **Our edge:** Market underestimates the Iran conflict tailwind on Combat Systems order flow and GDIT classified contract acceleration

## Catalyst 2: Iran Conflict / Global Defense Spending Supercycle

- **What:** Active US-Iran military conflict driving defense budget increases. NATO allies increasing to 3.5% GDP target. European rearmament (€800B ReArm Europe). US FY2027 defense budget likely to exceed $900B
- **When:** Ongoing through 2026, budget decisions H2 2026
- **Impact:** +15-25% if sustained conflict drives supplemental appropriations. Combat Systems and Marine Systems are direct beneficiaries
- **Probability:** High — conflict is already active, bipartisan defense spending support
- **Market expectation:** Already partially priced (stock up 33% YoY, near ATH $369.70)
- **Our edge:** Market prices a conflict premium but underestimates the DURATION and follow-on orders for munitions, vehicles, and submarine acceleration

## Catalyst 3: Columbia-Class Submarine Program Ramp

- **What:** GD's Electric Boat division is the sole builder of the US Navy's Columbia-class ballistic missile submarines (12 planned, ~$9B each). Production ramping through 2026-2030
- **When:** Ongoing deliveries and milestones throughout 2026
- **Impact:** +5-10% on milestone achievements. This is $100B+ lifetime program value. Marine Systems was $13.7B in FY2025 revenue
- **Probability:** High — program is national priority, virtually uncancellable
- **Market expectation:** Largely priced in as known backlog
- **Our edge:** Cost overruns have been the worry, but Q4 2025 showed improving Marine margins. If margins normalize toward 10%, incremental profitability is significant

## Catalyst 4: GDIT AI/Cloud/Cyber Contract Wins

- **What:** GDIT (Technologies segment) is bidding on massive government AI modernization, zero-trust cybersecurity, and cloud migration contracts. $10-12B Technologies segment revenue. Key programs: Army IT enterprise, intelligence community cloud, DOD AI/ML platforms
- **When:** Contract awards throughout 2026, especially H2 when Iran conflict drives intelligence/cyber spending
- **Impact:** +3-5% per major win ($500M+ single-award contracts common)
- **Probability:** Medium-High — GDIT is a top-3 government IT contractor
- **Market expectation:** Moderate growth; Technologies seen as steady but unexciting
- **Our edge:** GDIT's AI and cybersecurity capabilities are underappreciated. Iran cyber threats should accelerate zero-trust and AI defense spending

## Catalyst 5: Gulfstream Aerospace Delivery Cycle

- **What:** Gulfstream G700 and new G800 ultra-long-range deliveries ramping. Aerospace segment is highest-margin (~16%+ operating margin). New G400 program announcement possible
- **When:** Delivery milestones quarterly; potential G400 announcement at NBAA-BACE (Oct 2026)
- **Impact:** +3-5% on production rate increases. Aerospace is ~20% of revenue but premium margin
- **Probability:** Medium — supply chain constraints easing but labor remains tight
- **Market expectation:** Steady improvement in Aerospace margins and deliveries
- **Our edge:** Corporate jet demand from new wealth creation (AI/tech boom) may drive above-consensus backlog. G700/G800 pricing power is underestimated

## Catalyst 6: Stryker/Abrams Modernization + European Combat Vehicle Contracts

- **What:** Combat Systems revenue boost from: (a) Abrams M1A2 SEPv4 upgrades, (b) Stryker ICVVA modernization, (c) European allied vehicle procurement (potential Leopard 2 competitor bids, AbramsX next-gen demonstration)
- **When:** European procurement decisions H1-H2 2026. US Army modernization budget FY2027
- **Impact:** +5-8% on major European contract wins. Combat Systems was ~$8.5B FY2025
- **Probability:** Medium — European orders are competitive (Rheinmetall/KNDS are strong competitors)
- **Market expectation:** Slow but steady Combat Systems growth
- **Our edge:** Iran conflict creates urgency for NATO to restock and modernize ground forces faster than planned. Munitions demand (GD-OTS) is already surging

## Catalyst 7: Share Buyback Acceleration

- **What:** GD has been steadily reducing shares (269M vs 280M five years ago). With $3.96B FCF and debt/equity of only 0.38, there's significant capacity for accelerated buybacks
- **When:** Ongoing quarterly; could accelerate in H2 2026 if FCF continues to grow
- **Impact:** +2-3% EPS uplift from reduced share count annually
- **Probability:** High — management has consistently returned capital
- **Market expectation:** Continuation of current pace (~2% share reduction/year)
- **Our edge:** If FCF hits $4.5B+ in FY2026 (as growth compounds), buyback pace could accelerate above historical norms

## Catalyst 8: Supplemental Defense Appropriation (Iran War)

- **What:** Congressional supplemental defense spending package for Iran military operations, similar to Ukraine supplementals. Could be $50-100B+
- **When:** H1-H2 2026, depending on conflict intensity
- **Impact:** +10-15% if GD wins proportional share of supplemental. GD captures ~8% of DOD spend
- **Probability:** Medium-High — bipartisan support for defense supplementals during active conflict
- **Market expectation:** Some pricing in, but magnitude uncertain
- **Our edge:** GD's diversified portfolio (subs, vehicles, IT, munitions) means it captures dollars across every domain of the supplemental
