# GD Leader Profile — Phebe Novakovic

*Research date: March 9, 2026*

---

## CEO: Phebe Novakovic

- **Role:** Chairman & CEO since January 2013 (13+ years)
- **Background:** Former CIA operative; joined GD in 2001, rose through Combat Systems and became COO before CEO
- **Age:** ~68 (born 1957)
- **Compensation:** Historically ~$20-23M/year (salary + stock awards)

### Track Record

Phebe Novakovic is widely regarded as one of the best defense CEOs of her generation:

- **Revenue growth:** From ~$31B (2013) to $52.55B (2025) — 70% growth during her tenure
- **Margin discipline:** Maintained ~10% operating margins while competitors fluctuated
- **Capital allocation:** Reduced debt from peak $11.5B to $8.0B, grew dividends every year, consistent buybacks
- **Strategic acquisitions:** CSRA acquisition ($9.7B, 2018) transformed GDIT into a top-tier federal IT franchise
- **Stock performance:** GD stock roughly 3x since she took over (from ~$120 to $362)

### Leadership Style

- **Conservative guidance:** Consistently under-promises, over-delivers. Jefferies specifically noted FY2026 outlook is "conservative"
- **Operational focus:** Known for demanding accountability from business unit presidents
- **Low profile:** Rarely does media appearances; lets results speak
- **Succession risk:** At 68, succession planning is a growing question. No public successor named yet — this is a notable risk for the next 2-3 years.

## Insider Activity

- **Insider Ownership:** 0.60% (~$587M at current prices)
- **Insider Transactions:** -1.70% (net selling, typical of scheduled sales from stock-based compensation)
- **No notable large insider BUYS recently** — contrast with some of our higher-conviction picks (SOFI, AUPH, RDDT where insiders were buying aggressively)
- **Institutional Ownership:** 86.93% — very heavily owned by institutions

### Key Observation

Insider selling is routine/scheduled, not a red flag. But the LACK of insider buying is notable. In defense stocks, insiders rarely buy because they're already well-compensated with stock awards. This is neutral, not bearish.

## Recent Hires & Organizational Changes

- **Canada Gulfstream certification (Feb 2026):** Resolved a trade dispute that had threatened the Aerospace segment. Novakovic's relationship with the Trump administration likely helped here.
- **Electric Boat workforce expansion:** 20%+ hiring growth at submarine yard in Groton, CT and Quonset Point, RI — critical for Columbia-class execution
- **GDIT expanding AI/ML capabilities:** Hiring for cleared AI engineers and data scientists

## Public Statements (Q4 2025 Earnings Call, Jan 28, 2026)

Key takeaways from the earnings call:

1. **Backlog remains robust** — estimated $90B+ total backlog across all segments
2. **Gulfstream demand strong** — order-to-delivery timelines extending
3. **Submarine workforce challenge "improving"** — key positive signal for Marine margins
4. **FY2026 guidance viewed as conservative** by multiple analysts (Jefferies, UBS, Morgan Stanley)
5. **Capital return commitment** — continued buyback authorization, dividend just increased

## Assessment

**Grade: A-**

Novakovic is the right CEO for this phase. She's a proven executor managing complex defense programs. The conservative guidance style creates persistent upside surprise potential. Main risk is succession — at 68 with 13 years in the role, transition planning is increasingly important. No red flags on insider activity.
