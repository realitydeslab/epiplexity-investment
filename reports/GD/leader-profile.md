# General Dynamics (GD) — Leader Profile 👤

*Research date: March 9, 2026*

---

## CEO: Phebe Novakovic

- **Title:** Chairman & CEO (since January 2013 — 13+ years)
- **Background:** Former CIA officer, served in multiple intelligence roles before joining GD in 2001. Rose through planning, strategy, and Marine Systems before becoming CEO
- **Compensation:** ~$22M total (2024); heavily equity-weighted, aligned with shareholders
- **Track record as CEO:**
  - Revenue: $31.2B (2012) → $52.6B (2025) = +69% growth
  - EPS: $6.60 → $15.45 = +134% growth
  - Stock: ~$67 (2013 start) → $362 = +440% total return
  - Shares outstanding: 340M → 270M = 21% reduction (consistent buybacks)
  - Dividend: $2.24 → $6.00 = +168% growth (32 consecutive years of increases)

### Assessment
Novakovic is arguably the best defense CEO of the past decade. Former intelligence background gives her unique customer insight with DOD/IC. She's disciplined on capital allocation (no value-destroying M&A), relentless on buybacks, and has navigated supply chain disruptions well. She is 67 years old — **succession risk is the main concern**. No announced retirement, but the market will react negatively when succession transition begins.
