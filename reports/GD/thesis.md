# General Dynamics (GD) — Investment Thesis 📋

*Research date: March 9, 2026 | Price: $361.98 | Market Cap: $97.7B*

---

## THE CATALYST

Iran conflict + European rearmament = multi-year defense supercycle. GD captures dollars across **every domain**: submarines (Marine), vehicles (Combat), AI/cyber (GDIT), and private wealth (Gulfstream). Next earnings April 22 is the first read on whether wartime order flow is accelerating backlog beyond consensus expectations.

## THE TRADE

**Rating: HOLD / BUY-ON-DIP**

GD at $362 is 2% from ATH — not ideal entry. But the quality is exceptional: 0.37 beta fortress, 32 years of dividend growth, nuclear submarine monopoly, $4B FCF, elite CEO.

- **Entry:** Accumulate on pullbacks to $340-350 (SMA50 area). Or buy breakout above $370 for momentum.
- **Target (12-month):** $400 base, $430 bull
- **Stop-loss:** $310 (below SMA200)

## THE RISK

1. **Iran de-escalation** — removes the war premium instantly (-10-15%)
2. **Valuation premium** — 23.4x P/E is historically rich. PEG of 2.46 says growth is expensive
3. **Revenue deceleration** — 12.9% → 10.1% → 7.1%E. Growth slowing, not accelerating
4. **CEO succession** — Novakovic is 67. When she announces retirement, expect a 5-8% dip

## THE VERDICT

**GD is the boring stock you buy for boring reasons: it works.** Impenetrable submarine moat, diversified revenue streams, elite management, and a defense spending tailwind that could last years. The problem is the price — you're paying a premium for quality.

**For new money:** Wait for a 5-10% pullback. There will be one — either on a market correction, profit-taking, or Iran ceasefire rumors. Set limit orders at $340-345.

**For existing holders:** Hold. Add on weakness. The thesis hasn't changed, just the price.

**This is a 3-5% core portfolio position, not a high-conviction swing trade.** GD won't double. But it also won't cut in half. In an uncertain world, that's worth something.
