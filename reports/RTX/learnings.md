# RTX Corporation (RTX) — Structured Intelligence 🧠

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## What This Tells Us About the Sector

1. **Defense is in a multi-year super-cycle.** Iran conflict + European rearmament + Indo-Pacific buildup = sustained demand through 2030+. This isn't a one-quarter pop.

2. **Munitions are the bottleneck.** Modern conflicts consume precision munitions at rates that far exceed peacetime production capacity. The U.S. industrial base needs years to replenish. RTX, LMT, and NOC are the primary beneficiaries.

3. **Commercial aerospace aftermarket is a stealth catalyst.** Airlines can't get new planes fast enough (Boeing delays, Airbus backlog). They're spending heavily on maintaining existing fleets. P&W and Collins Aerospace benefit enormously.

4. **Defense primes are becoming AI companies.** RTX's CCA autonomy work (Sidekick), DARPA X-ray vision program, and AI-powered missile systems signal the industry's AI transformation. Defense AI spending may grow faster than enterprise AI.

## Contrarian Angle — What's the Market Missing?

### What consensus thinks:
- RTX is a solid defense play benefiting from Iran conflict
- Growth will moderate to 6-7% in FY2026

### What they might be missing:
1. **Backlog conversion acceleration.** RTX's Q4 2025 revenue growth of 12.1% was the highest in years. If this pace sustains, FY2026 revenue could hit $98B+, not $94B.

2. **FCF inflection is underappreciated.** $7.94B FCF in FY2025 (+75%) signals the business model is generating far more cash than the market expects. Once debt is below $35B, capital returns could surge.

3. **P&W is becoming a cash cow.** The GTF recall masked P&W's structural margin improvement. As recall costs wind down, P&W could contribute 40%+ margins on aftermarket work.

4. **AI/autonomy is not priced in.** The market values RTX as a traditional defense prime. The CCA autonomy work, DARPA AI programs, and next-gen sensor fusion could eventually warrant an AI premium.

### The bear case the market IS pricing:
- Near-ATH valuation (42x P/E) suggests significant optimism is already priced
- Any de-escalation in Iran would remove the geopolitical premium quickly
- Defense budget uncertainty in a potential political transition
