# RTX Corporation (RTX) — Investment Thesis

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

---

## The Catalyst
The U.S.-Iran conflict (March 2026) is consuming precision munitions — Tomahawk, Patriot PAC-3, SM-3, SM-6, StormBreaker — at unprecedented rates. RTX's Raytheon segment manufactures most of these weapons. Restocking will take 3-5 years. Simultaneously, European NATO rearmament (3.5% GDP targets) is driving record Patriot system and F-35 engine orders. Commercial aerospace aftermarket is booming as airlines maintain aging fleets. All three growth vectors are firing simultaneously for the first time since the 2020 merger.

## The Numbers
- **FY2025:** Revenue $88.6B (+9.7%), EPS $4.96 (+40%), FCF $7.94B (+75%)
- **FY2026E:** Revenue $94.3B (+6.4%), EPS $6.90 (+39%) — consensus likely too low
- **Growth accelerating:** Q4 2025 revenue +12.1% YoY, up from +5.2% in Q1 2025
- **Operating margins:** 10.5%, highest since merger, expanding
- **Backlog:** Massive, multi-year visibility

## The Trade
- **Entry:** $210 (current), or scale in with limit at $195-200 on pullback
- **Bull target (12mo):** $270 (+28.6%) — Iran + guidance raise + European orders
- **Base target (12mo):** $235 (+11.9%) — consensus growth + modest beats
- **Bear target (12mo):** $175 (-16.7%) — Iran de-escalation + P/E compression
- **Stop-loss:** $185 (-12%)
- **Position size:** 3-5% of portfolio

## The Risk
1. **Valuation is stretched** — 42x P/E, 30x forward. Much good news priced in.
2. **Iran de-escalation** would remove the geopolitical premium rapidly
3. **Stock has nearly doubled** from April 2025 lows — late entry risk
4. **P&W recall** could resurface if new defects found

## The Verdict

**BUY — but size appropriately.** RTX is the #1 U.S. missile maker in a hot war. FCF is surging. Growth is accelerating. But valuation is elevated and the stock is near ATH after an 87% run. This is a "right company, right time" story with execution risk limited by massive backlog. Best for investors who want defense exposure with diversification across military + commercial aerospace.
