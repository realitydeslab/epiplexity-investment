# RTX Corporation (RTX) — Catalyst Map 🎯

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## Active Geopolitical Catalyst: U.S.-Iran Conflict Escalation

The most significant near-term catalyst is the escalating U.S.-Israeli military operation against Iran (early March 2026). This is driving defense stock surges across the board, with RTX particularly well-positioned as the maker of Patriot missile systems, Tomahawk cruise missiles, and precision-guided munitions.

---

## Catalyst 1: Iran Conflict — Munitions Replenishment Cycle
- **What:** U.S./Israeli military operations against Iran are depleting missile and munitions stockpiles. RTX's Raytheon segment makes Patriot PAC-3, Tomahawk, StormBreaker, SM-3, SM-6, and Stinger missiles — all critical.
- **When:** NOW through 2026-2027 (multi-year restocking cycle)
- **Impact:** +15-25% on Raytheon segment revenue. Could add $3-5B in new contract awards.
- **Probability:** HIGH — conflict already active, munitions being consumed
- **Market Expectation:** Market is pricing in elevated defense spending but may underestimate the multi-year restocking tail
- **Our Edge:** The last time Tomahawk inventories were depleted, replacement orders took 3+ years. This conflict is orders of magnitude larger.

## Catalyst 2: Q1 2026 Earnings (April 20, 2026)
- **What:** First quarterly earnings reflecting Iran conflict contract awards and continued momentum from FY2025's strong finish (Q4 rev $24.24B, +12% YoY)
- **When:** April 20, 2026 (estimated)
- **Impact:** Beat could drive +5-8% single-day move
- **Probability:** HIGH — defense spending accelerating, backlog converting
- **Market Expectation:** Consensus EPS ~$1.40-1.50 for Q1; revenue ~$21-22B
- **Our Edge:** FY2025 demonstrated accelerating growth trajectory (Q4 strongest quarter). Iran conflict orders likely to boost guidance.

## Catalyst 3: 2026 Guidance Raise
- **What:** FY2026 consensus is $94.3B revenue / $6.90 EPS. Iran conflict + European rearmament + commercial aero aftermarket could force guidance well above
- **When:** April 20, 2026 (with Q1 earnings) or mid-year update
- **Impact:** +10-15% if raised materially above consensus
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH
