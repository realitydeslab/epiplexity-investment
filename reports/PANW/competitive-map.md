# PANW Competitive Map 🗺️

*Research Date: March 8, 2026*

## Direct Competitors

### CrowdStrike (CRWD)
- **Growth:** 29% revenue growth, $920M ARR
- **Strength:** Falcon platform dominance in endpoint/cloud security, strong SaaS model, network effects
- **Weakness:** July 2024 outage still haunts brand trust. More expensive per-seat pricing.
- **vs PANW:** CRWD leads in endpoint detection. PANW leads in network + platform breadth. Different entry points to same customer.

### SentinelOne (S)
- **Growth:** 29% growth, approaching $1B ARR, now GAAP profitable
- **Strength:** AI-native from day one, attractive valuation gap vs CRWD
- **Weakness:** Smaller scale, less platform breadth, stock down 32%
- **vs PANW:** SentinelOne is endpoint-focused. PANW's platform play threatens to absorb endpoint into broader suite.

### Fortinet (FTNT)
- **Strength:** Strong in mid-market, hardware firewall installed base, good margins
- **Weakness:** Less cloud-native, smaller AI/SOC capabilities
- **vs PANW:** Fortinet competes on price in mid-market. PANW targets enterprise/government platform consolidation.

### Zscaler (ZS)
- **Strength:** Cloud-native SASE leader, strong zero-trust positioning
- **Weakness:** Pure-play SASE, less breadth than PANW platform
- **vs PANW:** Zscaler competes directly with Prisma SASE. PANW's platform bundle threatens to undercut standalone SASE.

### Cisco (CSCO) + Splunk
- **Strength:** Massive installed base, Splunk acquisition adds SIEM/observability
- **Weakness:** Integration challenges, slower innovation cycle
- **vs PANW:** XSIAM directly targets Splunk displacement. Cisco's legacy complexity works in PANW's favor.

## Who Benefits from the Same Catalysts?

| Catalyst | Also Benefits |
|----------|--------------|
| AI threat acceleration | CRWD, S, FTNT, ZS — all cybersecurity names |
| Federal cyber spending | CRWD (FedRAMP), PANW (strongest), Leidos (LDOS) |
| Platform consolidation | Only PANW is truly positioned — this is their unique catalyst |
| Enterprise GenAI adoption | PANW (Prisma AIRS), CRWD (Charlotte AI) |

## Who Loses if PANW Wins?

- **Splunk/Cisco** — XSIAM directly replaces SIEM
- **IBM QRadar** — legacy SOC being displaced
- **Point product vendors** — Any single-function security tool gets absorbed into the platform
- **Zscaler** — if PANW's Prisma SASE gains share in enterprise deals

## Pair Trade Opportunities

1. **Long PANW / Short ZS** — Platform vs point product. If platformization wins, PANW gains SASE share at ZS's expense.
2. **Long PANW / Long CRWD** — Own the two platform winners. Different entry points (network vs endpoint) but converging.
3. **Long PANW / Short CSCO** — AI-native platform vs legacy integration challenges.

- Consolidation trend favors 2-3 platform winners
- PANW is the leading consolidator thesis
