## The Leader

**Nikesh Arora** — CEO since June 2018. Previously President & COO of SoftBank Group, Chief Business Officer at Google. Stanford MBA.

### Execution Track Record
- Transformed PANW from firewall company to leading cybersecurity platform
- Revenue: ~$2.3B (FY2019) → $11.3B guided (FY2026) — nearly 5x under his tenure
- Market cap: ~$20B → $130B+
- Successfully integrated multiple acquisitions (Demisto, Prisma Cloud, Cortex)
- Drove platformization strategy that defines the company narrative

### Is He Right for This Phase?
**Yes.** Rare CEO who thinks like a platform builder and executes operationally. Willing to sacrifice short-term margins (free product transitions) to lock customers into the platform — NGS ARR went from near-zero to $6.3B. CyberArk + Chronosphere acquisitions show full-stack vision. OneGov deal shows federal go-to-market maturity.

**Risk:** Net seller of shares. Compensation is equity-heavy so selling is expected, but optics aren't great during pullback.
