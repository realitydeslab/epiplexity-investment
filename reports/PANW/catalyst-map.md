- **What:** NGS (Next-Generation Security) ARR hit $6.3B growing 33% YoY in Q2 FY2026. The "platformization" strategy — consolidating fragmented cybersecurity tools into a single AI-driven platform — is the core growth engine.
- **When:** Q3 FY2026 earnings (~May 2026), Q4 (~Aug 2026), Q1 FY2027 (~Nov 2026)
- **Impact:** If NGS ARR accelerates to 35%+, stock could re-rate +20-30%. Deceleration below 25% could trigger -15%.
- **Probability:** High (33% current trajectory, strong pipeline)
- **Market Expectation:** Consensus expects gradual deceleration to ~28-30% by FY2027
- **Our Edge:** The OneGov GSA deal (60% discounts for federal agencies) and MSIAM 2.0 launch create new consolidation vectors not yet reflected in consensus. FedRAMP High authorization across all three platforms is a unique moat.

## Catalyst 3: XSIAM / MSIAM 2.0 SOC Disruption

- **What:** Cortex XSIAM: 98% MTTR reduction, 100% MITRE ATT&CK coverage, 300% ROI. MSIAM 2.0 launched Feb 2026 with industry-first Breach Response Guarantee (250 hours IR support).
