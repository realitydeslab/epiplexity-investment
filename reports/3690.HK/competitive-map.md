# Competitive Map — Meituan (3690.HK)
*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## Who Benefits from the SAME Catalysts?

### If China Consumer Recovery Plays Out:
- **Alibaba (9988.HK / BABA)** — Broad consumer exposure, Hema/Ele.me also benefit
- **JD.com (9618.HK / JD)** — Consumer spending recovery + JD's own food delivery push
- **PDD Holdings (PDD)** — Lower-tier city consumption
- **Trip.com (9961.HK / TCOM)** — Travel/hotel recovery (Meituan also in this space)

### If Drone/AI Logistics Thesis Plays Out:
- **Hesai Group (HSAI)** — Direct beneficiary as Meituan's LiDAR supplier for M-Drone 4L. Mass production of FTX LiDAR starting 2026.
- **EHang (EH)** — eVTOL/UAM company benefiting from CAAC's pro-drone regulatory stance
- **XPeng (9868.HK)** — Robotaxi/autonomous tech; benefits from same Chinese autonomous vehicle regulatory tailwind

### If Price War Subsidy Curbs Work:
- **Ele.me (within Alibaba)** — Also benefits from reduced competitive spending, but as the smaller player
- **All food delivery globally** — DoorDash, Uber Eats, Deliveroo; China moderating signals global platforms may also see less competitive intensity

## Who LOSES if Meituan Wins?

### Direct Competitors:
1. **ByteDance/Douyin** — Food delivery ambitions threatened if Meituan successfully defends 60-70% share. Douyin's food delivery is integrated with short-video content; losing the subsidy war means their delivery aspirations stall.
2. **Alibaba (Ele.me)** — Second-largest food delivery platform. Alibaba has been trying to divest non-core assets; prolonged losses in food delivery may accelerate an Ele.me exit or scale-down.
3. **JD.com** — Entered food delivery aggressively in 2025 with heavy subsidies. JD has less tolerance for sustained losses in this vertical vs. its core e-commerce business.
4. **Dada Nexus (DADA)** — JD's delivery arm; margin pressure if JD scales back food delivery ambitions

### Indirect Losers:
- **Wing (Alphabet)** — Meituan's national drone license creates a nearly insurmountable lead in commercial drone delivery. Wing's route-by-route approach in the US/Australia cannot match this scale.
- **Amazon Prime Air** — Same dynamic; FAA's BVLOS rulemaking is years behind CAAC's approach
- **Traditional food delivery couriers** — Drone scale-up gradually reduces demand for human riders on short-distance routes

## Pair Trade Opportunities

### Long Meituan / Short JD.com
- **Rationale:** JD's food delivery push is burning cash with questionable long-term viability. JD entered the market primarily to hurt Meituan, not to build a sustainable business. If competitive intensity eases, JD loses its offensive weapon while Meituan's core business recovers.
- **Risk:** JD's core e-commerce could outperform on its own merits, decoupling from the food delivery narrative.

### Long Meituan / Short KWEB (Hedge)
- **Rationale:** If you believe Meituan is specifically undervalued but want to hedge China macro risk, shorting the KWEB ETF (where Meituan is a top holding) creates a "relative value" position. You capture Meituan's alpha while neutralizing beta.
- **Implementation:** Long 3690.HK, Short KWEB in ~70% of the notional (given Meituan's weight in KWEB).

### Long Meituan + Long Hesai (Drone Stack)
- **Rationale:** If Meituan's drone delivery scales, Hesai is a direct supply chain beneficiary. This creates concentrated exposure to the drone delivery thesis without putting all capital in one name.
- **Risk:** Both are China-listed/HK-listed, so correlated on macro. Hesai has its own execution risks in LiDAR production.

## Market Structure

### Meituan's Position
```
China Food Delivery Market Share (est. 2025):
  Meituan: 60-65%
  Ele.me (Alibaba): 25-30%
  Douyin (ByteDance): 5-8%
  JD.com: 2-4%
  Others: <2%
```

### The Competitive Dynamic
The market is a near-duopoly (Meituan + Ele.me = ~90%) being disrupted by two deep-pocketed entrants (ByteDance + JD) who are willing to subsidize for market share. However:
- ByteDance and JD are using food delivery as a **feature** of their platforms, not a core business
- Meituan's food delivery is **the core business** — they will fight harder and invest more to defend it
- Historical precedent: When Meituan faced Ele.me's backed-by-Alibaba assault in 2018-2019, Meituan increased share and Ele.me plateaued. The current war has more combatants but the pattern favors the incumbent.

---

**Key Insight:** The competitive map strongly favors Meituan as the long-term winner. The question is timing — how long does the price war last? Every quarter it continues, Meituan's stock stays depressed, but every quarter it continues, Meituan also builds stronger drone/AI/international moats that competitors cannot replicate.
