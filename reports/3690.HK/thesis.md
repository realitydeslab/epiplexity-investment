## THE CATALYST

Meituan is trading at its 52-week low (HK$78.70, down 58% from peak) after issuing a FY2025 profit warning of CNY 23.3-24.3B net loss — a CNY 60B swing from the prior year's CNY 35.8B profit. The loss is not structural: it's driven by a food delivery subsidy war (JD, ByteDance, Alibaba all flooding the market with discounts), a $717M acquisition (Dingdong), and heavy investment in the world's most advanced drone delivery network and AI logistics.

**The catalyst:** FY2025 annual results due end of March 2026 — within weeks. If the loss comes in at the better end of guidance and management signals margin recovery in 2026 (supported by China's market regulator curbing subsidy wars), the stock is primed for a 30-50% rally. With 23.6% short interest, any positive surprise triggers violent covering.

**The hidden asset:** Meituan holds the world's first and only national-scale commercial drone delivery license (CAAC, April 2025). No other company globally — not Amazon, not Google Wing — has anything comparable. 740,000 orders completed, 65 routes, nighttime operations, and now scaling nationally. The market assigns approximately zero value to this.
