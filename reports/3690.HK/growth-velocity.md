### Assessment: Growth is DECELERATING

Revenue growth decelerated significantly in H2 2025:
- **FY2024:** 22% growth (accelerating from prior years)
- **Q4 2024:** 20.1% — strong momentum
- **H1 2025:** Estimated 15-18% — still solid
- **Q3 2025:** Only 2% YoY — sharp deceleration

The Q3 2025 slowdown reflects both competitive pressure (more spending to defend share = less efficient revenue growth) and China's consumption weakness. The question is whether Q3 was the trough or the beginning of a trend.

## User/Customer Growth Trajectory

- **Monthly Active Users:** 81 million (stable to slightly growing)
- **Courier network:** 5 million riders — the largest delivery fleet in the world
- **Transaction users:** Growing as Meituan expands beyond food delivery into instant retail, travel, hotel, and local services
- **Keeta international:** Achieved #1 market share in Hong Kong food delivery and #2 in Saudi Arabia within one year of launch — extremely fast user acquisition

**User growth is healthy despite competitive pressure.** The subsidy war is expensive but it's working — Meituan is not losing users, it's paying more to retain them.

## Order Backlog / Pipeline

- **Dingdong acquisition:** Adds 2,000+ fulfillment centers, 50+ cities, and an established user base in Shanghai/Yangtze River Delta
- **Drone delivery:** 740,000 orders completed by Dec 2025 across 65 routes; national license enables city-by-city scaling
- **Instant retail pipeline:** Expanding categories beyond food (groceries, pharmacy, electronics) — each category expands the addressable market

**Historical pattern:** Management tends to be conservative. The FY2024 beat was enormous. The FY2025 profit warning may similarly represent a conservative (worst-case) estimate. Watch for the actual number to come in at the better end of the range.

## Unit Economics: Deteriorating (Temporarily)

- **Profit per delivery order:** Fell 60% to CNY 0.74 in Q2 2025 (from ~CNY 1.85)
- **CLC operating margin:** Collapsed from 25.1% (Q2 2024) to 5.7% (Q2 2025) — a 19.4pp drop
- **Cause:** Deliberate subsidy matching, not structural deterioration
- **Counter-signal:** AI route optimization saving $230M/year, reducing delivery times 21% and distances 24% — these efficiency gains are real but masked by competitive spending

### The Bull Case for Unit Economics

When the price war moderates (via regulatory intervention or competitor fatigue):
1. AI efficiency gains immediately flow to margins (+$230M/year baseline, growing)
2. Dingdong integration creates procurement/logistics scale advantages
3. Drone delivery (zero marginal labor cost per order) begins replacing human couriers on short routes
4. Combined effect: margins could snap back to 15-20% within 2-3 quarters of competitive normalization

## Path to Profitability

Meituan is **already profitable on a normalized basis** — FY2024 showed 35.8B CNY net profit. The FY2025 loss is driven entirely by:
1. Price war subsidies (estimated RMB 30-40B incremental spend)
2. Dingdong acquisition costs
3. Keeta international investment
4. AI/drone CapEx

If competitive spending reverts to FY2024 levels, Meituan returns to strong profitability immediately. This is an investment cycle, not a broken business model.

---

**Bottom Line:** Revenue growth is decelerating near-term due to competitive dynamics and China macro, but the business is structurally sound. The margin collapse is deliberate strategic spending, not demand erosion. When competitive intensity normalizes, growth velocity should re-accelerate toward 15-20% with dramatically improved unit economics from AI/drone investments.
