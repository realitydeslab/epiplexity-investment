# Catalyst Map — Meituan (3690.HK)
*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## 1. FY2025 Annual Results Release (End of March 2026)
- **What:** Full-year 2025 results announcement. Meituan issued a profit warning on Feb 13, 2026 forecasting a net loss of CNY 23.3B–24.3B (vs CNY 35.8B profit in FY2024). Markets have been pricing this in — stock dropped from HK$189.60 to HK$73.60 low.
- **When:** Late March 2026 (within 2-3 weeks)
- **Impact:** ±15-25%. If loss is at the better end (CNY 23.3B) or management provides bullish 2026 guidance on margin recovery, expect 15-25% relief rally. If worse-than-warned or no visibility on price war end, further 10-15% downside.
- **Probability:** HIGH (event is certain; outcome direction is the question)
- **Market expectation:** Consensus has absorbed the loss warning. Focus shifts entirely to 2026 outlook and Q1 indicators.
- **Our edge:** Management may signal that the worst of the subsidy war is behind them. The State Administration for Market Regulation's intervention to curb excessive subsidies could be cited as a structural shift. The stock is already at 52-week lows, creating asymmetric upside on any positive surprise.

## 2. Drone Delivery National Scale-Up (Q2-Q4 2026)
- **What:** Meituan received China's first-ever CAAC national full-territory drone delivery license in April 2025 — no other company globally holds a comparable authorization. They completed 740,000 commercial drone orders across 65 routes by Dec 2025. The M-Drone 4L with Hesai FTX LiDAR enables nighttime autonomous operations. Hesai mass production starts 2026.
- **When:** Continuous through 2026; major milestones expected at each quarterly earnings
- **Impact:** +10-20% if drone order volumes scale materially (e.g., 2M+ orders in 2026). The drone logistics TAM is projected at $27.5B by 2031 (32.68% CAGR from $5B in 2025).
- **Probability:** HIGH for continued expansion; MEDIUM for reaching "material" financial contribution in 2026
- **Market expectation:** Market treats drones as a long-term option, not near-term revenue driver. Any data showing unit economics improvement or commercial scale surprises to the upside.
- **Our edge:** The full-territory license is unprecedented globally. No competitor (Wing, Amazon, DJI) holds anything comparable. Meituan can scale city-by-city without route-level approval. The market underappreciates how fast this can compound.

## 3. Dingdong Integration & Instant Retail Consolidation (H1 2026)
- **What:** $717M acquisition of Dingdong Maicai announced Feb 5, 2026. Creates 2,000+ fulfillment centers across 50+ cities. Fills geographic gaps (Meituan strong in Guangdong/Beijing, Dingdong commands Shanghai/Yangtze River Delta).
- **When:** Integration Q1-Q2 2026; first synergy numbers expected in H1 2026 results
- **Impact:** +5-10% on successful integration signals; -5% if integration proves costly
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH (integration execution risk exists)
- **Market expectation:** Market views instant retail as structurally unprofitable (Dingdong: 80M CNY profit on 6.66B revenue in Q3 2025). Consensus skeptical on near-term contribution.
- **Our edge:** Combined scale could achieve procurement/logistics efficiencies that neither could alone. The instant retail endgame favors the player with the most fulfillment centers — this consolidation removes a competitor and gains coverage.

## 4. Price War Stabilization & Margin Recovery (H2 2026)
- **What:** China's State Administration for Market Regulation has mandated curbs on excessive food delivery subsidies. JD.com, Alibaba (Ele.me), and ByteDance (Douyin) poured >RMB 10B into discounts in 2025, forcing Meituan to match. If regulatory intervention holds, margins could snap back quickly given Meituan's ~60-70% market share.
- **When:** Gradual through 2026; meaningful evidence by Q2/Q3 2026
- **Impact:** +25-40%. CLC operating margin went from ~25% to 5.7%. Even partial recovery to 12-15% would dramatically improve profit outlook.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM (competitors may find new ways to subsidize)
- **Market expectation:** Consensus expects prolonged margin pressure. If price war eases faster than expected, the stock re-rates significantly.
- **Our edge:** Historically, China tech subsidy wars burn out when competitors realize the market leader's position is unassailable. Meituan's 60-70% share with RMB 100B cash reserves makes it the most likely survivor.

## 5. Keeta International Breakeven (Q3-Q4 2026)
- **What:** Keeta (Meituan's overseas brand) became #1 in Hong Kong food delivery and #2 in Saudi Arabia within one year of launch. Management targets breakeven in overseas markets by 2026.
- **When:** Target H2 2026
- **Impact:** +8-12%. Proves Meituan's model is exportable beyond China, opening a massive long-term growth narrative.
- **Probability:** MEDIUM (aggressive timeline; Saudi market still investing)
- **Market expectation:** Most analysts discount overseas operations as cash-burning experiments. Breakeven would be a major positive surprise.
- **Our edge:** Keeta's speed to #1 in HK and #2 in Saudi Arabia demonstrates execution capability that no other Chinese tech company has shown in food delivery internationally.

## 6. AI Efficiency Gains Becoming Visible in P&L (Q2-Q3 2026)
- **What:** Meituan is investing RMB 100B over 3 years in AI/logistics. AI route optimization already saves $230M/year, reduces delivery times 21%, reduces courier distances 24%. LongCat AI improved customer service efficiency 20% and reduced peak labor costs 44%.
- **When:** Incremental improvement each quarter; most visible in Q2 2026 if competitive spending normalizes
- **Impact:** +5-10% as efficiency gains become the dominant margin story
- **Probability:** HIGH (already demonstrating savings; question is whether competitive spending masks it)
- **Market expectation:** Market acknowledges AI investment but views it as a cost center, not a near-term margin driver.
- **Our edge:** The AI savings are real but currently hidden by the price war spending. When the war cools, these efficiency gains will flow directly to the bottom line, creating a double tailwind (less spending + more efficiency).

## 7. Potential HSI / Stock Connect Rebalancing (Mid-2026)
- **What:** At HK$78.70, Meituan's market cap (~HK$481B) makes it a top-20 Hang Seng component. Any index committee rebalancing or increased Stock Connect flows from mainland investors could drive passive buying.
- **When:** June/December 2026 semi-annual reviews
- **Impact:** +3-5% from passive flows
- **Probability:** MEDIUM
- **Market expectation:** Already a major component; limited incremental effect unless foreign fund flows reverse.
- **Our edge:** If China tech sentiment improves (DeepSeek/AI enthusiasm, government stimulus), Meituan as a top HK tech stock benefits disproportionately from passive inflows.

## 8. China Consumer Stimulus / Macro Tailwind (Throughout 2026)
- **What:** China government has signaled continued consumer stimulus. Any boost to domestic consumption directly benefits Meituan as the dominant local services platform.
- **When:** Policy announcements at NPC (March), State Council meetings
- **Impact:** +5-15% depending on stimulus size and targeting
- **Probability:** MEDIUM-HIGH
- **Market expectation:** Market is deeply skeptical on China consumption recovery. Low expectations = high surprise potential.
- **Our edge:** Meituan is uniquely leveraged to consumer spending recovery — food delivery, hotel booking, travel, in-store services all benefit from improved consumer confidence.
