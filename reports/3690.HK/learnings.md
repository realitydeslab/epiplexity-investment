# Learnings & Intelligence — Meituan (3690.HK)
*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## What This Tells Us About the Sector/Market

### 1. China's Internet Platform War Has Entered a New Phase
The subsidy war in food delivery mirrors the ride-hailing wars of 2015-2018. The playbook: burn cash → consolidate → monopolize → monetize. The difference this time: multiple well-capitalized participants (Meituan, Alibaba, ByteDance, JD) means the war lasts longer, but the endgame is the same — the player with the most capital, best unit economics, and deepest moats wins. That's Meituan.

### 2. China's Low-Altitude Economy Is Real — and Investable
Meituan's CAAC national drone delivery license is a watershed moment for the global drone logistics industry. The fact that China is 3-5 years ahead of the US/EU on commercial drone delivery regulation creates a structural advantage for Chinese players. The drone logistics TAM ($5B → $27.5B by 2031) is large enough to be material for Meituan by 2028+.

### 3. AI Efficiency in Logistics Is No Longer Theoretical
Meituan's AI route optimization is delivering measurable savings ($230M/year, 21% faster deliveries, 24% shorter distances). This is not a research project — it's deployed at scale across 5M couriers. The lesson: logistics companies globally should be valued on their AI/automation investment pipeline, not just current margins.

### 4. Chinese Tech Valuations Are at Decade Lows
Meituan at ~1.2x TTM revenue with a dominant market position, massive cash reserves, and world-leading drone technology is extraordinarily cheap by any historical standard. The discount reflects China macro pessimism, geopolitical risk, and near-term losses — all of which are cyclical, not structural.

## Contrarian Angle — What's the Market Missing?

### 1. The Loss Is an Investment, Not a Problem
The market is treating Meituan's FY2025 loss as evidence of a broken business. It's not. Revenue is still growing ~20%+. The loss is entirely attributable to deliberate competitive spending and strategic investments (drones, AI, Keeta, Dingdong). Strip out the subsidy war costs and Meituan is highly profitable.

### 2. The Drone Moat Is Underpriced
No competitor globally holds a comparable regulatory authorization. 740,000 orders across 65 routes with a national license to expand anywhere is not a pilot program — it's a commercial-scale operation. The market values this at approximately zero because it's not yet P&L material. By 2027-2028, it could be a multi-billion CNY revenue stream with near-zero marginal labor costs.

### 3. Keeta's Speed Is Remarkable
Achieving #1 in HK and #2 in Saudi Arabia within one year of launch is an execution feat no other Chinese tech company has matched in food delivery internationally. If Keeta proves the model is exportable, Meituan's TAM expands dramatically beyond China. The market assigns near-zero value to overseas operations.

### 4. Short Squeeze Potential
23.6% short-sell ratio at 52-week lows with a major earnings catalyst imminent. If FY2025 results beat the warning or management provides bullish 2026 guidance, the short covering could drive a 20-30% rally in days.
