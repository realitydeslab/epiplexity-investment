# Leader Profile — Meituan (3690.HK)
*Research Date: March 9, 2026*

---

## CEO: Wang Xing (王兴)

### Background
- **Age:** ~46 (born 1979)
- **Education:** Tsinghua University (Electronic Engineering); University of Delaware (PhD dropout)
- **Founded Meituan:** 2010 (after founding Fanfou, China's first Twitter clone in 2007, and Xiaonei/RenRen, China's Facebook)
- **Track record:** Serial entrepreneur who survived China's "Groupon wars" — Meituan was one of ~5,000 group-buying startups in 2010-2012; Wang Xing was the only founder who emerged dominant
- **Reputation:** Known as China's "serial survivor" — methodical, data-driven, willing to fight brutal subsidy wars and outlast competitors

### Assessment: Is He the Right Leader for This Moment?

**YES — this is exactly the kind of war Wang Xing was built for.**

Wang Xing's entire career has been about winning wars of attrition:
- Won the "Thousand Group War" (2010-2013) against 5,000+ competitors
- Acquired Dianping (merged 2015) to dominate local services
- Fended off Alibaba/Ele.me's first major assault (2018-2019)
- Now fighting JD.com, Alibaba, and ByteDance simultaneously

His explicit statement: **"We're willing to bleed for the long-term."** This signals he won't capitulate on market share to improve near-term margins — a strategy that has historically worked for Meituan.

### Insider Activity

**Key insider signals:**
- Wang Xing holds ~8.7% of Meituan (Class B shares with 10:1 voting rights, giving him effective control)
- **No significant insider selling reported** despite the stock falling 60%+ — Wang Xing has not reduced his position
- Tencent (major shareholder with ~17% stake) has been gradually reducing its position since 2021 but retains a substantial holding
- **Company announced share buyback program** — Meituan repurchased shares during the 2025 selloff, signaling management confidence in intrinsic value

### Key Hires & Departures (Last 6 Months)
- **Keeta overseas team expansion** — Significant hiring in Saudi Arabia and additional Middle East markets
- **Drone team expansion** — Increased headcount in autonomous delivery division following CAAC national license
- **AI/algorithm team** — Continued investment in LongCat AI and delivery optimization teams
- No notable C-suite departures reported

### Public Statements on Near-Term Outlook
- Feb 2026 profit warning: Management framed the FY2025 loss as **investment-driven, not structural** — citing competitive spending, Dingdong acquisition costs, overseas expansion, and AI/drone investment
- Earnings call (Nov 2025, H1 results): Emphasized that competitive spending would moderate and AI-driven efficiency gains would become visible
- CEO framing: Positioned 2025 as an "investment year" — deliberately trading near-term profit for long-term competitive moats
- Signal: Management has historically been conservative in guidance, suggesting the profit warning may represent the low-water mark

### Governance Concerns
- **Dual-class share structure** — Wang Xing has voting control well beyond his economic ownership. This is standard for Chinese tech but means minority shareholders have limited recourse
- **VIE structure** — Standard for Chinese companies listed in HK; adds jurisdictional risk
- **Government relations** — Meituan was fined 3.44B CNY in 2021 for anti-competitive practices. Relations have improved; the recent SAMR intervention to curb subsidy wars actually benefits Meituan as the incumbent

### Verdict on Leadership

**Wang Xing is one of the most battle-tested tech CEOs in China.** His track record of winning wars of attrition (Groupon wars, Dianping merger, Ele.me defense) is directly relevant to the current challenge. The decision to invest aggressively in drones, AI, and overseas expansion while competitors burn cash on subsidies is a classic Wang Xing playbook move — absorb near-term pain to build durable moats. His refusal to sell shares during a 60% drawdown speaks volumes.

**Risk:** The current war involves multiple deep-pocketed opponents (JD, Alibaba, ByteDance) simultaneously. Even Wang Xing has never fought on this many fronts at once. But Meituan's RMB 100B cash reserves and 60-70% market share give him the strongest hand.
